> Use the **Master Zoom Ring** of the Xiaomi 17 Ultra Leica edition as a universal knob: like the Apple Watch Digital Crown, scroll lists 1:1 in direct response to your touch; while watching videos, use it as a progress ring to fast-forward and rewind; you can also continue with actions such as volume, brightness, and launching apps.
