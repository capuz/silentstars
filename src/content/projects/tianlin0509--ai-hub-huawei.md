# AI HUB-Huawei · Beta Pioneer Edition (CodeAgent)

Puts the company intranet's **Code Agent CLI** into a Windows desktop workbench: standalone sessions, multi-AI group chats, card view, hibernation recovery, history search, and automatic naming. This is the company edition of AI Hub (product name AI HUB-Huawei, internal beta pioneer edition). The current version is **v5.6.59**, synced from upstream **1.6.738** (see `community-edition.json` for the specific commit).
