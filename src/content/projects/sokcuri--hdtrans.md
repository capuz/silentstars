# Hardtrans (hdTrans)

An offline translator that translates Japanese into Korean. It runs on your own computer without an internet connection, and can be used directly with games and translation tools that used EZTrans.

Creator: Sokcuri ([Homepage](https://sokcuri.neko.kr/), [X](https://x.com/sokcuri))

Before installing, you can try it right away in the [web translator](https://trans.kuri.kr).

| Japanese | EZTrans | hdTrans |
|---|---|---|
| 肉がジュージューと焼ける | The meat Jews Jews burns | The meat sizzles as it cooks |
| バイトに行く | Goes to baito | Goes to a part-time job |
| おっと、すまんね | Husband, sorry | Whoops, sorry |
| 夢みたい | Want to see a dream | It's like a dream |
| くそっ！ | Poop! | Damn! |
| やったー！ | Did it-! | Yes! |

EZTrans results were taken from the [public comparison post](https://x.com/sokcuri/status/2111443062698451143/photo/1), and hdTrans results were translated with 0.8.4.
More comparisons are in the [translation quality benchmark](https://github.com/sokcuri/hdTrans/blob/HEAD/docs/benchmarks.md).
