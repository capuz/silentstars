# 하드트랜스 (hdTrans)

An offline translator that translates Japanese into Korean. It runs on your own computer without an internet connection, and it can be used directly with games and translation tools that used 이지트랜스.

Creator: 소쿠릿 ([Homepage](https://sokcuri.neko.kr/), [X](https://x.com/sokcuri))

You can try it right away in the [web translator](https://trans.kuri.kr) before installing.

| Japanese | 이지트랜스 | 하드트랜스 |
|---|---|---|
| 肉がジュージューと焼ける | The meat burns together with Jews | The meat sizzles and cooks |
| バイトに行く | Goes to baito | Goes to a part-time job |
| おっと、すまんね | Husband, sorry | Oops, sorry |
| 夢みたい | Want to see a dream | It's like a dream |
| くそっ！ | Poop! | Damn! |
| やったー！ | Did it-! | Yes! |

The 이지트랜스 results are from a [public comparison post](https://x.com/sokcuri/status/2111443062698451143/photo/1), and the 하드트랜스 results were translated with 0.8.4.
For more comparisons, see the [translation quality benchmark](https://github.com/sokcuri/hdTrans/blob/HEAD/docs/benchmarks.md).
