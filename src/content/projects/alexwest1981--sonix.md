# SONIX STUDIO - Professional Native Linux DAW

> **Languages:** [🇬🇧 English](https://github.com/alexwest1981/sonix/blob/HEAD/README.md) · [🇸🇪 Svenska](https://github.com/alexwest1981/sonix/blob/HEAD/README_SV.md) · [🇩🇰 Dansk](https://github.com/alexwest1981/sonix/blob/HEAD/README_DA.md) · [🇳🇴 Norsk](https://github.com/alexwest1981/sonix/blob/HEAD/README_NB.md) · [🇩🇪 Deutsch](https://github.com/alexwest1981/sonix/blob/HEAD/README_DE.md) · [🇪🇸 Español](https://github.com/alexwest1981/sonix/blob/HEAD/README_ES.md) · [🇫🇷 Français](https://github.com/alexwest1981/sonix/blob/HEAD/README_FR.md)

A modern, lightning-fast graphical Digital Audio Workstation (DAW) built with **Rust** and **egui**, featuring FL Studio-style direct timeline audio editing, magnetic loop snapping, FL Studio/VST3/CLAP plugin **scanning & cataloguing**, Soundtrap-style creative panels, an integrated modular synthesizer, 16-step Channel Rack, Piano Roll, Vocal Studio with live microphone recording, a multichannel Effects Mixer, and Touch Instruments for Linux.
