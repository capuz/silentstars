# SONIX STUDIO - Professional Native Linux DAW

> **Languages / Språk:** [🇬🇧 English](https://github.com/alexwest1981/sonix/blob/HEAD/README.md) | [🇸🇪 Svenska](https://github.com/alexwest1981/sonix/blob/HEAD/README_SV.md)

A modern, lightning-fast graphical Digital Audio Workstation (DAW) built with **Rust** and **egui**, featuring FL Studio-style direct timeline audio editing, magnetic loop snapping, FL Studio/VST3/CLAP plugin **scanning & cataloguing**, Soundtrap-style creative panels, an integrated modular synthesizer, 16-step Channel Rack, Piano Roll, Vocal Studio with live microphone recording, a multichannel Effects Mixer, and Touch Instruments for Linux. Audio runs through the system's realtime backend via **cpal/ALSA** (PipeWire works through its ALSA layer). See **[Features & Implementation Status](#-features--implementation-status)** for exactly what is real today and what is still to come. Full development plan: **[ROADMAP.md](https://github.com/alexwest1981/sonix/blob/HEAD/ROADMAP.md)**.
