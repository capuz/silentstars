# Besidka: open-source BYOK AI chat

Besidka is an open-source BYOK (bring your own key) AI chat app. Pay the AI provider only for what you use, see the price of every message, and use all the models in one chat. No subscription, no ads, no markup. Self-hostable.

## Who it's for

Besidka suits people who use AI every day for search, translation, PDFs, images or code, do not want another subscription, and are fine creating an API key in a provider's developer console. It is probably not for you if you already pay for ChatGPT Plus or Pro, Claude Pro or Google AI Pro and rarely hit their limits, since those apps do more than a chat window, or if you would rather not deal with API keys, or with prepaying credit at a provider. For scale, the maintainer's own non-work use costs between $1 and $5 a month. That is one person's usage, not a promise.
