HyperLyric displays regular line-by-line, character-by-character, and separated lyrics in Xiaomi Island, and also allows customization of lyrics style, island content, and system media cards. The main usage method is **Xposed / SystemUI mode**, while retaining a lightweight notification lyrics mode without root.

## Features

### Lyrics Display

- **Line-by-line and Character-by-character Lyrics**: Lyrics are displayed line by line; when word-level timeline is available, it highlights character by character following playback progress; when only line-level timeline is available, the entire line switches; lyrics without timeline can also scroll.
- **Separated Lyrics**: Split a line of lyrics to display on the left and right sides of the Island, while retaining character-by-character and scrolling effects; length can be fixed or change with the lyrics.
