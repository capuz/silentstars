## What it is

A PWA for keeping a D&D 5e character. Open the link and start playing right away. Characters are stored in the browser, and everything works offline after the first load. No sign-up is needed and there is no server of its own — your data stays on your device. Optionally, you can sync your devices through a file in your Google Drive ([what is stored](https://d1manych.github.io/dnd-app/privacy.html)).
