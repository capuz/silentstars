> [!IMPORTANT]
> Supports 64-bit Windows 11 (since 1.7.0, Windows 11 is the development and verification target for Windows). The Mac version was provided as test builds up to 1.20.0. Development stopped permanently on 2026-10-08, and it has been removed from the repository. The historical Releases (including the 1.6.0 Intel Mac build and the 1.20.0 Apple Silicon test build) remain as-is in the historical releases [1.6.0](https://github.com/Oiawlm/floating-transfer-station/releases/tag/v1.6.0) and [1.20.0](https://github.com/Oiawlm/floating-transfer-station/releases/tag/v1.20.0). They will not be withdrawn or updated.
