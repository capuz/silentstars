# 好好唱 · 家庭 KTV · v1.1.5

Keep the song library on a NAS at home. Open a TV, phone, or tablet and you can request songs and start singing. 好好唱 (Haohaochang) connects song organization, accompaniment production, and multi-device playback, so daily use doesn't require moving files around again and again.

[Official website](https://dunn.fun/haohaochang/) · [Download latest version](https://github.com/xudong7587/haohaochang-KTV/releases/latest) · [User guide](https://github.com/xudong7587/haohaochang-KTV/blob/HEAD/docs/USER-GUIDE.md) · [Changelog](https://github.com/xudong7587/haohaochang-KTV/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md) · [Buy the author a coffee](#support)

- Centralized song library management on the NAS. Automatically recognizes song metadata and matches song/album cover art, singer photos, and synced lyrics. Items that need checking stay in the task list, and you can also organize in bulk or edit manually.
