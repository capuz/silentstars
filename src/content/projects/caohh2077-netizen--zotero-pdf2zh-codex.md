# Zotero PDF2zh × Codex

On Windows, right-click an English paper in Zotero and use your own ChatGPT account to generate a bilingual PDF with English on the left and Simplified Chinese on the right. Both the original and the translation are saved in your library. PDF splitting and layout are done locally. The segments to be translated are sent to OpenAI through Codex, and usage counts against your Codex quota. Translating with a subscription does not require an API key.
