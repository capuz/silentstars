**V1.3-B22 (0.4.0)** supports reviewing proposed changes by finished product, plant, engineering version, and specified BOM line. Orders are matched by explicit version reference, and configuration, execution status, and dates are listed separately as evidence still to be verified. The web and Markdown reports share the same review results. [Engineering version fields and examples](https://github.com/JulieY7899/bom-ec-change-impact/blob/HEAD/docs/engineering-versions.md)

V1.2-B19's procurement commitment, receipt, return, and reversal checks remain available. [Procurement import guide](https://github.com/JulieY7899/bom-ec-change-impact/blob/HEAD/docs/procurement.md)

The local workflow turns business questions into review parameters that can be confirmed. Preflight results can be carried into "01 Data Preparation," and rule-based review is then run against the current snapshot to generate a report. Five perspectives are supported: R&D, procurement, sales, manufacturing, and engineering. "03 Impact Review" gives engineering roles a standalone PPT knowledge reference. [Workflow guide](https://github.com/JulieY7899/bom-ec-change-impact/blob/HEAD/docs/workflow.md)
