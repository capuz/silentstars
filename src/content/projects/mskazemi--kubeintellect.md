KubeIntellect is an open-source, LLM-orchestrated multi-agent framework for **autonomous Kubernetes operations**. Ask a question in plain English — it fans out to specialized agents that query **kubectl**, **Prometheus** (PromQL), and **Loki** (LogQL) live, correlates the evidence, and answers. Any change to the cluster pauses for **explicit human-in-the-loop…
