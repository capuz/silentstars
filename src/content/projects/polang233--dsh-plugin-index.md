# Open Plugin Market · DSH Plugin Market

**DeepSeek Harness (DSH) Plugin Index and Daily Plugin Recommendations** · Open Plugin Market

[Official site](https://dshopen.org/zh/) &nbsp;|&nbsp; [Usage statistics](https://dshopen.org/zh/stats/) &nbsp;|&nbsp; [Submit a plugin](https://github.com/polang233/dsh-plugin-index/issues/new?template=plugin-add.yml) &nbsp;|&nbsp; [English](https://github.com/polang233/dsh-plugin-index/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

The community **plugin market** and **plugin index** for DeepSeek Harness (DSH), listing hundreds of DSH community plugins: tools, skills, memory, interfaces, and workflows, and more. You can add it as an index source for official "Discover," or install a plugin to get a standalone open market page in DSH. You can also search, filter, and like plugins directly on [dshopen.org](https://dshopen.org/zh/).

**Not just star🌟 counts.** Default recommendations are updated daily and focus on plugins that are growing fast, updated often, and high quality. They also take verification, downloads, and stars into account, so classic works don't dominate the list. New projects and new works from small developers have a chance to appear on the first…
