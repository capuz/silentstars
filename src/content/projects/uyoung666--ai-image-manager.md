## v2.0.0 Initial Positioning

v2.0.0 is a brand new public release starting point for AI Image Manager. This project is aimed at photographers, content creators, designers, and Windows users with long-term photo libraries, providing a photo management workflow that works around local folders: first index in-place, then find photos through natural language, similar images, metadata and folder structure, and finally complete organizing, curating, analyzing, exporting or sharing.
