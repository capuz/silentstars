## 📖 Introduction

Momei Blog is a modern blogging platform built on **Nuxt**. It aims to provide technical developers and cross-border content creators with an efficient and intelligent authoring experience through AI and deep internationalization support. Whether it's smart translation, automated summarization, or multi-language route management, Momei helps you easily connect with readers around the world.

## ✨ Core Features

-   **AI-driven**: Deeply integrated AI assistant supporting fully automated translation, smart titles, summary generation, and more, greatly improving authoring efficiency.
-   **Multimodal content workflow**: Already supports AI image generation, ASR, reusable voice input, manual distribution to Memos / WechatSync, and scheduled task automation, covering the complete pipeline from idea capture to publishing.
-   **Native internationalization (i18n)**: Built-in multi-language support, deeply integrated from the UI to content management, helping you easily reach readers worldwide.
