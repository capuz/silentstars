## 📖 Introduction

Momei Blog is a modern blogging platform built on **Nuxt**. It aims to give technical developers and cross-border content creators an efficient, intelligent creative experience through AI and deep internationalization support. Whether it's smart translation, automated summarization, or multilingual route management, Momei helps you easily connect with readers around the world.

## ✨ Core Features

-   **AI-driven**: Deeply integrated AI assistant that supports fully automatic translation, smart titles, summary generation, and more, greatly improving creation efficiency.
-   **Multimodal content workflow**: Already supports AI image generation, ASR, reusable voice input, manual distribution to Memos / WechatSync, and scheduled task automation, covering the full pipeline from idea capture to publishing.
-   **Native internationalization (i18n)**: Built-in multilingual support, deeply integrated from the UI to content management, helping you easily reach readers worldwide.
