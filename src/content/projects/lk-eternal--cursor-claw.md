# Cursor Claw

Remote collaboration application — Transform Cursor into a 24/7 online digital employee, collaborating with AI anytime and anywhere through Feishu / WeChat.

## Why is it needed?

The interaction of Cursor Agent is locked in the local IDE, and once you leave the computer, all AI collaboration comes to a halt.

**Cursor Claw** breaks this limitation:

- AI questions are sent to your phone via Feishu bot or WeChat, and after you reply, AI continues working automatically.
- Even if the Cursor session disconnects, the daemon can automatically reconnect and initiate a new session.
- Supports private chat + group chat multi-session parallelism, with each session having an independent workspace.
- Supports scheduled tasks and temporary independent Agents, allowing AI to execute tasks automatically as planned.
- Supports **multi-node workflows**: requirement analysis → coding → review → delivery, with nodes that can be rejected and redone.
- Remotely manage Agents, MCP, Rules, Skills, scheduled tasks, and workflows through the Feishu / WeChat command system.
