# DO Knowledge Studio

A local-first knowledge studio for rich notes, knowledge graphs, mind maps, client-side semantic search, TRIZ inventive problem-solving, and AI-assisted synthesis — all inside your browser with **zero server backend required**.

The visual language follows **Editorial Paper & Saffron**: warm paper backgrounds, crisp ink typography, and a purposeful saffron accent. See [DESIGN.md](https://github.com/d-oit/do-knowledge-studio/blob/HEAD/DESIGN.md) for the complete design token reference.

> Screenshots are generated by `scripts/generate-readme-screenshots.mjs` (Playwright, light/dark + desktop/mobile) so they do not go stale; see `plans/206-onboarding-1064-2026-11-08.md`.

## Why local-first

Your notes stay in this browser (IndexedDB + a small localStorage envelope). No account, no server. Only opt-in flows leave the device: AI providers, web research, sync signaling and model downloads. Privacy details: [ADR 045](https://github.com/d-oit/do-knowledge-studio/blob/HEAD/plans/ADRs/045-sync-threat-model.md). Live demo: <https://do-knowledge-studio.vercel.app>
