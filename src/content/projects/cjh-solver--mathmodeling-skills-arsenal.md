# MathModeling Skills Arsenal (数模军械库)

Organizes problem reading, modeling and solving, scientific plotting, paper writing, and final-draft review into traceable AI agent workflows.

Suited to modeling teams that already have the problem, data, and human judgment, and want clearer division of labor, evidence, and deliverables between AI and people. Covers CUMCM, MCM/ICM, MathorCup, and other contests. Specific formats and AI-use requirements follow the official rules of the current year's contest.
