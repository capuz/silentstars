# Ficbook Exporter — FB2, EPUB, PDF and TXT Downloader

Userscript for downloading works from [Ficbook](https://ficbook.net) and reading them offline.

## How the button looks

</p>

The "Download" button is added to the standard action panel of the work.

Supported formats: **FB2**, **EPUB**, **PDF** and **TXT**.

## Features

- downloading a work from the work page or any open chapter;
- loading all available chapters while preserving their order and titles;
- export to **FB2**, **EPUB**, **PDF** and **TXT**;
- saving the cover in FB2, EPUB and PDF;
- support for JPEG, PNG and WebP covers with conversion for reader compatibility;
- preserving footnotes and text structure;
- table of contents in FB2, EPUB and PDF;
- clickable table of contents with page numbers in PDF;
- saving available information from the work header:
    - author, co-authors, translators, beta readers and gamma readers;
    - links to participant pages;
    - fandom, pairings and characters;
    - rating, direction, status and size;
    - series, tags, description, notes and publication conditions;
    - links to the original work page.
