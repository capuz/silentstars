# Brain Core

> A self-hosted workspace for notes, visual thinking, recordings, and personal knowledge.

Brain Core runs on a computer you control. Its database, uploads, and optional
recordings live in persistent local volumes. The standard Docker installation
needs no cloud account, API key, GPU, or external sync service.

**Notebook** adds handwriting-focused pages alongside rich-text notes and Infinite: editable pen/highlighter strokes, typed text boxes, multiple sheets and selectable paper. See the [Notebook guide](https://github.com/lizard-wizard42/Brain_Core/blob/HEAD/docs/NOTEBOOK.md) for controls, saving and device limits.

Android **1.1.5** also preserves automatic voice suggestions in the native timeline and reuses confirmed voice references. Android 1.1.4 introduced native reminder and transcription notifications, a white status-bar icon, and background startup preparation. Notes supports direct checklist toggles, recoverable deletion with Undo and Trash, and default reminder dates in your device timezone.
