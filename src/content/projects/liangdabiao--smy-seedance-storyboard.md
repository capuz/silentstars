# smy-seedance-storyboard · Shangmeiying-Style Short Drama Generator

> In one sentence: **You give it a story, and it returns a set of Shangmeiying-style short drama production documents that you can directly use to generate images and videos.**

"Shangmeiying" = Shanghai Animation Film Studio (上海美术电影制片厂), which has the flavor of retro hand-painted animation like *Monkey King: Havoc in Heaven* (《大闹天宫》), *Tales of Tianshu* (《天书奇谭》), and *Nine-Colored Deer* (《九色鹿》): generalized character design, hand-crafted linework, and large flat areas of mineral-pigment color.

## What can I use it for?

For example, you say:

> "Make a 5-episode Shangmeiying-style short drama about Wu Song fighting a tiger"

It will then give you five things, all in Chinese, copy-and-use ready:

| What you get | What it's for |
|----------|----------|
| ① Script | A 5-episode storyboard script, 15 seconds per episode, with storyteller narration, dialogue, and subtitle-card timing |
| ② Color palette | A color list specific to this series (e.g., 朱砂红/鎏金/墨黑/青瓷绿 = cinnabar red / gilded gold / ink black /…
