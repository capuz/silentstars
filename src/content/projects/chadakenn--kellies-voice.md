# Kellie's Voice 🎙️📺

**Turn your Android TV into a Home Assistant voice companion, without turning your TV into a dashboard.**

Kellie's Voice is a native Android TV / Google TV app with a companion **Home Assistant custom integration**. Talk to your Home Assistant Assist agent, see useful answers in a floating overlay, receive quiet smart-home announcements (optional spoken with audio ducking), and pop up security-camera views **over what you're already watching** when Android allows it.

**Current public release: [v0.16.14](https://github.com/chadakenn/Kellies-Voice/releases/tag/v0.16.14)** (signed Android APK + matching HACS 0.16.14). For everyday use, install the [latest signed stable release](https://github.com/chadakenn/Kellies-Voice/releases/latest). Testers may opt into beta updates **separately in HACS and on their TV**; both default to stable. Routine development builds stay as Actions artifacts, not public Releases. [Learn how beta testing works](https://github.com/chadakenn/Kellies-Voice/blob/HEAD/docs/BETA_TESTING.md).
