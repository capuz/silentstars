# Welcome to **AI-PM**!

**AI-PM** (AI Product Manager) is an integrated knowledge site for AI product management. It offers knowledge and practice on AI product methodology, models and tools, prompt engineering, agents and workflows, and evaluation and iteration, to help you learn AI product design faster and more deeply.

Site address: [https://aipm.ac](https://aipm.ac)

* * *

## Contents

- Product methodology: requirements analysis, user research, product design, project management
- AI fundamentals: LLM capability boundaries, prompt engineering, RAG, agents and workflows
- Tools and platforms: mainstream AI products and platforms, evaluation and feedback systems
- Case studies: AI product teardowns and retrospectives

* * *
