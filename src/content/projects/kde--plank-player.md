# Plank Player

Multimedia Player for playing local files on Plasma Bigscreen

#### Requires:

- Plasma Bigscreen (control library)
- Gstreamer (All Plugins)
- QtMultimedia Library, Plugins and QML Modules
- QML Labs FolderList Modules
- Kirigami2
