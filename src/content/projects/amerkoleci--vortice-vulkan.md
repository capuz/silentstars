# Vortice.Vulkan

**Vortice.Vulkan** is **.NET 9** and **.NET 10** low-level bindings for [Vulkan](https://www.khronos.org/vulkan/), [VulkanMemoryAllocator](https://github.com/GPUOpen-LibrariesAndSDKs/VulkanMemoryAllocator), [SPIRV-Cross](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/SPIRV-Cross) and [shaderc](https://github.com/google/shaderc).
