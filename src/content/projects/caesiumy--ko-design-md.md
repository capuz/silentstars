# ko-design-md

**Design languages of Korean brands, condensed into a single cited DESIGN.md**

**🔗 Live catalog → [getdesign.kr](https://getdesign.kr)**

---

## What is ko-design-md?

Ask an LLM or coding agent to "make it like Toss" and the results differ every time, and they don't come out looking like that brand. The design materials brands publish are scattered across different places in inconsistent formats, so they're hard to drop directly into a context.

ko-design-md is an open catalog that condenses the design languages of Korean brands (colors, typography, spacing, components, principles) into one **DESIGN.md** per brand. Every entry follows the same format, so people can read them side by side and agents can take them and use them as they are. You can see which brands are included at [getdesign.kr](https://getdesign.kr).

## Using it right away

### 1. Copy from the site

Pick a brand on [getdesign.kr](https://getdesign.kr), then press the **Copy full DESIGN.md** button on the detail page, or download it as a file and paste it into the chat or IDE you're using. No sign-up or installation is required.
