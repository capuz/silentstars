## ❄️ About the project
**YKTFLOW** is a project born at the coldest point in the world. Its task is to provide stable and fast network access despite any obstacles. The system operates on the principle of "zero involvement": it finds sources on its own, checks them for "survivability" on its own, and updates the showcase for users on its own.

### Key features:
- 🚀 **Turbo-Check**: Multithreaded check (500+ threads) written in Go.
- 🧊 **Smart Cache**: Filtering of duplicates and "zombie servers".
