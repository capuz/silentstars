# Verix

**Digital reputation platform for school students in IT**

Prove your skills with AI, find internships and grants, and level up with a personal roadmap.

[Open Verix](https://verix-khaki.vercel.app) · [GitHub Pages mirror](https://romiisromie.github.io/verix/) (without AI)

---

## Features

- **Profile** — reputation score, skills, XP and level, day streak, weekly challenges, achievements. Editing, saving as PDF, “Share”.
- **SkillProof** — you provide a link to a public GitHub repository and/or a project description. AI reviews the code, README and commit history and assigns an overall score, originality, complexity and level. The skill appears in the profile with a “code ✓” mark.
- **TalentMatch** — internships, grants, hackathons and competitions from `data/opportunities.json`. Matching is based on the profile and verified skills; filters by type, city and grade; deadlines. Cards are swiped, and an application can be written directly in the app.
- **Rankings** — students, schools, skills and competitions; student profiles and chat.
- **EduPath** — a personal roadmap: AI builds 4 phases toward the goal, grade, hours per week and skills. Tasks are checked off, and…
