# Zero-Quake

## Notices

- Please use this app correctly, based on accurate knowledge about earthquakes.
- Because information is retrieved over the internet, delays or missing information may occur.
- We do not restrict use in videos or live streams. However, please check for yourself and use at your own responsibility regarding third-party rights attached to the information sources, synthesized voice systems, and other materials used in this app.
- The real-time shaking information shown on the map includes everyday vibrations such as living vibrations.
- Information from Early-Est is experimental and has not been verified by experts. Enable it at your own responsibility.
