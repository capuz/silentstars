# Zero-Quake

## Notes

- Please use this app correctly, based on accurate knowledge of earthquakes.
- Because information is obtained over the internet, delays or missing information may occur.
- We do not restrict use in videos or streams. However, please check the third-party rights attached to the information sources, synthetic voice systems, and other materials used in this app yourself, and use them at your own responsibility.
- Real-time shaking information shown on the map includes everyday vibrations, such as those from daily life.
- Information from Early-Est is experimental and has not been verified by experts. Enable it at your own responsibility.
