Provides natural, readable, fluent, and clear writing guidelines for Chinese or English copy, while staying within the bounds of the facts. Applies to blogs, speeches, PPT copy, website and product introductions, personal bios, WeChat official account posts, and social media posts. The rules have a clear bias but do not require all content to follow the same structure.

## Revision Effects

| Common phrasing | How oil-tone handles it |
| --- | --- |
| `主流 AI Coding Agent：先选工作方式` | `主流 AI Coding Agent 的工作方式` |
| `单文件 HTML 的结构：简单，但不随意` | `单文件 HTML 的基本结构` |
| `先把流程搞顺` | Describe the specific things to check, modify, or confirm |
| `真正重要的是，我们理解了工具的边界` | End right after the content has been explained |

These changes are not meant to uniformly shorten sentences. They remove attitude, rhythm, and elevation that the material does not support, so headings and body text keep providing useful information.
