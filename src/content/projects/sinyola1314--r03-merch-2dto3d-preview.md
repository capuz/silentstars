# R03 Red Panda 3D Preview Machine

**All-in-one toolbox for previewing merchandise from 2D to 3D**

English name: R03 Merch 2D-to-3D Preview

Turn PNG design files into rotatable 3D merch mockups. Supports acrylic, badges, paper cards, boxes, and combinations. The color palette, watermark, background, and text are set in one place under “Settings”.

- **Online use**: https://sinyola1314.github.io/R03-Merch-2Dto3D-Preview/
- **Offline use**: Download `index.html` from this repository and double-click it to open in a browser. No internet connection or installation is required.
- **Current version**: V2.6.29 (2026-11-27)
- **Author**: 囿一九_YOKKY
- **Language**: The first section under “Settings”, “语言 / Language”, lets you switch between Chinese and English. You can also switch on the agreement page, in the top-right corner. On first open, the language is chosen based on your browser language, and your choice is remembered afterward. Adding `?lang=en` or `?lang=zh` to the URL sets the language directly, which is handy for sharing with international friends.
