# Rivolt

> Open-source tracker for Rivian owners, free during the beta: what your Rivian actually
> costs. Charging cost from what Rivian billed, your rate, or a labelled estimate, trips, efficiency and lease miles.

**[Try the demo](https://demo.rivolt.dev)** (sample truck, no sign-in)
 · **[Use it at rivolt.dev](https://rivolt.dev)** (sign in with Google or GitHub)
 · **Self-host:** [docker compose](https://github.com/apohor/rivolt/blob/HEAD/docker-compose.yml), [Helm chart](https://github.com/apohor/rivolt/blob/HEAD/deploy/helm)
 · [Sign-up guide](https://github.com/apohor/rivolt/blob/HEAD/docs/SIGNUP.md) · [MIT license](https://github.com/apohor/rivolt/blob/HEAD/LICENSE)

Rivolt is an **unofficial** companion app for Rivian vehicles. It is not
affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Rivian.

## What it does

- **Every charge and trip priced from what Rivian billed, your rate, or a labelled estimate**, with the source of each
  number shown (billed, home rate, network estimate, or entered by you).
- **Trip planner** that estimates what a road trip will cost, with weather
  and efficiency analysis.
- **Lease miles:** enter your allowance and term and see projected miles…
