# 拾音（ShiYin）

An Android local music player built around lyrics: **tap any line of lyrics to jump straight to it**; **translate foreign-language lyrics with one tap, using either cloud AI or a local offline engine**; **for Japanese vocals or songs without lyrics, AI recognition generates timestamped lyrics in real time and translates them line by line**; **sleep timer**.

> ### 💝 Enjoying this project? Support the author
> 拾音 is completely free, ad-free, and has no in-app purchases. If you like it, feel free to buy the author a coffee on 爱发电. Every bit of encouragement keeps updates coming ❤️
