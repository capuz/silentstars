# I AM ML Engineer

A repository that digs into what you've read down to "why it was built this way"

**Full-text search** · **Topic-based table of contents** · **Diagram rendering** · **Automatic following of the system theme**

It reads just as well on a phone.

## About

I read and summarize papers, technical blogs, and open-source repositories. The goal is not summaries but notes in a **form you can use for decisions**.

Each write-up follows these principles:

- **Check the original**: Figures and quotations are verified directly against the original source. Secondhand material is not copied over
- **State limitations**: Note the conditions under which results were obtained and how far they generalize
- **Cross-link**: Materials addressing the same problem are linked to each other
- **Points of application**: Principles from what I read that can be transferred elsewhere are extracted separately
