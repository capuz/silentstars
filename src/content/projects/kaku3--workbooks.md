# Work Problem Collection for Engineers

## Introduction

Let me start with a question.

**"What is the work of an engineer?"**

In the old days, there were many jobs with teams of about 10 people, and "engineers" existed who wrote **only** programs. In recent years, however, many jobs have teams of 2-3 people, and it doesn't seem enough to write **only** programs.

In other words, a typical engineer today seems to be doing **"a job like playing baseball with 3 people, a game that used to need 9."**

Furthermore, besides the 9 players on the field, the coach's and manager's duties also have to be covered by the members who are present. If you try to fill the gaps left by the 6 missing people, everyone seems to need to be a **playing manager with a wide range of coverage**.

As an aside, since there aren't even substitutes on the bench, **taking paid leave is also difficult**.

And since 2024, the situation has changed even further.  
AI (GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) has rapidly become practical, and the way engineers work is itself changing.  
For better or worse, it feels like **"a new team member called AI"** has joined. I'll explain in detail later.
