## Arabic

> **In short:** محافظ is a platform where completed works (a game, anime, series, film, manga, or book) are rated, stating what they contain and to what degree, so people can be informed before they start them, without spoilers. It identifies cautions and produces a ready card you can share. Ratings come from followers to followers, and the project is open source, so you can contribute to it.

**محافظ** is a free, open-source platform where followers classify the creative works they have finished, stating the cautions each one contains and their degree, to inform those who want to watch, play, or read them later. It follows conservative standards derived from Islamic values. You choose the work you finished, specify its cautions and their degrees, and get an elegant card ready to share.

The site requires no account creation or login.
