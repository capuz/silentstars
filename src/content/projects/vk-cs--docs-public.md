# VK Cloud Documentation

Here is the [public documentation for VK Cloud](https://cloud.vk.com/docs). You can suggest edits or make them yourself.

## Suggest Changes

If you notice a typo or error in the documentation or want to add to a section, you can suggest changes in two ways:

- Open an Issue and write your comments and suggestions to us; we will consider the possibility of implementation and inform you of the decision.
- Open a Pull Request and propose your additions and corrections yourself (more details in the [how-to-contribute.md](https://github.com/vk-cs/docs-public/blob/HEAD/guides/how-to-contribute.md) file).

To suggest changes, you must read the "Contributor License Agreement" and confirm your agreement with its terms. Detailed information on how to do this and links to the text of the Agreement are provided in the [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/vk-cs/docs-public/blob/HEAD/CONTRIBUTING.md) file.
