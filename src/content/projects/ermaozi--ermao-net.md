## Airport Recommendation Criteria

I often receive private messages and comments asking me to review certain airports. Here are my basic recommendation criteria.

- The airport has been operating for 2 years or more.
- The official website is accessible from mainland China, and it supports payment methods commonly used there, such as Alipay or WeChat Pay.
- It has a fairly active user community.
- It has a working support ticket channel.
- The price is not too high.
- It does not frequently drop connections, and its subscription links do not often change automatically.
- There is not a lot of negative online reputation or disputes.
- If you know of an airport that meets these criteria, feel free to recommend it in the comments or by private message!
