## Airport Recommendation Instructions

Recently, I often receive private messages or comments asking me to review certain airports. Here is a brief explanation of the recommendation criteria.

- The airport has been in operation for 2 years or more.
- The official website can be accessed in mainland China and supports commonly used payment methods such as Alipay or WeChat.
- There is a relatively active user community.
- There is an effective ticket handling channel.
- The price should not be too expensive.
- It should not frequently disconnect or have subscription links that change automatically.
- There should not be a lot of negative information or disputes online.
- If anyone has airports that meet the criteria, feel free to comment or send a private message to recommend!
