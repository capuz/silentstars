## What is this

VLA Expert Skill is a **composable domain knowledge module**—it does not manage your development process or replace your workflow tools. It only does one thing:

**When your AI assistant encounters VLA (Vision-Language-Action) related questions, it provides expert-level judgment.**

Usually, it stays quiet. When you ask about VLA architectural choices, paper value, directional judgments, or deployment pitfalls, it gets activated, provides in-depth analysis based on over 332 papers, and then hands back control.

> 💡 It is **complementary** to development process frameworks like [Superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers): Superpowers manages **how you write code**, while VLA Expert Skill manages **what decisions you make in the VLA field**. Both can be installed simultaneously without interference.
