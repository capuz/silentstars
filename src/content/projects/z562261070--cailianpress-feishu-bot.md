# 🚀 A-share Microstructure Monitoring and Quantitative Review System

This is an automated trading assistance system that combines **real-time information push** and **in-depth quantitative review**. It can gain insights into market sentiment fluctuations, analyze the sustainability of themes, and help traders build a "big picture".

---

## 🧘 Core Philosophy: The Way of Quantification
> **"The great way is extremely simple, and empirical evidence comes first."**
> This system does not pursue predictions but presents the underlying logic of the current market by statistically analyzing micro data (limit-up, limit-down, circuit breakers, hot spots), helping you to follow the trend.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 1. 📢 Real-time Information Center (Caixin)
- **Precise Push**: Real-time capture of Caixin telegrams, automatically marking important intelligence through specific keywords (e.g., favorable, important, urgent).
- **Information Filtering**: Separates important information from general information, generating a daily Markdown telegram collection.
