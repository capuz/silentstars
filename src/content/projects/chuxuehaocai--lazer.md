## What it can do

**Listening**

- Real music library, search, recommended playlists, Private Radar, account and favorites.
- Artist pages show popular songs and paginated albums; album details include a description and the full track list, and tapping a name takes you straight in.
- Hot / latest comments, paginated loading, likes and unlikes. Guests can read, and you need to log in to post.
- System media controls: recognized on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS.

**Lyrics**

- LRC / YRC parsing, per-character emphasized spring animation, a 35% focus band, progressive masking, and distance-based blur.
- Adjust font size and weight on the fly, with instant preview; multi-select copy and export to PNG are also supported.
- Lyrics follow the music. After you scroll the lyrics manually, they smoothly return to following.
