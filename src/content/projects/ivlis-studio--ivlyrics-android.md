# ivLyrics Android

Korean | [English](https://github.com/ivLis-Studio/ivLyrics-Android/blob/HEAD/README_EN.md)

Enjoy the music you listen to on Spotify along with its lyrics. ivLyrics Android displays lyrics, translations, and pronunciation guides for singing along, synced to the song that is playing. You can view them large in a separate app, or use a module to see lyrics inside Spotify. Spotify handles music playback.
