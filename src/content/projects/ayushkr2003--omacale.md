# Omacale

A [Caelestia](https://github.com/caelestia-dots/shell)-style desktop shell for [Omarchy](https://omarchy.org).

*The Glass theme (Settings › Glass): frosted drawers over Hyprland's blur, lit along their edge.*

Omacale is a single Omarchy bar plugin (`omacale.bar`). It runs inside Omarchy's own shell: no extra daemon, no build step, no required Hyprland changes. Caelestia provides the look; Omarchy stays the engine.

## Features

- **Frame and drawers**: Caelestia's shader-drawn screen frame, with dashboard, launcher, sidebar, utilities, session and settings sliding out of it.
- **Liquid motion** (optional, off by default; Settings › Motion): the drawers move on springs that overshoot and settle and can be turned round mid-way, are joined to the frame by a liquid neck that is thickest halfway, are a little narrower on the way and round as a drop before they square up, bulge on the overshoot, and sag and shake once as they land. They can slide out of the frame as Caelestia's do, or grow out of it from a narrow drop.
