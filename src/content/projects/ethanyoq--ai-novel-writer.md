<h1 align="center">AI 小说作家 / AI Novel Writer</h1>

  A local-first AI long novel writing tool for Chinese creators: it transforms the process from "premise → character → worldview → chapter blueprint → draft → review → revision → final draft" into a memory-enabled production line, allowing the AI to write only the current chapter each time, always referring to the blueprint, and passing through review for each chapter.

## 🎯 One-sentence description

AI novelist is not a chatbot, nor a simple AI writing assistant.

It only does one thing: **turn the creative process of writing a long novel from "back-and-forth with AI" into a memory-enabled, structured, and review-driven assembly line. You provide ideas, AI provides the labor; you modify settings, structure synchronizes automatically;…
