# Xingyu Music Vault / 星语音库

> Self-hosted music metadata management and OpenAPI service for personal music libraries, NAS, home servers, and private deployment environments.

## Theme Preview

Currently, four seasonal theme resources have been completed: "春日晨光 / Spring Dawn", "仲夏星河 / Midsummer Starlight", "秋日唱片 / Autumn Vinyl", and "冬夜雪境 / Winter Moonlight". Version 1.0.3 adds winter and spring theme material directories, theme metadata, and candidate configurations, with a lightweight theme switch entry in the management backend header covering the four seasonal themes in spring, summer, autumn, and winter order, and the selection results continue to be saved in the browser locally. This version still does not include a complete server-side theme management system, nor does it modify backend business logic, OpenAPI contracts, or database structure. Resource descriptions can be found at [春日晨光主题资源接入](https://github.com/wangjiqing/xingyu-music-vault/blob/HEAD/docs/themes/spring-dawn.md), [仲夏星河主题资源试接](https://github.com/wangjiqing/xingyu-music-vault/blob/HEAD/docs/themes/midsummer-starlight.md),…
