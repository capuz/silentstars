# Bili FM

[English](#english) | [Chinese](#中文)

---

## Chinese

Bili FM is a cross-platform desktop application for listening to Bilibili video content through audio, supporting Windows, macOS and Linux. It can serve as a lightweight music player, and is also well suited to background listening to courses, interviews, podcast-style videos and other long-form video content.

Current stable version: **3.1.2** (mygo native edition). Upgrading from the old Tauri edition migrates automatically; see [Upgrading from the Tauri edition](#从-tauri-版升级).

### Features

- Listen to Bilibili videos via audio: search, subscriptions, favorites and recommendations in one place
- Automatic skipping of community sponsor segments (based on SponsorBlock, optional)
- 0.5x to 3.0x playback speed, volume leveling (EQ), danmaku list and danmaku heatmap timeline
- Chapter navigation, subtitle transcripts (switching between languages and versions), timestamp jumps and configurable desktop subtitles
- “Listen in sequence” related-recommendation radio; UP creator collections and personal playlists managed separately, automatically saved and restored
