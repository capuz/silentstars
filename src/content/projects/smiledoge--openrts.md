# openRTS

[English version](https://github.com/SmileDoge/openRTS/blob/HEAD/README_EN.md) · **[Installation and launch](https://github.com/SmileDoge/openRTS/blob/HEAD/INSTALL.md)** · [Discord](https://discord.gg/NGTU7mKJXZ)

**openRTS** is an open, independent reimplementation of the RTrainSim (RTS) railway simulator. The goal is to run existing RTS and MSTS content (routes and rolling stock from the CIS region: ЭД4М, ЭД9М, ЧС4Т, ВЛ10, 2ТЭ10 and others) on a modern engine, reproducing RTS behavior wherever the player can see it: physics, brakes, signalling, and sound.

RTS behavior is being reconstructed from its documentation, content files, and analysis of `rts1.exe`. Each finding is marked with a confidence level (CONFIRMED / PROBABLE / UNKNOWN) in `docs/reverse/`.
