## Discover every shot worth keeping

Locution Pro brings **media management, frame search, shot organization, and clip export** together in one workspace. From massive footage to selected shots, it makes finding easier and organizing more orderly.

Landscape establishing shots, event footage, and long interview recordings can all be started right away. **Browse, search, and organize footage without subtitles.** When subtitles are available, you can also find clips by dialogue text or meaning.

## From footage to finished work, stay focused

| Gather footage | Find frames | Organize shots | Export deliverables |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Centrally manage video, audio, images, and optional subtitles | Browse storyboards and describe the desired scene in natural language | Mark candidates, categorize and filter by keyword | Verify ranges, export individually or merge into a finished cut |

**Prepared for different creative scenarios**

- **Footage organization** — Browse multiple videos together, select usable frames, and build reusable shot categories.
- **Establishing shots and scene search** — Describe people, actions, or environments to find frames that match your intent…
