## What You Should Do

This is meant to be downloaded as a one-off to get your project started. It contains styles and structures to help scaffold your ideas. It is not an updatable dependency.

## Who this is for

Built to teach humans the methodology — grab it, use it, extend it on your own. It has also turned out to be a good source to pull from when starting an AI-agent-built project: the global layer (tokens, voices, scales, modes) makes the taste decisions once, so an agent building new interfaces and layouts mostly just composes — its judgment calls are layout and spacing, and the vocabulary won't let it invent a one-off color or font-size. Same system, two readers. The examples and labs here are worked specimens for both: humans learn the reasoning, agents pattern-match the rules held in place.
