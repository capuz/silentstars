# Cataclysm: Cleanwater Bomb（大灾变：净化协议）

Cataclysm: Cleanwater Bomb（大灾变：净化协议）is a roguelike, single-player, turn-based apocalyptic survival game set in a post-apocalyptic world with science-fantasy elements.

In this game, you will face a complex environment of procedurally generated terrain, filled with zombies that keep respawning, harsh and persistent survival pressure, and terrifying erosion from an alien otherworld. You must struggle to survive in a collapsing world, searching the wreckage of a dead civilization for food and equipment. In the future, we also hope to add some cool gameplay, such as industrialization and rebuilding homes.

Based on the [CleverRaven/Cataclysm-DDA](https://github.com/CleverRaven/Cataclysm-DDA) `cdda/master` branch, commit: **221c786e7d**
