# 2600 Malmö Meeting

2600 meetings exist as a forum for all interested in technology to meet and talk about events in technology-land, learn and teach. 
Meetings are open to anyone of any age or level of expertise.

Adding or correcting a talk? See the [contribution guide](https://github.com/2600Malmo/2600-Archive/blob/HEAD/CONTRIBUTING.md). All talk descriptions, for every year, live in `_talks/`.
