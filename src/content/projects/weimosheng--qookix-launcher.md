# QookiX Launcher

**A free, clean, ad-free Minecraft launcher**

</div>

</div>

## Why choose it?

- **Pure and ad-free**: No pop-ups, no recharge portals, no telemetry; data is only saved on your own computer
- **Out-of-the-box**: Automatically detects Java, multi-threaded downloads, only a few steps from zero to playing
- **Multi-instance management**: One launcher manages multiple "game + mods" combinations without interfering with each other
- **Vast content**: Built-in Modrinth and CurseForge, mods / modpacks / shaders / resource packs available at will
- **Cross-platform experience**: Supports Windows, macOS, Linux and various mainstream distributions
