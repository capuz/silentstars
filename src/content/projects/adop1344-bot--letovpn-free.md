# 🚀 LetoVPN_free

**Automatic collector and checker of VPN configs (VLESS, VMESS, TROJAN)**

---

## 📌 About the project

LetoVPN_free is an automatic service that:

- 🔍 **Collects** configs from open sources
- ✅ **Checks** their operability (TCP + HTTP proxy)
- 🌍 **Determines** the server's country and city
- ⚡ **Sorts** by speed and protocols
- 📁 **Updates** files every 2 hours
