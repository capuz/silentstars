# 🌌 Auto-Crypto-Tradingjournal — Next-Gen Autonomous Futures Journal & AI Trader

---

## 🧭 Overview — The Observatory For Your Futures Mind

Imagine a trading journal that doesn't just record your trades — it *thinks alongside you*. **Auto-Crypto-Tradingjournal** is a self-hosted command center for crypto futures traders who operate on centralized exchanges and refuse to fly blind. It is a wide-angle lens and a microscope fused into one: wide enough to capture every venue, every timeframe, every emotional twitch; deep enough to dissect a single liquidation into a hundred teachable fragments.

This repository is the spiritual successor to journaling tools that stop at "note-taking." Here, the journal is alive. It ingests your executions, reconstructs your decision tree, scores your discipline, and — when you allow it — deploys an **AI trader companion** that mirrors your strategy in sandbox conditions, so you can watch your own logic play out before risking anything real.

If traditional journals are paper diaries, this is a *planetarium for your portfolio*.
