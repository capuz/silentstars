# Pipiclaw

Pipiclaw is an AI assistant runtime, centered around [`pi-coding-agent`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@earendil-works/pi-coding-agent), which complements the essential capabilities needed for long-term use as a work assistant: DingTalk integration, AI Card process display, sub-agents, layered memory, scheduled events, and a workspace that persists by conversation channel.

If you want the AI assistant to do more than just chat, but to continuously work in DingTalk, retain context, execute tasks, and inform you in real-time about what it is doing, then Pipiclaw is what you need.
