# Ocarina ReTuned

A native, single-screen Recomp of OOT3D for Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, iOS, Apple TV, and experimental Nintendo Switch. Ocarina ReTuned is a substantially modified fork of TriAevum, integrating Ocarina ReFramed gameplay with its own interface, ROM-preparation flow, and platform support.

**Preview 0.6.5 is available.** Expect rough edges and share feedback.

[Download the Preview](https://github.com/OTPR26/Ocarina-ReTuned/releases/tag/v0.6.5) · [Report an issue](https://github.com/OTPR26/Ocarina-ReTuned/issues)

## Features

- Single-screen gameplay with the Ocarina ReFramed HUD, menus, and camera controls.
- **Optional dual-screen support:** Android dual-screen devices and experimental iPhone Duo support, with an optional bottom equipment panel.
- **Manual jump:** choose X or ZR as a dedicated jump button.
- **PAK Loader for custom player models:** import child and adult models on Android and desktop, or include them during preparation for iOS, Apple TV, and Switch.
