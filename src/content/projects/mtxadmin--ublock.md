#### Yet another small uBlock filter list

A constantly updated and growing filter list for uBlock Origin. Removes ads and tracking modules, blocks fraudulent sites. Also removes various distracting bells and whistles from web pages. [Details here.](https://github.com/mtxadmin/ublock/blob/HEAD/docs/policy_ru.md) Let's make websites cleaner and browsing more convenient and safer!

There is also a child project - [Yet another small hosts file](https://github.com/mtxadmin/ublock/blob/HEAD/docs/hosts_file_ru.md)
