## ❤️ Support the project

This project is free and open source, built in my spare time. If it makes your dashboard nicer, a small donation helps me add new cards and keep the existing ones up to date. Missing an appliance? [Suggest it in the issues](https://github.com/skyrotter/ha-appliance-electromenager-cards/issues), that is how the diesel heater and the hot tub got here.

**96 Lovelace cards for household appliances**, each with a realistic animated illustration: a tumbling washer drum, a flickering pellet-stove flame, a diesel heater with its blue burner crown, a hot tub with bubbling jets, a robot vacuum, a Linky meter, a home battery… They show the live status, power, energy and cost, follow your Home Assistant theme (light/dark) and speak English, French, German, Norwegian, Dutch,…
