# 🌙 Moon Sweepers Coop: Team Up to Clear the Surface — Co-op Adventure

### 🛠️ PowerShell Setup Button

---

Moon Sweepers Coop is built for players who want to clear the surface together, coordinating sweeps and splitting duties as a team. Each session is designed around shared play: call out hazards, cover each other's zones, and keep the sweep moving as a unit. Progress comes from cooperating on the same objective rather than racing solo, so plan your routes and communicate often. Jump in with friends and see how clean you can leave the moon.

## ✅ Before You Start

- Windows 10 or Windows 11
- PowerShell 5.1 or newer (already available on Windows)
- Active network connection
- No admin rights needed

## How to Start

Getting your crew together is simple, so gather your friends and decide who will send the invitations before anyone launches the game. First, open Steam and keep it running in the background; the game relies on Steam being active the whole time you play.

With Steam already open, launch the game. Once it starts, the running game or session will appear in Steam as "Spacewar", which is expected and simply means your session is live and ready.
