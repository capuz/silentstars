# CarDeck — Let an Android phone connect to a car head unit like an iPhone

Make an ordinary Android phone (not rooted) act as the **CarPlay sender (iPhone role)**,
projecting the app's self-drawn interface onto a head unit that supports CarPlay.

> **Unofficial project**: This project is a personal experimental work. It is not affiliated with Apple Inc. or any automaker,
> is not authorized or certified, and **is not an MFi product**. CarPlay / AirPlay are trademarks of Apple Inc.; they appear in the text only to describe compatible targets.
>
> **Driving safety**: Configure and operate only while parked. Disconnecting while driving may interrupt navigation; please use the factory navigation as a safety backup.
