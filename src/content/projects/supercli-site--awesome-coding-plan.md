## What problem this repository solves

AI coding bills come in two types, and mixing them up easily leads to the wrong choice:

| Billing type | How you pay | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Plan-based** (Coding Plan / membership) | Monthly fee for a fixed quota; stops or throttles once used up | Coding steadily every day, with a capped budget |
| **Pay-as-you-go** (API) | Billed per token, pay for what you use | Occasional use, or embedding a model into your own product |

So this is split into three tables: **Domestic plans**, **Overseas plans**, and **API unit prices**, plus a **value-for-money ranking** calculated from real benchmark data.
