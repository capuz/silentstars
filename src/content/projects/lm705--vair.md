# Vair

**Русский** | [English](README_EN.md) | [فارسی](README_FA.md) | [中文](README_ZH.md)

GUI client for checking and connecting to VPN configurations of protocols **VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks (including SS2022), Hysteria2, TUIC**. Built on [Xray-core](https://github.com/XTLS/Xray-core) and [sing-box](https://github.com/SagerNet/sing-box).

Only on **Windows**.

[Wiki](https://github.com/lm705/vair/wiki)

> 💡 Suggestions, errors, and any requests, please write in [**Issues**](https://github.com/lm705/vair/issues).

## vless_alive.txt

A list of free VLESS configurations working in Russia.

Preloaded in the **SOURCES** tab of the program. The **RELOAD** button loads a fresh version of the file.

Configurations are updated every hour, undergoing automatic checks for availability and speed on a server in one of the most blocked regions of Russia. Unavailable and slow configurations are filtered out. Most configurations are operational at the time of update.

Configurations are gathered from open sources.

The country in the name may differ from the outgoing IP.
