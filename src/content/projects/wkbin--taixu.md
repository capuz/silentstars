## 🌌 What is TaiXu

From the Liezi · Questions of Tang: "East of the Bohai Sea... within it there is a great ravine, which is truly a bottomless valley. Below it there is no bottom, named Guixu. The waters of the Eight Poles and Nine Wilds, the flow of the Milky Way—all flow into it without increase or decrease."

**TaiXu** draws inspiration from this: within Android's application sandbox and permission boundaries, establish a realm of Linux and AI automation that is **runnable, observable, recoverable, and evolvable**.
