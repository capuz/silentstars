# Niulai Dingpan (牛来盯盘) · Bring the Market into VS Code

The built-in chart at the top of each stock shows quotes, moving averages, technical indicators and turnover rate for the selected date or period; the 5-day intraday view shows daily turnover rate.

**Check watchlist quotes, market temperature, financial news and major events in the editor you already use.**

Click the **gear** on the watchlist bar to open the extension settings, where you can adjust quotes, charts, news and alerts in one place. When a watched stock rises or falls by more than **7%**, a popup alert can appear. By default, alerts are off while Boss Key is on. Each stock triggers at most one alert per direction per day.

Niulai Dingpan gives VS Code a compact status bar, a collapsible quote list and a standalone chart page. During trading, check whether turnover is expanding, open the watchlist to see multi-timeframe trends, and follow Hong Kong and US stocks, funds, global indices, commodities and major financial events.
