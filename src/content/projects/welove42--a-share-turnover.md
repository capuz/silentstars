# Niulai Stock Monitor · Bring Market Quotes into VS Code

The top of the built-in stock chart shows quotes, moving averages, technical indicators, and turnover rate for the selected date or period. The 5-day intraday view shows daily turnover rate.

**In your familiar editor, check watchlist quotes, market temperature, financial news, and important events.**

Click the **gear** on the watchlist bar to open the extension settings and adjust quotes, charts, news, and alerts in one place. When a watchlist stock rises or falls by more than **7%**, a popup alert can appear. Alerts are off by default when the boss key is enabled. For each stock, each direction alerts only once per day.

Niulai Stock Monitor gives VS Code a compact status bar, a collapsible quote list, and a standalone chart page. During trading hours, check whether turnover is expanding. Click into your watchlist to view multi-period trends. You can also follow Hong Kong and US stocks, funds, global indices, commodities, and major financial news.
