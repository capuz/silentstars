## Introduction

OpenSpeech is a cross-platform desktop voice input tool: in any application, any input field, press a hotkey to start speaking, press again to write the transcribed text to the cursor position. Windows / macOS / Linux are released simultaneously on all three platforms.

**Speak freely and what appears at your cursor becomes a structured document.** Recording → Transcription → AI cleanup—all verbal mistakes, filler words, and self-corrections are smoothed out, then reformatted as you want:

## Features

**Turn voice directly into the text you want**
Press a hotkey to start speaking, press again to end, text appears at the cursor. Utterances like "uh", "ah", "um", verbal mistakes, and corrections are automatically organized by AI into clean text, not typed out verbatim. VS Code, chat boxes, email, terminals—all…
