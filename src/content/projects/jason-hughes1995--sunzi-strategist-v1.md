Sunzi Strategist is a purpose-built AI skill for Codex that translates ancient strategic wisdom into actionable modern decision-making. It guides users through uncertainty by evaluating confidence levels, isolating the unknowns that truly shift outcomes, and recommending the most favorable position before entering any competitive or cooperative situation.

Designed for strategists, project managers, analysts, and anyone facing high-stakes decisions with incomplete data, this tool replaces pure intuition with a structured method.
