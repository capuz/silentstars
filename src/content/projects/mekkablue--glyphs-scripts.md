# ABOUT

These are Python scripts intended for font production in the [Glyphs font editor](http://glyphsapp.com/).

# INSTALLATION

## Glyphs 3

1. **Install the modules:** In *Window > Plugin Manager,* click on the *Modules* tab, and make sure at least the [Python](https://github.com/mekkablue/Glyphs-Scripts/blob/HEAD/glyphsapp3://showplugin/python) and [Vanilla](https://github.com/mekkablue/Glyphs-Scripts/blob/HEAD/glyphsapp3://showplugin/vanilla) modules are installed. If they were not installed before, restart the app.
2. **Install the scripts:** Go to *Window > Plugin Manager* and click on the *Scripts* tab. Scroll down to [mekkablue scripts](https://github.com/mekkablue/Glyphs-Scripts/blob/HEAD/glyphsapp3://showplugin/mekkablue%20scripts) and click on the *Install* button next to it.

Now the scripts are available in *Script > mekkablue.* If the mekkablue scripts do not show up in the *Script* menu, hold down the Option key and choose *Script > Reload Scripts* (Cmd-Opt-Shift-Y).
