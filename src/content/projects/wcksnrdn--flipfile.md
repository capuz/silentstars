# flipfile

### Drop a file. Point to a format. Done.

**The file converter that lives on your desktop as a single glass circle.**<br>
Photos, PDFs, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, data, video, audio, scans and recordings,<br>
converted, compressed, upscaled and transcribed **right on your computer**.<br>
No uploads. No accounts. No waiting.

## What's new in 0.13

> **Tip**
>
> **Upscale is now three tools, each made for what you drop.** **Photo** makes a photo larger *and* better, in its own colours. **Text** retypes the words of documents, screenshots and chats so they come out crisp instead of garbled. **Plain** only makes it larger. And **Colorize** brings colour to black-and-white photos.
