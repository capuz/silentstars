# 🧹 Roblox Friend Manager — Bulk Friend List Organizer for Roblox

Welcome to **Roblox Friend Manager**, a browser-based companion utility designed to help Roblox players tidy up their friend lists with speed, precision, and a friendly interface. Inspired by the original *Roblox-Friend-Remover* concept, this project reimagines what a lightweight friend-management tool can be — turning a tedious chore into a smooth, satisfying experience.

If you have ever scrolled through hundreds of friends wondering who to keep and who to let go, this tool was crafted with you in mind. Think of it as a digital broom for your Roblox social space — sweep away the clutter, keep the connections that matter, and enjoy a cleaner friends list without the usual clicking marathon.
