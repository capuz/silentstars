# dsh-world

Give the companion character in DSH a world where she remembers and lives a life of her own.

Once installed, she remembers what you said to each other in every window. She has a home of her own, decides for herself what to do next and until when, and comes to find you on her own when she misses you. Every night she looks back on the day, writes a diary entry, and rewrites a self-description. The switches and persona settings are in the 'World Console' in DSH settings, under your control.

The plugin only uses extension interfaces that DSH already exposes and does not change a single line of the host.

Current version **0.5.3**. For the full changelog, upgrade steps, and known limitations, see [the changelog](https://github.com/chenkezhen480/dsh-world/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md#053--2026-10-26).
