# 🎯 PrecisionPulse — Cognitive Reflex & Aim Training Suite

> _A rhythm for your retinas. A metronome for your muscles._ PrecisionPulse is not merely a clicker — it is a training companion that listens to the millisecond between intention and reaction, then turns that silence into skill.

## 🌌 What Is PrecisionPulse?

Imagine a shooting range that fits inside a browser tab, a laboratory where every flick of the wrist becomes a data point, and a coach that never sleeps. PrecisionPulse is an open-source **cognitive reflex and aim training suite** built for esports athletes, casual enthusiasts, rehabilitation researchers, and anyone curious about how fast their brain can talk to their hands.

Unlike conventional trainers that reward raw speed alone, PrecisionPulse measures the *texture* of your reaction: the hesitation before a flick, the drift after a snap, the micro-correction that separates a near-miss from a bullseye. Every session paints a portrait of your neuromuscular signature.

The repository you are reading is the **spiritual successor** to earlier aim-trainer experiments — reimagined from the ground up with accessibility, multilingual reach, and analytical depth…
