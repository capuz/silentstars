## Introduction

VulWiki compiles Chinese analyses and reproduction materials for vulnerabilities in web applications, middleware, network devices, and systems. Articles are stored as local Markdown. You can read them directly on GitHub, clone the repository to search locally, or browse and search by category through the online Wiki.

**Project scope**: collecting, merging, organizing, and improving public technical materials for researchers' reference. Maintainers are not required to actually reproduce the vulnerabilities. Maintenance focuses on content fidelity, correspondence between materials, formatting, resources, and fast retrieval.
