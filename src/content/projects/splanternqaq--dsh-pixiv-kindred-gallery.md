# Pixiv Kindred Gallery

**A local gallery for DeepSeek Harness: run a round → score → save to the library → learn your taste**

It doesn't process images itself. Searching, viewing, and downloading are handled by [刷Pixiv](https://github.com/splanternQAQ/dsh-pixiv-Kindred-Gallery) (`dsh-pixiv-scroll`). The gallery arranges the results as cards, lets you score them, and remembers your taste.
