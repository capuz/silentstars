## What Zhiheng Can Do

Zhiheng is designed for teams that want to self-host, manage models and knowledge in one place, and provide web and API services to their members.

- **View real operating status**: The workbench summarizes call trends, confirmed tokens, credit consumption, runtime alerts, and recent resources. Personal and platform-wide statistics are authorized separately.
