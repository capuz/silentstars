Meowpuut can type Zhuyin (注音) and Pinyin, and output Traditional or Simplified characters. On first use, the input method and character forms default based on your system language and region. You can change them later in Settings.

「中日混打」(Mixed Chinese-Japanese typing) is a toggle that is off by default on new installs. When it's on, if you want to put a Japanese word in the middle of Chinese typing, just type it in romaji. For example, type `sushi`, and the candidate with a cat paw, 「寿司」, is the Japanese word. The toggle is in 「鍵盤設定」(Keyboard Settings) in the Meowpuut app on iPhone, on the 「試打」(Trial Typing) page of Meowpuut settings on Mac, and in the settings on Android and Windows. People who were already using it keep it as it was after upgrading (on), and can turn it off themselves. English is not affected by this toggle. It still gets typed directly inside Pinyin: `wozaiyongiphone` is 「我在用iPhone」.

The candidate bar is a piece of glass. The black Mimimo and the cream-colored Momo rest their front paws on it.
