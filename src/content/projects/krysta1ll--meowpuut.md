Meowpuut supports Zhuyin and Pinyin input, and outputs Traditional or Simplified characters. On first use, the input method and character forms default based on the system language and region. You can change them later in Settings.

"Mixed Chinese-Japanese typing" is a switch, off by default on new installs. When it is on, if you want to insert a Japanese word partway through typing Chinese, just type it in romaji: type `sushi`, and the candidate marked with a cat paw, 寿司 (sushi), is the Japanese word. The switch is in the Meowpuut app's "Keyboard Settings" on iPhone, the "Try It" page of Meowpuut settings on Mac, and in the settings on Android and Windows. People already using it keep the existing behavior after upgrading (on), and can turn it off themselves. English is not affected by this switch and is still typed inline within Pinyin: `wozaiyongiphone` is 我在用iPhone ("I'm using an iPhone").

The candidate bar is a piece of glass. The black Mimimo and the cream-colored Momo rest their front paws on top of it.
