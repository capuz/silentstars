# Supervised Learning + Econometrics Introduction

An introduction to supervised learning and econometrics for undergraduate students.

- Roadmap

    - Predictive models (introductory)
    
    - Supplement: Overview of LLMs

    - Comparison after balancing (introductory)
    
    - Supplement: Social mechanisms and connecting to the population

    - Predictive models (advanced): model aggregation
    
    - Comparison after balancing (advanced): direct use of predictive models
    
    - Supplement: Comparison after balancing (advanced): estimation of heterogeneity

- Please use as review materials.

    - [Estimating the "auxiliary line" of the population mean](https://github.com/tetokawata/NoteBLP)
    
    - [Comparison after balancing](https://github.com/tetokawata/NoteBalance)
