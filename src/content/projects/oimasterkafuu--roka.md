# Roka

Roka is a real-time grid-based territory war game, similar in gameplay to generals.io. Players expand territory, build capitals and command posts, and in FFA or team matches, capture the enemy capital and survive to the end.

The server is built with TypeScript, Fastify, and Socket.IO, and the frontend is mainly vanilla JavaScript. Matches are settled by the server per Tick, with support for reconnection after disconnects, spectating, and full replays.
