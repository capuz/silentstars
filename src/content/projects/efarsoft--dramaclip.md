# 🎬 DramaClip

**Drop in an 80-episode short drama series, and produce a wall full of promotional short videos ready to publish directly.**

AI watches the entire drama for you, writes the script, clones character voice as narration voice, aligns edit points to camera cuts, mixes volume to publishing standards —
all media processing runs on your machine, materials never leave your local system, interrupt and resume anytime.
