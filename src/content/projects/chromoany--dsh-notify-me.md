# dsh-notify-me

**Leave the DSH page and still know in time when you need to come back.**

Simplified Chinese | [English](https://github.com/chromoany/dsh-notify-me/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

[Install](#安装) · [Preview](#效果预览) · [Configuration](#在-dsh-设置里改配置) · [Compatibility](#兼容性) · [Known limitations](#已知限制) · [Changelog](https://github.com/chromoany/dsh-notify-me/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md)

A notification plugin for DSH Web / Desktop, so you can skip keeping full access open and don't have to watch the page all the time.

- **When you need to act**: approvals, plan confirmations, and questions are flagged with notifications, a sound, and the tab title.
- **When a background reply finishes**: come back in time to see the result; click the notification to return to the matching conversation. Reminder timing is selectable: background only / current conversation silent / always remind.
- **You step away but the window stays**: the window remains in the foreground while you've left your seat; no input for more than 3 minutes also counts as away, and reminders still fire as usual. A notification you received won't disappear on its own before you return.
