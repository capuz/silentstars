> [!IMPORTANT]
> **SimXRD is the first truly large-scale foundation dataset for AI-driven X-ray diffraction research.**  
> Before SimXRD, progress in machine learning for crystallography was fundamentally constrained by the absence of sufficiently large, physically realistic, and systematically benchmarked diffraction datasets. SimXRD fundamentally changed this landscape by introducing over 4 million high-fidelity simulated XRD patterns spanning more than 119,000 crystal structures under 33 physically diverse experimental conditions.
