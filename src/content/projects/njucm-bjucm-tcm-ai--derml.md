## 🌟 Highlights

- **Low-quality multi-view problem framing**: formulates real-world TCM dermatologic diagnosis & treatment as robust learning from incomplete, weakly aligned, heterogeneous multi-view clinical data (lesion images + history + symptoms).
- **Staged decomposition**: a **three-stage multimodal framework** progressively fusing *localized lesion perception* → *multi-view aggregation with TCM pathogenesis modeling* → *holistic syndrome-based clinical reasoning*.
- **Resource-efficient adaptation**: built on a lightweight VLM (**Derml-VL-7B** = Qwen2.5-VL-7B with its language model replaced by **Tianyi**).
- **Comprehensive evaluation**: automatic NLG metrics, **knowledge-enhanced LLM-as-a-Judge** with a curated TCM dermatology knowledge base, and a **multi-center human evaluation** across **9 hospitals / 14 dermatologists**.
