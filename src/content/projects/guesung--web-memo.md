## 🤔 Background of Development

Hello! Web Memo is a project that grew out of the inconvenience I felt while reading web pages. I'm sure you've had similar experiences.

### 1. The hassle of taking notes

While reading an article, there are times when you think, "I should keep a record of this." Each time, I had to open a notes app and split the screen in half to write things down, which was tedious. This experience made me feel that I needed a way to record everything at once without opening a separate notes app.

### 2. The hassle of writing the title and URL every time

After opening a notes app, I had to manually enter the title and URL to record which article the note came from. I started wondering whether there was a way to handle this automatically.

### ⇒ "Web Memo"

Based on these experiences, I developed an extension that lets you **take notes easily while reading articles**! Web Memo was created to relieve the inconvenience I experienced and to help others take notes more easily.
