## Features

- One library for movies, TV, music and books — see the [roadmap](https://github.com/DataHearth/streamline/blob/HEAD/docs/wiki/Roadmap.md) for what is shipped and what is next
- **Music**: MusicBrainz metadata, album and discography-pack grabs, tags and artwork written into your files, and a Subsonic server for music apps (as an [extension](https://github.com/DataHearth/streamline/blob/HEAD/docs/wiki/Extensions.md))
- **Books**: Hardcover metadata, books and series with an ebook and an audiobook slot each, an OPDS catalog for e-reader apps (as an [extension](https://github.com/DataHearth/streamline/blob/HEAD/docs/wiki/Extensions.md)), and send-to-Kindle by email
