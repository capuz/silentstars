## Download and Usage

The YourTJ mobile app is now officially released, supporting browsing the community, viewing course reviews, planning timetables, and reading the campus Wiki on phones and tablets.
Available in Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, and German interfaces, with light and dark appearances.
