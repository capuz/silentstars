# OpenAccountants

**Free AI skills (tax rules your AI follows) your AI can cite, with sources and published review status.**

This library includes accountant-reviewed skills and source-cited drafts. Check each skill version's review record before relying on it; a named author is distinct from a professional reviewer. Skill content is source-available under the terms in [LICENSING.md](https://github.com/openaccountants/openaccountants/blob/HEAD/LICENSING.md).

**1,903 AI skills** across **230 jurisdictions** · **51 accountant-reviewed** · **44 named accountants** · **19,965 questions answered** through connected AIs
