Neuro OJ (NOJ) is an independent community project with no official relationship to CCF, LMCC, IOAI, or NOAI.

> [!CAUTION]
> Neuro OJ
> is still in development and has not been formally validated in production. Do not use it to judge any official competition.

> [!TIP]
> Thanks to Teacher Liu Shuran of the China Computer Federation (CCF) and Nanjing University (NJU) for recommending Neuro OJ during an LMCC online training session.

## Introduction

NOJ provides a complete workflow from "register → solve → submit → judge." It can be used for LMCC exam preparation and mock training, AI certification and selection, course practical training, and model evaluation competitions.

The main difference from traditional OJs is that problems use an evaluator to call user-implemented functions and score them, rather than only comparing standard input and output (stdin/stdout). This approach is better suited to evaluating LLM applications and AI engineering skills.

## Core Features

- **Function-call-based evaluation**: The evaluator drives tests and customizes scoring logic, covering objective questions, coding problems, LLM engineering problems, and artifact submission problems.
