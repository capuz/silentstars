<h1 align="center">Astravia · Star Orbit</h1>

An open-source desktop AI Agent for real work—local-first, extensible, under your control.
</p>

</p>

  <b>English</b> ·
</p>

---

Astravia brings models, project files, local tools, and reusable capabilities into one desktop workspace. It's suitable for coding, documentation, data, research, creative production, and repeatable workflows, while letting work continue in the environment of your choice.

It's not just a chat interface: Astravia can understand your workspace, invoke tools within visible permission boundaries, deliver real files, and preserve executable processes for inspection.
