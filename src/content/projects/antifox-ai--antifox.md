## Introduction

AntiFox is an AI autonomous penetration testing platform. Given an authorized target, a multi-agent system of planner / worker / orchestrator agents, built around a **dual-graph architecture** (a global asset graph + a per-task exploration graph), autonomously carries out reconnaissance → attack surface discovery → exploit verification → vulnerability recording → **report delivery**, with full visualization, intervention capability, and deliverables.

Unlike most automated penetration testing tools that “excel at discovery but stop at discovery,” AntiFox focuses on **delivery**: turning the vulnerabilities the AI finds into reports that engineers can understand, reproduce, back with evidence, and deliver directly to clients.
