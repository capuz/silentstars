# CDU AI

An open AI learning site and community project for all students at Chengdu University. Start from a specific problem, find suitable tools, and then organize reproducible methods into guides.

Initiated by the AI direction of the Chengdu University Computer Association. **Not an official school platform.**

**Visit the site: [wiki.cduai.top](https://wiki.cduai.top/)**
