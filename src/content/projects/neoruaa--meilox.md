# [MeiloX](https://github.com/NEORUAA/MeiloX)

### A high-fidelity Apple Music-style NetEase Cloud Music Android client, reworked from Mei

---

## Project Introduction

MeiloX is a third-party NetEase Cloud Music client reworked from [Mei](https://github.com/ljyh223/Mei). Built with **Jetpack Compose**, it attempts to bring the layering, Liquid Glass, and immersive player experience of iOS 27 to Android.

The project focuses on three directions:

- Reorganizing NetEase Cloud Music content using an iOS-style page structure;
- Using Liquid Glass, dynamic backgrounds, and album covers to make playback more immersive;
- Keeping familiar NetEase Cloud Music habits such as search, playlists, lyrics, and profiles.

> MeiloX is an unofficial personal open-source project and is not affiliated with NetEase Cloud Music or Apple. The project itself does not provide any music resources; music content and related copyrights belong to their respective rights holders.

## Feature Overview

- NetEase Cloud Music cookie login and account information display;
- Search for songs, albums, artists, and playlists;
- Play queue, shuffle, repeat, and sleep timer;
- Play history, liked music, playlist…
