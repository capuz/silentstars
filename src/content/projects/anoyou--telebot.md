# TelePilot

Self-hosted Telegram multi-account UserBot console.

TelePilot integrates multiple Telegram accounts, plugins, interactive bots, AI routing, financial ledgers, Webhooks, logs, and online updates into a Web/PWA workspace. Each account runs in an independent Worker subprocess, suitable for individual users or small teams who wish to control their data, keys, and automation rules.

> TelePilot operates Telegram user accounts, not a standard Bot API hosting service. Automatic replies, mass sending, frequent operations, and third-party plugins may pose account risk control or security risks. Please use it with an understanding of the risks and comply with Telegram rules and local laws.
