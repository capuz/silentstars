## What this is

A Windows-first Dynamic Island that integrates WeChat, QQ, and email notifications, clipboard history, quick translation, a Codex status light, media controls, daily to-dos, and more.

fly-island runs as a lightweight, borderless, always-on-top floating island. It expands when needed and returns to a compact state when you're done, reducing how often you switch windows to get a single piece of information.

## Why I built it

In daily work or study, I often need to write a to-do list in one app. To block out noise, I open a music app and set the volume to just the right level. Work and study also often require translation websites, and I repeatedly copy and paste code. At the same time, it's hard to avoid messages from different group chats and friends.

Switching back and forth between these interfaces not only reduces efficiency but also easily distracts. So I started wondering: could these high-frequency features be integrated into one sufficiently lightweight place?
