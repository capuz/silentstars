# denpa

**A home TV recording server that works right away just by inserting a tuner and starting it up.**
You don't write a single line of configuration file. The tuner distinguishes between types (terrestrial / satellite) by itself, and PX-Q3U4 and the like don't even need drivers on the host (bundled with the agent image). After that, just press scan on the tuner screen. There's no need to set up Mirakurun or EDCB separately and connect them, and yet you can change anything you want to modify from the screen.

Reservations are made with just a press from the program guide. Commercials are skipped automatically, and you can watch both live and recordings in the browser with subtitles and data broadcasting. You can also send it to VLC on your TV, or download it and watch it in your favorite player. You don't need to set up a media server.
