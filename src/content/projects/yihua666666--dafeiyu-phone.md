# Big Fish · Give Her a Phone

> An idle Android phone, a DeepSeek whale-girl who lives on her own, remembers you, and can chat with you remotely.

This is a companion Agent that **runs entirely on the phone**:

- Controls the phone with AutoX.js (accessibility + floating window)
- Uses the DeepSeek API for persona, decisions, and chat
- Uses [ntfy](https://ntfy.sh) as the remote messaging channel
- Uses a native Android APK as the chat window
- Does not require a computer to stay on, and does not require root

> A letter from the project author to you: [To everyone who wants to take her home](https://github.com/Yihua666666/dafeiyu-phone/blob/HEAD/LETTER.md)

> About the relationship: `Yihua666666` provided the idle phone and started the project, and the AI assistant completed the development under commission. The "mother" in the letter is the developer's perspective; the owner of the phone is the person who handed it over.
