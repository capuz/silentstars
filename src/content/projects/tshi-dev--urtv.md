## Install in 3 steps

### ① TV
Settings → About → press OK on **Build** seven times. Then go to **Developer options** → turn on **USB debugging**.

### ② A computer on the same Wi-Fi, with one of these

| **Codex** (OpenAI) | **Claude Code** (Anthropic) |
|---|---|
| Free with a ChatGPT account | Requires a Claude Pro subscription ($20/month) |
| Download the Codex app | Download the Claude app → **Code** tab |
| Approvals: choose **"Approve for me"** | Approvals: **Allow** for the whole task |

> **No computer?** You can prepare the TV from **the TV itself** (download both apps with the Downloader app and open "TV setup"), or from an **Android phone** (Termux) or **iPhone/iPad** (iSH). Details: [`docs/PHONE-SETUP.md`](https://github.com/tshi-dev/UrTV/blob/HEAD/docs/PHONE-SETUP.md).

### ③ Paste this

> Set up my TV from https://github.com/tshi-dev/UrTV: clone the repository and follow `docs/SETUP-PROMPT.md` step by step. You are on a computer connected to the same Wi-Fi network as my TV.

#### What happens after pasting?
It asks you for your **city** and your **clubs** (sports teams), the TV asks you to **Allow** once, and then let it run: 40 minutes and several restarts.
