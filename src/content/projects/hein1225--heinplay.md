# Hain Film and Television

A cross-platform film and television playback application developed based on Flutter. The TV version is optimized for Android TV and large screen devices, supporting remote control focus navigation; the mobile version is a vertical touch screen version that shares the business layer with the TV version. It also supports Web and Windows desktop.

## Features

### Film and Television Browsing

- **Home Recommendations**: Aggregates popular film and television content, watch and play directly on the homepage.
- **Category Browsing**: Four main channels: Movies, TV Series, Variety Shows, and Animation, supporting horizontal small category switching.
- **Filtering and Sorting**: The category page supports filtering by type, region, year, etc., and sorting by recent popularity, ratings, time, etc.
- **Poster Grid**: TV-style poster wall, supports smooth browsing with remote control directional keys; the top right corner of the poster displays Douban ratings, and the animation "Daily Broadcast" shows Bangumi ratings.
