# Kite

**Kite is a self-hosted email inbox for your own domains that runs in your own Cloudflare account.** It receives mail through Cloudflare Email Routing and sends through Cloudflare, **Resend** or **Amazon SES**. Open source, AGPL-3.0, with every feature free.

> **Based on [Mailflare](https://github.com/hieunc229/mailflare) by hieunc229.** Kite is an independent fork under the same AGPL-3.0 license. The foundation is theirs, and the original copyright notices and credits are kept in [NOTICE](https://github.com/midego1/kite/blob/HEAD/NOTICE) and [Credits](#credits). See [How this version differs](#how-this-version-differs).

> **Status: early beta.** Kite is young. It works, it is tested, and a handful of things are still rough or missing (see [Known limitations](#known-limitations)). Please try it and [tell us where it breaks](https://github.com/midego1/kite/issues/new/choose).

A 39-second tour with invented demo data (some screens use stand-in data); [the MP4](https://github.com/midego1/kite/blob/HEAD/docs/assets/kite-tour.mp4) (a download) is smoother and in full colour. `npm run demo:record` records it again (see [Screenshots](#screenshots)).
