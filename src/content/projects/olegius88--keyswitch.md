# KeySwitch

[**Russian**](https://github.com/olegius88/keyswitch/blob/HEAD/README.md) · [English](https://github.com/olegius88/keyswitch/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

KeySwitch is a desktop application for Windows 10/11 x64, 32-bit Windows 10, and Windows 11 on ARM, Ubuntu/Xubuntu (X11 and Wayland: GNOME, KDE Plasma 6, sway), and macOS 13 and later, which automatically corrects a word typed in the wrong keyboard layout. It is a free alternative to Punto Switcher, Caramba Switcher, and EveryLang, and unlike them, KeySwitch also works on Linux. Recognition runs locally, typed text is never sent anywhere, and the layouts are taken from the system's EN/RU pair.
