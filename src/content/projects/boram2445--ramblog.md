# 💙 RamBlog | A blog for all developers

```
🧡 RamBlog is a simple blog project for all developers.

⭐️ You can use the blog like a diary by recording your daily study logs.

✨ You can post and share study content that you want to remember for a long time on the blog.

🎀 The blog includes a portfolio feature, allowing other users to view the user's skills and projects.
```

### 👉 [Go to Service](https://ram-blog.vercel.app/)

<br/>

<h1>🤔 Background of the Project</h1>
</div>

- As a `lazy perfectionist`, I always hesitated to post on my blog because I thought I had to post perfect content.
- However, I wanted to `show off what I have learned` and also wanted to `record how hard I worked today`.
- I was also disappointed that many people post their `portfolios` on their self-developed blogs, but platforms like velog or tistory do not have that tab.
- So I thought, let's create my own blog! And this project, **`RamBlog`**, was started to make it available for other developers who think similarly.🤩
