# Cyber Pinball

**A free, open-source frontend for virtual pinball cabinets. Describe your cabinet once: Cyber Pinball sets up everything else, you drive it all from the flipper buttons or your phone, and you play online with your friends' cabinets.**

[Français](https://github.com/gocode/cyberpinball/blob/HEAD/README.fr.md) · [Download](#download) · [Every feature](https://github.com/gocode/cyberpinball/blob/HEAD/docs/features.md) · [Technical manual](https://github.com/gocode/cyberpinball/blob/HEAD/docs/manual.md) · [What's new](https://github.com/gocode/cyberpinball/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.en.md)

## Download

- **[⬇ Download Cyber Pinball](https://github.com/gocode/cyberpinball/releases/latest/download/CyberPinball-Setup.exe)** (`CyberPinball-Setup.exe`, about 110 MiB): the recommended installer. Visual Pinball X is downloaded and checked on first launch.
- **[⬇ Full installer](https://github.com/gocode/cyberpinball/releases/latest/download/CyberPinball-Setup-complet.exe)** (`CyberPinball-Setup-complet.exe`, about 280 MiB): Visual Pinball X included, for a cabinet without Internet access.
