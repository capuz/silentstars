# Charoite

The experimental [chronological meeting feed](https://github.com/charoiteai/Charoite_audio/blob/HEAD/docs/live-feed.md) runs locally and is enabled in the example configurations; existing configurations without its switch retain classic mode.

**Local-first AI meeting assistant with speaker diarization and a self-updating knowledge graph. Meeting processing is local by default.**

Enabling cloud or remote-model features sends transcript or graph text off your Mac. Update checks and model downloads also use the network. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/charoiteai/Charoite_audio/blob/HEAD/PRIVACY.md) for destinations, controls and limitations.

Charoite listens to your meetings (microphone + system audio, no bots joining calls), transcribes them locally, tells speakers apart, answers questions mid-meeting, and after each meeting builds an Obsidian knowledge graph that remembers people, systems, decisions and recurring topics — across all your meetings.
