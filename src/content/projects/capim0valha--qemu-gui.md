# 🖥️ QEMU-GUI - Easy GUI for Managing QEMU

## 🚀 Getting Started

Welcome to QEMU-GUI! This application provides a user-friendly interface for QEMU, making it simple to manage virtual machines on your computer. Whether you are using Windows, macOS, or Linux, you can easily set up your virtual environment without needing to dive into complex commands.

## 📦 System Requirements

Before downloading QEMU-GUI, ensure your system meets these requirements:

- **Operating System**: Windows 10 or higher, macOS 10.12 or higher, or a compatible Linux distribution.
- **Python**: Version 3.6 or higher.
- **QEMU**: Installed on your machine. You can download it from the official QEMU website.
- **Memory**: A minimum of 4 GB of RAM is recommended for optimal performance.
- **Disk Space**: At least 200 MB of free disk space.
