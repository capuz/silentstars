# RedMagic Duo · RedMagic System Enhancement Module

RedMagic Duo is an Android enhancement module for RedMagic phones. Features are configured through a standalone app and executed by Root, Modern LSPosed, and the OEM components. It covers the status bar, notifications and control center, gaming and shoulder keys, fan, launcher, app management, and Xiaomi Sports Health.
