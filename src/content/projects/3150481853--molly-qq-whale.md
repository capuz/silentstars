> **⚠️ Must read before deployment (work out the API costs first, then get started)**
> - **This project does not bring its own brain**: Molly's replies all come from a large model API. Without a working API/gateway, it won't chat after installation. It will only throw errors or hang.
> - **Where to get the API**: connect directly to the **DeepSeek official API**. It is accessible within China, supports top-up via Alipay/WeChat Pay, and bills by usage. You can also use a **relay station** or a self-hosted **gateway / reverse proxy**. Relay stations vary widely in quality, pricing, and stability (quota limits, queuing, and outages are common), so vet them yourself. The instability is on the relay's side, not the official API's.
> - **Deployment really costs money**: multiple groups, long context, speech recognition, and animated-image understanding all consume tokens for real. **A few dozen yuan is a common starting point**, and more groups and more chatting mean more cost. If your budget is tight, do a small-scale trial run first.
> - **The deployment process itself also costs money**: AI assistants that deploy this for you (such as DSH) consume tokens for every step of reasoning…
