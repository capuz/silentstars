> **⚠️ Must-read before deployment (work out the API costs first, then start)**
> - **This project does not come with its own brain**: all of Molly's replies come from a large model API. Without a usable API or gateway, it won't chat after installation; it will only error out or hang.
> - **Where to get the API**: connect directly to the **DeepSeek official API**. It is accessible from mainland China, you can top up via Alipay or WeChat Pay, and it is billed by usage. You can also use a **relay station** or a self-hosted **gateway / reverse proxy**. Relay stations vary widely in quality, price, and stability (quota limits, queuing, and outages are common), so vet them yourself. The instability is on the relay side, not the official API.
> - **Deployment really costs money**: multiple groups, long context, speech recognition, and animated-image understanding all consume real tokens. **A few dozen RMB is a common starting point**, and more groups and more chatting mean more cost. If the budget is tight, do a small trial run first.
