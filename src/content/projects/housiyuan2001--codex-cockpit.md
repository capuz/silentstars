## Your Codex, your little butler is online 🐾

When you have Codex write code, revise papers, or run projects, the last thing you want is to stop and dig through logs or calculate usage.

The Cockpit tidies up these numbers for you: normally it's a quiet little floating window, and when you need it, click to open it and see how much you've used today and how much remains. Less guessing about "will I run out suddenly," more time to focus on your work.

This is an independent community project, not an official OpenAI product.
