# GPT AI Assistant

[Traditional Chinese](https://github.com/SanHsien/gpt-ai-assistant/blob/HEAD/README.md) · [English](https://github.com/SanHsien/gpt-ai-assistant/blob/HEAD/README.en.md) · [Full documentation site](https://sanhsien.github.io/gpt-ai-assistant-docs/) · [Latest Release](https://github.com/SanHsien/gpt-ai-assistant/releases/latest)

**GPT AI Assistant** is a self-hostable **LINE personal AI assistant**. After you deploy it with your own LINE, OpenAI, and Supabase settings, you can chat directly in LINE, send voice messages or images, search for information, manage schedules and tasks, receive reminders, and check the weather.

It is not just another chat interface shell. It is a personal assistant service that uses **LINE as the frontend and a durable queue as the reliability core**: paid AI work and LINE delivery are checkpointed separately, schedules, tasks, and reminders have persistent state, and Google Calendar / Tasks can optionally be integrated.
