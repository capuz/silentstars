# Muv-Luv Series Steam Simplified Chinese Localization Completion Project

  <strong><a href="#patch-downloads">Patch Downloads</a></strong> ·
</p>

Provides Steam Simplified Chinese localization patches for The Imperial Capital Burns, TDA00–03, photonflowers, and photonmelodies.
**All seven works now have BETA patch downloads**, with continuous refinement of translations, Chinese interface, fonts, and formatting, updated through player feedback and collaborative proofreading.
This project is unofficial and non-commercial; also includes the original release entry for the Director's Protective Association's ATE localization, for easy reference.

Currently provides standalone patches for **TDA00–03 (Muv-Luv UNLIMITED: The Day After), The Imperial Capital Burns, photonflowers*, and photonmelodies***.
The series' Japanese name is **マブラヴ**, also commonly written as **MuvLuv / Muv Luv**; each work has different applicable versions and installation methods.
See [Works and Version Comparison](https://github.com/RepressedAlliance/muvluv-series-steam-cn-patch/blob/HEAD/docs/player/game-names.md). Patches are still in BETA and continue to accept feedback and collaborative proofreading.…
