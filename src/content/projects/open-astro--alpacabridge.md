# AlpacaBridge

**The engine at the telescope.**

AlpacaBridge turns a single-board computer into a control server for your entire rig. Native ASCOM Alpaca drivers, running right at the telescope. No ASCOM Platform. No Windows box. No vendor drivers to chase down.

Flash it. Plug in your gear. Image from anywhere on your network with N.I.N.A., APT, CCDciel, Sequence Generator Pro, SharpCap, or [Ara](https://www.openastro.net).

#### [5.0.0] - 2026-10-21 &middot; [Changelog](https://github.com/open-astro/AlpacaBridge/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md)

## Why AlpacaBridge

- **Proven, not promised.** Every driver is [validated with ASCOM ConformU](https://github.com/open-astro/AlpacaBridge/blob/HEAD/SUPPORTED-DRIVERS.md), on the actual hardware it supports.
- **92 validated devices. Sixteen brands. One server.** Altair, Astroasis, Celestron, Gemini, iOptron, Canon and Nikon DSLRs, OnStep, Player One Astronomy, QHY, Sky-Watcher, SVBONY, ToupTek Astro, Unihedron SQM-LE (WeeWX plugin), WandererAstro, WeeWX, and ZWO.
- **Plug in and go.** Vendor SDKs and udev rules come bundled. USB and Wi-Fi devices are auto-detected. No port hunting.
