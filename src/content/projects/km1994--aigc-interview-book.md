## What is This

A **Chinese-written, organized by topics, continuously updated** large model algorithm position interview question bank.

Unlike "copying others' interview experiences," each question here follows the same structure: **provide a one-sentence answer first, then break down the why, and finally what follow-up questions the interviewer might ask**. The questions don't test memorization, but whether you can clearly explain the tradeoffs.

> Take a real question: **Can long-context models (Gemini 1.5 Pro 1M tokens) replace RAG?**
> The interviewer doesn't want to hear "yes" or "no". They want to hear you break it down from **three dimensions: cost, timeliness, and traceability** — being able to identify these dimensions is what earns a high score.
