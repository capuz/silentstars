# Tailscode

**Claude Code, from your phone.** A native remote for [Claude Code](https://github.com/guitaripod/claude-bridge), Codex, [Hermes](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com) and any [ACP](https://agentclientprotocol.com) agent, [Oh My Pi](https://github.com/guitaripod/omp-bridge) and [opencode](https://opencode.ai) running on your own machines: a universal iPhone and iPad app with the turn on your Lock Screen, and open-source desktop clients for Linux and the Mac. Point-to-point over your own [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com) tailnet: no relay, no sign-in, no SSH session or tmux to keep alive. The transport's security is Tailscale's WireGuard, not something Tailscode implements. Screenshots, the full feature tour and pricing live at [midgarcorp.cc/tailscode](https://midgarcorp.cc/tailscode).

Free is the whole core with one server; **Pro** is a one-time $14.99 purchase for unlimited servers, concurrent Live Activities and the delegate board.
