# Features
## Playback

• Stream music from online sources

• Add 'em to your library

• Background playback

• Stream music directly from links

## Library

• Build your own music library

• Playlists

• Import and export your library

• Offline access to your library

## Lyrics support

• Offline synced lyrics in your library (if available in LRCLIB)

## Interface

• Lightweight and focused

• No recommendation algorithm

• No unnecessary feeds

• Built around finding and playing the music you want

# Why Audiobasics?

Audiobasics is built around a simple idea:

You choose the music.

Found something? Search it. Play it. Save it if you like it. Build your own library.
