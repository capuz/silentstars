# HyperEars

[English](https://github.com/silverpoetry/HyperEars/blob/HEAD/README_EN.md) · [Installation Guide](https://github.com/silverpoetry/HyperEars/blob/HEAD/docs/installation.md) · [Compatibility](https://github.com/silverpoetry/HyperEars/blob/HEAD/docs/compatibility.md) · [Control App Scope](https://github.com/silverpoetry/HyperEars/blob/HEAD/docs/control-apps.md) · [Troubleshooting](https://github.com/silverpoetry/HyperEars/blob/HEAD/docs/troubleshooting.md)

HyperEars is a third-party Bluetooth headset system integration module for Xiaomi HyperOS. It brings supported vivo / iQOO, OPPO Enco, OnePlus, Technics, Bose, Edifier, StarRing, ROSESELSA, NiceHCK, 水月雨, 荣耀, 华为, QCY, Dreame / TheAstro, Sony and Samsung headphones into the MiLink unified device center. Without taking over Android audio routing, it supplements the compatibility information needed for battery level, noise-cancellation status and device handoff.
