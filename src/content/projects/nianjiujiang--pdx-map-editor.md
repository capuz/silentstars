# Paradox Cartography

> **Current version: v1.11.0 stable release (2026-11-03)**

> Author: 永夜廿九 (same name on Bilibili) · Code released under the MIT license · [Download the latest version](https://github.com/Nianjiujiang/pdx-map-editor/releases)

A tool for editing Paradox game maps directly in the browser: fill colors, change colors, rename, and adjust borders, then export as PNG or as a color file.

The editor reads the map data bundled with the game and draws countries, provinces, and place names as an interactive map for the scenario year. Click a tile to change its color, hold and drag to paint a continuous area, right-click to pick a color, and scroll to zoom. It suits making video covers, drawing fictional worlds, illustrating battle reports, or simply viewing the distribution of power in a given year.

No installation or login is required. Once a map pack is imported, it runs entirely locally, so you can keep drawing without a network connection.

## What it can do

- Includes maps from five games: CK3, EU4, HOI4, VIC3, and EU5, showing power distribution by scenario year. EU4 covers 12 scenarios from 1444 to 1792.
- Four editing types: painting, filling, eraser, and…
