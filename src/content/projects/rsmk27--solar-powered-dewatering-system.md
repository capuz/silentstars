# Solar-Powered Dewatering in Copper Mining

## 📌 Overview
Mine dewatering is essential to maintain safe and efficient operations in open-pit and underground mining. However, conventional systems rely heavily on diesel generators or unreliable grid power, leading to **high operating expenses, fuel logistics challenges, emissions, and frequent maintenance**.

This project proposes a **solar-powered hybrid dewatering solution** designed to reduce costs, enhance reliability, and meet sustainability goals.

---

## ❌ The Problem
Current diesel- or grid-based dewatering systems face multiple challenges:

- **High Fuel & Power Costs**: Continuous generator operation consumes large volumes of fuel.
- **Carbon Emissions**: Diesel engines release CO₂, NOx, and particulates.
- **Maintenance & Reliability Issues**: Frequent oil/filter changes, engine breakdowns, and unplanned outages.
- **Remote Site Limitations**: Many mines are off-grid, and transporting diesel is costly and logistically complex.

These challenges can represent **15–40% of a mine’s operating costs**1.
