## 💡 Why Build PlaylistOut (Take Your Playlist With You)?

We've spent considerable time and effort organizing playlists on major music platforms, but often encounter such awkwardness:
When wanting to switch platforms for listening, the old platform's playlists cannot be migrated directly; when wanting to back up your personal collections, there's nowhere to export song lists in a clean format; or simply wanting to organize songs into a spreadsheet for printing or statistical analysis, yet can only manually copy and paste one song at a time.

The closed ecosystems of mainstream music platforms keep users' playlists tightly locked within a single app.

**PlaylistOut (Take Your Playlist With You) has a simple goal: parse public playlists into structured data and return it to your hands.**
