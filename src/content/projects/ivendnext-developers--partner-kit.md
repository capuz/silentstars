# The iVendNext partner kit

Build the app a retail chain needs (a rule at the till, a record of its own, a link to its systems) without changing iVendNext itself.

**Start here: [Build your first app](https://github.com/ivendnext-developers/partner-kit/blob/HEAD/site/pages/first_app.md)** ([on the site](https://developer.ivendnext.com/first_app.html)). It takes you from one of the kit's samples to your customer's App library in six steps, and it is all you need to read to build one.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/ivendnext-developers/partner-kit.git
export PATH="$PWD/partner-kit/tools/ivn:$PATH"
ivn login
ivn new acme_po_approval --from s11
```

**Who it is for:** iVendNext Partners, licensed customers' IT teams and the CitiXsys Consulting Team. Anyone may read the kit. To get a sandbox, [request one](https://developer.ivendnext.com/sandbox.html); to build for an iVendNext customer, [become an iVendNext Partner](https://www.ivendnext.com/partners/apply/).

**Building with an AI assistant?** Point it at [AGENTS.md](https://github.com/ivendnext-developers/partner-kit/blob/HEAD/AGENTS.md) first.
