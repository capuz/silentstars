macOS Tahoe style for KDE Plasma 6.6, 6.7 and 6.8+.

This project customizes the look and feel of your KDE desktop on Linux. It does not include or connect to Apple services. If you need Apple services, [shop directly with Apple](https://www.apple.com/store).

> **Caution**
>
> **Rolling Release Philosophy**
> Experimental. Receives daily updates. Updates may break compatibility. Avoid production systems; [report issues here](https://github.com/lestercorderomurillo/macos-tahoe-liquid-kde/issues/new).
