# FrameEcho (帧迹)

**Freeze Every Frame You Love · Freeze Every Frame You Love**<br/>
Android video frame extraction app · High-Performance Video Frame Capture for Android

Precisely capture frames from any video, with support for HDR, motion photos, and full EXIF preservation. Format, quality, export folder, filename, muting, and more can be adjusted via quick-access buttons and the bottom settings panel.

Extract precise frames from any video with HDR awareness, motion photo export, and full EXIF preservation. Quick‑access toggles and a settings sheet let you choose format, quality, export folder, filename template, and audio muting.

## ✨ Features

- **Frame-accurate positioning** — Always uses `MediaMetadataRetriever.OPTION_CLOSEST` to precisely locate frames, without falling back to keyframe search
- **Still image & motion photo export** — Standard high-quality JPEG export; motion photos follow the [Google MicroVideo specification](https://developer.android.com/media/camera/motion-photo-specification) (XMP + appended MP4)…
