# Honghu Yixue

> Automatic learning and assignment assistance with built-in AI answering for mainstream online courses including Chaoxing Xuexitong, Zhihuishu, Zhijiaoyun, Yuketang, and U-Campus

## Introduction

Honghu Yixue offers two ways to use it: a browser extension and a desktop application, helping university students complete online courses and assignments efficiently. It supports mainstream online course platforms including Chaoxing Xuexitong, Zhihuishu, Zhijiaoyun, Yuketang, China University MOOC, and U-Campus, with built-in AI answering capability.

## Features

- Supports online course platforms including Chaoxing Xuexitong, Zhihuishu, Zhijiaoyun, Yuketang, China University MOOC, and U-Campus
- Automatic learning and assignment assistance
- Built-in AI answering (supports large models such as OpenAI, DeepSeek, Anthropic)
- Question bank configuration and online question search
- Question bank caching
