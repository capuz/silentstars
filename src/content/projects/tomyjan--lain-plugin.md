`Lain-plugin` is a multi-adapter plugin developed around `Miao-Yunzai`, allowing Miao-Yunzai to connect to third-party platforms such as `QQ Channel`, `WeChat`, `Shamrock`, etc., no longer limited to ICQQ.

# The main developer (Zyy955) of this plugin stopped development on February 20, 2024 at 18:00:00 and announced permanent discontinuation. This repository will not be deleted (there are other developers, depending on their mood whether to update...)

Original backup repository: [all-thoughts-are-broken/Lain-plugin](https://gitee.com/all-thoughts-are-broken/Lain-plugin)

### Special notice here:

- Update Miao-Yunzai to the latest version
