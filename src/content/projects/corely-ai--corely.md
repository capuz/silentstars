# Corely — Connected, AI-native apps

Corely is a collection of connected, AI-native apps for running a team’s
internal operations. Teams start with the capabilities they need and expand
into finance and specialist operations as their work demands.

Start with the [product strategy](https://github.com/Corely-AI/corely/blob/HEAD/docs/products/corely/README.md),
[focused launch proposal](https://github.com/Corely-AI/corely/blob/HEAD/docs/products/corely/launch-plan.md),
[32-app map](https://github.com/Corely-AI/corely/blob/HEAD/docs/products/corely/app-catalog.md), and
[repository evidence](https://github.com/Corely-AI/corely/blob/HEAD/docs/products/corely/repository-alignment.md).
The catalog is a long-term map, not a delivery commitment or availability list.
Finance is optional; accounting is not a prerequisite for operational apps.
