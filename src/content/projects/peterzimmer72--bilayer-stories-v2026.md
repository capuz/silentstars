## Overview

Bilayer Stories offers an HTML-native system designed to merge machine-generated storytelling with custom ASCII art assets. Built for instant browser execution, it eliminates complex build toolchains so authors can immediately preview, tweak, and distribute text-focused multimedia pieces online.

This platform caters to authors and developers crafting experimental literature, text-driven visual experiences, or tailored landing pages driven by GPT narrative pipelines. Its clean architecture emphasizes clear content presentation while offering flexible layers for inline graphic elements and thematic text design.
