> Pley is still in testing: expect rough edges, and keep backups of your data.

## Features

- Plays files as they are, or transcodes on the GPU
- HDR10, HLG and Dolby Vision tone mapping
- Live TV from M3U and Xtream Codes, with XMLTV guides
- Requests for movies and shows, fetched by Radarr and Sonarr
- Profiles, PINs and two-step sign-in
- Works offline on the home network

[All features](https://github.com/pley-media-server/pley/blob/HEAD/docs/FEATURES.md)

## Apps

Web, Windows, macOS, Linux, [Android](https://github.com/pley-media-server/pley/blob/HEAD/docs/ANDROID.md), [Kodi](https://github.com/pley-media-server/pley/blob/HEAD/docs/KODI.md),
[Roku](https://github.com/pley-media-server/pley/blob/HEAD/docs/ROKU.md), [Samsung TV](https://github.com/pley-media-server/pley/blob/HEAD/docs/TV_PLATFORMS.md),
[LG TV](https://github.com/pley-media-server/pley/blob/HEAD/docs/LG.md). Jellyfin's apps work too, and tools built on Plex's API
such as Kometa and Tautulli, through the
[compatibility APIs](https://github.com/pley-media-server/pley/blob/HEAD/docs/COMPATIBILITY.md).
