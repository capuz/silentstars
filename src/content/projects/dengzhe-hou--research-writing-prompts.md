From Chinese-to-English translation, polishing, and removing "AI flavor" to abstracts, experiment analysis, figure captions, and responding to reviewers, there is one preferred prompt for each of the 22 types of tasks often involved in writing papers. Click to expand and copy below.

All prompts are plain text and do not depend on any specific model. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, and Kimi can all use them. Most are compiled from public projects, and each one is marked with its source.

> [!TIP]
> The [full catalog](https://github.com/dengzhe-hou/research-writing-prompts/blob/HEAD/CATALOG.md) contains all 41 candidate templates, including alternative wordings for most scenarios and the source and license of each. Where a candidate includes several short prompts, pick the one you need.

## How to use

1. Find your task below, click to expand it, and copy it with the button at the top right of the code block.
2. Paste it into the chat box of your LLM, and replace the bracketed hints (such as `[在此处粘贴你的中文草稿]`) with your own content.
3. Models may fabricate data and references, so check the numbers and citations in the output one by one.
