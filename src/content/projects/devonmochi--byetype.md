# ByeType

ByeType is a Markdown-driven AI voice input tool. You can freely define rules, set custom vocabulary, and choose output styles, without being limited by the fixed dictionaries and restricted customization options of traditional input methods. The model understands the raw audio directly and handles person names, industry terms, number formatting, spoken-language cleanup, and mixed Chinese-English content during transcription. You can finish speaking with your eyes closed and send right away without checking whether the recognition is correct: what you say is what gets sent.

ByeType is free and open source, and uses your own API Key. It supports macOS and Windows, and iPhone and iPad through iOS Shortcuts.

## 📱 iPhone / iPad

With iOS Shortcuts, you can get the same custom vocabulary and transcription results on your phone and tablet as on the desktop version.
