# my-claude-skills

Skills for [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/claude-code) — the ones I wrote, and the third-party ones
I actually use, kept in one place so my setup is reproducible.

**Install any of them:** copy the folder into `~/.claude/skills/<skill-name>/` and restart Claude Code.

**Licence:** everything in `mine/` is MIT, see [LICENSE](https://github.com/GuruDevvv/my-claude-skills/blob/HEAD/LICENSE). The skills in `external/` keep their
authors' own licences.

Two folders, and the distinction is not cosmetic:

- **[`mine/`](https://github.com/GuruDevvv/my-claude-skills/blob/HEAD/mine/)** — written by me, maintained here. See [mine/README.md](https://github.com/GuruDevvv/my-claude-skills/blob/HEAD/mine/README.md).
- **[`external/`](https://github.com/GuruDevvv/my-claude-skills/blob/HEAD/external/)** — **not mine.** Third-party MIT skills, vendored with full attribution
  and the original licence texts. Prefer installing those from upstream; see
  [external/README.md](https://github.com/GuruDevvv/my-claude-skills/blob/HEAD/external/README.md).
