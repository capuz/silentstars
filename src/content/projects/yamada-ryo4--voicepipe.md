VoicePipe is a lightweight, high-performance audio routing and mixing tool for Windows. It allows you to accurately capture audio from specified applications, seamlessly mix it with your microphone input in real-time, and output the mixed result to a virtual audio line (e.g., VB-Cable).

This is very useful for streamers, podcasters, and gamers, as you can share game sounds or music in voice chat software (such as Discord, Zoom, or Teams) without broadcasting other system noises (such as notification sounds, background web video sounds, etc.).

## ⬇️ Download and Use

- **Direct Download**: [VoicePipeSetup.exe](https://github.com/Yamada-Ryo4/VoicePipe/releases/download/v1.2.5/VoicePipeSetup.exe) (includes runtime environment and virtual sound card driver, double-click to install with one click)
- **Usage**:
  1. Open VoicePipe after installation.
  2. Select the application you want to capture sound from in the top dropdown menu (e.g., a game or music player).
  3. Select your physical microphone in the microphone dropdown menu.
  4. Click **Start Mixing** to begin mixing.…
