## Description

Official repository for "Near-Optimal Nonconvex-Strongly-Convex Bilevel Optimization with Fully First-Order Oracles" and a series of follow-up works. In these works, we give comprehensive studies on the computability of the problem, lower complexity bounds, and near-optimal deterministic/stochastic algorithms. See the code for [1,2] in the `LLM` folder, and the code for [3,4] in the `medium_sized` folder.

## News

* (2026/11) Our lower bound paper [4] was accepted by JMLR 2026.

* (2026/10) [Gu, Wu, & Yang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21905) and [Zhang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03633) proved large stochastic lower bounds using GPT, showing that our stochastic upper bounds in [1-3] are tight in $\epsilon$. The gaps in $\kappa$ are also small.

* (2025/11) We posted a paper [4] about lower bounds on arXiv.
