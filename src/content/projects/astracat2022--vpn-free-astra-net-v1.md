## 📥 How to use the config

### 🪟 For Clash (PC: Windows/macOS/Linux)

1. Install [Clash for Windows](https://github.com/Fndroid/clash_for_windows_pkg) or [Clash Verge](https://github.com/clash-verge-rev/clash-verge-rev).
2. Open the application.
3. Go to the **Profiles** section.
4. Click **"+"** or **"New Profile"**.
5. Paste the link:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ASTRACAT2022/VPN-FREE-astra.net-V1/refs/heads/main/website/configs/raw_configs.yaml
6. Click **"Download"**, then **"Set as Active"**.
