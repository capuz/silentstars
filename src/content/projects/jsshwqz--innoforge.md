# InnoForge

**Repository:** [GitHub](https://github.com/jsshwqz/innoforge) | [Gitee (Domestic)](https://gitee.com/jsshwqz/innoforge)

**InnoForge** is designed for developers and R&D professionals, helping you gradually advance your initial ideas into decision-making basis, execution plans, product implementation, and intellectual property protection, reducing blind investment and improving the conversion efficiency from ideas to results.
