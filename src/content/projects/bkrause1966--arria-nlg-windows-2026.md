## What Arria NLG Does

Arria NLG is a Windows desktop tool that converts structured information into clear, readable language. Within the broader product profile, it also supports workflows involving Windows and macOS. The application is intended for teams that create recurring reports, produce consistent text from data, or need a more efficient process for generating documents.

The 2026 edition focuses on processing work locally, making it appropriate for users who want their tasks handled on their own computer. Its accessible interface and configurable templates support repeatable writing while allowing teams to adapt the resulting reports and documents to different styles and formats.
