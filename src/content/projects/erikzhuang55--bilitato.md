# Bilitato Quick Start
Bilitato is a free open-source browser plugin that uses AI large models to help you efficiently watch Bilibili videos. It supports one-click download of audio and video, automatic subtitle parsing, transcription of videos without subtitles, content summarization, chapter segmentation, ad recognition, content verification, and you can directly ask AI about video content. It supports custom APIs, models, and Prompts.

## Supported Browsers
Bilitato is a browser plugin developed based on the Chromium kernel, theoretically compatible with all mainstream browsers based on the Chromium kernel (such as Chrome, Edge, Opera, Quark, QQ Browser, etc.), and does not support Safari. If you are unsure whether your browser is based on the Chromium kernel, you can also download it to see if it can be installed normally; it is very likely that it can.
