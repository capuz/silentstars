# MMPI-CHN

A free MMPI-2 Chinese test project launched in February 2023. The entire site is static pages, no registration required, and answering, scoring, and results display can be completed directly in the browser.

**Online Use** → <https://mmpi-chn.github.io/>

---

## What Is This

- All **567 questions** of MMPI-2, with Chinese-English comparison; also supports short form mode with only the first 370 questions
- Output raw scores, K-corrected scores, T scores, two personality profile charts, basic interpretations, critical items, and original responses
- The two profile charts and 13 basic interpretation tables use **approximate Chinese T scores** calculated based on Chinese sample statistics
- Complete technical tables retain US lookup T scores for professionals to verify the entire scoring chain
- Chart.js is released with the repository; the core results page does not depend on charting CDN
