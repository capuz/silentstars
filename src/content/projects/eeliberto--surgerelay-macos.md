# Surge Relay (macOS)

" />
</p>

A macOS application for centralized management, conversion, editing, and publishing of Surge modules.
</p>

Built on the local conversion capabilities of <a href="https://github.com/Script-Hub-Org">Script-Hub</a>.
</p>

Surge Relay is suitable for users who need to maintain a large number of Surge modules simultaneously, especially in scenarios where Loon, Quantumult X, or other proxy tool formats are frequently converted to Surge `.sgmodule` through Script-Hub.

Its goal is to centralize module conversion, address maintenance, rule editing, and multi-device synchronization on a single Mac. You only need to maintain upstream addresses and conversion rules in Surge Relay, and the final Surge modules will be published to stable distribution addresses, with all devices only needing to subscribe to these fixed URLs.
