A cross-platform Bilibili third-party client focused on viewing experience<br>
  Built with Flutter and Dart, for Windows, Android, macOS, iOS, and Linux

---

BiliSail (哔帆) focuses on viewing videos, films and TV shows, and live streams. It offers dual/multi-tab browsing, native playback, danmaku (bullet comments), cloud-synced watch history with local resume playback, and a download queue with offline playback. The 晴湾 interface uses a desktop grouped sidebar and a four-item bottom navigation bar on narrow screens, combined with adaptive grids, hover previews on video cards, split-pane playback details, and configurable keyboard shortcuts.
