# velocity.report

**Measure velocity, not identity**

</div>

velocity.report measures what traffic does on a street, so the people who live there can make
their case with evidence rather than being asked to bring louder anecdotes. Radar and LiDAR
measure speed and movement: no cameras, no licence plates, and no person is identified, tracked,
or recorded. The data belongs to the community that collected it.

</div>

- 📊 PDF reports: speed distributions, percentile statistics, before-and-after comparisons
- 🔒 Privacy by architecture: the hardware cannot collect what the design never asked for
- 📡 Radar speed measurement and LiDAR object tracking (working toward [sensor fusion](https://github.com/banshee-data/velocity.report/blob/HEAD/docs/plans/lidar-l7-scene-plan.md), combining both sensors)
- 🏠 Runs locally in your neighbourhood, offline-first; optional map/prior
  downloads are explicit opt-in
- 🔒 Open source and auditable, because trust should be verifiable
