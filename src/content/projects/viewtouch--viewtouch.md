## ViewTouch Modern

**ViewTouch is now a ground-up rewrite of ViewTouch in C++23 and Qt 6 (QML).** It keeps ViewTouch's best idea, that every screen is a page of buttons you design yourself, and adds what a restaurant needs today. It runs on Linux PCs, touch screens, Raspberry Pi and Android tablets, entirely on site.

📖 **[User manual (PDF)](https://github.com/ViewTouch/viewtouch/releases/download/modern-manual-0.9/ViewTouch-User-Manual.pdf)** · 🛠️ **[Developer README](https://github.com/ViewTouch/viewtouch/blob/HEAD/modern/README.md)** · 🗺️ **[Plan](https://github.com/ViewTouch/viewtouch/blob/HEAD/modern/docs/PLAN.md)**
