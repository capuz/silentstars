# EverAgent

**Open Knowledge × Personal Context**

Here you'll find close readings of papers, analyses of technical blog posts, research on open-source projects, podcast notes, and small experiments. Since March 2026, they have been continuously organized with the help of AI Agents, and have accumulated 500+ reports and 300+ Wiki entries.

The other practice is Personal Context: recording the materials you've looked up, the decisions you've made, and the preferences you've confirmed, so they can be used in the next task. The methods and cases can be shared; personal data is kept by the user.
