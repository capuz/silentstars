## What is Cookie Clicker?

Cookie Clicker is a web-based idle game released by Orteil in 2013. It became a hit after landing on Steam last year.

As Orteil puts it, this is an "endless" game. The focus is on clicking a giant cookie to earn cookies, until you have enough cookies to buy upgrades. Its seemingly endless gameplay makes it a game that can go on indefinitely, or at least until your device can no longer run it.

· Orteil has made the Cookie Clicker web version free: `https://orteil.dashnet.org/cookieclicker/`

· The full version of Cookie Clicker is available for purchase on Steam: `https://store.steampowered.com/app/1454400/Cookie_Clicker/`

· Cookie Clicker Wiki: `https://cookieclicker.fandom.com/wiki/Cookie_Clicker_Wiki`
