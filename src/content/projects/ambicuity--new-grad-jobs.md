# 2026 New Grad Positions

**Fully automated** list of entry-level tech positions for 2025 & 2026 new graduates.

Unlike manually curated lists, this repository pulls directly from configured company APIs and refreshes **every 5 minutes**, 24/7.

**Contribute** by submitting an [issue](https://github.com/ambicuity/New-Grad-Jobs/issues/new/choose). See the [contribution guidelines](https://github.com/ambicuity/New-Grad-Jobs/blob/HEAD/CONTRIBUTING.md) to get started.
