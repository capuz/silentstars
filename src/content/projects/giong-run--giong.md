Named after Thánh Gióng, the child of Vietnamese legend who lay silent for three years and
then rose a giant: giong costs next to nothing while idle and uses every core the moment
load arrives.

> **Status: 0.8.** Complete enough to write real servers: TCP, UDP, Unix sockets, files,
> timers, multi-core, graceful shutdown, and eight companion crates:
> [`giong-http`](https://github.com/giong-run/Giong/blob/HEAD/giong-http/README.md) (a strict HTTP/1.1 and HTTP/2 server and client),
> [`giong-web`](https://github.com/giong-run/Giong/blob/HEAD/giong-web/README.md) (a router, middleware and JSON on it),
> [`giong-tls`](https://github.com/giong-run/Giong/blob/HEAD/giong-tls/README.md) (TLS 1.2/1.3 on rustls), for serving HTTPS directly and connecting to TLS services,
> [`giong-postgres`](https://github.com/giong-run/Giong/blob/HEAD/giong-postgres/README.md) (a pipelining PostgreSQL client and pool),
> [`giong-mysql`](https://github.com/giong-run/Giong/blob/HEAD/giong-mysql/README.md) (the same for MySQL 8 and MariaDB 10.6+),
> [`giong-redis`](https://github.com/giong-run/Giong/blob/HEAD/giong-redis/README.md) (a Redis client with Pub/Sub and a pool),
