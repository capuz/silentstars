# Pixel Screening Room · Home Edition

**Turn your Feiniu NAS into a home arcade.**

v1.4.3: Fixes navigation after selecting a controller in the lobby and handover between two gamepads; keeps the skippable walk-up-to-cabinet entrance animation; continue-save and reduce-motion modes now enter directly. Rebuilt the forward-sloping control console, thick front edge and lower cabinet body to match real arcade structure; unified the joystick center axis and added an arcade hall ambient background, keeping the front-facing screen and rounded glass. Added an account sync module that can connect to an existing authentication service, saving favorites, history, display settings, and save-state thumbnails. The home edition still defaults to local mode; for connecting an account service, see [Account Sync Guide](https://github.com/infinitytom/pixel-arcade-family/blob/HEAD/docs/account-sync.md). Download the [latest release package](https://github.com/infinitytom/pixel-arcade-family/releases/latest). For existing installations, follow the [Update & Deployment Guide](https://github.com/infinitytom/pixel-arcade-family/blob/HEAD/docs/update.md) to keep configuration and data.
