SpiderBox (虫盒) is a crawler reverse-engineering resource navigation site. It aggregates and collects resources related to data collection, web crawlers, JS reverse engineering, APP reverse engineering, mini-program reverse engineering, AI reverse engineering, and more. It covers packet capture, management frameworks, efficiency plugins, Hook, obfuscation, deobfuscation, device fingerprinting, TLS fingerprinting, environment patching, fingerprint browsers, automation, RPC, RPA, unpacking, Root, jailbreaking, Trace, decompilation, CAPTCHA recognition, trajectory simulation, domestic and overseas proxy providers, data/tool vendors, WeChat official account bloggers, Pojie (吾爱破解) bloggers, CSDN bloggers, Bilibili bloggers, self-hosted blogs, discussion forums, practice platforms, paid knowledge content, high-quality projects, AI reverse-engineering MCP, Skills, AI conversational programming, AI image and video, AI document and office work, and various other resources.
