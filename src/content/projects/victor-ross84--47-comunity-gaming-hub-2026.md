## Overview

47 comunity is a web-accessible community site intended to act as a common front door for multiple gaming groups. Instead of spreading each community across separate tools or heavier platforms, it keeps everything centered in one simple website.

Because it is built as a static HTML site, it works well with straightforward hosting and standard content delivery. That makes it a practical fit for teams looking to publish a shared hub for CSGO, GTA 5, FiveM, and Minecraft communities without extra application complexity.
