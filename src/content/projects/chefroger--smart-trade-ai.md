# Smart Trade AI

[Chinese](https://github.com/chefroger/smart-trade-ai/blob/HEAD/README.md) | [English](https://github.com/chefroger/smart-trade-ai/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

> Runs locally · your data never leaves your computer. Installation requires stable access to GitHub. AI-generated content is for reference only; verify key decisions yourself (see [Before You Install](#安装前须知)).

**Write outreach emails, research customer backgrounds, and maintain B2B platforms. This tool takes care of this repetitive work in about 10 minutes. The interface supports switching between Chinese and English, and it also suits foreign colleagues or overseas teams.**

## Get Started in 3 Minutes

**macOS / Linux**

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chefroger/smart-trade-ai/main/scripts/install.sh | bash
```

**Windows (PowerShell)**

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chefroger/smart-trade-ai/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex
```

The script automatically installs Hermes, a Python 3.14 environment, the Trade application itself, and 45 skills. After installation, type `trade` in the terminal and the chat interface will open automatically in your browser.
