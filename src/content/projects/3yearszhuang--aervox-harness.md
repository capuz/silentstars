**Your Jarvis, or your Aemis.**

A personal assistant that remembers you, cares about the present moment, and proactively offers help with your authorization.

[Get started](https://github.com/3yearsZhuang/Aervox-harness/blob/HEAD/docs/getting-started.md) · [Documentation index](https://github.com/3yearsZhuang/Aervox-harness/blob/HEAD/docs/README.md) · [Contributing](https://github.com/3yearsZhuang/Aervox-harness/blob/HEAD/CONTRIBUTING.md)

思隅 is a proactive intelligent personal assistant for life, work, and study: it picks up conversations, organizes memory and daily records, handles tasks through tools and plugins, and also keeps you company as a character you like. Learning and review is one of its capabilities.

**Developer preview** · Continuous conversation, memory, journaling, and tool extensions are already implemented; full-domain awareness, smart home, and health connections are still being refined and validated. For capability and release status, see [Requirements traceability](https://github.com/3yearsZhuang/Aervox-harness/blob/HEAD/docs/reference/REQUIREMENTS_TRACEABILITY.md); back up your local data before upgrading.
