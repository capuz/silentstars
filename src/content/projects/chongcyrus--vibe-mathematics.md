# Vibe Mathematics — Multi-Agent Mathematical Problem Solving and Verification Framework (Four Architectures)

[English](https://github.com/ChongCyrus/Vibe-Mathematics/blob/HEAD/README.en.md) | Chinese

> A set of **agent presets** (`vibe-math-v2` / `vibe-math-v3` / `vibe-math-v4` / `vibe-math-v5`) running inside the **DeepSeek Harness**. They use multi-agent collaboration to automatically solve mathematical problems and cross-verify conclusions across agents. The four presets share the foundational capabilities of '**checkpoint resumption**, **mid-run human intervention**, **progress reporting**, and **natural-language driving**', but use four generations of different solving architectures:
> **💡 v4/v5 first**: `vibe-math-v4` (the 'persistent self-organizing collaborative research' architecture) and `vibe-math-v5` (the latest 'research institute system') are the **current primary maintenance direction**. `vibe-math-v2` and `vibe-math-v3` are **classic/early architectures** that have **matured and been finalized**; going forward they receive only **compatibility adaptation** maintenance (new capabilities go only into v4/v5).
