## 中文 (Chinese)

Asky is a native macOS efficiency launcher built with SwiftUI and AppKit. Local global search and clipboard history work fully offline and independently, while the AI assistant is summoned on demand, combining an extremely lightweight system experience with powerful extensibility.

🌐 **Official Website**: [https://asky.niallyoung2002.workers.dev/](https://asky.niallyoung2002.workers.dev/)  
📖 **Tutorial**: [Feature Guide and Operation Manual](https://asky.niallyoung2002.workers.dev/docs)
