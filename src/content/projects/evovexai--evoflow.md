## Introduction

EvoFlow hands long-running tasks to collaborative Agent Teams: it first clarifies and plans, then executes tools and sandboxed jobs in isolated contexts, with failures recoverable and progress verifiable. The control plane covers conversations, Plan, Goal, the task center, workflows, and observability.
