# NewsNook（有所闻）

A local-first, out-of-the-box, freely extensible information aggregation and reading platform.

News, RSS, Web / CMS sites and independent content communities converge in a clean, controllable reading experience.

---

## What is NewsNook

NewsNook（有所闻）is neither a single RSS reader nor a traditional news client.

It aspires to become **a user's own information gateway**: after installation, you can read built-in news and topics, and you can also add your own RSS / Atom / JSON Feed, integrate recognizable Web / CMS directory sites, and use content communities like Zhihu through independent site workspaces.

These different sources ultimately converge into a unified set of reading capabilities: full-text reading, images and videos, translation, AI speed reading, read later, history, local search, offline caching, sharing and optional sync.

> NewsNook does not produce or host news content, nor does it drive content distribution through ads, cloud profiling, or engagement time.
> You decide what to read; the app takes responsibility for organizing content well, presenting it smoothly, and saving it to your own device.
