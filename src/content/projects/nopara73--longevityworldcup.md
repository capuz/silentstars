# Longevity World Cup

Longevity World Cup is an open competition where longevity athletes rank by improving biological age measures. Athletes submit biomarker data, biological aging clocks turn those results into biological age, and the leaderboard ranks athletes by Age Reduction.

For more context, read the [project story](https://github.com/nopara73/LongevityWorldCup/blob/HEAD/LongevityWorldCup.Documentation/About.md), the [competition rules](https://github.com/nopara73/LongevityWorldCup/blob/HEAD/LongevityWorldCup.Documentation/Ruleset.md), and the [history of longevity as a sport](https://github.com/nopara73/LongevityWorldCup/blob/HEAD/LongevityWorldCup.Documentation/LongevitySportHistory.md).
