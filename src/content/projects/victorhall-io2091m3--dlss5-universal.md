## 🎮 What is This?

**DLSS5-Universal** is a comprehensive tool that enables **DLSS 5 Neural Rendering** on graphics cards from **NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel**.

DLSS 5 Neural Rendering debuted with NBA 2K27 and was officially exclusive to RTX 50-series GPUs . However, the community quickly extracted the `nvngx_dlssnr.dll` file and created mods to bring the technology to older cards .

This project combines multiple community projects:
- **DLSS5oneclick** – automatic setup for any DX11/DX12 game 
- **DLSS-NR-on-AMD** – compatibility layer for AMD Radeon GPUs 
- **DLSS5-Feeder** – synthetic DLAA contract for games without native DLSS 
- **DLSS5-Swapper** – manage DLSS 5 installations
