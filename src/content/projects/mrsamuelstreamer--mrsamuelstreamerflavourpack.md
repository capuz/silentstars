# MSS Tweaks and Fun, formerly the Mr Samuel Streamer Flavour Pack

An all-encompassing tweaks mod that adds a ton of thematic content, modding frameworks, and other little features - all of which are fully controllable and toggleable in the mod options menu!

## New Content

### Haunts
- Haunts System
Colonies can become haunted, and a little ghost of the dead character will actually follow them around the colony offering various buffs, or maybe debuffs for a vengeful enemy! Exorcise the bad spirits and appease the good ones!

Thank you to the various modders who gave permission to add haunts based on them, full list in the credits!

### New Animals
- Frogge - a rideable amphibian mount with stun attacks and water traversal.
- Raven - flying unit for scouting.

### Lovers Retreat Incident
- Two lovers can head off-map for some alone time in the woods, with a chance they'll come back pregnant! Toggleable gender restrictions to allow anyone to get pregnant from a rendezvous in the woods!

### Trek Beamer Incident
- A strange off-worlder has arrived on your map that your colonists find strangely attractive. I'm sure they won't cause any heartbreak!
