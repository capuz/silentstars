# Multimodal Levels

Real, runnable **agent systems** for multimodal + agentic apps on Gemini — one level per folder, each verified end-to-end on live Google Cloud. Distilled from the [Way Back Home](https://github.com/cuppibla/multimodal-production-system) teaching app; architectures follow the [gca way-back-home solutions](https://github.com/gca-americas/way-back-home).

| Level | The verb | What actually runs | Verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`01-beacon/`](https://github.com/cuppibla/multimodal-levels/blob/HEAD/01-beacon/) | **Express** — it creates | multi-turn chat-session generation (portrait→icon, one face) · the **ADK consistency engine** (state + callback + ref-image pin) · **Veo async video** (poll the ticket) · deterministic verify gate | ✅ |
| [`02-perceive/`](https://github.com/cuppibla/multimodal-levels/blob/HEAD/02-perceive/) | **Reason** — it coordinates | a full multi-agent system: **ParallelAgent** specialist crew · a **custom FastMCP server** (soil image + flora **video with audio**) · the **Google-managed BigQuery MCP** (real star-catalog lookups) · evidence on **GCS** · code-judged beacon | ✅ |
