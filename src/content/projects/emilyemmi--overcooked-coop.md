# Overcooked 64!

(NOTICE: This mod is a work-in-progress.)

In this mod, you'll work together with your fellow chefs to serve various dishes in wacky scenarios. Can you complete all 10(?) levels?

A collaboration made for Blocky's "Cooperation" competition, based on the "Overcooked!" series by Ghost Town Games.
