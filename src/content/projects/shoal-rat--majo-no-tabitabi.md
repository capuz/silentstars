# Witch's Journey · Travel by Broom

Play as Elaina, setting out from a desk in your hometown, heading into the forest to learn magic from Flan, then riding a broom west until the land of mages rises from the plains. This free third-person fan game adapts the first two episodes of the anime, and its dialogue has been rewritten for the game. The whole journey is one connected large map. You can fly, walk, climb, and swim, and along the way you'll meet monsters, trials, and travelers who need a hand.

**[Start your journey in the browser](https://weikezhang.cn/majo-no-tabitabi/)** · Simplified Chinese · On desktop, use keyboard and mouse; on phones and tablets, use touch in landscape. The first launch needs to download game assets, and progress is saved in the current browser. Choose "Embark on the Journey", then walk to the desk and press E to open the travel log.

This project is an unofficial, non-profit fan game made by enthusiasts, and it has no connection with the rights holders of the original work.
