# MeteoLab

Numerical atmospheric laboratory in the browser. Inside is **StormLab**: an idealized cloud-resolving model of deep convection that runs in real time, with 3D visualization and diagnostics in the spirit of operational synoptic forecasting. You set a sounding (profiles of temperature, humidity and wind) and a surface, and then the model integrates the equations of motion, thermodynamics and microphysics (rain, ice, snow, graupel, hail) on a grid of about 1 km.

**Status.** Version 2.10.0 “Laboratory”: a research and teaching cloud model with experiments (A/B comparison, ensemble, CAPE × shear regime map, batch series), data for your own analysis (NetCDF following CF conventions, CSV with version, seed and a link to the experiment), and a [validation report](https://github.com/Bandananor/MeteoLAB/blob/HEAD/docs/validation.md) that assembles itself. Earlier: in 2.8, supercell structure (FFD/RFD, θv′ outflow, TVS, satellite); in 2.7, lightning from electrification; in 2.6, advection and synoptic situations; in 2.2, real sounding data (for what changed, see [`CHANGELOG.md`](https://github.com/Bandananor/MeteoLAB/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md)).
