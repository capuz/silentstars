## 🌈 Platform Introduction

qOntology is an open-source ontology engineering platform for enterprise-grade knowledge engineering. Based on ontology theory and following Semantic Web standards such as OWL, RDF, and SHACL, it focuses on enterprise ontology governance and semantic governance. Through unified modeling and semantic asset management, it connects knowledge graphs, enterprise operational ontologies, and ontology-driven AI, building a unified, open, and evolvable enterprise semantic infrastructure.
