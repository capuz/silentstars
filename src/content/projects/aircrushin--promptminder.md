# PromptMinder

A professional prompt management platform that makes AI prompt management simpler and more efficient

## 🌟 Features

### Core Features

- ✅ **Prompt Version Management** - Support for version rollback and history viewing
- ✅ **Version Diff Comparison** - Side-by-side comparison view similar to Git diff, quickly identify prompt update changes
- ✅ **Version Approval Workflow** - Team spaces support configurable approval switches, supporting submit/approve/reject/withdraw
- ✅ **Approval Discussion & @Mentions** - Single-thread approval comments, supporting `@team members` for collaborative review
- ✅ **Change Subscription & Notification Center** - Support subscribing to prompt changes, unified in-site notifications
- ✅ **Tag Management** - Custom tags for quick categorization and retrieval
- ✅ **Public/Private Mode** - Support for private prompts and public sharing
- ✅ **AI Smart Generation** - Integrated AI models for intelligent generation of quality prompts
- ✅ **Team Collaboration** - Support for team creation, member management, and permission control
