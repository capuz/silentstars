# Wandscape

**Chinese** | [English](https://github.com/CheesePaste/Wandscape/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

Minecraft NeoForge 1.21.1 mod — **"Magic Town"** (Wandscape).

> **You are the town mayor; mages do the work.**

## Introduction

Wandscape is a **magic × town building × simulation management** mod (Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge): place a block, and a town springs to life — mage NPCs harvest elements, lay bricks to build houses, craft potions, and defend walls for you; you just manage. Then tourists flock to your roads: shopping, hanging out in taverns, staying in inns, and telling the world how good your town is.

## Getting Started in Ten Minutes

1. **Place the Town Hall** — the first building of each type is free; founding a town costs nothing. Give your town a name and open for business.
2. **Build a warehouse and several element nodes** — the warehouse starts with 6000 of each element in stock; node mages will then begin harvesting on their own.
3. **Watch the mages work** — every building comes with construction blueprints; NPCs lay bricks, transport materials, and repair damage automatically; you don't need to lift a finger.
