# FMphoto (HarmonyOS)

A Feiniu NAS album client for Harmony devices, focusing on "more convenient gallery interaction + more impressive visual presentation".

## ** Light up STAR to help me break the mold **

## The official Feiniu Harmony version APP has started internal testing applications, and it is believed that it will be online soon! Friends who want to apply for the internal test version can click the link below to enter the official community post for detailed information: https://club.fnnas.com/forum.php?mod=viewthread&tid=67389&extra=page%3D1

## Project Highlights

- **HDR Vivid Display Link**: Thumbnail and large image decoding support HDR, allowing for higher dynamic range and richer layers when viewing the original image on supported models.
- **Immersive Light Sense Visual Effects**: Centered on full-screen browsing, timeline organization, and high-quality thumbnails, highlighting the atmosphere and presence of photos.
- **Harmony Album-style Operation Habits**: Long press for multi-selection, continuous swipe selection, pull-to-refresh, tactile feedback, and other interactions have been specifically optimized.
