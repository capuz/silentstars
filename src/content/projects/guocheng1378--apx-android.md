# AllPeriph (All-in-One Peripherals)

> **Turn your phone into a computer peripheral**: touchpad, keyboard, multimedia keys, microphone and speakers, secondary display, **camera**, and bidirectional mutual control and file transfer.

```
 📱 Phone  ──── USB / Wi-Fi / Bluetooth ────▶  💻 Computer
                    ◀─────────────────
 📱 Phone  ◀──── Wi-Fi (9511) ──────▶  📱 Another phone
 📱 Phone  ◀──── Wi-Fi (9511) ──────▶  📺 TV
```

**Wired (USB)** offers the best performance and charges the phone at the same time · **Wireless (Bluetooth + Wi-Fi)** requires no root and no cable

## ✨ What It Can Do

| Scenario | Description |
|------|------|
| 🖱️ **Touchpad + keyboard** | Use your phone as the computer's mouse and keyboard, with two-finger scrolling and gesture right-click |
| ⌨️ **Remote input** | Type on your phone and the text appears on the computer instantly; supports incremental input, whole-paragraph paste, **voice input**, and send reconciliation |
| 🎵 **Media control** | Volume, play, pause, next track |
| 🔊 **Speakers + microphone** | Computer audio plays through the phone (optional output device); the phone's microphone works as the computer's microphone |…
