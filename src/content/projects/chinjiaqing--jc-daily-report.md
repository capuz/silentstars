# ⚽ Football Signal Radar · Daily Signal Report

> **Set emotion aside and watch only the signals.**
> Cross-validate market movements across three channels. No bets at low odds, and no entry when the signal is unclear. Automatic execution before matches, automatic settlement after.

## 🚀 Online Site

- **[https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/](https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/)**
- Updated automatically every hour, with historical dates archived for review at any time
- Covers 500 to 1300+ matches per day (full 竞彩 and Asian handicap coverage), automatically flagging signals and bets

## The Detours We Skipped

We tried **models**: piled on parameters until the room was full. Looking back, the model just recited prices the market had already set. No matter how many parameters, it could not produce anything new.

We tried **studying the market makers**: guessing at the bookmakers' moves, and the more we guessed, the more it looked like paranoia. In the end it became clear that guessing the bookmaker is less useful than watching the price.

We also tried **AI predictions**: injuries, motivation, form. Each one sounded convincing, and each had already been…
