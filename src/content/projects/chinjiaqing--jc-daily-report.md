## The Detours Taken

Tried **models**—stacked parameters everywhere, but looking back, all the model did was recite market prices over again; no matter how many parameters, nothing new emerges.

Tried **studying market manipulation**—stared at dealer tactics and guessed back and forth, the more guesses the more it felt like persecution delusion. Finally understood: guessing the dealer is worse than reading the price.

Also tried **AI prediction**—injuries, fighting spirit, form, each point sounds perfectly reasonable, but each is already priced into the odds. No matter how eloquent,…
