# ⚽ Football Signal Radar · Daily Signal Report

> **Set emotion aside and watch only the signals.**
> Three-channel cross-validation of handicap movements. No bets at low odds, no bets when the signal is unclear. Pre-match execution is automatic, and settlement after the match is automatic.

## 🚀 Online Site

- **[https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/](https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/)**
- Updated automatically every hour, with historical dates archived so you can look back at any time
- Covers **500 to 1300+** matches per day (full Jingcai/Asian handicap coverage), with automatic signal and bet tagging

## The Detours We Bypassed

We tried **models**. We stacked up parameters until the room was full. Looking back, the models only repeated the prices the market had already set. No matter how many parameters we added, nothing new came out.

We tried **studying the bookmakers**. We kept guessing at the operators' moves, and the more we guessed, the more it looked like paranoid delusion. In the end we realized: guessing at the house is less useful than watching the price.

We also tried **AI prediction**: injuries, motivation, form. Every point was…
