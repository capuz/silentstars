# ⚽ Football Signal Radar · Daily Signal Report

> **Set emotion aside and watch only the signals.**
> Cross-validation of odds movements across three channels. No bets on low odds; no bets when the signal is unclear. Automatic before matches, automatic settlement after.

## 🚀 Online Site

- **[https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/](https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/)**
- Updated automatically every hour, with historical dates archived for review at any time
- Covers 500 to 1300+ matches per day (full Jingcai and Asian handicap coverage), automatically flagging signals and bets

## The Detours We Bypassed

We tried **models**: piled up parameters by the dozen. Looking back, the model just recited prices the market had already set. No matter how many parameters, it could not produce anything new.

We tried **studying the operators**: guessing at the bookmakers' tactics over and over, and the more we guessed, the more it looked like paranoia. In the end it became clear that guessing the bookmaker is pointless; watch the price instead.
