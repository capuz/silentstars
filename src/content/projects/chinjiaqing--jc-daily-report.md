# ⚽ Football Signal Radar · Daily Signal Report

> **Set emotion aside and watch only the signals.**
> Cross-validation across three channels of odds movement. No bets at low odds; no bets when the signal is unclear. Bets are placed automatically before kickoff and settled automatically after the match.

## 🚀 Online Site

- **[https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/](https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/)**
- Updated automatically every hour, historical dates archived, available to review anytime
- Covers **500–1300+** matches per day (full 竞彩 / Asian handicap coverage), automatically flagging signals and bets

## The Detours We Skipped

We tried **models**: piled on parameters until the room was full. Looking back, the model just repeated prices the market had already set. No matter how many parameters we added, it could not produce anything new.

We tried **studying the bookmakers' moves**: guessing at the house's tactics, and the more we guessed, the more it looked like paranoia. In the end it became clear that guessing at the bookmaker is worth less than simply watching the price.

We also tried **AI predictions**: injuries, motivation, form. Each one…
