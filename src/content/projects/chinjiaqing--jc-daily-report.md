# ⚽ Football Signal Radar · Daily Signal Report

> **Set emotions aside, watch only the signals.**
> Cross-validation of market movements across three channels. No bets on low odds, no bets when signals are unclear. Runs automatically before matches and settles automatically after.

## 🚀 Online Site

- **[https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/](https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/)**
- Updates automatically every hour, historical dates archived, look back anytime
- Covers **500~1300+** matches per day (full Jingcai/Asian handicap coverage), automatically flags signals and bets

## The Detours We Bypassed

We tried **models**: piled up a room full of parameters. Looking back, the model just recited prices the market had already set. More parameters couldn't produce anything new.

We tried **studying market makers**: guessing at the bookmakers' moves, and the more we guessed, the more it looked like paranoid delusion. In the end we realized: guessing at the house is less useful than watching the price.

We also tried **AI predictions**: injuries, motivation, form. Each sounded convincing, and each had already been priced into the odds long before. However…
