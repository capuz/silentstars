# ⚽ Football Signal Radar · Daily Signal Report

> **Set emotion aside, watch only the signals.**
> Cross-validation of odds movements across three channels; no bets on low odds, no bets when signals are unclear. Pre-match execution is automatic, and post-match settlement is automatic.

## 🚀 Online Site

- **[https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/](https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/)**
- Updated automatically every hour, with historical dates archived for review at any time
- Covers **500–1300+** matches per day (full Sporttery / Asian handicap coverage), with signals and bets automatically flagged

## The Detours We Bypassed

We tried **models**: we piled up parameters until the room was full. Looking back, the model was just repeating prices the market had already set, and no amount of extra parameters could produce anything new.

We tried **studying the bookmakers**: guessing at the house's tactics, guessing again, and growing more paranoid with every guess. In the end the realization was that guessing at the bookmaker is less useful than watching the price.

We also tried **AI prediction**: injuries, motivation, form. Each point sounded convincing,…
