# ⚽ Football Signal Radar · Daily Signal Report

> **Set emotions aside, watch only the signals.**
> Cross-validation of line movements across three channels: no bets at low odds, no bets when signals are unclear. Pre-match bets are placed automatically; post-match settlement is automatic.

## 🚀 Live Site

- **[https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/](https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/)**
- Updated automatically every hour; historical dates archived for review at any time
- Covers **500–1,300+** matches per day (full 竞彩 and Asian handicap coverage), automatically flagging signals and bets

## Detours We Took

We tried **models**: piled on parameters until the room was full. Looking back, the model merely recited prices the market had already set. No matter how many parameters, it could not produce anything new.

We tried **studying the bookmakers**: guessing at the house's moves, guessing more and growing more paranoid. The conclusion: guessing at the bookmaker is worth less than watching the price.

We also tried **AI prediction**: injuries, motivation, form. Every point sounded convincing, and every one had long been priced into the odds. However lively…
