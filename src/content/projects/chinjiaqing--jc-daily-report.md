## The Detours We Bypassed

We tried **models**: piled on parameters until the room was full. Looking back, the model merely recited the prices the market had already set. No matter how many parameters, it couldn't produce anything new.

We tried **studying the bookmakers**: guessing at the house's moves, the more we guessed, the more it looked like paranoid delusion. In the end it became clear: guessing at the bookmaker is less useful than watching the price.

We also tried **AI predictions**: injuries, motivation, form. Each point sounded convincing, and each had long been priced into the odds. No matter how lively…
