## The Detours We've Avoided

Tried **models** — piled up parameters everywhere, but looking back, the model just re-read what the market had already priced, and no amount of parameters could generate anything new.

Tried **studying house tactics** — staring at the bookmaker's methods, guessing endlessly, the more you guess the more it resembles paranoia. Finally understood: guessing the house's moves is not as good as watching the price.

Also tried **AI predictions** — injuries, team morale, form, each point sounds perfectly logical, but all are already priced into the odds. No matter how convincingly…
