# ⚽ Football Signal Radar · Daily Signal Report

> **Set emotion aside, watch only the signals.**
> Cross-validation of line movements across three channels; no bets on low odds, no entry when signals are unclear; execution is automatic before the match, settlement is automatic after.

## 🚀 Online Site

- **[https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/](https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/)**
- Updated automatically every hour, historical dates archived, available for review at any time
- Covers **500–1300+** matches per day (Jingcai/Asian handicap, full coverage), automatically flagging signals and bets

## The Detours We Bypassed

We tried **models** — piled up parameters until the room was full. Looking back, the model merely recited prices the market had already set; no matter how many parameters, it produced nothing new.

We tried **studying market manipulation** — guessing at the bookmaker's moves, and the more we guessed, the more it resembled paranoid delusion. In the end we realized: guessing the house is worse than reading the price.

We also tried **AI prediction** — injuries, motivation, form; each point was argued eloquently, and each had already…
