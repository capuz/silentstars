# ⚽ Football Signal Radar · Daily Signal Report

> **Set emotion aside, watch only the signals.**
> Cross-validation of line movements across three channels. No bets at low odds, no bets when signals are unclear. Pre-match execution is automatic, and post-match settlement is automatic.

## 🚀 Online Site

- **[https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/](https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/)**
- Updated automatically every hour, with historical dates archived for review at any time
- Covers 500–1,300+ matches per day (full Jingcai and Asian handicap coverage), with signals and bets automatically flagged

## Detours We Skipped

We tried **models**: we piled up parameters until the room was full. Looking back, a model only recited prices the market had already set. No matter how many parameters we added, it could not produce anything new.

We tried **studying the bookmakers' moves**: guessing at the house's tactics, and the more we guessed, the more it looked like paranoid delusion. Eventually we realized that guessing the bookmaker is less useful than watching the price.

We also tried **AI predictions**: injuries, motivation, form. Every point sounded convincing,…
