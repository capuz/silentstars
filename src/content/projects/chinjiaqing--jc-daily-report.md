# ⚽ Football Signal Radar · Daily Signal Report

> **Set emotions aside and watch only the signals.**
> Three-channel cross-validation of market movements: no bets on low odds, no bets when signals are unclear. Automatically executed before matches, automatically settled after.

## 🚀 Online Site

- **[https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/](https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/)**
- Updated automatically every hour, historical dates archived, available to review anytime
- Covers **500~1300+** matches per day (full Jingcai/Asian handicap coverage), automatically flags signals and bets

## The Detours We Bypassed

We tried **models**: stacked parameters until the room was full. Looking back, the model only recited prices the market had already set. No matter how many parameters, it couldn't produce anything new.

We tried **studying the bookmakers**: guessing at the bookmakers' moves, the more we guessed, the more it looked like paranoid delusion. In the end I realized: guessing the bookmaker is less useful than watching the price.

We also tried **AI predictions**: injuries, team motivation, form. Each one sounded convincing, and each had already been priced…
