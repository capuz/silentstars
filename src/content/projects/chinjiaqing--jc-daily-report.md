# ⚽ Football Signal Radar · Daily Signal Report

> **Set emotion aside, watch only the signals.**
> Cross-validation of three channels of handicap movement; no bets on low odds, none when signals are unclear. Automatic before kickoff, automatic settlement after the match.

## 🚀 Online Site

- **[https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/](https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/)**
- Automatically updated every hour, historical dates archived, available for review at any time
- Covers **500~1300+** matches per day (full JingCai/Asian handicap coverage), automatically flags signals and bets

## The Detours We Bypassed

We tried **models**: piled up parameters until the room was full. Looking back, the model merely recited prices the market had already set; no matter how many parameters, it could produce nothing new.

We tried **studying the bookmakers**: guessing at the bookie's moves, guessing more and more, until it looked like paranoia. Eventually it became clear: guessing the bookie is less useful than watching the price.
