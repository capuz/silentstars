# ⚽ Football Signal Radar · Daily Signal Report

> **Set emotions aside and watch only the signals.**
> Cross-validation of line movements across three channels. No bets on low odds; no entry when signals are unclear. Execution is automatic before matches, and settlement is automatic after.

## 🚀 Online Site

- **[https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/](https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/)**
- Updated automatically every hour, with historical dates archived for review at any time
- Covers **500–1300+** matches per day (full Jingcai/Asian handicap coverage), with signals and bets automatically flagged

## The Detours We Bypassed

We tried **models**: we piled up parameters until the room was full. Looking back, the model merely recited the prices the market had already set, and more parameters could not produce anything new.

We tried **studying the market makers**: guessing at the bookmakers' moves, we guessed more and more, until it looked like paranoid delusion. Eventually we realized that guessing at the bookmaker is less useful than watching the price.

We also tried **AI predictions**: injuries, team motivation, form. Each one sounded convincing, and…
