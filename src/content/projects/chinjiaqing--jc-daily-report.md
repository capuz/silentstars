# ⚽ Football Signal Radar · Daily Signal Report

> **Set emotions aside, watch only the signals.**
> Cross-validation of odds movements across three channels. No bets on low odds, no entry when signals are unclear. Execution is automatic before kickoff, and settlement is automatic after the result.

## 🚀 Live Site

- **[https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/](https://chinjiaqing.github.io/jc-daily-report/)**
- Updated automatically every hour, historical dates archived and available to review anytime
- Covers **500–1300+** matches per day (Jingcai / Asian handicap, full coverage), with signals and bets automatically flagged

## The Detours We Bypassed

We tried **models**: piled up a roomful of parameters, only to look back and realize the model was just reciting prices the market had already set. No matter how many parameters, it couldn't produce anything new.

We tried **studying market manipulation**: guessing at the bookmakers' moves, and the more we guessed, the more it looked like paranoia. In the end we figured out that guessing the house is worse than reading the price.

We also tried **AI predictions**: injuries, motivation, form. Each one sounded perfectly…
