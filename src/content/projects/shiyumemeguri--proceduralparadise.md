# ProceduralParadise

Using **Blender Geometry Nodes + Python/CLI**, reconstruct game worlds in a procedural and modular way. All geometries are generated from GN assets, and all layouts, dimensions, color schemes, and camera settings are defined in JSON data; scenes can be rebuilt and rendered with a single command-line keystroke, or saved as `.blend` files for free camera control in Blender.

Current content (left: renders, right: original artwork):

* **Millennium Science Gakuen (Millennium) Club Activity Room** (`BG_Milleniumclub`), including the Millennium campus tower visible outside the window, Kivotos city, and sky halo;

* **Shanhaijing Senior High School (Shanhaijing) Tea House** (`BG_ShanTeaHouse_Night`, night scene): wooden beam frames and hanging columns, red pillar corridors, antique shelves and moon gates, eight-directional crabapple round windows, angled school emblem walls with stone lions, tea tables, pendant lights and hanging chairs, red lanterns. See [`BlueArchive/Shanhaijing/Rooms/TeaHouse/README.md`](https://github.com/ShiyumeMeguri/ProceduralParadise/blob/HEAD/BlueArchive/Shanhaijing/Rooms/TeaHouse/README.md) for details.
