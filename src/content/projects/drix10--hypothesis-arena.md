## What is this project trying to do?

Beat a plain statistical trading baseline, net of all costs, using free public data plus AI judgment, and **prove it** with logs instead of backtest stories.

The bet, in one paragraph: public information (SEC filings, macro releases, calendars, market structure) contains real but diffuse trading signal. AI agents are good at reading that diffuse information; they are bad at sizing positions, managing risk, and knowing when they are wrong. So MiroHedge splits the job: AI reads and suggests, a calibrated gatekeeper scores, and deterministic C++ code…
