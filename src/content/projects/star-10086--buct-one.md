# BUCT One · One-Stop BUCT

**Native Liquid Glass campus workspace for Beijing University of Chemical Technology (BUCT)**

A native Android campus client for BUCT students and staff. Sign in once through the school's unified identity authentication, and connect timetables, home-screen widgets, grades and training plans, course selection and enrollment rush, BUCT Online, Second Classroom five-dimensional evaluation, practice teaching, fitness tests and physical education, campus email, and campus smart card. Local-first, with zero third-party reporting.
