# Fahrmony 合拍

> A local interoperability bridge tool built for Android Auto, enabling seamless display and partial control of your frequently-used Chinese streaming audio and instant messaging notifications when driving with Android Auto overseas.

## What is Fahrmony?

**Fahrmony** is named after the German word for driving (**Fahren**) and the English word for harmony (**Harmony**), translated to Chinese as **合拍**.

Many Chinese expats who drive vehicles supporting Android Auto face a frustrating reality: the car's native app ecosystem barely includes the Chinese software they habitually use. While their phones contain their favorite music apps or podcasts, once in the car they often revert to the most primitive Bluetooth audio: the car's infotainment system and steering wheel cannot skip songs or control playback; when receiving new messages from WeChat, Feishu, and other messaging apps during driving, they are forced to risk taking out their phones to check them.
