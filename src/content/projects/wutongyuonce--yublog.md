# YuBlog

[Live preview](https://www.wutongyu.site/) · [Documentation index](https://github.com/wutongyuonce/YuBlog/blob/HEAD/docs/README.md) · [Development & deployment](https://github.com/wutongyuonce/YuBlog/blob/HEAD/docs/guides/development.md) · [English](https://github.com/wutongyuonce/YuBlog/blob/HEAD/README_ENG.md) · [Changelog](https://github.com/wutongyuonce/YuBlog/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md)

Wutongyu's personal blog, built with Astro 7, TypeScript, and Markdown/MDX. It focuses on reading articles, and also includes projects, books/films/games, a personal introduction, and friend links. It offers light and dark themes, optional accent colors, full-text search, and full-text RSS.

Pages and articles are generated as static HTML at build time, so normal reading does not depend on a backend. The Last.fm chart on the homepage is fetched through a runtime API, and comments are provided by GitHub Discussions / Giscus.
