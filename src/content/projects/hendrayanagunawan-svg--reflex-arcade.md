# 🎯 PrecisionPath — Reflex Geometry Trainer

---

## 🧭 A Different Kind of Target Practice

Most aim trainers treat your cursor like a hammer and targets like nails. **PrecisionPath** takes the opposite approach — it treats your aim like a **language**. Every flick, every micro-correction, every hesitation is a word in a sentence your hand is writing to your brain. This tool teaches you to speak that language fluently, not just faster.

Built for gamers who want **competitive edge**, esports aspirants, and even office workers who want surgical mouse precision, PrecisionPath transforms raw clicking into **cognitive conditioning**. It's not about shooting more — it's about *reading the geometry of movement* before you even move.
