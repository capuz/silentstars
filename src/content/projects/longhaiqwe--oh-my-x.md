## Why this project

The X web interface requires several detours for common actions, for example:

- Under long posts and regular posts, there are many replies from emoji-stuffed traffic accounts and porn-traffic accounts;
- When replying under a post of several thousand words, you have to scroll down several screens before reaching the input box;
- To quote your own past posts or long-form articles in a new post, you have to open another tab to find the link;
- In your Following list, you can't tell who isn't following you back. You have to open each profile one by one to check.

oh-my-x is built on top of 星川's ([@star_flow_ai](https://x.com/star_flow_ai)) [X Focus](https://x.com/star_flow_ai/status/2097583620936585339), and adds features requested by the author and by friends on X. Any feature you don't need can be turned off in settings.
