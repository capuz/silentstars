## 🚀 Introduction

This project provides an enterprise-level low-code data visualization big screen solution, allowing for the creation of beautiful dashboards through simple drag-and-drop operations. The project aims to address issues such as **complicated development processes**, **high development costs**, and **high maintenance costs** associated with enterprise visualization big screens. Based on years of experience, we have found that big screens developed with significant effort often become difficult to maintain due to production changes, ultimately becoming **window dressing**. This solution effectively addresses these issues and promotes digital transformation for enterprises.
