Make mid-platform development easier -- A React business component library based on a secondary encapsulation of Ant Design.

## ✨ Features

- 📦 **Out of the box** — 40+ business components, 40+ field components, covering high-frequency scenarios in mid-platform.
- 🛡 **TypeScript** — Fully written in TypeScript, providing complete type definitions and intelligent prompts.
- 🎨 **Theme customization** — Easily adapt to business brand colors based on less variables and ConfigProvider.
- 🌍 **Internationalization** — Built-in multilingual capabilities, supporting on-demand expansion.
- 🧩 **Schema-driven** — Accompanied by a schema-render rendering engine, configuration is the page.
- 📐 **Design specifications** — A unified design language and interaction specifications to maintain mid-platform consistency.
