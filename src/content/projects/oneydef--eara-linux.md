> Independent community project — **not** affiliated with Nothing Technology or CMF.

EarA brings battery, ANC, EQ, gestures, find-my, and Bluetooth audio routing to Linux desktops. Tested with **Nothing Ear (a)**; other Nothing / CMF models use the same protocol family ([LINEUP.md](https://github.com/oneydef/EarA-linux/blob/HEAD/LINEUP.md)).
