# Kazumi

A client for aggregating and watching anime online based on custom rules, with danmaku (bullet comments).<br>
Native SwiftUI implementation. The interface uses the iOS 26 Liquid Glass material and changes with the system appearance and accessibility settings;<br>
The same interface also runs on Apple Silicon Macs via Mac Catalyst.

## Features

- **Rule-based collection**: Each site is defined by a custom rule, which can be imported, shared, or installed directly from a rule repository. When a site is redesigned, just update the rule without waiting for an app update.
- **Online playback**: Built-in mpv playback engine with nine speed levels from 0.5x to 3x. Playback progress is saved automatically, and playback works in the background and on the lock screen.
- **Super-resolution**: Built-in Anime4K v4.0.1 shaders with three modes: Off, Efficiency, and Quality. Processing happens in real time during playback, with no pre-transcoding needed.
- **Danmaku**: Connects to dandanplay danmaku and overlays them on the timeline. Position, font size, speed, outline, color, and deduplication are adjustable, and keyword blocking is supported.
