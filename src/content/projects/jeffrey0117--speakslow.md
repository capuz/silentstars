# 聲聲慢 (SpeakSlow)

### Speaking is several times faster than typing

**The fastest local voice input built for Chinese. Speak, and the text appears right where your cursor is. It runs entirely on your own computer, and not a single character goes to the cloud. Free and open source.**

🌐 **[Official website](https://jeffrey0117.github.io/SpeakSlow/)** ・ 📖 **[User guide](https://jeffrey0117.github.io/SpeakSlow/#/guide)** ・ ⬇️ **[Direct download](#-下載windows--macos--linux)**

> Press **Right Alt / Right Ctrl** → speak Chinese → the text is **automatically pasted where your cursor is**.
> Speech recognition **runs entirely on your local machine**, and not a single character is uploaded to the cloud.

🎬 <a href="https://jeffrey0117.github.io/SpeakSlow/demo.mp4">High-definition video version</a> (pasted as soon as you finish speaking, entirely local)

## Why did someone speak 18,000 characters in one day with it?

Because **speaking is naturally faster than typing**, especially in Chinese. You just need a tool that **turns your speech into clean text the moment you finish and places it right at your cursor**, and you don't want to upload your voice anywhere.
