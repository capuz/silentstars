Hongguo TV is an Android TV client for Hongguo short dramas. The UI is written from scratch with native Kotlin + Compose, and playback uses ExoPlayer.
**The data layer is not in the APK**: the catalog, details, playback URLs, danmaku, and accounts are all handled by a `main.js` script that is delivered from the cloud and runs locally on the device.

Updating the script does not require a new release. This is the most important design decision in this project.
