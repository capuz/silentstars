# KoralPaper

**Draw your thinking.** A free, offline sketch studio where Claude draws with you:
hand-drawn diagrams, sticky-note boards, LinkedIn carousels, charts from pasted
data, and stop-motion time-lapse animations. It runs in any browser from a single
HTML file. No account, no server, no install; your work never leaves your machine.

**[▶ Open the app](https://karagos.github.io/koralpaper/)** · it works offline and installs as an app after the first visit.

A local, offline vector diagram and sketch studio: warm cream paper, faint
grid, wobbly hand-drawn shapes, sticky notes, label chips, curved and elbow
arrows, serif headlines, and the coral KoralPaper asterisk. Built for
thinking out loud: diagrams that look sketched by a confident hand, not
generated by a tool.
