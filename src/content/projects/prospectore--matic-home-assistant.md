# Matic for Home Assistant

Make Matic part of your home: rotate fairly through rooms, save each room's
cleaning preferences, and start or stop plans as your household comes and goes.
Pair once over Bluetooth; everyday control, maps, and state use your local network.

**[Install](#install) · [Cleaning guide](https://github.com/ProspectOre/matic-home-assistant/blob/HEAD/docs/cleaning.md) · [Automations](https://github.com/ProspectOre/matic-home-assistant/blob/HEAD/docs/automation.md) · [All docs](https://github.com/ProspectOre/matic-home-assistant/blob/HEAD/docs/README.md)**

## Home Assistant cleaning workflows

- **Give every room a turn.** Smart Order starts with the rooms and custom areas that have waited longest. Short trips away won't keep restarting the same first rooms, and confirmed room work from the Matic app can count too.
- **Build a plan around your home.** Save rooms and custom areas in one order, each with its own vacuum, mop or vacuum and mop setting and Quick, Optimal or Heavy duty coverage. Preview the next run before starting it.
