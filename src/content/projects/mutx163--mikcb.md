## Project Overview

Qingyu Timetable is built on the design philosophy of "non-intrusive schedule awareness," tightly coordinating the academic calendar, phased dynamic reminders, a persistent notification bar, home screen widgets, and a system-level dynamic capsule. Without frequently opening the app, you can instantly see accurate course progress on the lock screen, status bar, and home screen.

For detailed interface demonstrations and design specifications, see the [official site](https://163366.xyz).

## Core Features

### 1. Deep System Integration and Dynamic Reminders
* **HyperOS Super Island integration**: Deeply integrates with Xiaomi HyperOS focus notification system, supporting millisecond-level scheduling of the dynamic capsule and expansion into large cards. Expanded row content supports custom show/hide and sorting.
* **Phased smart scheduling**: Supports dynamic transitions across multiple stages (before class, during class, and approaching the end of class), with differentiated visual feedback.
