## Overview

Apple Business Roles & Permissions Checker provides a focused browser interface for examining Apple Business Manager roles and their associated permissions. Administrators can use it to inspect access configuration without installing a larger or more complex application.

The project is delivered as a static website, making it simple to open locally or publish through static hosting. It is suited to quick access reviews, recurring audits, and targeted checks of business account permissions.
