# 墨鸽 Inkdove

**A workspace for export sales teams using WhatsApp, LINE & Telegram: company numbers are shared externally and customers are assigned to salespeople. When needed, AI retrieves grounding from the knowledge base via RAG (full-text search + built-in vector store) and drafts replies, which are sent only after human confirmation.**
