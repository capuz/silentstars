# Agent Infinite Creation · Turn Your Story into Hundreds of Millions of Frames

Chinese | [English](https://github.com/SwotAtmk/infinite-creation/blob/HEAD/README_EN.md)

## Project Introduction
- A free, open-source local Agent project for fully automatic video generation ✨. No actors, voice-over artists, directors, or editors are needed. You can generate long videos locally with one click. Use it for novel-to-video, comic-drama ads, and story explainer videos, and turn the stories you imagine into hundreds of millions of frames.
- One-click novel-to-video generation with no length limit. Submit a novel or script, and the local ComfyUI-MiniMaxH3 + Agent + Skill pipeline fully automatically produces videos endlessly.
