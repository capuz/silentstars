## What It Does

Arena Auto Pilot is a browser automation utility for reducing manual work across recurring game tasks, reward collection, and farming activities. It targets web-based processes that may combine gameplay-style actions with crypto or airdrop operations, including sequences involving Telegram and EVM wallets.

Rather than handling only isolated clicks, the script coordinates complete task sequences through several stages. Its adaptive execution approach is intended to manage ordering, retries, and conditions that may shift while a web workflow is running.
