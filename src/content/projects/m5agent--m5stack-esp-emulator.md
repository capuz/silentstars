# M5Stack Firmware Simulation Platform

Simplified Chinese · [English](https://github.com/m5agent/m5stack-esp-emulator/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

Run M5Stack firmware in a browser or local service to view the screen and serial port, and operate buttons, touch, and modeled peripherals. The platform uses C/QEMU and separate device backends. UIFlow2 validation coverage is the most complete, and compatible Arduino, ESP-IDF, and MicroPython images can also run.
