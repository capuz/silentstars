# 🎵 Yandex Music — Plus Unlocker & Downloader

A browser userscript that bypasses the Plus subscription and regional restrictions, removes ad and preview tracks, blocks ads, and lets you download tracks, albums, playlists, and artist discographies directly from Yandex Music.

---

---

Script features:
- **Native integration** into the site interface: buttons, windows, and settings look like native Yandex Music elements;
- **Automatic embedding of metadata (tags) and covers** directly into downloaded files without third-party programs, with each tag individually enabled or disabled;
- **FckCensor** (optional): restores original titles, covers, and audio where Yandex “bleeped” or replaced them;
- **Unified “Settings” page**: token, FckCensor, scrobbling, lyrics, lyrics translation, Karaoke, download parameters, tags, cosmetics (“My Wave” and color scheme), Effects, auto-skip, and backup of settings and playlists in one place, with search across settings and a reset button for each section;
- **Downloading without extra prompts:** set a default quality, and tracks will download immediately;
