# 🎵 Yandex Music — Plus Unlocker & Downloader

A browser userscript that bypasses the Plus subscription, regional restrictions, and ad/preview tracks, blocks ads, and lets you download tracks, albums, playlists, and artist discographies directly from Yandex Music.

Script features:
- **Native integration** into the site's interface: buttons, windows, and settings look like native Yandex Music elements;
- **Automatic embedding of metadata (tags) and covers** directly into downloaded files without third-party programs, with each tag able to be turned on or off separately;
- **FckCensor** (optional): restores original titles, covers, and audio where Yandex "bleeped" or replaced them;
- **Single "Settings" window**: token, FckCensor, texts, "My Wave" view, color scheme, tags, download parameters, sound effects, and autoskip all in one place;
- **Download without extra prompts:** set a default quality, and tracks will download immediately;
- **Fast bulk download:** several tracks download simultaneously (from 1 to 8, default 4), the script automatically retries on failure, and the "Retry failed" button re-downloads…
