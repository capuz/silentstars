## What this is

Calab is a corporate messenger where the main thing is **voice**. Enter a room and you immediately hear your colleagues; nearby is a chat with files and reactions; at any time you can share your screen. Everything runs on your server: one `docker compose up`, PostgreSQL and LiveKit inside, no external services or subscriptions.

Designed for teams of up to 20–30 people simultaneously in voice and up to 3 streams in a room (MVP); scales through Kubernetes.
