# Real-time Updates of Latest American and British TV Series

An aggregation and display platform focused on information about American and British TV series, automatically collecting the latest release information from TMDB and integrating Douban rating data. The project updates automatically every day, providing multi-dimensional filtering features to help users quickly discover and track the series they are interested in. It supports filtering by region, rating, streaming platform, type, and other combinations of conditions, equipped with an interactive year timeline navigation for more convenient and efficient browsing of series.

Online Experience: https://stevenspage.github.io/latest_tv

## Features

### 🎬 Multi-dimensional Filtering
- **Region Filtering**: Supports switching between American and British series
- **Rating Filtering**: Filter by Douban rating (All, >9 points, >8 points, >7 points, high scores in the last 2 years)
- **Streaming Platform Filtering**: Supports mainstream platforms such as Netflix, Apple TV, Hulu, Prime Video, Disney, Paramount, HBO, ABC, FOX, CBS, NBC, etc.
