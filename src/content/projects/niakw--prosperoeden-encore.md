> [!IMPORTANT]
> **FC27 performance — live master tracker:** [F07 P0/P1/P2 priorities and sparse-JIT qualification](https://github.com/niakw/ProsperoEden-Encore/blob/HEAD/docs/FC27_F07_MASTER_TRACKER.md). [F01–F06 host exit gates](https://github.com/niakw/ProsperoEden-Encore/blob/HEAD/docs/FC27_EXIT_GATES_2026-10-22.md) are separate from on-console playability. Source-review totals are not remaining optimization counts.
>
> **Recommended/tested firmware: 13.60, the only firmware actually tested with Encore on a console.** Earlier firmware versions **may work** when the homebrew loader, PS5 native APIs, Vulkan and filesystem access are compatible; this remains **unverified**, and no minimum firmware has been established. Newer versions are also untested. Even on 13.60, frame pacing and gameplay freezes remain under investigation. This is **not** advice to update your console.
>
> **Before a console test:** [source, logs, upstream and package checklist](https://github.com/niakw/ProsperoEden-Encore/blob/HEAD/docs/PRETEST_AUDIT_2026-10-14.md) — verify that the artifact and symbols match the exact tested SHA.
