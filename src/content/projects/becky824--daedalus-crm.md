# Daedalus CRM

**The next-generation CRM, running on your own computer. AI drafts, you make the call.**

**Desktop customer management system (Mac / Windows)**: leads → customers → follow-ups → opportunities → contracts signed, with referral attribution calculated automatically.<br/>
**Personal edition free**: for one-person companies, independent consultants, and salespeople who bring their own clients. Install and use right away; your data is a single file on your computer.<br/>
**Team edition priced per seat**: several people, each using their own desktop app; customers, follow-ups, and opportunities sync automatically.<br/>
The home page is an agent chat panel. Ask a question and it decides on its own what to look up. If you want to change data, it only offers suggestion cards, and nothing is written until you confirm.
