# dsh-suite

> 🌐 Chinese · [English](https://github.com/whyihaveyou/dsh-suite/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

**Stop going through the `dsh-plugin` topic anymore—here are only plugins that still work.**

`dsh-suite` is a **living directory** of [DeepSeek Harness](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness) (DSH) plugins—**automatically refreshed hourly, compatibility-tested daily**—plus a built-in DSH **plugin store**, `create-dsh-plugin` scaffolder, several first-party plugins, and a **[unified workstation](https://github.com/whyihaveyou/dsh-workstation)** (🚧 under development).

**🧭 Ecosystem**

- 🌐 [Directory Website](https://whyihaveyou.github.io/dsh-suite/zh.html) — Browse all plugins online
- 📖 [DSH Chinese Documentation · dsh-docs.com](https://dsh-docs.com) — Chinese tutorials and documentation, ready to use without VPN
- 📕 [DSH Plugin Development Guide](https://github.com/whyihaveyou/dsh-plugin-tutorial) — Published in both Chinese and English
- 🎨 [Theme Gallery](https://whyihaveyou.github.io/dsh-themes/) — 151 skins with day/night dual preview
