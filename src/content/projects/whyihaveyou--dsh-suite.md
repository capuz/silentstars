# dsh-suite

> 🌐 Chinese · [English](https://github.com/whyihaveyou/dsh-suite/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

**Stop digging through the `dsh-plugin` topic. Everything here is a plugin that still works.**

`dsh-suite` is the **living directory** of [DeepSeek Harness](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness) (DSH) plugins, **automatically refreshed every hour and compatibility-tested daily**. It also includes the DSH built-in **plugin store**, the `create-dsh-plugin` scaffolder, several in-house plugins, and an **[all-in-one workstation](https://github.com/whyihaveyou/dsh-workstation)** (🚧 under development).

**🧭 Ecosystem / Ecosystem**

- 🌐 [Directory website](https://whyihaveyou.github.io/dsh-suite/zh.html): browse all plugins online
- 📖 [DSH Chinese docs · dsh-docs.com](https://dsh-docs.com): Chinese tutorials and documentation, ready to use without a VPN
- 📕 [DSH Plugin Development Guide](https://github.com/whyihaveyou/dsh-plugin-tutorial): published as a book in Chinese and English
- 🎨 [Theme gallery](https://whyihaveyou.github.io/dsh-themes/): 151 themes with day and night previews
