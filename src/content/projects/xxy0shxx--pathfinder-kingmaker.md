# 🗺️ Interactive map and quest log for Pathfinder Kingmaker

A convenient tool for `Game Masters` and `players`. It lets you visually manage territory exploration, move your party across hexes, and keep track of all in-game quests in one place.

---

## 🌟 Features for players and Game Masters

### 1. Global strategic map 🗺️
* **Land exploration:** The entire map is initially hidden by `fog of war`. New hexes are revealed as your party advances.
* **Party movement:** You can place a `party flag 🚩` at any point on the map.
* **Every map cell can be customized:** You can mark objects: `Landmarks 🏛️`, `Resources 💎`, or `Mysteries ❓`.
* **Terrain settings:** For each hex you set the terrain type, from `plains 🌾` to `mountains ⛰️` and `swamps 🐸`, `travel difficulty 🎲` and `danger level 💀`. You can paint zones, give them names, and leave notes.
* **Convenient controls:** The map can be freely dragged with the mouse or swiped on smartphones, and zoomed with the scroll wheel.
