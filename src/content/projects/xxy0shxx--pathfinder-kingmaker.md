# 🗺️ Interactive map and assistant for live play of Pathfinder Kingmaker

An interactive digital tabletop for `🧙‍♂️Мастеров` and `🛡️игроков` based on the **Pathfinder 2e Kingmaker** system.

**The main idea of the tool is to preserve the magic of live dice rolls at the table:** players and the GM still roll real physical dice, while all the routine math, modifiers, current check DCs, tracking of resources, weather, and party status are instantly calculated and shown clearly on a screen or TV panel.

---

## 🌟 Features for live games

### 1. Global strategic map 🗺️
* **Land exploration:** The entire map is hidden behind a configurable `🌫️туманом войны`. New hexes open as the group advances, and the traveled route is recorded as a `🧭линии пути`.
* **Party movement:** Place a `🚩флаг группы` or a 3D figure (`♛ферзь`, `♜ладья`, `🏰цитадель`, `🎲кубик d20`) with a choice of color and an arrival animation at any point on the map.
* **Hex card and development:** For each cell, objects are set (`🏛️Достопримечательности`, `💎Ресурсы`, `⚜️Задания`,…
