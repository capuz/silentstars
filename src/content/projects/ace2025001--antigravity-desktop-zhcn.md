# Antigravity Desktop Localization Pack (Antigravity Chinese) | Windows / Mac Simplified Chinese Language Pack

An open-source Simplified Chinese localization pack (Antigravity Chinese Language Pack) for the Google Antigravity Desktop client. Works on both Windows and Apple Silicon Mac. Extract and use. After clicking update in the client, it is automatically re-localized, and you can restore English at any time with one click.
