# Antigravity Desktop Localization Pack (Antigravity Chinese) | Windows / Mac Simplified Chinese Language Pack

An open-source Simplified Chinese localization pack for the Google Antigravity Desktop client (Antigravity Chinese language pack). It works on Windows and on Mac with Apple chips. Extract it and use it. After you click update in the client, it is automatically localized again, and you can restore English at any time with one click.
