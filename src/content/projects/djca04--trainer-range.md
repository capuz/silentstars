# 🎯 Precision Playbook - Advanced Finals Shooting Simulator

---

## 🏀 Overview

**Precision Playbook** is a next-generation finals shooting simulator crafted for elite performance trainers, sports academies, and competitive coaching staff who demand granular control over every rep, release, and rebound. Where the original concept focused on capturing the rhythm of a single shooter, Precision Playbook zooms out and treats the entire training environment as a living, breathing organism — one where every variable, from crowd noise decibels to the arc of a wrist flick, can be tuned, tracked, and mastered.

Think of it as a maestro's baton for the hardwood. Instead of a simple shot chart, you get a rehearsal stage. Instead of static numbers, you get a story told through latency, biomechanics, and decision fatigue. This repository is the heartbeat of that stage.

The simulator is engineered around a resilience-first architecture: it performs gracefully whether you're running a laptop courtside at a regional final or a full workstation in a performance lab. Every session becomes a data artifact, and every artifact becomes a coaching insight.
