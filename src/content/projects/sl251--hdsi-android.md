Run the narrative and long-term memory engine of HDS-Interlude locally on Android phones.

---

## ✦ The chat ends, but the story doesn't necessarily end

HDSI characters have long-term memory, mood, and schedule systems. They can reach out to you proactively, publish posts, or choose not to reply. Chats, memories, and posts are separated by story, so when you switch stories, posts from different storylines are not mixed together.

HDSI Android ports the core engine of the upstream [HDS-Interlude](https://github.com/ISEKAI-MomoiCore/HDS-Interlude) to run locally on Android, and adapts phone features such as notifications, audio recording, files, and background running. No extra computer or VPS deployment is needed, and no manual Termux configuration is required.
