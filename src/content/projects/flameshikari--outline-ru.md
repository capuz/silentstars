# 📚 [Outline](https://github.com/outline/outline) with Russian translation [](https://github.com/flameshikari/outline-ru/actions) [](https://github.com/flameshikari/outline-ru/releases/latest)

## ❓ Why

Support for the Russian language in [Outline](https://github.com/outline/outline) was discontinued in version [0.71.0](https://github.com/outline/outline/releases/tag/v0.71.0) for [some reasons](https://github.com/outline/outline/discussions/5706).

## 📝 Notes

- The image is available on [Docker Hub](https://hub.docker.com/r/flameshikari/outline-ru/tags) and [GHCR](https://github.com/flameshikari/outline-ru/pkgs/container/outline-ru)
- The translation is based on [this commit](https://github.com/outline/outline/commit/228d1faa9fd3cbb82409d98e1443fed65adc5715)
- You can report incorrect translations [here](https://github.com/flameshikari/outline-ru/discussions/8)
