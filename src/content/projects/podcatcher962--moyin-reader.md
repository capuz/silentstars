# 墨吟 · MoYin Reader

**墨吟**（pinyin mó yín）｜English name **MoYin Reader**｜repository `moyin-reader` — search for 「墨吟」or 「moyin reader」to find this project.

**[Chinese](#中文) | [English](#english)**

🌐 **[Use online (no installation)](https://podcatcher962.github.io/moyin-reader/)** — open directly in a browser. Imported books and reading progress are stored on your device; nothing is uploaded to any server.


A single-file, offline-by-default Chinese **TXT / EPUB / PDF / DOCX / MOBI / AZW3** reader (drag in a `.zip` archive to import the whole package at once; scanned PDFs can also be paged through as the original pages), with read-aloud, highlight annotations and reading summaries. Its layout reaches the level of mature reading software: scrolling or full-screen page turns, two-column layout on widescreen and single-column on phones, six themes, auto-reading and immersive full screen. Read-aloud uses a 16 kHz Chinese multi-speaker library (187 speakers: 39 male, 148 female). **Every voice can be the lead character.** The desktop edition features 50 curated voices and the mobile edition 30; one 20-character sentence takes about 0.9 seconds.
