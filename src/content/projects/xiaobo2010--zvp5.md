# ZVP5 · Zhenhai High School Volunteer Management Platform

> An open-source management platform for secondary school volunteer service: **activity organization → volunteer registration → hour accounting → statistical reports → audit trail**,
> with a controllable AI assistant (ZVAgent) and an administrator operations console.
>
> Frontend: Vue 3 + PWA. Backend: FastAPI + Oracle Autonomous Database. Deployed with systemd + nginx, with no container dependency.
