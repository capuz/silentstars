## 🌌 What Is ToastyLink Continuum?

Imagine a lighthouse that doesn't just shine across a dark sea — it *remembers every ship it has ever guided*. That is the spirit behind **ToastyLink Continuum**, a next-generation introspection workspace for people who love to understand how their favorite applications behave in memory. Born from the lineage of pointer-chain tooling and shareable observation tables, Continuum reframes the entire discipline: instead of chaotic snapshots and one-off scans, you build a **persistent atlas** of the values you care about, watch them breathe, and travel between them along golden threads that survive restarts, updates, and platform migrations.

Whether you are a reverse-engineering hobbyist, a speedrun researcher studying frame data, a student of operating-systems internals, or a power user who simply wants to understand why an app keeps forgetting a setting, ToastyLink Continuum gives you the lens, the compass, and the cartography.

The name says it all: **Link** the values. **Toast** the moment they change. Stay **Continuum** — always connected, always…
