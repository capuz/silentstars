## About Delta Executor

Delta Executor is a native Windows application designed for Roblox players who want to run Lua scripts during their game sessions. Unlike browser-based or online alternatives, this tool operates locally, giving you full control over injection timing and script management. The interface is simple: choose a script from the built-in hub or your local files, click inject, and the script runs inside the active Roblox client.
