<b style="font-size: 28px;">Make X Great Again</b>

<b>Less junk, more human conversation.</b><br>
You browse X as usual, and the community-built public blacklist helps you identify ad accounts and porn bots · Chrome extension · AGPL-3.0 open source

---

## What does this project aim to solve?

The current problems with X are well known:

- Half of the comments are from ad accounts and porn bots, drowning out normal discussions.
- When trying to follow a newcomer, it’s hard to tell if they are a real account or a bot.
- The algorithm decides who you see, rather than you deciding.
- To see what someone has talked about and their hottest posts, you have to manually scroll through dozens of layers.

**Make X Great Again (MXGA)**, once installed, uses a community-built public blacklist to help you mark these junk accounts and hide them locally with one click — the default "local hide" mode does not call any X interfaces, and the extension itself makes zero network requests; if you want to go further, you can choose to use your own X login state in the settings to call X's native mute/block (still not going through our servers and not collecting any data).
