# Koma

A comic bookshelf and reader for HarmonyOS. Put local comics, private libraries, and self-added book sources on the same shelf, save your reading position, and pick up where you left off.

## What it can do

- **Local reading**: import CBZ / ZIP, CBR / RAR, CB7 / 7z, CBT / TAR, image-based EPUB, and image folders.
- **Connect private libraries**: supports Komga, Kavita, OPDS, and WebDAV. Specific content formats and permissions depend on the server.
- **Add your own book sources**: install and update `.koma` sources from a subscription URL, or import them locally. You can search for books within a source, or use global search to open results from different sources.
- **Reading and offline saving**: single-page, double-page, vertical paged, and continuous scroll reading, plus chapter downloads, history, and continue reading.
- **Shelf maintenance**: manual and smart categories, chapter update records, cross-source migration, personal notes, and backup and restore.
- **Image plugins**: install processing algorithms from separate subscriptions, view their purpose, configure their parameters, and enable or disable each one separately.
