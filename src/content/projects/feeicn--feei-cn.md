## What Is This

**A long-term publicly accumulated system of judgment and methodology.**

It uses documents, data and retrospectives to record how a person continuously makes choices in health, career, wealth and life experience. Cybersecurity is the long-term main line, and other directions are extensions of the same problem: how to establish more stable judgment and action systems in uncertain environments.
