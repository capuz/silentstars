## What This Is

This is a long-term, publicly maintained personal system. It uses documents, data, and retrospectives to record how I keep making choices in health, career, wealth, and life experiences. Cybersecurity is the long-term main thread, and the other directions extend from the same question: how to build a more stable system of judgment and action in an uncertain environment.

If you are visiting for the first time, you can start with [Creating a Certain Life](https://feei.cn/life-certainty), or go directly to any of the long-term directions above.
