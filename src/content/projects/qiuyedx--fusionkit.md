**FusionKit** is a cross-platform desktop application for subtitle and translation work. It brings local audio and video transcription, AI subtitle translation and revision, format conversion, and batch export together in one workspace, and includes **FusionKit Agent**: describe what you want in a sentence, and the Agent finds files, prepares tasks, and completes the whole workflow after you confirm.

- **All-in-one subtitle workbench**: transcription, translation, proofreading, previewing the original audio, and export, all in one place.
- **Local first**: audio and video transcription uses Whisper models on your device, so media files are not uploaded; subtitle documents are stored on your machine.
- **Reviewable and reversible**: every AI edit shows a diff preview first and is written only after you confirm, and it can be undone.
- **Bring your own model service**: the app does not provide a model API, so AI features require your own API Key. DeepSeek, OpenAI, and any OpenAI-compatible endpoint are supported, and token usage and estimated cost are visible in real time.
