## 🌟 Why Rhiza?

**Unlike traditional project templates** (like cookiecutter or copier) that generate a one-time snapshot of configuration files, **Rhiza provides living templates** that evolve with your project. Classic templates help you start a project, but once generated, your configuration drifts away from the template as best practices change. Rhiza takes a different approach: it enables **continuous synchronization**, allowing you to selectively pull template updates into your project over time through automated workflows.
