# Iron Man Observer Next

2026 iThome Iron Man Challenge daily observation dashboard. Static site, GitHub Actions fetches data regularly, Cloudflare Pages free hosting.

🔗 Live site: https://ithome-ironman-observer.happyhacking.ninja/

The website has four pages:

- **[Race Observation Platform](https://ithome-ironman-observer.happyhacking.ninja/)** (homepage): One card for each competing series, showing progress and popularity
- **[Team Leaderboard](https://ithome-ironman-observer.happyhacking.ninja/teams/)**: Treat teams as participating units, with total views, per-capita views, average progress, today's articles, and lag warnings
- **[Hall of Fame](https://ithome-ironman-observer.happyhacking.ninja/hall-of-fame/)**: Lists publicly known competing authors (such as Gao Jian-Long), with one-sentence introductions, verifiable source links, and their series articles
- **[Insights Analysis](https://ithome-ironman-observer.happyhacking.ninja/insights/)**: Analysis of the entire competition, with charts of article posting times, popularity distribution, categories, title text, etc.
