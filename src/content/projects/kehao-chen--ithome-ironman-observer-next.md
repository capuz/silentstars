# Iron Man Observer Next

2026 iThome Iron Man daily observation dashboard. Static site, GitHub Actions fetches data on schedule, hosted free on Cloudflare Pages.

🔗 Online site: https://ithome-ironman-observer.happyhacking.ninja/

The site has four pages:

- **[Event Observation Board](https://ithome-ironman-observer.happyhacking.ninja/)** (Homepage): One card per competing series, showing progress and popularity
- **[Team Leaderboard](https://ithome-ironman-observer.happyhacking.ninja/teams/)**: Treats team formations as competing units, showing total views, per capita, average progress, today's posts and lag alerts
- **[Hall of Fame](https://ithome-ironman-observer.happyhacking.ninja/hall-of-fame/)**: Lists competing authors with public recognition (e.g., 高見龍), with one-line introduction, verifiable source links and their series articles
- **[Insights Analysis](https://ithome-ironman-observer.happyhacking.ninja/insights/)**: Analysis of the entire competition, with charts on post timing, popularity distribution, categories, title text and more
