# StarTrails AI

StarTrails AI is an open-source desktop app for stacking and finishing nightscape photographs. It offers three workflows:

| Mode | Use it for | Main capabilities |
| :---: | --- | --- |
| <img width="50%" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gkyle/startrails/HEAD/src/startrails/ui/icons/project_startrails.png" alt="Startrails"><br>**Startrails** | Long star trails that show the sky's motion | Detect and remove satellite and airplane streaks, create star trail stacks, and fill gaps in the trails. |
| <img width="50%" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gkyle/startrails/HEAD/src/startrails/ui/icons/project_denoise.png" alt="Denoise"><br>**Denoise** | A cleaner nightscape with point-like stars | Create tight sky masks with sky detection, refinement, and painting tools, align and stack the sky separately from the ground, and tune noise. |
| <img width="50%" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gkyle/startrails/HEAD/src/startrails/ui/icons/project_finish.png" alt="Finish"><br>**Finish** | Finish a single nightscape image | Separate stars from the sky, denoise and sharpen the starless layer, then recombine and export. |
