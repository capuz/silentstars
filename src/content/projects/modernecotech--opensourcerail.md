# OpenSourceRail

OpenSourceRail develops affordable urban rail and open tools to **plan, design, build, operate and renew it**, keeping design knowledge, manufacture and repair capability in-country.

The **v0.6 reference** uses comfort cars on passive steel-wheel bogies, conventional rails and precast concrete. Station propulsion alternatives remain under comparison; no installed architecture or powered length is selected. **This is a research and prototype design, without manufacturing or operating release.**

**Start here:** [comprehensive illustrated ebook](https://github.com/modernecotech/OpenSourceRail/blob/HEAD/OpenSourceRail-Book.pdf) · [published v0.6.1 software](https://github.com/modernecotech/OpenSourceRail/blob/HEAD/docs/releases/v0.6.1/README.md) · [v0.7 execution and open findings](https://github.com/modernecotech/OpenSourceRail/blob/HEAD/docs/design-engine/v07-execution/README.md) · [city designs and maps](https://github.com/modernecotech/OpenSourceRail/blob/HEAD/cities/catalogue/README.md) · [engineering Workbench](https://github.com/modernecotech/OpenSourceRail/blob/HEAD/docs/workbench/current/index.html).
