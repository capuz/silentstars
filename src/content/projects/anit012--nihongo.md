# nihongo

## Prohibited uses

Do not use this for fraud, phishing, impersonation, or sentences intended to deceive others. It is also prohibited under the terms of use of each AI service, such as Claude and ChatGPT.

nihongo values not deceiving readers. It does not write reasons, promises, or numbers that were not requested, and it does not misrepresent the sender.

## What is nihongo

### nihongo — A Japanese guard for AI editing

It eliminates “good work, but can't be sent as is.” It takes on the finishing and checking of AI edits in one go.

Japanese written by AI may look clean at first glance, yet the honorific level may be one step too high, promises nobody asked for may creep in, and dates, times, or “必ず” (“certainly”) may get dropped during revision. nihongo v3.0 is a mechanism that **on the first day reduces common mismatches that nearly everyone tends to run into, and as you keep using it, learns your own writing habits and expressions to avoid one line at a time, reducing the amount of rework.** It divides the finishing and checking work among the following three parts.
