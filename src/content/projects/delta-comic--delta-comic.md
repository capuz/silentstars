## Why Choose Delta Comic

- **Multiple sources, one app**: Presents content from several sites in a unified view through plugins, so you don't have to switch between sites.
- **User first**: Centered on the interface and reading experience, with support for common reading habits such as theme switching and night mode.
- **Friendly UI**: Imitates the mobile interface of **BiliBili**, which has been validated in the market, so it's more comfortable to use.
- **Open source and extensible**: The plugin model lets more sites be integrated quickly (transparent to ordinary users).
