## Why Choose Delta Comic

- **All-in-one, multiple sources**: Presents content from multiple sites in one unified view through plugins, so you don't need to switch between sites.
- **User first**: Centered on the interface and reading experience, supporting theme switching, night mode, and other common reading habits.
- **Friendly UI**: Mimics the mobile interface of **BiliBili**; proven by the market, more comfortable to use.
- **Open source and extensible**: The plugin model lets more sites be integrated quickly (transparent to ordinary users).
