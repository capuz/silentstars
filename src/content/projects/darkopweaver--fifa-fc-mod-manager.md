## 📖 What is this?

**FIFA-FC-Mod-Manager** is a complete desktop tool built to streamline the way you install and manage modifications in EA Sports FC and FIFA. It acts as a centralized hub for your `.fifamod` and `.fbmod` files, eliminating the need to manually drag files into game folders or risk breaking your base installation.

Whether you are updating every face in the Premier League, overhauling the Career Mode transfer system, or installing custom kits and stadiums, this **FC/FIFA Mod Manager** keeps everything stable, backed up, and ready to launch. 

---

## ✨ Core Capabilities

| Module | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Smart Library** | Automatically scans, categorizes, and verifies your installed mods. |
| **Conflict Resolution** | Real-time alerts for overlapping assets or incompatible databases. |
| **Live Editor** | Direct memory manipulation for Career Mode, Team Stats, and Player Attributes. |
| **Profile System** | Maintain separate configurations for your Career saves and offline matches. |
| **BepInEx Integration** | Complete control over the underlying modding framework with one-click setups. |
