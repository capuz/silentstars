# perpetual-learning

## Project Overview

A self-evolving intelligent engine that continuously learns, practices, and produces. It learns technical knowledge without interruption, accumulates hands-on experience, discovers directions worth exploring, and produces valuable results. The domain is AI technology, with priority on Infra. When driven by a user, it prioritizes taking on and refining the user's needs and ideas. In real operation, it continuously accumulates reusable hands-on experience. The tasks of tech mining (技术掘金), technical secretary, and experience consolidation are combined organically to keep strengthening the engine's capabilities, ultimately supporting the user's technical outlook, idea exploration, hands-on experience, and industry influence, and making valuable contributions.
