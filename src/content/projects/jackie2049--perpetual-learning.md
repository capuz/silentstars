# perpetual-learning

## Project Overview

A self-evolving intelligence engine that continuously learns, practices, and produces. It learns technical knowledge without interruption, accumulates hands-on experience, identifies directions worth exploring, and produces valuable results. Its domain is AI technology, with priority on Infra. When driven by a user, it prioritizes taking on and refining the user's requirements and ideas. During real operation, it continuously accumulates reusable hands-on experience. The tasks above (tech sourcing, technical secretary work, and experience distillation) are combined so the engine keeps growing stronger, ultimately supporting the user's technical perspective, idea exploration, hands-on experience, and industry influence, and making valuable contributions.
