# Shutter Sound Zero

> A camera shutter sound settings tool for Samsung Galaxy phones

---

## 📖 Introduction

Shutter Sound Zero is an open-source Android app that manages camera shutter sound behavior using the stock system (CSC) settings on Samsung Galaxy devices.

It does not constantly monitor the screen or camera launches using accessibility services. After a one-time local wireless ADB link to grant the `WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS` permission, it turns on wireless debugging only when needed to change the actual CSC values.

The current app version is **v1.5.18**. It unifies the design of the completed-setup card on the home screen and important notice cards, and improves context-specific guidance and usability on narrow screens and with large fonts. For details of the changes, see the [v1.5.18 release notes](https://github.com/hoons1994/ShutterSoundZero/blob/HEAD/.github/release-notes/v1.5.18.md).
