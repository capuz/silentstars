# KumaROOT

**ROOT Introduction for Bears ／ ROOT for Bearginner**

This is a document summarizing how to use tools used in high-energy physics, such as ROOT.
It was originally compiled on the [ShotakahaDokuWiki](https://www-he.scphys.kyoto-u.ac.jp/member/shotakaha/dokuwiki/doku.php) (no longer accessible) that I set up in my former lab.
It is now published and updated at [KumaROOT - Read the Docs](https://kumaroot.readthedocs.io/ja/latest/).

## "KumaROOT": Origin of the Project Name?

When I joined the lab and started using ROOT, the first thing my senior gave me was "ROOT that even monkeys can use" (nicknamed "SaruROOT").
With that title in mind, I chose "Kuma" (bear). I hope to make "KumaROOT" the one people read after "SaruROOT."

The intended readers are students and researchers who have used ROOT a little.
Rather than comprehensive explanations of packages and classes, which are left to the official documentation, I organize the content by purpose ("I want to do XX"), aiming for something usable as a "reverse lookup dictionary."
