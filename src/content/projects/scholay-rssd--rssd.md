# RSSD

Starting from RSS, web pages, and your own materials, complete research, writing, and translation, then publish to your own content site.

RSSD connects a personal writing device, a content hub, and independent sites. It suits continuous reading and creation, and it also suits running content across multiple domains. This repository is the project's unified entry point.
