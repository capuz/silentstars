# cyberdeck-zero-apps

Apps for the [CyberdeckZero Launcher](https://github.com/OSRdesign/cyberdeck-zero-launcher) (a touch launcher for
Raspberry Pi cyberdecks), installable from the deck's **Settings > Apps** menu.

An independent hobby project, not affiliated with M5Stack (see the
[launcher's disclaimer](https://github.com/OSRdesign/cyberdeck-zero-launcher#readme)).

**Writing an app? Read the [app guidelines](https://github.com/OSRdesign/cyberdeck-zero-apps/blob/HEAD/docs/APP-GUIDELINES.md) first** (screen sizes, input, packaging, testing, checklist). List rows must show what can be tapped: see
[row kinds](https://github.com/OSRdesign/cyberdeck-zero-apps/blob/HEAD/docs/APP-GUIDELINES.md#row-kinds-what-can-be-tapped). What the launcher gives an app (the status strip,
the state files, requests such as a beep or a toast, the launcher versions and the pin):
[section 3](https://github.com/OSRdesign/cyberdeck-zero-apps/blob/HEAD/docs/APP-GUIDELINES.md#3-the-launcher-contract) of the guidelines; the normative text is the launcher's
[APP-CONTRACT.md](https://github.com/OSRdesign/cyberdeck-zero-launcher/blob/main/docs/APP-CONTRACT.md).
