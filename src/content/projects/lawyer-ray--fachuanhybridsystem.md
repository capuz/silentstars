## Why Create This System

Law Chuan AI Copilot originated from a rough system built with Django in 2022—just a database with a few forms, placeholder replacement to generate Word documents, and a separate script for case filing, completely disconnected from the system. After three years of rough management, I always wanted to refactor it, but lacked motivation.

Later, I participated in a competition organized by the judicial bureau and quickly coded a project, which the judges sent to the finals. The finals required serious preparation, leading to the creation of Law Chuan AI Copilot—revisiting what I wanted to do before.
