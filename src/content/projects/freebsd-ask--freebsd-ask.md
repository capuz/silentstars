# FreeBSD from Beginner to Running Away

🎉 Welcome to the world of BSD!

FreeBSD continues the unfinished business of the Computer Systems Research Group (CSRG) and is a truly free (Liberty) **operating system** that steadfastly adheres to the BSD UNIX philosophy in this ever-changing world—**upholding ancient rules and pursuing true freedom**.

## Content Summary

This book is an open-source research work about the FreeBSD operating system.

[~~The FreeBSD project is about to be archived (Archived)~~, to protect our beloved operating system... what we can do is to write a book!](https://www.bilibili.com/bangumi/media/md3068) (paraphrasing the classic slogan from Love Live! School Idol Project[Z]. Japan: SUNRISE, 2013-01-06.)

"Getting started" is a joy, and "running away" is also a kind of openness. "I set out with enthusiasm and return when the excitement is over, why bother with FreeBSD!"
