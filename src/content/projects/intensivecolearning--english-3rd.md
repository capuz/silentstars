# English Brutal Co-Learning

## Introduction

In the fast-developing new Web3 world, full of infinite possibilities, English is becoming an indispensable core skill for every learner. Because there are few purely English-speaking environments, most domestic learners' English ability often stays at the level of reading and writing. To address this pain point, LXDAO launched "English Brutal Co-Learning" to create an open, positive, purely English learning and communication environment for learners who want to improve their English and use it well. Through planned, deliberate practice, it aims to comprehensively improve reading and writing, and especially listening and speaking.

## Keywords

English

## Target Audience

There is no looking at foundations, no asking about starting points, and no threshold at all, only partners breaking through together. If you have the heart to learn English, the motivation to improve your listening and speaking, and the courage to challenge yourself, you are welcome to join.
