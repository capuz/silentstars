# WoTBTools

A replay toolkit for World of Tanks Blitz (《坦克世界闪击战》（World of Tanks Blitz）): parses `.wotbreplay` battle data, and provides Excel export, an online damage leaderboard, battle statistics, multi-damage-rate and League Rating, AI tactical review (personal / team), and Keycloak authentication.

Entry: [https://wotbtools.com](https://wotbtools.com) · Repository: [https://github.com/A158Coke/WotbTools](https://github.com/A158Coke/WotbTools)

## What it does

On Android, after opening a replay from a file manager, it is parsed automatically on the device (no login required; replays are not uploaded). If reading fails, you can retry. See [Android replay handoff](https://github.com/A158Coke/WotbTools/blob/HEAD/docs/android/replay-intent.md).

The replay workbench permanently displays data, 2D replay, 3D replay, shot analysis, and AI review. Data and 2D are available anonymously. Your own replays require login for 3D, shot analysis / armor reproduction, and AI. Regular users and administrators have the same capabilities.
