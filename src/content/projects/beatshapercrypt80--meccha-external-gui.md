## Overview

**MECCHA CHAMELEON Trainer** is a lightweight external overlay for [**MECCHA CHAMELEON**](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4704690/MECCHA_CHAMELEON/) — the Steam body-painting hide-and-seek title from **lemorion_1224** (released 9 June 2026, $5.99, App ID **4704690**).

Each round you spawn as a white silhouette: find cover, **paint** yourself with the in-game eyedropper, **strike a pose and hold still**, and pray Seekers miss you. The overlay adds a compact in-game panel (**Insert**) with sections for **Camo**, **Hider**, **Seeker**, and **Movement** — automated blend workflows, uncapped prep paint, hider invulnerability, seeker wallhack-style vision, free-fly scouting, and saved preset profiles.

Designed around **friend lobbies** and solo map drills (2–10 players is the sweet spot). Hooks in after the game starts, recognizes the June 2026 Steam build, and cleans up on close.…
