# 水仙.skill

### If there were another you in the world, how would they catch this sentence?

Write your tone, your favoritisms, and your pauses into a mirror companion skill that can be calibrated slowly.

[Try it now](#一分钟试玩) · [Real trial chats](https://github.com/Cyh29hao/shuixian-skill/blob/HEAD/docs/try-it.md) · [English](https://github.com/Cyh29hao/shuixian-skill/blob/HEAD/README_EN.md) · [First-time use](https://github.com/Cyh29hao/shuixian-skill/blob/HEAD/docs/quickstart.md) · [MIT](https://github.com/Cyh29hao/shuixian-skill/blob/HEAD/LICENSE)

**The way you already speak can have a new landing point.** First try a public preset that doesn't require private chat, then use a few representative materials to gradually adjust it from "good vibe" to "this sentence sounds a bit like me."

Public presets are only for trying out the vibe. Your own 水仙 starts from the materials you are willing to provide, and whether it fits is calibrated gradually through actual chats.

## Choose a way to get closer first

- **Sweet lover** · intimate, lightly teasing, slowly drawing closer. Preset `sweet-gender-flipped`.
- **Late-night shared mind** · quiet, leaving space, figuring things out…
