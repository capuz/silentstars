# lc-backend

## Service

1. A store owner (`owner`) registers a campaign (`campaign`). An administrator (`admin`) creates first-come, first-served coupon flyers from templates generated by an LLM, then pays for them to charge points.
2. Ad coupon feeds are served (`adserving`) to members (`member`). When a member views a coupon, points are charged (`billing`) based on that impression.
3. When a first-come, first-served coupon event starts, members receive a coupon (`coupon`). The store owner (`store`) scans the dynamic QR code in the member's coupon wallet to process coupon use.
