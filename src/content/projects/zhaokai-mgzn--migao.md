# 观星台（MIGAO）— 布艺行业 AI 经营平台

> A multi-tenant AI operations platform for soft furnishings / curtain merchants: on the consumer side, **米宝** provides 24/7 intelligent customer service and order placement; on the business side, **黄金策** covers products, orders, production, quality, warehousing, finance, and supply chain.
> The business loop = quoting and material calculation → order → production (cutting / sewing / finishing) → warehousing → shipping → after-sales and reconciliation.
>
> Intelligent customer service is part of the 'customer service' capability domain and no longer represents the system's entire positioning
> (for the history of the positioning, see issue #6463 / for the brand rename, see issue #6525).
