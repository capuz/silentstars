## Overview

BECE Grade Checker is a web-based education calculator for students, parents, teachers, advisers, and others interpreting Ghanaian BECE results. It calculates an aggregate using grades from the four core subjects together with the two strongest additional subjects.

Ghanaian Language and French are supported as part of the subject entry process. Once the grades are supplied, the calculator returns programme suggestions without delay. Results can also be displayed in a print-friendly format for saving or sharing on paper.
