> Direct a team of coding agents by voice or chat, on your own machine. Linear for agents, drawn as a pixel farm.

**VibeValley** (Coder Farm Story) lets one person direct a team of coding agents with a single sentence. You tell the **assistant** what to do, and it breaks the request into tickets, picks **helpers**, dispatches them, and reports back to you. Helpers are real coding agents on your machine (pi, Claude Code, Codex, ...). They open branches in your project, change code, run tests, and commit. You review the changes, and only then merge them into the main branch. It is an AI version of Linear that runs on your local machine, drawn as a pixel farm: crops grow along with real progress ([concepts](https://github.com/vibevalleyhq/vibevalley/blob/HEAD/docs/concepts.md)).
