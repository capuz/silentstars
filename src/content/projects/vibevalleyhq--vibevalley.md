> A team of coding agents you direct by voice or chat, drawn as a pixel farm. Runs on your own machine.

**VibeValley** (码农场物语) is a team of coding agents you command by voice or typing, drawn as a pixel farm.

You only talk to the **butler** (管家). The butler assigns jobs to **hands** (帮工), which are real coding agents on your machine (pi, Claude Code, Codex……). They open branches in your project, change code, run tests, and commit. The crops in the field grow according to their real progress: they sprout when code is read, grow leaves when files are changed, and yield wheat when the work is done. You review the **harvest list** (收成清单) before merging a branch into the main branch.

You, the butler, and the hands talk in the same group chat, the **field edge** (田头): each job has a ticket thread attached below it, where the hands confirm, ask questions, and report, and you can jump in anytime. In short, VibeValley is an AI version of Linear that runs on your machine: tickets are assigned to coding agents that work in the project turn by turn, and you review the changes before merging. The farm is the skin on the outside…
