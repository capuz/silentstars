> A team of coding agents you direct by voice or chat, drawn as a pixel farm. Runs on your own machine.

**VibeValley** (Code Farm Story) is a team of coding agents you command by voice or typing, drawn as a pixel farm.

You only talk to the **steward**. The steward assigns jobs to **farmhands**, which are real coding agents on your machine (pi, Claude Code, Codex, ...). They open branches in your project, change code, run tests, and commit. The crops in the field grow with their real progress: they sprout while code is being read, grow leaves when files are edited, and bear wheat when the work is done. You review the **harvest list** before merging a branch into the main branch.

You, the steward, and the farmhands talk in the same group chat, the **field edge**: each job has a ticket thread hanging under it, where farmhands confirm, ask questions, and report, and you can jump in at any time. In short, VibeValley is an AI version of Linear that runs on your machine: tickets are assigned to coding agents, which work through the project round by round, and you review the changes before merging. The farm is the skin on the outside…
