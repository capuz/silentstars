A refined, smooth third-party Android client that brings you the full experience of the <a href="https://pawchive.pw">Pawchive</a> platform.<br/>
  Aggregates creator content from platforms such as Patreon, Fanbox, and Discord, with support for browsing, searching, favorites, and immersive media playback.

---

## Highlights

### Content Browsing
- **Home feed**: Paginated loading of the latest content, with keyword filtering; optional "show only one post per author" and "hide posts from favorited creators"
- **Creator profile**: Paginated browsing of posts / announcements / linked accounts / similar creators (swipe left or right to switch), tag filtering, and a follower card overview
- **Post details**: Full body text (whitelist HTML rendering), comments, revision history, attachment downloads
- **Multi-platform aggregation**: Patreon / Fanbox / Discord and other sources, with platform tags color-coded by brand

### Precise Search
- **Keyword search**: Searches posts and creators simultaneously; switch between them with tabs
- **File hash reverse lookup**: Paste a file hash (MD5 / SHA-256) into the search box to trace a material's source; Discord results are supported
