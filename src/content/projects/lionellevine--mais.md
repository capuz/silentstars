# Math for AI Safety

This is the home of [*Math for AI Safety: An Invitation for Mathematicians*](https://github.com/lionellevine/MAIS/blob/HEAD/papers/P1/) by [Lionel Levine](https://lionellevine.github.io/) (Cornell University).

## What's here?

Beyond the [invitation paper](https://github.com/lionellevine/MAIS/blob/HEAD/papers/P1/), this repo hosts three kinds of pre-publication research:

| Kind | What it is |
|------|------------|
| [**Open Problems**](https://github.com/lionellevine/MAIS/blob/HEAD/open-problems/) | A short exposition of a single open problem |
| [**Agendas**](https://github.com/lionellevine/MAIS/blob/HEAD/agendas/) | A cluster of related open problems and partial progress on resolving them |
| [**Papers**](https://github.com/lionellevine/MAIS/blob/HEAD/papers/) | Papers in progress |

The intended life cycle is **open problem → agenda → paper → publication**: open problems get incorporated into agendas, and a maturing agenda spins off papers. Cited external work is summarized in [references/](https://github.com/lionellevine/MAIS/blob/HEAD/references/), one page per paper, suitable for stuffing an LLM's context.
