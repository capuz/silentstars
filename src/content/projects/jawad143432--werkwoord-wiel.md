# 🌷 Sterke Werkwoorden Atlas — Dutch Strong Verbs Trainer

An opinionated, offline-first companion for anyone who has ever stared at *schrijven*, *schuiven*, and *schijnen* and quietly wondered whether the Dutch language was invented to test human patience. This repository is a lovingly engineered training environment for the strong, irregular, and downright mischievous verbs of the Dutch language — the ones that refuse to follow the polite rules and instead demand to be memorized like old family recipes.

Where the original project offered a compact drill tool, the **Sterke Werkwoorden Atlas** reimagines the whole discipline as a cartography exercise. Every verb is a landmark. Every tense is a coastline. Every conjugation pattern is a river system that you slowly learn to navigate without a map — until, eventually, you *are* the map.
