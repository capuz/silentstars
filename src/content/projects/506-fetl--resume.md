## Role-specific customization and version history

The same project experience may need different emphasis when applying for a frontend role versus a full-stack role. GResume derives independent copies from the base resume according to the job description (JD), preserving source and change records, so each role version is unaffected by the others. You can compare before and after edits, and restore from version history when a change doesn't work out.

Two kinds of relationships are distinguished here: which resume a role version was derived from, and which changes were made to the same resume over time. The former is recorded as a parent-child relationship; the latter stores document snapshots. When restoring, the snapshot is written back to the current document and a new restore record is added, so subsequent edits remain traceable.

The job tracker links roles to resumes and keeps interview rounds and next steps together, making it easier to prepare for interviews and track progress. The tracker links to the resume being continuously edited; to preserve the content as it was when you applied, use a [fixed share snapshot](#分享与审阅明确对外版本) or an exported file.
