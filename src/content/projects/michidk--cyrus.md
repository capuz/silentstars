# cyrus

**A faithful rebuild of *The Elder Scrolls Adventures: Redguard* in Rust and Bevy**

Bring your own GOG install. cyrus ships no game data.

[Playing and controls](https://github.com/michidk/cyrus/blob/HEAD/docs/PLAYING.md) · [Setup](https://github.com/michidk/cyrus/blob/HEAD/docs/SETUP.md) · [Delivery issues](https://github.com/michidk/cyrus/issues/92) · [How cyrus is built](https://github.com/michidk/cyrus/blob/HEAD/docs/METHOD.md) · [Contributing](https://github.com/michidk/cyrus/blob/HEAD/CONTRIBUTING.md)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> cyrus is early in development. Startup presentation and world viewing work,
> but a complete gameplay and fidelity playthrough has not been accepted.
