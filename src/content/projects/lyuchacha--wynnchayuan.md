# WynnChaYuan

English: **[README.en.md](https://github.com/LyuChaCha/WynnChaYuan/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)**

A multi-language translation module for Wynncraft (Fabric 1.21.11). **Original text preserved**, with translations displayed alongside or directly replacing the original text.

[Download](https://github.com/LyuChaCha/WynnChaYuan/releases/latest) ·
[Changelog](https://github.com/LyuChaCha/WynnChaYuan/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md) ·
[Report Issues](https://github.com/LyuChaCha/WynnChaYuan/issues) ·
[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/lyuchacha)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Currently in Beta.** Some translations were manually entered and then converted through AI, a small portion was translated directly by AI, and most of the basic content has been proofread by humans. Other languages are currently mostly based on Traditional Chinese and then translated through AI, so there may be mistranslations and inconsistencies in terminology.
> If you encounter issues, find **LyuChaCha** on Discord, or report to [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/LyuChaCha/WynnChaYuan/issues).
