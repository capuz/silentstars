# news-daily

Automatically fetches daily headlines from Reuters, Bloomberg, and The Wall Street Journal.

## Data files

- `news.json` — structured JSON data
- `news.md` — Markdown summary

## Update frequency

Updated automatically every day at 8:00 and 20:00 Beijing time (GitHub Actions).
