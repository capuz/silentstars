**A Muslim's daily companion — for every prayer, and everything between.**

  Prayer times, the adhan and a qibla compass; the Madinah muṣḥaf in four riwāyāt and the IndoPak print, with recitation, tajweed colours on Ḥafṣ and Warsh, a word reader and tafsir; Tilāwah for listening surah into surah with the screen off; a hifz tracker that brings every portion round again on the schedule a hafiz actually keeps; duas, tasbih, and a fasting and prayer journal; home-screen widgets on three platforms; a Minimal mode that leaves the sky and the countdown alone on the page; and sync between your own devices with no account and no server. No ads, no analytics, no tracking — and every line of it open source.
