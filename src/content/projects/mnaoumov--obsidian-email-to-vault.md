# Email to Vault

Things worth keeping arrive by email — a receipt, a confirmation, a thread you want to think about — and getting them into [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) means copying, pasting and re-saving the attachments by hand. This plugin makes an email address the way into your vault: send or forward mail to it and each message becomes a note, with From, To, CC, Subject, Body and attachments preserved.

Use the built-in disposable mailbox (Mail.tm) for a one-click address that needs no account, or connect your own IMAP server — Gmail, Outlook, self-hosted — and keep everything on infrastructure you already trust. No backend and no paid service either way. Inspired by [`Save emails into Evernote`](https://github.com/mnaoumov/obsidian-email-to-vault/blob/HEAD/<https://help.evernote.com/hc/en-us/articles/209005347-Save-emails-into-Evernote>).
