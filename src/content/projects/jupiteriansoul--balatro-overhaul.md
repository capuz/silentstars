# Balatro Overhaul

**An overhaul of [Balatro](https://www.playbalatro.com/) with new modes, multiplayer, integrated mods support, cross platform and much more !**

[**Download**](https://github.com/JupiterianSoul/Balatro-Overhaul/releases/latest) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Install guide](#install) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Changelog](https://github.com/JupiterianSoul/Balatro-Overhaul/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Report a bug](https://github.com/JupiterianSoul/Balatro-Overhaul/issues) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/jupiteriansoull)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> You need to own Balatro. The downloads here don't contain the game, the launcher builds the mod from your own copy.
