# Qianlima Personal Edition

[中文] | [English](https://github.com/Brian-dai694/qianlima/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

A locally prioritized Amazon operations AI workbench.

> Current version: `v2.8.12`

Qianlima is not a chatbot shell, nor is it a keyword tool that only generates titles. It breaks down your operational tasks into reusable workflows, allowing the Agent to:

```text
Understand tasks -> Select relevant materials and Skills -> Calculate or execute -> Verify evidence -> Provide retrievable results
```

Codex, Claude Code, etc. are responsible for interaction and reasoning; Qianlima is responsible for task routing, business caliber, memory selection, permission boundaries, execution receipts, and result verification.

## First, look at the conclusion

- General Q&A and follow-up questions on the same topic take a quick path, without starting complex runtime.
- Learning, research, reports, and document analysis load relevant context on demand, without reading the entire historical database.
- Skills will first check capabilities and risks, then use them within a limited scope; they will not quietly expand file, network, or write permissions.
