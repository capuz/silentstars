> **Warning**
>
> **Unsigned program. On first run, add it to your antivirus allowlist:** the launcher has no code-signing certificate, and it uses system facilities to extract update packages. It also ships with `hpatchz.exe` and `VelaEndfield.exe` (fallback components for Endfield's legacy launch chain). Together these are exactly the traits that trigger antivirus false positives, so it may be blocked or quarantined. Add the **entire install directory** (especially the `resources` subdirectory) to your antivirus exclusions before running it.
