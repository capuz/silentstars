> **LucidProse · AutoProof (`v1.38.0`)** —— A pre-generation behavior navigation, pure-local 0-Token purification, batch refinement, and traceable proofreading engine built for [DSH Tavern](https://github.com/flizzywine/dsh-tavern), for long-form interactive fiction and roleplay.

---

## Positioning and Design Philosophy

In practice, long-form interactive fiction and tavern roleplay most often run into not simple typos, but models gradually drifting over many generation rounds into **mechanical contrast ("not A, but B"), inflated action beats, sandwich-dialogue templates, excessive physiological-reaction rendering, formulaic openings, and costly paragraph-by-paragraph repetitive polishing**.

**澄文 · AutoProof** adheres to four core principles: **"minimal intervention, preserving the character's soul, pure-local first, and reviewable and reversible after edits"**. Without altering existing events, causality, or character relationships, it uses a four-stage adaptive pipeline to pull text back to authentic, clean, and breathing human narrative.
