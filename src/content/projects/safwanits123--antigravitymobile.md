# 🚀 AntigravityMobile - Access Your AI Conversations Anywhere

## 📥 Download

## 🌟 Overview
AntigravityMobile is a feature-rich mobile dashboard for Antigravity IDE. It allows you to stream live AI conversations, monitor model quotas, and browse or edit workspace files from any device on your local network. Enjoy a distraction-free Lite Mode and secure your access with optional PIN security.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 📱 System Requirements
- **OS:** Android 5.0 or later, iOS 12.0 or later
- **Network:** Local network access for smooth operation
- **Storage:** At least 100 MB of free space
- **Permissions:** Basic storage and network access

### 🔍 Key Features
- **Live Streaming:** Engage in real-time conversations with AI models.
- **Quota Monitoring:** Check your usage statistics at any time.
- **File Access:** Browse and edit your workspace files easily.
- **Lite Mode:** Focus on your AI tasks without distractions.
- **PIN Security:** Keep your mobile dashboard safe with a personal PIN.
