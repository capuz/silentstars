## This Is Not a Knowledge Repository

Knowledge is becoming infinite, instant, and increasingly cheap. Organizing one more set of concepts or restating one more consensus has limited value.

What is truly scarce is what remains after knowledge passes through a specific person, a real experience, and actual work: what was done, why it was done that way, where it failed, and how judgment changed as a result.

> AI can rewrite a passage of text, but it cannot experience a failure for me, make a choice for me, or pay the price of a long-term judgment for me.
>
> This repository stores exactly those parts that cannot be ghostwritten.

[cubxxw.com](https://cubxxw.com/zh/) is my public writing workshop. There are no “learn a new concept every day” tasks here, and no chasing answers that merely look correct. Articles only settle three kinds of things: practices I have personally verified, reusable workflows, and reflections that hold up when looked back on.
