**KoiPage** is a local Galgame / visual novel manager for Windows. It scans game directories, matches work information, and organizes your collection with covers and tags. Open the home page to continue your last game, record every play session, or randomly pick your next title. Region-switched launch, window scaling, game translation, and archive extraction can all be found under Utilities.

## Download and Installation

Go to **[Releases to download the latest version](https://github.com/morilove37/KoiPage/releases/latest)** and choose the installer ending in **`_x64-setup.exe`**. If you need the MSI format, choose **`_x64_zh-CN.msi`**.

- Requires Windows x64 and Microsoft WebView2. Regular users do not need to install development tools.
- The installer includes the region switching, scaling, translation, and extraction tools. They are extracted on first use and take up extra disk space.
- Installed users can check for updates under **Settings → About**, then download and install after confirmation.
- `Source code` is the source code. `latest.json` and `.sig` are used by the update system and do not need to be downloaded manually.
