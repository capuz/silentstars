# Chimera Project

> 🎨 **Project emblem.** A heraldic chimera tears through a ring of chains, and the lightning from its lion's head brings down the tower of the all-seeing eye branded “ТСПУ”. It has as many heads as needed: each protocol holds its own path to a free internet. In the corners of the medallion: a lighthouse with free birds, “Mare Liberum”, the free sea as the project's goal; a gate that opens to three knocks, port knocking; a tunnel beneath a grate, tunneling transports through blockades; a mask passing by the eye, REALITY masking. File: [`assets/chimera_poster.png`](https://github.com/inferno1978/Chimera-Project/blob/HEAD/assets/chimera_poster.png).

**Multi-Protocol Anti-DPI Installer**, a multi-protocol installer for bypassing censorship: VLESS REALITY/xHTTP, Hysteria2, AmneziaWG, TrustTunnel, MTProto, NaiveProxy, Mieru, FPTN, Slipgate, and others. Full automation from installation to monitoring, with clustering, load balancing, a web panel, and a REST API.
