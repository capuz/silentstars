Raise a little pig that grows up, goes to school, works, travels, and chats with you. It's a single-player raising game in the style of QQ pets, with a Nook Phone interface from Animal Crossing. The same codebase comes in two forms: the DSH plugin, which lives inside the DeepSeek Harness web page, and a standalone desktop version.

You can do everything with the mouse. When you're busy, the pig uses items from your backpack to take care of itself. The game does not register model tools or inject pig status into conversations, and caring for the pig uses no model tokens.

## Highlights

- **Raising**: up to level 60, 33 classes, 15 travel locations, 12 personalities, and 1008 lines of dialogue.
- **Garden**: 29 crops across 18 plots, growing in real time. Water, weed, control pests, fertilize, and harvest across multiple seasons. You can hire helpers and schedule patrols.
- **Mini-games**: a 20-floor mine, blind boxes and gacha, fishing with 40 fish, and a Pomodoro timer.
- **Multiple desktop pigs**: up to 4 pet windows sharing the same owner's coins, backpack, and garden.
