**Open-source manuscript of *Recommender Systems: Industrial Architecture and Core Algorithms* + AI-native learning workbench**

Using the published chapter PDFs as the authoritative knowledge source, a local-first AI agent connects the manuscript to cited retrieval, guided courses, review and assessment, and runnable recommender-system practice. The knowledge map helps readers locate content; the agent helps them learn it and put it to use.

> Intended for graduate students with a background in machine learning or recommendation, recommendation and algorithm engineers, architects, and technical researchers. Readers without a foundation should first study machine learning and deep learning basics. The manuscript and main learning materials are primarily in Chinese.
