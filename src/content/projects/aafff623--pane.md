## <a id="intro"></a>Is Pane right for you?

Every AI tool keeps its quota in its own console: Claude has a 5-hour session, Codex tracks a weekly quota, relay sites track balances per key, and CLI spending is scattered across local logs. To find out "how much is left, when it resets, which account is about to hit its limit, and how much I spent today," you have to open five web pages and dig through three local files.

**Pane brings them together in one desktop panel that you can summon anytime with `Alt + 2`:**

- **See everything in one place**: 5-hour / 7-day / monthly quotas, balances, reset countdowns, and account status. Press `Shift` to switch views;
