# Class of 2027 Job Information Board · Job Radar

This is a personal job information board for **2027 graduate job hunting**, not just a collection of scrapers. The goal is to consolidate opportunities scattered across official websites, Guopin (国聘), central and state-owned enterprise announcements, university career sites, Nowcoder (牛客), WeChat official accounts, and job-hunting groups into one workbench that can be filtered, reviewed, acted on, and pushed.

Current focus areas:

- Product, strategy product, AI product, data product
- Data analysis, business analysis, data science, data mining, machine learning/algorithms
- Roles closer to decision-making: business operations analysis, strategic analysis, industry research, digital transformation, management trainee/project management
- Non-internet opportunities to look at separately: central and state-owned enterprises, finance, FMCG/e-commerce, consulting, Hunan/Changsha, manufacturing digitalization
