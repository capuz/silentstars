# Independent replay of Alpöge’s RH release

Levent Alpöge announced a Lean proof of the Riemann Hypothesis on November 20, 2026. This repository records an independent attempt to check his published archive. Credit for the claimed proof belongs to Alpöge and his collaborators. [Original announcement](https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf)

## Current status

**Full replay in progress.** No full RH verification receipt has been produced by this project. The [two-worker pilot](https://github.com/rohanarun/alpoge-rh-replay/blob/HEAD/evidence/pilot-receipt.json) transferred 150 previously unbuilt modules; the primary reused them and kernel-checked one downstream module.

Current cloud progress is recorded in the [worker status report](https://github.com/rohanarun/alpoge-rh-replay/blob/HEAD/STATUS.md). See the [live progress dashboard](https://rohanarun.github.io/alpoge-rh-replay/) for the module ledger breakdown.
