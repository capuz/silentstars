# QvQChat

Let AI chat like a real person. Driven by the [ErisPulse](https://github.com/ErisPulse/ErisPulse) framework, with a fully functional Dashboard for management.

## Why Choose QvQChat?

Ordinary AI chatbots only provide mechanical responses. QvQChat allows AI to **participate in group chats like a real person**:

- **Can eavesdrop** — Silently observes in group chats, only speaking up when mentioned with @, called by name, or when the topic is relevant, without interrupting.
- **Has emotions** — Senses the tone of others, gets excited when happy, and offers gentle comfort when sad.
- **Knows the time** — Speaks differently at various times of the day: groggy in the morning, energetic in the afternoon, and relaxed in the evening.
- **Typing delay** — Varies response length and typing speed, avoiding instant replies that reveal its robotic identity.
- **Can remember** — Automatically remembers things said by group members and brings them up naturally in future conversations.
- **Multiple messages** — Sends messages in several parts like a real person, rather than dumping a large block of text.

All these behaviors are **customizable** — you can freely enable/disable them,…
