# Sky Daily Task Title

**Task 1: Catch the floating light particles in the gliding race in 霞谷 0/1**

Completion method:

Approach and collect the three light particles to complete.

 **Location: 霞谷 Gliding Track**

  
 **Task 2: Collect 30 candlelight 0/30**

Completion method:

1. Claim the task

2. Approach the candlelight above the lit candles on the map / the candlelight after purifying dark plants, and collect 30 drops to complete.

****

 **Task 3: Sit on a bench with a stranger 0/1**

Completion method:

Sit on a stone bench together with a stranger player to complete.

 **Note: Except for 晨岛 and 遇境, stone benches are in front of the hub of every other map.**

 **Task 4: Wave to a friend 0/1**

Completion method:

Click the action bar at the top center of the screen, find the wave emote, face a friend, and click…
