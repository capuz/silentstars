# HuddleFM

A self hosted music bot for Slack huddles, controlled from Slack.

## Features

- Search YouTube for songs, albums and playlists, or paste links from YouTube, SoundCloud and more
- A shared queue to reorder, shuffle and loop
- Playback, volume and permissions, all from buttons in Slack
- Album art, progress and synced lyrics on the bot's video tile
- Autoplay from YouTube or a Huddle mix of what people listen to, and 💖 to hear more like a song
- Video mode: watch YouTube or Jellyfin together on a screen share, with captions and chapters
- Live streams from YouTube and Twitch
- Scrobbling to Last.fm and ListenBrainz
- Auto-ducking while people talk
- Multiple huddles at once, recaps and a stats canvas
- One Docker container to self host

## Client plugins

Plugins for [Taut](https://github.com/ingoau/huddlefm/blob/HEAD/clients/taut/) and [Slick](https://github.com/ingoau/huddlefm/blob/HEAD/clients/slick/) put the controls, queue and settings in the huddle's own toolbar. Download them from [the website](https://ingoau.github.io/huddlefm/#plugins).
