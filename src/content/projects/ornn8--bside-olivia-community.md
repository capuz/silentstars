# BSide Olivia Community

**Keep the letters, replies, and life continuity with 林离 on your computer.**

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/Ornn8/bside-olivia-community/releases/latest)** · [Quick Start](#快速开始) · [Changelog](#更新记录) · [Documentation index](https://github.com/Ornn8/bside-olivia-community/blob/HEAD/client/docs/README.md) · [Report an issue](https://github.com/Ornn8/bside-olivia-community/issues)

BSide Olivia Community is an **unofficial** companion recreation project for Windows. It connects a local backend inside an isolated copy of the original client that you legitimately obtained. The experience of writing letters, waiting, and receiving replies stays unchanged. It adds long-term memory, 林离's daily world, QQ chat, voice, photos, and songs.

> [!NOTE]
> **Letters and memories stay on your machine; generation happens in the cloud.** Letters, long-term memory, world records, and received media are stored on your computer. Replies, semantic judgments, and voice, photos, songs, and videos are generated by Olivia's cloud. You only need an Olivia account Key. No dedicated GPU is required.
