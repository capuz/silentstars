# Thunder Pelican · 雷霆鹈鹕

> A pelican with lightning in its eye rides a bicycle through the storm coast.

`Canvas 2D` · `No dependencies` · `No build step` · `GitHub Pages ready`



[Release notes for this update](https://github.com/imnotorangewong/Thunder-Pelican/blob/HEAD/更新说明.md)

## Project Overview

The main game is in `index.html`: the classic pelican, bicycle, coast, lightning, and UI are all drawn with Canvas. There are also 12 original rider skins and 13 rider forms, with transparent WebP illustrations loaded on demand. The Day & Night Twin Dragons is a skin that can switch between dark and light forms. Each character's themed emblem should stay on the character itself, keeping the shape and proportions of the reference image. It should be clearly recognizable but not enlarged to overshadow the main subject, and must not be stretched or distorted.
