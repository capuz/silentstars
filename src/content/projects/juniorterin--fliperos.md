Ubuntu 24.04 for arcade cabinets with a 15 kHz tube, set up from the cabinet itself.

Ubuntu 24.04 (amd64) for arcade cabinets with a CRT monitor, in the style of GroovyArcade: a **15 kHz kernel**, Switchres, emulators and frontends ready to play, and a setup menu (`fliperos-setup`) made for the tube's screen. A PC alternative to GroovyArcade, Batocera, Recalbox, ReplayOS and RGB-Pi for 15 kHz arcade CRTs.

In development: 0.27 is tested in QEMU and on a 15 kHz CRT cabinet. Full documentation in the **[wiki](https://github.com/juniorterin/fliperOS/wiki)**. Website: **[fliperos.juniorter.in](https://fliperos.juniorter.in)** (source in [`website/`](https://github.com/juniorterin/fliperOS/blob/HEAD/website)). The **[Telegram group](https://t.me/fliperos15khz)** has questions, photos of cabinets and help from other users; each release is posted there with pictures and how the new features work.
