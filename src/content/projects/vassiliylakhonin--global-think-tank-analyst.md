# Global Think Tank Analyst

> **STRATEGIC-RISK REASONING CONTRACT FOR AI AGENTS** — a domain reasoning contract that runs inside AI agents and produces structured policy-risk, sanctions, regulatory, geopolitical and trade memos with decision framing, evidence boundaries, scenarios and confidence. Open-source. No live data. No legal, compliance or investment advice.

> **Horizontal reasoning method in the Agenda Intelligence portfolio.** Use directly via paste/attach into Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, or another agent. When sources are supplied, export the memo's externally checkable claims through the [evidence-packet handoff](https://github.com/vassiliylakhonin/global-think-tank-analyst/blob/HEAD/docs/evidence-packet-handoff.md) and run Agenda Intelligence MD before human review.

**Strategic-risk analysis skill for AI agents.**

A reusable domain skill for agents that produce policy-risk, sanctions, regulatory, geopolitical, trade, and strategic-risk memos.

LLMs are good at summarizing geopolitical events. They are weak at turning them into decision-ready intelligence: framing the decision, separating facts from assessments, stating uncertainty, reasoning through actor incentives,…
