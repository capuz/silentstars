# UBAA

**Smart BUAA Remake: An all-in-one campus assistant for BUAA students**

Timetables, assignments, grades, course selection, reservations, schedules, and cloud storage, all in one app.

[**Download and Usage Guide**](https://github.com/BUAASubnet/UBAA/blob/HEAD/docs/README.md#用户) · [Feature Overview](#功能一览) · [Issue Feedback](https://github.com/BUAASubnet/UBAA/issues)

---

## Why Choose UBAA

- 📚 **One entry point**: No need to switch between a dozen campus systems. Check timetables, see assignment deadlines before submitting, grab Boya (博雅) courses, and book seats, all in the same app.
- 📱 **All platforms**: Works on phones, tablets, and computers, supporting Android, iOS, HarmonyOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux.
- 🌐 **Works on and off campus**: Automatically chooses between a direct connection and the school's WebVPN, and recovers automatically when the network changes.
- 📴 **Available offline**: Opens showing your last loaded content first, so you can still view timetables and grades without a network connection.
- 🔒 **Campus data is handled directly on your device**: Campus requests are not relayed through UBAA servers.
