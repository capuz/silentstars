## Video Introduction

The video “Maybe the Least Tool-Like Tool I've Used Lately” shows how 无线麦 turns a Bluetooth voice remote into a new way to jot down thoughts, control your computer, and work with AI. The product interface shown in the video reflects the version on display at the time.

Video author: [可乐不甜的跑焦日记](https://space.bilibili.com/327214328)

无线麦 SayAll Windows edition lets your Xiaomi Bluetooth remote speak for you and help with tasks on your PC: hold the remote's voice button to talk, and the text appears directly in the input box you're using. Remote buttons can be set to common keyboard shortcuts or used to open an application.

Two models are supported, both with real-device adaptation completed on Windows:

- Xiaomi Bluetooth Remote 2 (RC001)
- Xiaomi Bluetooth Remote 2 Pro (RC003)
