# RideTube

[English](https://github.com/JustAnotherPU/ridetube/blob/HEAD/README.md) · [简体中文](https://github.com/JustAnotherPU/ridetube/blob/HEAD/README.zh-CN.md)

RideTube shows in English, French, Dutch, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese or Korean
(Language setting). Chinese translation by 小白151
(JackieZ123430). The [Chinese guide](https://github.com/JustAnotherPU/ridetube/blob/HEAD/README.zh-CN.md) includes setup, playback settings, and font coverage.

> **Experimental and unofficial.** RideTube is an unofficial client for YouTube. It is not affiliated with,
> endorsed by or sponsored by Google or YouTube. Use it at your own risk. You are responsible for following
> YouTube's Terms of Service and the laws of your country, and for using it safely: by default the picture
> shows only while the car is parked.
