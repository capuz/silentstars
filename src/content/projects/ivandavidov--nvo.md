# НВО и ДЗИ — графично сравнение на училищата

Website for visual comparison of schools in Bulgaria based on results from the National External Assessment (НВО) and the State Matriculation Exams (ДЗИ).

**https://ivandavidov.github.io/nvo**

## What the site offers

- **Home page**: search and cards for the four exams with the latest results and trend
- **Comparison charts**: interactive charts for БЕЛ and МАТ (or the second matriculation exam) for selected schools, with the number of students who took the exam
- **Rankings**: schools ordered by average performance over the last 3 years, with the number of students who took the exam
- **Rankings by city and year**: separate pages for [every city](https://ivandavidov.github.io/nvo/7/sofia/) and [every year](https://ivandavidov.github.io/nvo/7/2025/)
- **Scores by parallel classes after grade 7**: minimum and maximum scores by parallel class and placement for [~130 cities](https://ivandavidov.github.io/nvo/7/balove/)
- **Top schools**: quickly add the top 3, 5, or 10 state and private schools from each city to the chart
- **School page**: [profile by code](https://ivandavidov.github.io/nvo/school/2216306/) with results for all…
