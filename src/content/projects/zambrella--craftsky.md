# CraftSky

A crafting-focused social platform built on the [AT Protocol](https://atproto.com), where makers share projects, techniques, and works in progress while keeping ownership of their identity and public records.

CraftSky is being developed for private beta. The Flutter client and Go backend are implemented, and production infrastructure is deployed. Full public launch still has [readiness checks and approvals](https://github.com/Zambrella/craftsky/blob/HEAD/docs/operations/releases.md#private-beta-and-full-public-launch) to complete.
