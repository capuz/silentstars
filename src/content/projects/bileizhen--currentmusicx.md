# 流声音乐（CurrentMusicX）

**Simplified Chinese** · [English](https://github.com/bileizhen/CurrentMusicX/blob/HEAD/README-en.md)

**Let covers, lyrics, and music flow together**

NetEase Cloud Music / QQ Music dual platform · Navidrome / Emby self-hosted libraries · Listen together

Native Android client built with Kotlin / Jetpack Compose / Miuix

**[Download App](https://github.com/bileizhen/CurrentMusicX/releases/latest)** · [Get Started](#开始使用) · [Changelog](https://github.com/bileizhen/CurrentMusicX/releases) · [Report Issues](https://github.com/bileizhen/CurrentMusicX/issues)

流声音乐 (CurrentMusicX) is the native Android client for [CurrentMusic](https://github.com/backrooms-yrc/CurrentMusic). The app's Chinese display name is 流声音乐, while its English name remains CurrentMusic. Circular covers, dynamic color extraction, word-by-word lyrics, and a floating player connect the music library, discovery, and playback smoothly, and the app adapts to portrait, landscape, and widescreen layouts.
