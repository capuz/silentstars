A business and finance management system for IDC / cloud service providers. It covers the complete business loop of product management, order billing, automatic provisioning, invoicing, top-ups, distribution commissions, tickets, notifications, and more. It provides three front ends (an admin backend, a website portal, and a user console) and connects to upstream suppliers and third-party services through an extensible plugin mechanism.

## ✨ Core Capabilities

| Area | Capabilities |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🛒 Products & billing | Product groups, product configuration options, multiple billing cycles (monthly/quarterly/annual), auto-renewal, inventory management |
| 📦 Orders & provisioning | Order placement, payment callbacks, automatic provisioning (integrated with upstream suppliers), failure retries, automatic suspension/release |
| 💰 Finance | Balance accounts, top-ups, invoices, refunds, reconciliation, transaction records and details |
| 👤 User system | Registration and login, real-name verification (optional), membership levels, distribution and commissions, promotion withdrawals |
