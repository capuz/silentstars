# NukeApp-OSS

NukeApp-OSS is an Android Hook module for **Douyin** (`com.ss.android.ugc.aweme`). Once installed, a new entry "NukeApp-OSS" appears in Douyin's settings page. Opening it shows more than a hundred feature toggles, and changes take effect after Douyin restarts.

## What it can do

The settings page lists features by category. Only "Disable double-tap to like" is enabled by default. Everything else is off by default, so turn on whatever you need.

| Section | Categories |
| --- | --- |
| Video | Recommendation feed, playback control, video quality and cache, video page, gestures and actions, danmaku, ads |
| Social | Comments, messages, chat features, sharing and reposting, personal profile, privacy |
| App | Layout and display, usage time, app and system, other |
