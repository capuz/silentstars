# NukeApp-OSS

NukeApp-OSS is an Android hook module for **Douyin** (`com.ss.android.ugc.aweme`). After installation, a line called 'NukeApp-OSS' appears in Douyin's settings page. Tapping it opens more than a hundred feature toggles. Changes take effect after Douyin restarts.

## What it can do

The settings page lists features by category. Only 'Disable double-tap to like' is enabled by default. Everything else is off by default, so turn on whatever you want.

| Section | Categories |
| --- | --- |
| Video | Recommendation feed, playback controls, video quality and cache, video page, gestures and actions, danmaku, ads |
| Social | Comments, messages, chat features, sharing and reposting, personal profile, privacy |
| App | Layout and display, usage time, apps and system, other |

A few examples: not reporting read status (can apply only to conversations on a blacklist or whitelist), custom Huoxing streak days (visible only to you), automatic streak continuation, automatic claiming of Huoxing gems (claimed once a day on a schedule, can be limited to selected friends), automatic red packet grabbing, chat message repeat (a '+1' next to the bubble), and removing the repost count…
