# 🧠 VocTrainer — The Vocabulary Coach That Thinks With You

Welcome to **VocTrainer**, a coaching-oriented vocabulary companion that treats every learner like an athlete in training — not a passive reader flipping through flashcards. Words are weights; repetition is cardio; context is technique. VocTrainer is the personal trainer standing beside you, adjusting the load, correcting your form, and celebrating the personal bests.

If you have ever felt that memorizing vocabulary is a lonely uphill sprint with no finish line, VocTrainer reframes the whole discipline as a coached practice: short, focused sessions; adaptive difficulty; and measurable progress you can feel in real conversations.
