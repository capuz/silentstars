Evalathan grades a product across its life cycle. Evalathan is one evaluation harness; [Hydra](https://github.com/pedramgoshATgmail/EVALATHAN/blob/HEAD/GLOSSARY.md#user-content-hydra) is the conductor - she orchestrates sixteen evaluators ('evalers'); each evaluator reports against a life-cycle gate.

**v0.3** was released on Nov 1 2026 and is a local runner. Hydra still conducts sixteen software evalers while [Dehydrus](https://github.com/pedramgoshATgmail/EVALATHAN/blob/HEAD/GLOSSARY.md#user-content-dehydrus) is introduced. Whereas Hydra routes and conducts 16 non-physical AI evalers, Dehydrus manages 8 evals specialized in [physical world models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_model_(artificial_intelligence)) interactions with LLMs. The 8 evalers are [LuDaTs](https://github.com/pedramgoshATgmail/EVALATHAN/blob/HEAD/GLOSSARY.md#user-content-ludats) (Limited-universe Data Test sets) evalers, frameworked under [PRETRUWs](https://github.com/pedramgoshATgmail/EVALATHAN/blob/HEAD/GLOSSARY.md#user-content-pretruws) paradigm.
