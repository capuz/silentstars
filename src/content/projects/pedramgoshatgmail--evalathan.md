Evalathan grades a product across its life cycle. It is one [evaluation harness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_harness) - a local tool that runs many separate checks and writes each result beside the others, instead of hiding everything behind one opaque number.

[Hydra](https://github.com/pedramgoshATgmail/EVALATHAN/blob/HEAD/GLOSSARY.md#user-content-hydra) is the [conductor](https://opensource.microsoft.com/blog/2026/05/14/conductor-deterministic-orchestration-for-multi-agent-ai-workflows/) for the software desk: like the person at the front of an orchestra who cues each player, she calls sixteen specialized checkers. Evalathan calls those checkers [evalers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evaluation) (short for evaluators). Each evaler reports against its own life-cycle gate.
