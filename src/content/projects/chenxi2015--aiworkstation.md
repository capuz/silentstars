## 📖 Project Positioning

**AI Workstation** is a **local-first (Local-First)** personal knowledge Agent workbench.

When browsing daily on platforms like Twitter (X), WeChat Official Accounts, Little Red Book, GitHub, and tech blogs, high-quality content is often scattered in various places and difficult to consolidate and reuse. Most bookmark management tools on the market (Raindrop / Pocket / Notion Web Clipper) remain in a passive storage mode of "human-driven → system-responsive".
