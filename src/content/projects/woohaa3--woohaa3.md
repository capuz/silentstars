WooHaa3 is a canvas-based multimedia creation workbench. Place text, images, audio, video, and 3D assets in the same workspace, and complete AI generation, editing, and version tracking around those assets.

## What you can do

- **Organize multimedia assets**: Put text, images, audio, video, and 3D assets on the same canvas, linking reference assets with creative results.
- **Generate content with AI**: Choose a model, enter a prompt, and combine existing assets to generate new works.
- **Keep editing and iterating**: Edit images, extract frames from videos or add annotations, and use the results in the next round of creation.
- **Create 3D scenes and shots**: Organize models, adjust cameras, and use framed views as reference for image or video generation.

## How it differs from other canvases

- **The 3D director's stage is built directly into the canvas**

  White-model previsualization is no longer a director's stage fully separated from the canvas. You can organize 3D scenes, shots, and assets in parallel on one canvas, and use framed views directly as references for image or video generation.
