# Web3 Internship Program - 2026 Winter

## Introduction

"Web3 Internship Program" is a growth-oriented entry-level program for university students and Web3 newcomers, co-initiated by ETHPanda and LXDAO. It aims to give newcomers who want to enter the blockchain industry systematic learning, hands-on practice, job placement connections, and community support, helping them get past information barriers, open practical pathways, and take the first step into Web3.

Learning panel: <https://web3career.build/programs/web3-internship-bootcamp-2025-winter#tab=learning>

## Keywords

Web3, Internship
## Target Audience

- Current students (technology, product, design, operations, and finance majors)
- Newcomers moving from Web2 to Web3 / newcomers to the Chinese-language Web3 community
- New members of the Chinese-language Web3 community
- Recruiters (to quickly identify quality interns)
