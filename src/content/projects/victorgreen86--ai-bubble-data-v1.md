## Overview

This project dissects the economic realities behind the current artificial intelligence boom, investigating whether the enormous capital funneled into AI represents genuine long-term value or speculative hype. Through methodical data analysis and investigative journalism, it exposes how America's trillion-dollar AI commitment may be built on shaky premises and under-scrutinized assumptions.

Tailored for economists, investors, policy researchers, and anyone fascinated by the nexus of technology and finance, AI Bubble offers transparent evidence and clear visual storytelling. Instead of taking market exuberance at face value, this inquiry probes the numbers, the fanfare, and the widening gap between perception and reality in the AI landscape.
