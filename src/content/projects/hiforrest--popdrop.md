# PopDrop

**Access recent files from multiple folders without switching the current window**

Appears when needed, and goes away when done. Suitable for Windows 10 / 11.

[Download the latest version](https://github.com/hiforrest/PopDrop/releases/latest) · [User Guide](https://github.com/hiforrest/PopDrop/blob/HEAD/USAGE.md) · [Changelog](https://github.com/hiforrest/PopDrop/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md)

Current version: **v0.9.0**

---

## Why PopDrop?

Imagine you are chatting, writing a document, or working on a project, and you temporarily need to send a recently modified file.

Files are not hard to find. The trouble is, you usually have to leave the current window, switch to File Explorer or file management software, find the file, and then switch back. Just to grab one file, your window and attention keep switching back and forth.

> **Window switching can easily disrupt your working memory.**

What PopDrop aims to solve is this small interruption:

1. Press the shortcut key to bring up the file panel above the current window;
2. Find the file and drag it directly to WeChat, Feishu, email, or other software;
3. After dragging, continue with your work, and the panel will…
