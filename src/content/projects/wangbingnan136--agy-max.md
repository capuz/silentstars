> **Design positioning and open-source origin notice**: `Antigravity Max` is independently maintained for heavy developers who want a 'full-power right out of the box' experience. Its underlying debugging attachment and telemetry foundation are derived from [WhiteBr1ck/agy-powertools](https://github.com/WhiteBr1ck/agy-powertools) (MIT). The optical theme and workbench interaction design draw on [RMvdesi/obsidian-prism](https://github.com/RMvdesi/obsidian-prism), and [INK-Q/agy-fast](https://github.com/INK-Q/agy-fast) (MIT, can be turned off with one click on the settings page) is integrated by default. Full attribution is given in [Open-source sources and acknowledgments](#开源来源与致谢) below.
