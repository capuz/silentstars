# 🚴 KICKR Legacy Motion Bridge (KLMB) — Reviving the Ride You Already Own

---

## 🧭 Overview

There is a quiet tragedy in the world of indoor cycling: a perfectly healthy smart trainer, mechanically sound, electronically capable, suddenly rendered "outdated" because a firmware branch stopped shipping. The **KICKR Legacy Motion Bridge (KLMB)** exists to argue with that fate — and win.

KLMB is an independent, community-minded software layer that re-imagines what a legacy Wahoo KICKR smart trainer can do when paired with modern cycling software. Instead of asking you to purchase new hardware, KLMB focuses on translating, bridging, and interpreting the signals your trainer *already sends* into the virtual shifting experience modern riders now expect.

Think of it as a translator standing between two people who speak related but incompatible dialects: your trainer speaks "resistance-ohms-and-watts," your riding app speaks "virtual-gear-and-cadence," and KLMB makes sure the conversation flows without awkward pauses mid-sprint.
