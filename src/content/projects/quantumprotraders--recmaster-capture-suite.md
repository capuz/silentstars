## 🧭 Overview

Welcome to **RecMaster-2026**, a screen recording companion for Windows 11 and Windows 10 that treats your desktop like a stage and your cursor like a performer worth capturing. Where ordinary recorders simply press "record," RecMaster-2026 thinks about *what* you are recording, *why* you are recording it, and *how* the final result should feel when you replay it three weeks later.

This repository is a documentation-first project. Rather than asking you to compile anything from source, it provides a clear, human-readable path to obtain the recorder, set it up on your machine, and get from "first launch" to "finished recording" in minutes. Think of it as a lighthouse: it does not build the ship, but it makes sure the ship finds the harbor.
