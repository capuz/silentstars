## What is a loop model?

> A shared learned layer, block, module, or operator is reused, either along depth within one forward pass or along time, by feeding a state it computed back through the same layers for the next token or step.

That covers looped, recurrent-depth, and weight-tied Transformers, adaptive halting over a reused block, deep-equilibrium and implicit layers, shared neural algorithmic processors, and models that fold depth into time by feeding high-level states back through the same layers…
