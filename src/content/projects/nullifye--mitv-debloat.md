# Mi TV debloat + Apple TV style launcher

A fast, minimal home screen for **Android TV and Google TV** (Android 8.0 or newer), with prayer times, a live system card, Now Playing, a running-apps screen and a phone remote. It was built on a Xiaomi Mi TV, which was also [debloated](https://github.com/nullifye/MiTV-Debloat/blob/HEAD/docs/xiaomi-debloat.md).

**Install it on your TV:** [Windows](https://github.com/nullifye/MiTV-Debloat/blob/HEAD/docs/install-windows.md) · [macOS](https://github.com/nullifye/MiTV-Debloat/blob/HEAD/docs/install-macos.md) · [Linux](https://github.com/nullifye/MiTV-Debloat/blob/HEAD/docs/install-linux.md) · [with an AI agent](https://github.com/nullifye/MiTV-Debloat/blob/HEAD/docs/install-with-ai-agent.md)
