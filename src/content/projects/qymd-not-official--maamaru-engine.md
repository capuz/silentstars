# まあ丸 `🦊` — Honmaru manager for 《刀剑乱舞 ONLINE》

**Simplified Chinese** · [English](https://github.com/qymd-NOT-official/maamaru-engine/blob/HEAD/README_EN.md)

I’ve been playing Touken Ranbu for nine years and still want to play, but I no longer want to tap through every round of repetitive actions myself, nor keep hand-calculating resources in Excel. So I made まあ丸: it looks after the honmaru for me when needed, and when I come back, I can flip through the record of what happened that day.

> **You decide what the honmaru should do today. Execution, supervision, wrap-up, and recording are handed to まあ丸.**

On the Chinese server, it can run tasks for you, or just be used for bookkeeping. On the Japanese server, it can connect to the game browser and update honmaru records as you play, and it can also repeatedly farm maps you have already cleared under “JP server sortie.”
