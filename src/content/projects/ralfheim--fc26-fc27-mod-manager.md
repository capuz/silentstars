## 📖 What is this?

**FC26 FC27 Mod Manager** is a comprehensive desktop application built specifically to handle modifications for the latest generation of EA Sports FC titles. It acts as a centralized hub for your `.fifamod` and `.fbmod` files, eliminating the need to manually drag files into game folders or risk breaking your base installation.

Whether you are updating every face in the Premier League, overhauling the Career Mode transfer system, or installing custom kits and stadiums in EA FC 27 or FC 26, this **FC26 FC27 Mod Manager** keeps everything stable, backed up, and ready to launch.
