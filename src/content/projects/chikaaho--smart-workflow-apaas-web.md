# Smart-WorkFlow-aPaaS-Web

Smart-WorkFlow-aPaaS-Web is the frontend interface for **CH-aPaaS** (Vue 3 / TypeScript / Element Plus). CH-aPaaS is a low-code platform for building your own business applications: model your business as forms and processes, and you can initiate, approve, and follow them up from the workbench.

Companion backend: [Smart-WorkFlow-aPaaS-server](https://github.com/Chikaaho/Smart-WorkFlow-aPaaS-server) (currently 1.1.0; this frontend is 1.1.1)

## Features

- **Workbench**: To-dos, items I initiated, and items CC'd to me are gathered in one place; frequently used items can be arranged as you like.
- **Form design**: Drag-and-drop form design, supporting attachments, images, default values, and display linkage; once designed, a form can be published for data entry.
