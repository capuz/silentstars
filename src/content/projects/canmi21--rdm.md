# Refined Download Manager

A refined GUI download manager for macOS, Linux & Windows; written in Rust and drawn with GPUI.

**Work in progress.** The window, the categories and the transfer engine are here; much of what
a finished download manager promises is not yet. Expect things to move.

## License

RDM is drawn with [GPUI](https://gpui.rs), the UI
framework of the [Zed](https://zed.dev) editor, under the Apache License 2.0; the one-line text
field began as GPUI's own example. The icons are [Lucide](https://lucide.dev), under the ISC
