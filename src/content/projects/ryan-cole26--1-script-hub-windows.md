## Overview

1 was built for anyone who needs a simple way to manage recurring file organization and data transformation jobs without writing scripts or installing heavy automation platforms. Whether you're archiving documents, prepping spreadsheets for analysis, or sorting downloaded files into tidy folders, this tool gives you a clean interface to define and run those tasks quickly.

What distinguishes 1 is its emphasis on low resource usage combined with practical capabilities such as live logging and native support for Excel and CSV formats. You get the efficiency of batch processing without the bloat of enterprise-grade software, making it a good fit for daily use on older or less powerful machines.
