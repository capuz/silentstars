# 🎵 EchoMind (回声) - AI Sound Effects Manager

[English](https://github.com/Bastzber/EchoMind/blob/HEAD/README.en.md) · [Changelog](https://github.com/Bastzber/EchoMind/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md) · [Releases](https://github.com/Bastzber/EchoMind/releases) · [GPL-3.0-or-later](https://github.com/Bastzber/EchoMind/blob/HEAD/LICENSE)

> ## Find it, or make it
>
> Describe the sound you want in one sentence: **if it's in the library, find it in 0.3 seconds; if it's not, generate one on the spot.**
>
> A local-first, AI-driven desktop sound effects manager.

The current source version is **v0.3.0-beta.16 (pre-release)**.

Beta V16 · [Releases](https://github.com/Bastzber/EchoMind/releases)
