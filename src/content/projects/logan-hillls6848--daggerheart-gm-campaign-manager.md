## Overview

Daggerheart Campaign Manager gives game masters a single web-based place to maintain their Daggerheart campaigns. It brings campaign organization, setting development, and routine session planning together, reducing the need to manage scattered notes while preparing and running games.

The tool is especially useful for GMs overseeing multiple campaigns or building a detailed central reference for locations, storylines, and other setting material. Its workflow is designed around practical campaign administration, so information can be arranged clearly and found again as play progresses.
