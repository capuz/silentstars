# V2EX Playground

Chinese name: V2EX 游乐场

An extension for browsing [v2ex.com](https://www.v2ex.com) in editors such as VS Code, TRAE, and Cursor.

Without leaving the editor, you can browse topics and nodes, view replies, search content, take part in discussions, and use common features such as daily check-in, favorites, reminders, and account information. It suits people who want to focus on reading V2EX, and also those who need a brief moment of slacking off (mō yú) when work gets exhausting.
