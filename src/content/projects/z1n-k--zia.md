# Zia

**Zen, made better.**

A [Sine](https://github.com/CosmoCreeper/Sine) mod that reshapes Zen Browser from the frame in. 

[Install](#install) · [Highlights](#highlights) · [Settings](#settings) · [Good to know](#good-to-know) · [Changelog](https://github.com/z1n-k/zia/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md)

## Install

1. Install [Sine](https://github.com/CosmoCreeper/Sine).
2. In Zen, open **Settings → Sine Mods**, and in Sine's settings turn on **installing JS from unofficial sources**.
3. Paste `z1n-k/zia` into the box under the marketplace, then restart Zen.

Zia works with all three of Zen's layouts: **Sidebar and Top Toolbar**, **Only Sidebar** and **Collapsed Sidebar**. If Zia doesn't load, open `about:support` and click **Clear startup cache**.

> [!TIP]
> Space and folder icons carry much of the look. [Watch how to set them up](https://vimeo.com/1228144298).

## Highlights

### One card, in the site's colour

The page and its toolbar are one rounded card. The toolbar takes the colour of the site you're on.

- The colour follows the page as you scroll, and each site's is remembered.
- Prefer another colour for a site? Right-click the toolbar, choose **Toolbar Color for This Site…**…
