电子爱 (ElecKoi) is a free, open-source Agent roleplay and character card authoring app powered by [DeepSeek Harness (DSH)](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness), available for Windows PC and Android. You can chat with AI characters and experience stories, and you can also create and edit your own character cards, letting AI help refine your character settings.

[Author SDK Documentation](https://eleckoi.github.io/ElecKoi/) covers native scripting, tutorials for developing character frontends, API references, and importable examples.

## Core Highlights

**Create characters the way you vibe code.** Just describe your idea. The once-tedious card-writing work can be handed off to Agents and tools, step by step.
