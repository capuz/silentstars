Vetro runs on your Samsung TV and gives you a dashboard, open from any computer or phone on your network. Root access enables the launcher, privacy switches and remapper. It has been reported working on selected TVs running **Tizen 6 through 10**, depending on the model and firmware. The dashboard, app list and diagnostics can work without root on supported models. See [Compatibility](#compatibility).

> **Important**
>
> **A Samsung firmware update can remove root access.** Vetro's **Block firmware and software updates** switch (Quick settings → General → Essential settings) requires root, is off by default and reapplies the block after a restart. Vetro 0.7.1 extends the block to Samsung's separate update mode. The original block was tested on a Q80A. An update reached a QE65S85F with blocking enabled before the 0.7.1 changes, which still need testing on a TV.
