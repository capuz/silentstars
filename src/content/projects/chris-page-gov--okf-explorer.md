# OKF Explorer

Use knowledge you can inspect with your AI. Open Knowledge Format (OKF) keeps
source-linked notes, records and relationships in a reusable bundle. Explorer
helps you browse that material and check what supports an answer.

**New to OKF Explorer? [Read the beginner guide (HTML, with chapter contents)](https://chris-page-gov.github.io/okf-explorer/docs/beginners/index.html).**

[Open the website](https://chris-page-gov.github.io/okf-explorer/) for examples,
learning guides and a route into Explorer, or use the
[documentation map](https://github.com/chris-page-gov/okf-explorer/blob/HEAD/docs/index.md) to find a specific task.

## Start here

[Try an existing bundle with AI](https://github.com/chris-page-gov/okf-explorer/blob/HEAD/docs/onboarding/try-a-bundle.md), then inspect
its evidence and limits. You can use a human browser route if your AI cannot
access page tools, links or files. Explorer does not itself run an AI model.
