# 🌟 Stella

**A QQ group chat bot that chats in a human-like way, driven by a memory system**

*— Designed for an 8K context window, compatible with both local small models and online APIs*

Chinese | [English](https://github.com/Enoa-devteam/Stella_project/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

---

Stella doesn't just throw messages at a large model and return a single reply. It **distills scattered information from group chats into memories that are retrievable, verifiable, and forgettable**, and proactively asks about you at the right moments.
