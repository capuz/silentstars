# Private Sprint

> A 25-day sprint to ship a real privacy application on Starknet mainnet.

**August 14 - September 7, 2026.** $5,000 USD paid in STRK:

- 🥇 **$2,500** for first
- 🥈 **$1,500** for second
- 🥉 **$1,000** for third

You build in your own public repository. Your progress appears on the hub at [strk20.starknet.io/hackathon](https://strk20.starknet.io/hackathon) throughout the sprint.

## How to apply

1. Fork this repository and add one object to [`registry.json`](https://github.com/starkience/strk20-hackathon/blob/HEAD/registry.json).
2. Open a pull request saying what you're building.
3. It merges on its own once the check passes, and your project appears on the hub within the half hour.

Registration stays open for the whole sprint. Merging puts your project on the hub.

This is the only pull request you open. Everything else - your pushes, your stack, your contracts,…
