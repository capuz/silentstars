## 🎮 What is This?

**FC26-Mod-Manager-2026** is a complete mod management solution for **EA Sports FC 26**. It supports .fifamod and .fbmod files, providing drag & drop import, load order control, conflict detection, and BepInEx integration. Perfect for enhancing graphics, kits, faces, stadiums, gameplay, and career mode.
