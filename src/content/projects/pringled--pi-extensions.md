# pi-extensions

A set of extensions that make [Pi](https://pi.dev) nicer to use. They're minimal by design: most are under 300 lines with no dependencies, and each one can be installed on its own to fit your workflow. All of them are part of my daily setup.

## Look and feel

| Package | What it does | npm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`pi-polish`](https://github.com/Pringled/pi-extensions/blob/HEAD/packages/pi-polish) | Claude Code-style tool rows and diffs. | [](https://www.npmjs.com/package/pi-polish) |
| [`pi-context-bar`](https://github.com/Pringled/pi-extensions/blob/HEAD/packages/pi-context-bar) | A one-line footer with the model, branch, context bar and cost. | [](https://www.npmjs.com/package/pi-context-bar) |
