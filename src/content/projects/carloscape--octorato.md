# Octorato

> *an octorato (n.): your AI's second brain. One brain, many sealed worlds (a client, a project, a topic, a course), with a receipt on every action. The same wall that seals a world is the one that can bill it.*
>
> <sub>the Octopus Brain Framework</sub>

> **Your AI agent forgets who you are and mixes your worlds. Octorato is the open-source brain layer that fixes that: one file-native "brain" (230+ skills and 160+ specialist agents in plain markdown under git) that one operator runs across many isolated "arms": a client, a project, a topic, a course, anything you keep separate.**
