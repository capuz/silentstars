## What is this

Good overseas podcasts often run two or three hours, and listening to English is tiring, with no Chinese subtitles. SubCast turns them into Chinese content you can “listen side by side, skim around, and ask directly”:

- Subscribe to YouTube channels, Apple Podcasts, Xiaoyuzhou, or any RSS feed, and new episodes are downloaded automatically.
- If official subtitles exist, they are used; otherwise speech is recognized locally, then translated into Chinese and aligned sentence by sentence.
- To quickly grasp what an episode covers, read the AI overview or ask it directly; clicking a timestamp in an answer jumps back to the original words.
