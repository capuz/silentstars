# YOLO Data Agent｜Enterprise Intelligent Data Q&A and Analytics Platform

YOLO Data Agent is for teams that need to unify business definitions, control data permissions, and retain analytical evidence. Users can ask questions in natural language, follow up, break down problems, and analyze directly. Administrators configure business subjects, metrics, datasets, models, Skills, and permission boundaries.

The platform does not treat “the large model generated a piece of SQL” as a reliable answer. The model is responsible for understanding questions, proposing plans, and explaining results. YOLO confirms context, compiles the query contract, enforces permissions, generates read-only queries, locks results, and saves evidence.

## Design Goals

- **Make business questions executable**: convert natural language into explicit metrics, dimensions, time, filters, sorting, and analysis goals.
- **Make query definitions governable**: business terms, metric definitions, field roles, enumerated value domains, and default rules are configured by the platform rather than guessed by the model on the fly.…
