# VoiceTyper

**Simplified Chinese** | [English](https://github.com/oyasmi/voice-typer/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

**Hold the hotkey and speak; release, and the text appears at the cursor.** Recognition runs entirely on your machine, and recordings never leave your computer.

VoiceTyper quietly lives in the menu bar or system tray. Reply to messages, write documents, or jot down ideas as they come up, without opening a web page, uploading audio, or copying and pasting between the transcript and the current window. It doesn't replace your input method, and it works anywhere you can type text.
