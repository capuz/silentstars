> 一念修行 · 云深不知处
  A text-based xianxia idle game with a Chinese-inspired visual design.

## Game Features

**Four Realms, Twenty-One Stages** — Four realms: the Mortal Realm (Qi Refining → Tribulation Crossing), the Immortal Realm (True Immortal → Great Luo), the Divine Realm (Divine Person → Divine Emperor), and the Chaos Sea (Chaos Spirit → Chaos Ancestor). Each stage has nine levels up to completion. Breakthroughs require surviving a tribulation. The five tribulation types (Thunder, Reversal, Soul Rending, Iron Body, Pressure) each have counter-methods.

**Spirit Roots Decide Fate** — 11 spirit root types (Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, Earth, Wind, Thunder, Ice, Light, Dark, and Chaos) determine cultivation speed, tribulation reduction, and technique affinity. 33 talents are graded in four tiers: Mortal, Spirit, Heavenly, and Dao Endowment.

**Techniques and Enlightenment** — 63 techniques are divided into main, auxiliary, and secret arts, and can be equipped simultaneously. Mastering a technique unlocks an enlightenment branch. 141 branches let the same technique develop into different schools.
