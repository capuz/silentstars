# Henji AI · Henji-AI

**Use all kinds of AI in one piece of software, bringing image, video, and audio creation together in a single desktop workspace.**

Henji AI is built for creators who repeatedly generate, edit, and organize materials. After connecting your own model provider accounts, you can generate content directly, or connect prompts, reference materials, and generated results in a node canvas to continue with follow-up creation.
