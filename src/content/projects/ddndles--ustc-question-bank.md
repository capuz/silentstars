A **structured, programmable question bank foundation** for undergraduate courses at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC). Each question includes a Markdown + LaTeX stem, answer, step-by-step solution, knowledge-point tree tags, and credibility grading. It can directly power quiz websites, LaTeX exam paper assembly, Anki card generation, RAG retrieval, and evaluation benchmarks.
