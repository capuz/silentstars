# 缤越助手 (GeelyToolbox)

A **in-car multi-function utility toolbox** customized and tuned for real vehicles for the **Geely Binyue COOL** (亿咖通 E02 / IHU516 chip, Android 9 head unit system).

As long as the head unit supports enabling wireless debugging (WiFi ADB), it can be installed and used directly over the air. It runs quietly in the background of the head unit and solves everyday driving pain points such as the factory head unit not allowing free software installation, steering wheel buttons not being customizable, pop-ups seizing the screen when exiting reverse or panoramic view, and no Bluetooth sound after the built-in multimedia is disabled.
