# AstralBeam

Website: https://astralbeam.ai · Docs: https://astralbeam.ai/docs

AstralBeam adds a Cursor-style AI agent to your app in minutes. It answers queries, interacts with your app, renders your UI components, and works with users' files. Self-host it or use [AstralBeam Cloud](https://astralbeam.ai).

Here's a [Linear clone](https://github.com/AstralBeamAI/astralbeam/blob/HEAD/examples/linearity-react) with the AstralBeam agent embedded:

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6bb3d981-b82d-40a2-9be1-d1bb6cfe5759

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/AstralBeamAI/astralbeam/blob/HEAD/CONTRIBUTING.md) for the codebase structure and local development.
