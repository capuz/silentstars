## Overview

Big Hosting delivers an online, browser-accessible environment for hosting bots. Rather than relying on a traditional desktop application, users access the service through the web to work with their hosted bots.

The platform is designed for users seeking a web-based bot hosting option, including those comparing hosting services or bringing their bot management tasks together in one online workflow. A compatible browser provides the entry point to the service.

- Interface available in a browser

- No desktop-focused interface is needed
