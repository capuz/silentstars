# Globulation 2

Globulation 2 is a real-time strategy game in which autonomous units carry out
your priorities. This repository contains the game engine, native and browser
clients, content tools, and the online multiplayer platform.

## Play

Visit [glob2.online](https://glob2.online/) for downloads, gameplay guides,
and community information. Play in your browser or use the online app at
[app.glob2.online](https://app.glob2.online/).

## Build and contribute

Start with [Contributing](https://github.com/Globulation2/glob2/blob/HEAD/CONTRIBUTING.md), then follow the
[development guides](https://github.com/Globulation2/glob2/blob/HEAD/docs/development/README.md) for your platform. Routine builds
use `python3 tools/dev_build.py`; prerequisites and platform options are in the
build guide. [Testing](https://github.com/Globulation2/glob2/blob/HEAD/test/README.md) explains how to choose and run checks.

Explore the [documentation library](https://github.com/Globulation2/glob2/blob/HEAD/docs/README.md) for architecture, gameplay
features, AI, maps, assets, scripting, multiplayer, hosting, and releases.
