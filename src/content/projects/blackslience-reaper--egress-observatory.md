# Egress · IP Purity Check

Open the page to detect your machine's outbound IP right away, and get a quality score that shows where every point was deducted.

**v2.0 · 2026-11-05**

[**Open website**](https://blackslience-reaper.github.io/egress-observatory/) · [Scoring rules](https://github.com/BlackSlience-Reaper/egress-observatory/blob/HEAD/SCORING.md) · [Data sources](https://github.com/BlackSlience-Reaper/egress-observatory/blob/HEAD/DATA-SOURCES.md) · [Browser extension](https://github.com/BlackSlience-Reaper/egress-observatory/blob/HEAD/browser-helper/README.md) · [Contributing](https://github.com/BlackSlience-Reaper/egress-observatory/blob/HEAD/CONTRIBUTING.md)

Egress combines more than 20 IP databases, 16 DNS blocklists, 17 threat feeds, proxy and datacenter lists, same-/24 neighbors, and the reputation of the entire ASN. It then runs live tests using your current browser and network: WebRTC, DNS, TCP fingerprinting, IPv6, Cloudflare Turnstile, and Google reCAPTCHA.

- **Multi-source cross-checking**: the same underlying database counts for only one vote, to avoid duplicate voting.
- **Explainable results**: every deduction keeps its source and reason.
