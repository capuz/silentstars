- This is a life guide for HFUT students, originally written mainly for the **Xuancheng campus**, and we are continuously improving content related to the **Hefei campus**. [Join us!](#贡献--参与方式)
- We hope to summarize campus study, life, administrative procedures, and common questions into a reliable entry point, in a more systematic and easily searchable way.
- Most pages on the site include rigorous citations of sources. It is completely ad-free and made purely out of passion, so feel free to use it with confidence.
