# 🧱 Live in Feixuan · Survive-HFUT

Survival Manual for Hefei University of Technology · A lifestyle guide more suitable for students of Hefei University of Technology

## Introduction

This is a lifestyle guide aimed at students of Hefei University of Technology, initially written mainly for the **Xuancheng Campus**, and is currently being continuously improved with content related to the **Hefei Campus**.

We hope to compile campus learning, life, administrative matters, and common questions into a reliable entry in a more systematic and easily searchable way.

### What you can find here

- Must-read for new students and frequently asked questions
- Campus facilities, transportation, and life services
- Learning and examination-related processes
- Organizations, activities, and campus culture
- Contact information and useful yellow pages
