## Platforms

- **LG webOS 4.0+** - roughly 2018 TVs and newer. OMP does not require root.
- **Android TV and Google TV** - Android 8 and newer. The same APK as on the phone.
- **Android phone** - Android 8 and newer.

Samsung (Tizen) is not supported yet. Xiaomi, Sber and Yandex set-top boxes install with the same APK: the installation assistant on the phone shows the steps.

## Features

- TorrServer catalog: categories, several view modes, series by season, shared history, and resume from where you stopped.
- Player. On LG, the TV's hardware decoder. On Android TV, the built-in 2160 Player (4K, HDR, Dolby Vision played as HDR10 without a hardware decoder; AC3, E-AC3, DTS and TrueHD sent to the receiver or decoded in software) or VLC. “Auto” mode switches between them automatically. On phone, the same built-in player.
- Tracks, subtitles, chapters, skipping intros and credits.
