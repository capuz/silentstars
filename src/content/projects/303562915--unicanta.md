Chinese | [English](https://github.com/303562915/unicanta/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

Based on the upstream Canta project, authorized by [Shizuku](https://shizuku.rikka.app/), package name `io.github.a303562915.unicanta`, internal codename **unicanta**.

---

## What this fork does

Upstream Canta works as: select an app → tap the trash can → uninstall. This branch adds three things around the uninstall step: knowing what you are deleting (device rules + community plans), noticing when you deleted the wrong thing (brick-recovery signals + batch guardrails), and **not depending on upstream servers** (a self-hosted telemetry backend that can be fully offline and self-hosted).
