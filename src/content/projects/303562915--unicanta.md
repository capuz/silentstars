---

## What this fork does

Upstream Canta works as 'select app → tap trash can → uninstall'. This branch builds on that with three additional things **before and after uninstalling**: knowing what you're removing (device rules + community plans), noticing when you removed the wrong thing (brick-recovery signals + batch guardrails), and **not depending on upstream servers** (a self-hosted telemetry backend that can be fully self-hosted offline).
