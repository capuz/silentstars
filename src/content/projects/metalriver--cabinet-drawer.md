# Drawer (抽屉柜)

**A local-first Windows desktop “drawer”: store apps, files, URLs, snippets and passwords in one small window, ready to grab at any time.**

No account required · No reliance on cloud services · No telemetry · Data stays only on your computer

---

## What is this

Drawer (抽屉柜) is a desktop organizing tool for heavy Windows users. Frequently used applications, files, folders, and URLs, the commands and snippets you use every day, and your scattered passwords all go into one lightweight small window. Fit what you can, and search to find what you need. Lock your screen with one click when you step away, and password fields are stored encrypted.

## Why Drawer

- **Cluttered desktop, slow to find things**: frequently used entry points are scattered across the desktop, Start menu, and browser bookmarks. Drawer gathers them into one window with global search.
- **Lots of repetitive work**: the same command or text gets typed several times a day. Put it in the snippet library and copy it with one click.
- **Want to manage passwords yourself**: if you don't trust putting passwords in the cloud, Drawer's passwords are stored only in your local database.
