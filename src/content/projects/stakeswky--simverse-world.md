# Simverse World — Cyber Immortality Open World

**Online Experience:** [https://simverse.world](https://simverse.world/)

Simverse World is a cyberpunk-style open-world multiplayer game/simulation platform. Players control characters ("residents") in a pixel-art virtual village, interacting with AI-driven NPCs, crafting items, trading, and engaging. Each NPC has an independent soul profile, endowed with a unique personality and memory by an LLM-driven dialogue system. Residents possess autonomous behavior capabilities—they wander around, chat with each other, form memories based on personality routines, and their personalities genuinely evolve over time and experiences.
