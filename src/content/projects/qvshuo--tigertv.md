# 小老虎爱看剧（TigerTV）

A cross-platform tool for searching and playing movies and TV shows, including an Android TV app, a Web/PWA, and a command-line tool for users and AI Agents.

## Features

- **Android TV app**: Multi-source search, episode selection, and playback adapted for the remote control
- **Web/PWA**: Search and play in desktop and mobile browsers, with support for installing to the device
- **Browsing and playback**: Popular Douban recommendations, continue watching, auto-play next episode, and remembered playback speed
- **Smart danmaku**: Automatically matches danmaku (comments), with manual selection of source and episode also available
- **Ad removal**: Filters out gambling interstitials that interrupt the plot from supported sources
- **Command-line tool**: Search, get links, generate Quantumult X direct-connection rules, and view logs
- **Agent Skill**: Guidance for movie search, link retrieval, direct-connection rules, and download workflows
