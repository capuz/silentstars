DBM, database management, integrates full lifecycle management of various database components such as MySQL, Redis, ES, Kafka, HDFS, InfluxDB, Pulsar, etc. It provides batch management capabilities for massive clusters, as well as a toolbox for cluster management of corresponding DB components. It also offers personalized services such as DB customization, high availability switching, and domain name management, along with comprehensive monitoring and alerting capabilities. This allows database administrators, operations, developers, and other users to easily complete database management tasks, managing databases more efficiently, securely, and comprehensively.
