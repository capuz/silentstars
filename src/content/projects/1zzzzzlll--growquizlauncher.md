## What is this

Kids watch one episode and want another, but parents can't always stay beside them.

**宝贝成长桌面** replaces the Android TV home screen with a small, fast launcher. At the interval you set, it brings a full-screen quiz to the front. Answer wrong and you get a different question to keep going. Answer right and you return to the home screen. All questions are built in and work offline. Volume and back keys can't get you past the cartoons: to keep watching, you must first answer one question correctly.

The same package also works on phones and tablets. There it can serve only as a “scheduled quiz + parental management console” without taking over the home screen. Forced pop-ups at the scheduled time depend on five permissions (overlay, alarms and reminders, unrestricted battery, notifications, full-screen alerts), which you can enable one by one under the device's “Children's Scheduled Quiz → Quiz Assurance.” No computer is needed.
