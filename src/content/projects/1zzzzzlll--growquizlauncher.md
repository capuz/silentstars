## What is this

Kids want to watch one more episode, and parents can't always be right beside them.

**宝贝成长桌面 (Baby Growth Desktop)** replaces the Android TV home screen with a small, fast launcher. At the interval you set, it brings a full-screen quiz to the front. Answer wrong and you get another question to keep going. Answer right and you return to the home screen. All questions are built in and work offline. The volume and back keys can't get past the quiz into cartoons. If you want to keep watching, answer one question correctly first.

The same package also works on phones and tablets. There it can serve only as a 'scheduled quiz + parental management console' without taking over the home screen. To make the quiz appear on time, it needs five permissions (overlay, alarms and reminders, unrestricted battery, notifications, and full-screen alerts). You can enable each one under 'Kids Scheduled Quiz → Quiz Guarantee' on the device. No computer is needed.
