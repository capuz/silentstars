## What is deco Studio?

Studio packages the infrastructure behind an internal AI rollout: model routing, MCP authentication, agent configuration, SSO, RBAC, audit logs, and usage accounting. Your teams get chat. You keep control.

Under the hood it's one control plane for your AI agents — one MCP endpoint for all your agents, tools, and models. Agents package context, tools, and policy into something you publish to the organization.
