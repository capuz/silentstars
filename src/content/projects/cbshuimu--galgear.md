GalGear is a local Galgame management tool for Windows. Add your game folders to browse covers, find games, organize collections, launch games, and manage saves and patches in one place.

> The current release is **v0.4.8 (2026-11-12)**. AI save search can recognize dedicated directories and scattered files. The tag filter adds a "Release group" search, and batch scraping lets you switch between multiple candidates and confirm them.
