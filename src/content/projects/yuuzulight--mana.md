Talking to a cloud AI assistant means handing your voice, your screen, and every conversation to someone else's servers. Character platforms and cloud VTuber stacks get you a voice and a face, but the moment their servers go down or the subscription lapses, so does the companion. Mana takes the other path: transcription, replies, TTS, memory, and screen awareness all run on your own Windows PC, so the assistant is actually yours — it works offline, it doesn't meter you, and…
