# humanizer-ru

Turns bureaucratic and overly smooth Russian text produced by a neural network into lively, natural text. It checks that numbers, sums, dates, names, quotes and references like `[14, с. 52]` are not lost or mixed up during editing, and reduces the risk of triggering Antiplagiat and AI detectors by removing invisible characters and lookalike letters.

Three ways to work:

- In the browser without installation: [Style and hidden character check](https://vladimir-human.github.io/humanizer-ru/check/)
  and [Fact check before and after editing](https://vladimir-human.github.io/humanizer-ru/sverka/)
  (they work offline, and the text never leaves the tab).
- In a regular web chat (ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, GigaChat, Alice): copy the [ready-made instructions (prompt)](https://github.com/Vladimir-Human/humanizer-ru/blob/HEAD/prompt/chat.md).
- In AI agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI): install the skill with the one command below. For Claude.ai (`.zip` archive) and other clients, see [all installation methods](#установка).
