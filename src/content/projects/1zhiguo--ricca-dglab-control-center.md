# Ricca × DG-LAB Control Center

[简体中文](https://github.com/1zhiguo/ricca-dglab-control-center/blob/HEAD/README.zh-CN.md) · English · [Website](https://ricca-dglab.web.app) · [Game Dashboard](https://dglab-x-game.web.app)

A standalone Windows companion that synchronizes **Holy Knight Ricca** with DG-LAB.
**1.2.0: BepInEx IL2CPP observation and direct PC Bluetooth**, preserving existing strategies and settings. [Setup and compatibility](https://github.com/1zhiguo/ricca-dglab-control-center/blob/HEAD/docs/BLE_SETUP.md).

## Features

- HP damage, hit reactions, armor break, sustained capture/struggle and death feedback.
- Independent A/B combat and story/gallery strategies: fixed wave, playlist, audio, or combined modes.
- Official waveform presets, a custom waveform editor, live monitors and an in-game HUD.
- DG-LAB V3/V4 Socket or direct PC BLE (Coyote 2.0/3.0 and Opossum); Coyote electrical output and independent Opossum vibration can run together.
- Separate Opossum presets, intensity and playlists; unified device workbenches and a dedicated manual-control page.
- Adjustable HUD size, position and displayed devices; network-adapter selection and connection diagnostics.
