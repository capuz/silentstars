## Why Liki

Asking a large language model to cast a chart directly can yield three different answers when the same chart is asked three times. Metaphysics is exactly where “a hair's breadth off, a thousand miles astray” applies: if a solar-term boundary is off by one day, the year pillar changes, and every judgment that follows is wrong too.

So Liki turns each error-prone step into an explicit process: charts are cross-checked across multiple sources, judgments come from a rule truth table, and times are normalized before they are examined. **Conclusions are computed, not improvised on the spot by a model.**

- ✅ **Reproducible charting** — the same input always yields the same chart; solar terms and true solar time are handled by the astronomical calendar engine
- ✅ **Traceable judgments** — every conclusion carries its factor basis and can be traced back to the original text in 《三命通会》 (*Sanming Tonghui*) and 《滴天髓》 (*Ditian Sui*)
