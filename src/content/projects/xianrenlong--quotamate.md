The "Daily Usage" section on the Statistics page and the "Usage Trend" on the Overview page always show the last 30 days. The period buttons at the top can filter the summary and details separately.

## What you can do with it

| See your quota | Understand usage | Manage habits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GPT subscription windows and reset times; locally collected Claude quota snapshots | Input / output / cache tokens, project summaries, per-entry records, and reference costs | Today / last 7 days / last 30 days, Chinese and English, light and dark themes, and CSV export |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **QuotaMate counts supported local records.** It does not sync the full history of ChatGPT or Claude web or regular desktop chats, and it does not provide model chat services. Claude currently uses passive local collection and does not actively poll subscription quota endpoints.
