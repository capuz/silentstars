Shiori is an open-source light novel reader focused on local reading. Import EPUB or TXT files, adjust the layout to your preferences, page through single pages on phones, and read two pages side by side on wide screens.

Books, progress, bookmarks, and reading statistics are stored on your device. Local reading requires no account or network connection. Each device stores its library and records independently, and there is currently no cloud sync.
