# franken_accounting

**A universal, evidence-native, double-entry accounting system of record in memory-safe Rust, built on FrankenGraphDB. The journal is the database: every balance is a certified fold over an immutable, bitemporal entry stream; every entry carries its evidence; every correction is lineage, never an edit; every report replays byte-for-byte; every proof verifies offline (FA-INV-14). Built for CPAs, regulators, litigators, forensic investigators — and for AI agents as the operators.**

> Verified at 331a5507ee28 (2026-10-30): UNRUN

> **Status (2026-10-25): implementation in progress.** The Rust workspace includes the embedded library, persistent CLI, book writer-lease daemon and registered MCP tools, exact money and calendar primitives, G0 constitutional registries and `tools/registry-check`, chart installation (with embedded chart templates), period transitions, posting and reasoned reversal, register folds and their verify targets (`fa verify book` runs every one of them), and an independent arithmetic and state-machine oracle. Trial balances support stock balances, period activity, civil-date cutoffs, and historical commit-sequence or retained-instant…
