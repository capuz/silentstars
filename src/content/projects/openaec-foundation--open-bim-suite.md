# Open BIM Suite

**A free 3D viewer for building models: it opens IFC, Revit, SketchUp, Tekla, Allplan, Archicad, Inventor, Vertex BD, BTL, DWG/DXF, MicroStation DGN, STEP, IFC5 (IFCX), CityJSON, CityGML, GeoJSON, Shapefile, LandXML, glTF (also Draco, meshopt and KTX2 compressed), 3D Tiles, quantized-mesh terrain, Smithsonian Voyager scenes, USD, Rhino, COLLADA, 3MF, AMF, FBX, 3DS, VRML/X3D, OBJ, OFF, STL and PLY models, models in OpenDRIVE, URDF, MagicaVoxel, DICOM files, structural analysis models (sam-json), and LAS, LAZ (also cloud-optimised COPC), E57, PTS, PTX, PCD and ASCII XYZ point clouds, and Gaussian splat scenes (splat PLY, SPZ, glTF), measures and sections them, saves them as IFC, and exports their floor plans, sections and sheets as DWG, DXF, IFCCAD, PDF, PNG and SVG, on the desktop and in the browser.**
