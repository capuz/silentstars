## About the Utility

ARK Survival Aim Assistant is built for Windows PC gameplay environments that use screen capture and computer-vision analysis. Its visual pipeline applies object-detection methods associated with YOLO and OpenCV to locate relevant targets and follow them as they move.

Target tracking and mouse automation are packaged together in a compact executable. Development and updates focus on detection behavior, tracking operation, and compatibility with the supported ARK Survival environment.
