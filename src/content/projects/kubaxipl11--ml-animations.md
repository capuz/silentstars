Welcome to ml-animations! This application helps you learn machine learning through interactive exercises and animations. Whether you're a complete beginner or just want to polish your skills, this application offers a hands-on approach.

## 📦 System Requirements

Before you get started, make sure your computer meets these requirements:

- **Operating System:** Windows 10/11, macOS Catalina or later, Linux
- **Processor:** Intel Core i3 or equivalent
