<h1>🚀 V2Ray Config Collector — Free and Automated Proxy Sharing</h1>

<p>
<strong>An intelligent, dependency-free, and highly optimized collector for automatically gathering V2Ray configs and similar protocols.</strong><br>
This project simultaneously utilizes <strong>GitHub repositories</strong> and <strong>public Telegram channels</strong> (without the need for an API, bot, or phone number), providing the most powerful source of free configs. All processes are executed <strong>completely automatically</strong> on GitHub Actions.
</p>
<p>
What sets this project apart from other collectors is its <strong>integrated architecture, intelligent Rate Limit management with dual tokens, and always fresh, split outputs</strong>.
</p>
