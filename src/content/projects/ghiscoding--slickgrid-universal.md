### Description
SlickGrid is one of the best JavaScript data grid, which was originally developed by `@mleibman`, it beats most data grids in terms of features, customizability & performance (running smoothly with even a million rows).

Slickgrid-Universal is a monorepo project (using [pnpm workspaces](https://pnpm.io/workspaces) and [Lerna-Lite](https://github.com/lerna-lite/lerna-lite)) which is regrouping a few packages under a single repository.
