## Overview

Acronis Disk Director brings disk, partition, and storage layout management into a single Windows-based utility. It is meant for people who need a practical way to resize volumes, reorganize storage, or prepare drives for different system configurations without jumping between separate applications.

In the 2026 edition, the focus stays on everyday administration work like partitioning, cloning, MBR and GPT conversion, and creating bootable media. That makes it suitable for workstation upkeep, migration projects, and general drive organization when controlled storage handling is important.
