# 🚂 MySummerCar-SaveWeaver — A Persistent World Companion for My Summer Car

> *"Because every grease-stained knuckle deserves a second chance at a perfect Saturday night."*

Welcome to **MySummerCar-SaveWeaver**, an independent, community-minded utility suite designed for players who want to reshape the rhythm of their Finnish countryside adventures without ever leaving the driver's seat. Where other tools shout and shove, SaveWeaver listens, suggests, and quietly adjusts the dials behind the curtain. Think of it as a co-pilot for your co-pilot — a mechanic who never drinks your beer and never borrows your van.

This project is a spiritual cousin to the well-known Najsr save editor, but it charts its own route through the potholed backroads of Suokumaa. Instead of simply toggling numbers, SaveWeaver treats your save file as a living document: a diary of every rally crash, every sauna session, and every regretful purchase at the Teimo store.
