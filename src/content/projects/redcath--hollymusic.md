# Holly Music

**A self-hosted music player compatible with LX Music sources.**

Search, discover, and save music on your computer and phone, and keep your playlists and listening history on your own server.

[Quick Deployment](#快速部署) · [Interface Preview](#界面预览) · [Audio Quality & Playback](#音质与播放) · [Platform Plugins & Soda Music](#平台插件与汽水音乐) · [User Guide](#使用文档) · [Community & Contributing](#交流与贡献)

## Why Holly Music

- **Reuse existing sources**. Import LX Music custom source scripts to provide playback and downloads for the selected platforms.
- **Extend search and discovery**. A single platform plugin script can provide single-track search, playlists, charts, or roaming content for multiple platforms.
- **Test sources before use**: check by platform and quality, run full inspection and preview, and import or update only after confirmation. Supports downloading a backup of the current script.
- **Bring your own playlists**: import NetEase Cloud Music CSV or public QQ Music playlists, and follow playlists that update with the platform. Song favorites support searching by song title, artist, or album.
