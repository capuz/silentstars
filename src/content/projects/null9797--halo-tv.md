**HaloTV is a secondary development based on [Aerial](https://github.com/pengchujin/aerial-tv), compiled and released by Null_. It is not the work of Aerial's original author and is not an official Aerial distribution. Thanks to the original author and all open-source contributors. The original copyright and MIT license are retained, and it is shared free of charge.**

## 3.1.1 Update

Improved phone weather settings, wallpaper switching focus, local video switching, and online video playback resume. Reduced extra decoding and repeated frame fetching during playback. Automatic quality can fall back to 1080p when severe frame drops persist.

Default settings are: settings icon fades out, lunar calendar on, classic glass slide focus, idle auto-cleanup on, soft key sound. Saved settings are not forcibly overwritten.

All three installation packages use a new package name and dedicated signature, and display version **3.1.1**. The internal build number is **195** for Standard / Guard and **51** for Lite.
