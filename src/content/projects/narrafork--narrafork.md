# NarraFork

Simplified Chinese · [English](https://github.com/NarraFork/NarraFork/blob/HEAD/README_EN.md)

**Efficient Durable Harness for multi-person collaboration, multi-model collaboration, and multi-device handoff**

NarraFork is a self-hosted AI programming collaboration workbench. Deployed on your own computer or server, you (and your team) organize AI sessions, operate code and terminals, and manage agents' context and permissions in the browser.
