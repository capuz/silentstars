## Introduction

Ever "unconsciously" opened an entertainment app when you wanted to focus? Once you're in and see a full homepage, you forget what you were supposed to be doing?

**FocusCat** can help you:

- **Freely choose apps to restrict**: specify the entertainment or distracting apps you want to control
- **Reflect before entering**: preset reflection questions, and pause to think before entering entertainment pages
- **Positive cat rewards**: each time you finish a reflection and exit an entertainment app, you earn cat food for your cat, raising its affection
- **Timed second reminder**: it's fine if you choose to enter; at a fixed time, your cat will remind you once more
- **Lock-screen focus mode**: screen switching is blocked during full focus, and you earn extra rewards for every half hour of focus

Turn "resisting distraction" from a painful restriction into gentle companionship.
