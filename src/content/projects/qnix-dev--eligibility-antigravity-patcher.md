## <a id="features"></a>✨ Features

`agy-manager` combines two key tools for convenient development in the Antigravity ecosystem:

- 🔓 **Bypassing Regional Restrictions:** Disabling local availability stubs ("not available in your location") in all three key applications (CLI, Manager, IDE).
- 👥 **Account Manager:** Secure storage and quick switching between multiple authorization profiles without the need to re-login through a browser.
