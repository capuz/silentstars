# TeXada Studio Local Edition

Edit LaTeX on your own computer, recognize images or PDFs, and check formulas and simple tables. Review the repair diff, save after review, then compile and export.

This is a local workbench that opens in your browser. Source files, documents, and run logs are stored on your machine. Image recognition and model suggestions use the local model service you configure.
