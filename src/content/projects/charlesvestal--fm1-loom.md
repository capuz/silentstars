# LOOM-8 for the M-VAVE FM-1

LOOM-8 turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a pocket groovebox: eight instrument tracks, each in its own colour, on
fourteen engines (nine synths: knit, rayon, jacquard, bellows, cane, voile, lace, tartan and acid, a TB-303; a sample player, a break slicer, a grain cloud, a drum kit, and MIDI out to drive another synth), 169 factory presets and 64 of your own; a 64-step sequencer with step tricks and parameter locks; players (arp,
strum, hold); weave, which crosses two patterns (bar by bar, step by step, or one's rhythm with the other's
notes); throws (momentary effects); a conductor track that moves the song through a progression; two
send-effect tracks (the reverb with lush, lush S and the PlayStation's reverbs among its modes), a mixer with EQ
and a compressor on the master, scenes and a song. MIDI over USB, Bluetooth and the TRS jack. Built on
[fm1-base](https://github.com/charlesvestal/fm1-base) and [felucca-bsp](https://github.com/hugelton/felucca-bsp),
from [Felucca](https://github.com/hugelton/Felucca) by Leo Kuroshita (Hügelton Instruments), with X0X's sequencer
discipline and Polly's menu paradigm.
