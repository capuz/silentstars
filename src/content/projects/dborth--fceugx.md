# FCE Ultra GX
https://github.com/dborth/fceugx (Under GPL License)

FCE Ultra GX is a modified port of the FCE Ultra Nintendo Entertainment
system for x86 (Windows/Linux) PCs. With it you can play NES games on your
Wii/GameCube.
