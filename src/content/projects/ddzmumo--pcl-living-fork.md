## Project Introduction

**PCL Living Fork (PCLLF, formerly LivingPCL)** is a Minecraft launcher independently maintained by [DDZmumo](https://github.com/DDZmumo). The project originally derived from [Plain Craft Launcher (PCL)](https://github.com/Meloong-Git/PCL). During the full **C# / .NET 10** modernization of its architecture, [PCL-CE](https://github.com/PCL-Community/PCL-CE) served as a homologous migration engineering example and a partial structural reference.
