Let your favorite characters exchange letters with you about music.

风灵音乐 (Sylphy Music) is a free, open-source personal music player that combines **music and letters**. It supports Android and Windows. You can choose or create characters and have them become your music pen pals. With their own stories, personalities, and tastes, they share melodies from your music collection, and the relationship continues through letters, conversation, and manageable memories.

Music is a reason to meet, and letters give longing an echo. The romance we hope to preserve can be freedom and distant places, or tenderness and standing side by side. Each character can have its own way of expressing itself, and each exchange can simply be a sincere sharing.

The player supports local music, private music servers, and WebDAV cloud drive directories, but it does not provide an online music catalog. **Character letters are powered by an optional LLM service. Even without enabling AI, you still have a complete private music player.** Cloud drive extensions can be used through the OpenList Lite special edition offered on the official website. Specific support depends on the corresponding driver.
