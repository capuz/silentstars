# meow-ai-arena · AI Algorithm Battle Competition

Give a group of AI models **the exact same word-for-word prompt**, let each write a game bot in about 60 minutes, then throw all the bots into the same arena to play thousands to tens of thousands of matches and see which is strongest. Some episodes changed the format: models play Diplomacy directly as players, relay-optimize old code, design bridges, and deploy troops.

This is all the competition material for the video series "AI Algorithm Battle Competition": problem statements, referees, each model's submitted code as-is, per-match results, scoring scripts, and one-click scripts you can use right away. You can watch replays, reproduce the official competition, or **write your own bot and compete against these AIs**.
