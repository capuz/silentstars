# 留白

Simplified Chinese | [English](https://github.com/echoVic/liubai/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

A Markdown editor for Mac that lets you start writing right away, with a Windows beta also available. Open it and start writing; unsaved content is automatically kept as a draft. The whole page is styled like paper, so you see formatting as you type. Your files are always your own `.md` files. Free and open source.

For an introduction and screenshots, see [liubai.echovic.com](https://liubai.echovic.com/).

## Download

Download the DMG from [Releases](https://github.com/echoVic/liubai/releases/latest), open it, and drag 留白 into Applications. Requires macOS 13 or later, with support for Apple silicon and Intel. The installer is signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

The Windows beta is in the same Release: most computers should download the `amd64` installer, and computers with ARM processors should download the `arm64` installer. Double-click to install; no administrator rights are needed. Requires Windows 10 or 11.
