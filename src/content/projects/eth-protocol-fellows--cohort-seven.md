# Ethereum Protocol Fellowship: Cohort 7

This repository is dedicated to coordination and resources for EPF Cohort 7

EPF is an open fellowship program for developers aspiring to work on Ethereum core protocol, clients, research and related tooling. 

> [!IMPORTANT]  
> Read the [announcement blog](https://blog.ethereum.org/2026/04/30/epf-7) and [submit your application](https://epf.wiki/#/wiki/epf)!

Learn more about EPF in [Program details](https://github.com/eth-protocol-fellows/cohort-seven/blob/HEAD/program-guide/program-details.md), watch the [intro to the current cohort](https://github.com/eth-protocol-fellows/cohort-seven/blob/HEAD/TBD) and learn about achievements of the previous one in [cohort 6 recap](https://ps.ethereum.foundation/blog/epf6-recap).
