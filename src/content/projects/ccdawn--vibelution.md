  A local-first AI agent workbench, Windows desktop first: let agents collaborate, let knowledge carry on, and keep data on your machine.

Why Vibelution: keep everyday work on your own machine. Planning, development, and review are handed to agents that each have their own role, and discussions and outputs accumulate into traceable knowledge. Your data stays on your own computer.

## One team, each with its own role

Hand planning, development, and review to different agents. View member roles on the same canvas, advance tasks through group chats and member messages, and keep discussion and handoff records.
