MoteDeck is a Windows tool centered on voice control of a computer with a remote control. It supports the **Xiaomi Bluetooth Voice Remote 2 Pro** (RC003), the **Xiaomi Remote 2** (RC001), and the **Google Remote** (Chromecast) in the Google TV version. Google TV remotes are made by third parties in multiple versions, so compatibility with all versions cannot be guaranteed. Through button mapping and voice input, it turns the remote into a computer's keyboard shortcuts and microphone. After connecting OpenMexia, you can also use voice to control Mijia devices.
