# 🏔️ PEAK Ascension Toolkit

**A next-generation companion utility for mountain-climbing enthusiasts who want to reshape the rules of the summit.**

---

## 🧭 Overview

**PEAK Ascension Toolkit** is an original, opinionated reimagining of the *PEAK-Hack-v2* concept — built from the ground up as a **configurable mountaineering assistant** rather than a rigid preset. Where the original project handed you a switchboard of toggles, Ascension Toolkit hands you a *canvas*. You decide how steep the mountain leans, how brightly your lantern burns, and how far your boots carry you per stride.

Think of it as the tuning fork of alpine traversal: a small, polished instrument that resonates with the way *you* want to climb. Whether you're a speedrunner shaving seconds off a vertical ascent, a casual explorer who just wants to see the summit without collapsing from fatigue, or a content creator staging dramatic footage, the toolkit adapts to your intent.

This project exists because the community asked for more — more control, more clarity, more languages, and a smoother experience across operating systems. It is intentionally not a "one-button solution." It's a **workshop**.
