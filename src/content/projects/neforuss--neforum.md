A free and open-source cross-poster for Android, iPhone and iPad, macOS, Windows and Linux.</p>

Write a post once. NEFORUM adapts it for each network (character limits, threads, hashtags, links, media rules), shows you a live preview of every version, and publishes it everywhere at once or on a schedule. Paste screenshots and copied photos straight into the composer (Ctrl+V, or ⌘V on a Mac), zoom everything in or out like a web page on a computer (Ctrl + and Ctrl −, ⌘ on a Mac, or Ctrl + mouse wheel), walk back and forward through the pages you visited with a mouse's side buttons, Alt+Left and Alt+Right (⌘[ and ⌘] on a Mac) or the arrows beside **New post**, and the post you are writing is kept when you close NEFORUM, until you publish or schedule it. Write a thread part by part with **+ Add to thread**, give each part its own photos and videos with the part's own **Add media** (or keep one media tray for the whole post: Settings → Posting → Media in a thread), move a part up or down with its arrows (its media goes with it), clear a part or the whole post with its **X** (with Undo), and cancel a post while it is being published. Once every network has a post, NEFORUM deletes it…
