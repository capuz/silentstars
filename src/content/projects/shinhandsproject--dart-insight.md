# dart-insight

dart-insight is a disclosure analysis web service that compares two DART periodic reports and shows the changed financial figures and sentences along with their original-text evidence. Even people unfamiliar with disclosures can search for a company without logging in, pick two periodic reports from the same company, and check what changed.

> The service name (Dart Insight or 공시레이더) has not been decided yet. Planning, scope, and decisions are based on Notion. See [Documentation guide](#문서-안내) below.

## MVP scope

**Flow:** Company search → report selection → comparison validation → analysis in progress → results (summary, changes in financial figures, changes in sentences) → check original text

| --- | --- |
