# 🎙️ Dictation Buddy Bot v2

</div>

**AI bot for Telegram that turns your voice messages into text and helps you process them quickly.**

## 🎯 Who is this bot for?

This bot is designed to solve real problems for busy people:

- 📱 **Receiving long voice messages from clients/partners?** No need to listen to them in meetings — the bot will immediately convert them to text
- 🚗 **Need to quickly dictate a long message while driving?** Record a voice message, the bot transcribes it — hands-free
- 💼 **Want to assign a task to your team after a call with a client?** Record a voice message in a taxi, turn it into a brief, and send it
- ✍️ **Struggling to formulate a prompt for AI in writing?** Speak it out, edit, and use it
- 🗣️ **Need to express yourself emotionally and in detail?** Speak as much as you want — get structured text

## ✨ The main feature — instant editing

After transcription, you can with one click:
- 📝 **Proofread** — correct mistakes and improve the text
- 😊 **Informal** — make the text sound natural, "by hand"
- 💼 **Business style** — format for business correspondence
- 📋 **Brief** — turn a conversation into a structured task specification with tasks
