## What is this

Picline is a fork of [Tendedero](https://github.com/alejandrobujan/tendedero) with modifications. The upstream was written by Alejandro Buján. **老张** built on it to strengthen image-receiving capabilities (reading the clipboard of third-party screenshot tools, cross-tool deduplication, and choosing which app opens images). This repository fixed multi-desktop support and added AirDrop, long-press preview, a Finder entry point, and selecting several images at once to use together.

Two platforms, same interaction:

| | macOS | Windows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Form | Menu bar icon + the rope at the top of the screen | Tray icon + the rope at the top of the screen |
