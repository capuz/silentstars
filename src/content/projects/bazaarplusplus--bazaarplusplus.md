# BazaarPlusPlus

**Born from passion** · An in-game mod for [《The Bazaar》](https://www.playthebazaar.com): Windows, macOS, and Android

[English](https://github.com/BazaarPlusPlus/BazaarPlusPlus/blob/HEAD/README_en.md) · [Official site](https://bazaarplusplus.com) · [Download](https://bazaarplusplus.com/download) · [Tutorial](https://bazaarplusplus.com/tutorial) · [Release Notes](https://github.com/BazaarPlusPlus/BazaarPlusPlus/releases) · [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/cauyxy)

---

BazaarPlusPlus does not change game balance. It only enhances information and records it: card encyclopedia, Ten-Win lineup recommendations, match history and battle replays, enchantment / upgrade previews, battle status bar, and more.

> **Note**
>
> The project code is mainly driven by [Codex](https://openai.com/codex) and completed in collaboration with [Claude Code](https://claude.com/product/claude-code).

Get the installer for your platform from the [download page](https://bazaarplusplus.com/download). For detailed steps, see the [tutorial](https://bazaarplusplus.com/tutorial).
