# ClearTV

**One app for Prime Video, YouTube, Pluto TV and Tubi on your Fire TV, with no page ads, no trackers and no app promos.**

**[Changelog](https://github.com/ddark1990/ClearTV/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md) · [Releases](https://github.com/ddark1990/ClearTV/releases) · [Contributing](https://github.com/ddark1990/ClearTV/blob/HEAD/CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Code of conduct](https://github.com/ddark1990/ClearTV/blob/HEAD/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [Security](https://github.com/ddark1990/ClearTV/blob/HEAD/SECURITY.md) · [License (GPL-3.0)](https://github.com/ddark1990/ClearTV/blob/HEAD/LICENSE)**

ClearTV replaces the separate service apps with one clean, simple app. You browse every service the same way with your remote, and on-demand videos play without the ads the apps put around them.

ClearTV is an ordinary app. It **does not change, root or modify your TV's operating system**, and it doesn't touch the other apps on the TV. To remove it, uninstall it like any other app.

ClearTV is **free and open source**. All of the code is here in this repo, so anyone can read exactly what it does, build it themselves, and suggest fixes.
