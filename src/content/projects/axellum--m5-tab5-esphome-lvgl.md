# M5Stack Tab5 — Home Assistant wall screen with ESPHome and LVGL

> **This page is also the home page of the website**, with a menu, a search and every page in English or French: **[axellum.github.io/M5-Tab5-ESPHome-LVGL](https://axellum.github.io/M5-Tab5-ESPHome-LVGL/)**.

**A Home Assistant wall screen that runs natively on the M5Stack Tab5 (ESP32-P4).** No browser, no polling: Home Assistant pushes what changed, and the screen redraws only that, in C++ with LVGL. Local "Okay Nabu" wake word, 15-day forecast, climate, lights, plants, energy, music, cameras, TV remote, alarm clock — and 8 offline games, in seven languages. Twenty-one themes, light or dark, chosen from Home Assistant. It is my everyday screen, shared in case it is useful to someone. It also runs on a 7-inch Waveshare board, the ESP32-P4 7B: supported, its firmware published with each release. I have tested three boards — two versions of the Tab5 and the 7B — and both the Tab5 and the 7B run in my home every day (the 7B since 15 October 2026), though I have tried a single 7B.
