# MobiCodex

**Your agents keep working even when you step away from the computer.**
Follow, reply to, and approve Codex, Claude Code, DeepSeek, CC Desk, and OpenCode sessions on your computer from your phone.

[Download](#下载) · [Three-Step Quick Start](#三步上手) · [Supported Agents](#支持的-agent) · [Release Notes](https://github.com/luoz76070-art/MobiCodex/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md) · [Report Issues](#反馈问题)

## Why use it

Agents can run for dozens of minutes, often pausing for you to tap an approval, answer a question, or check a result before deciding the next step. When you're away from your computer, they just wait. MobiCodex lets you handle these things from your phone.

- **Sessions follow you**: Sessions running on your computer can be found by project on your phone, watched in real time, and continued with text, images, and voice.
- **Notified only when needed**: When a task finishes or waits for your approval or answer, your phone sends a notification, and tapping it takes you straight to that session.
- **Dispatch work by voice**: Voice input runs up to 10 minutes, is transcribed as you speak, and can be organized into a prompt before sending.
