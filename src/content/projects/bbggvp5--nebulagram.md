# NebulaGram

**Your Telegram. Your style. Your connection.**

Unofficial Telegram client for Android and iOS with built-in NebulaLink, floating panels and flexible interface customization.

[**Download APK / IPA**](https://github.com/BBGGVP5/NebulaGram/releases) ·
[Features](#возможности) ·
[Full list of Android and iOS differences](https://github.com/BBGGVP5/NebulaGram/blob/HEAD/docs/USER-CHANGES.md) ·
[Report a bug](https://github.com/BBGGVP5/NebulaGram/issues)

---

NebulaGram keeps the familiar Telegram chats, channels, folders and accounts, adding its own styling and connection management right in the app. NebulaLink connects the messenger through a server you choose: a separate VPN app is not required for this.
