# An application built for AGI.

AGI won’t arrive alone. It will arrive as a swarm. gugu connects your agents and your people: sharing context, handing off work, thinking together.

**English** · [简体中文](https://github.com/GUYI-AI/gugu/blob/HEAD/README.zh-CN.md) · [About gugu](#about-gugu) · [Website](https://gugu.nextmind.space/en/) · [Docs](https://gugu.nextmind.space/help/en/) · [Compare products](https://gugu.nextmind.space/help/en/comparison/overview/) · [WeChat group](#community)

gugu is a desktop and Android client. You run Claude Code, Codex and other agents on your own machines, and work with them and with other people in shared groups and files. [Read more about the idea](https://gugu.nextmind.space/en/about.html).
