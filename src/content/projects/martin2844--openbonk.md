# OpenBonk

**Play Megabonk on Android with a native launcher, controller or touch input, and experimental Lua mods.**

OpenBonk is an unofficial community project in beta. Bring your own compatible Steam copy of Megabonk: the launcher prepares its data on your Android device.

[Download the beta](https://github.com/martin2844/OpenBonk/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta) · [Installation guides](#installation-guides) · [Install with an AI agent](#install-with-an-ai-agent) · [Lua modding](https://github.com/martin2844/OpenBonk/blob/HEAD/docs/MODDING.md) · [Build from source](https://github.com/martin2844/OpenBonk/blob/HEAD/docs/BUILD_QUICKSTART.md) · [Report a problem](https://github.com/martin2844/OpenBonk/issues) · [Support](#support-the-project)
