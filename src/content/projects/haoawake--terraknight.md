# TerraKnight (泰拉圣巢)

**Chinese** | [English](https://github.com/haoawake/TerraKnight/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

Forcibly crams Terraria into Hollow Knight: the protagonist is a Terrarian. Grab a copper pickaxe and dig through the Nest's walls, build blocks to skip class, and slash the Nest's bugs with Terraria weapons.

> 1.0: Playable from King's Pass all the way to Radiance and Godhome: four classes, over a thousand Terraria items, monster drops and a bestiary distributed by Terraria progression, bosses with Terraria's stat values, town NPCs and shops, mining and building, crystal tower teleportation, four difficulty levels, and creative mode. The trailer is in [Releases](https://github.com/haoawake/TerraKnight/releases).
>
> 2.0: Terraria-style LAN multiplayer (direct IP connection, up to 16 players), “Terraria settings” (auto-fire, key rebinding), 50 save slots, and after a wall slide, the fall height is recalculated based on the slide when you land.
>
> 2.0.1: The camera stays locked on the center of the character; monsters from neighboring scenes can walk over through door lines; Stag stations only lead to each station; all attacks can ring bells, pull levers, and break pots;…
