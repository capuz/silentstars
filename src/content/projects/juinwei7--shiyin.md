# 拾音 Shiyin

A meeting notes app for macOS. Record a meeting (in person or online), and see a live word-by-word transcript while recording. After recording, transcription and speaker identification run on your machine, and the result is handed to Claude, Codex, or an API to organize into meeting notes. Every note entry carries its recording timestamp; click it to hear the original words.

- Recording, transcription, and speaker identification all happen locally; recordings are not uploaded
- No need to install Python, Homebrew, or ffmpeg; just download the DMG and use it
- The interface is available in Traditional Chinese and English (follows the macOS language); meetings can be in Chinese, English, or Japanese
- Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 26

1. **Record or import**: Click "Record" (⌘R) in the toolbar. For online meetings, set the recording source to "Microphone + computer audio" so the other party's voice is recorded too. If you already have a recording file, just drag it into the window.
