Yuki is an open-source, self-hosted social AI agent. She takes part in conversations via QQ, Telegram, Feishu, Lark and personal clients, remembers people, preferences and shared experiences, and can also handle files, run programs, maintain projects and execute scheduled tasks in a persistent Linux environment. Persona identity, memory and tasks are stored in its own database, so they remain usable after switching models, accounts or gateways.
