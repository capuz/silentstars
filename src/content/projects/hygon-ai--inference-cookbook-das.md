## 📖 Introduction

This repository compiles experience and best practices for deploying, optimizing, and running AI models on HCU hardware, covering:

- **Large Language Models (LLM)** — text generation, dialogue, code completion, etc.
- **Multimodal Models (VLM)** — vision-language models, image generation, speech recognition, etc.
- **Omni Models (Omni)** — unified understanding and generation of text, images, and audio
