# Manage your Herdr agents by voice.

**Hands-free, from anywhere. Hear who's stuck, approve with a word, ask what broke.**

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4d0834e2-9d1a-453b-a88b-ff4b75428a5a

- **a voice-first agent harness** — a realtime voice model and a stateful orchestrator that reads your agents, wired together with rails in code (approvals only on your words) and tuned by evals, not prompt tricks. [how it works →](https://github.com/kccarlos/herdr-sheepdog/blob/HEAD/docs/dev/design-overview.md)
- **hands-free voice** — talk to your agents on your phone and earbuds, no screen needed. Real-time voice by the latest Gemini 3.8 Live or GPT-Live. [quick start →](https://github.com/kccarlos/herdr-sheepdog/blob/HEAD/docs/quick-start.md)
- **who's stuck, first** — "what's going on?" covers the whole fleet and names who needs you before anyone else. [commands →](https://github.com/kccarlos/herdr-sheepdog/blob/HEAD/docs/commands.md)
- **approve by voice** — an approval is answered only on your own words, enforced in code, not a prompt. [safety →](https://github.com/kccarlos/herdr-sheepdog/blob/HEAD/docs/safety.md)
