PC5

A launcher for your own PC games on a PS5 that runs homebrew. PC5 shows your games as a row of covers, installs games from a USB drive in one copy each, updates them from a newer copy with their saves kept, and uninstalls them with or without their saves. It runs the games with prospero-win (Wine on the PS5). The app is PPSA11018, its tile is called PC5. This is version 1.8.7. Temporary hotfix for 1.8.3

1. Open /data/prospero-win/pc5/settings.txt on the console (FTP).

2. Change the startup_sound = startup_sound = OFF (add the line if it isn’t there), and save.

No game is part of PC5, and PC5 downloads none: you install the games you own.

Getting started
Download PPSA11018.zip from the releases, unzip it, copy the PPSA11018 folder to the console the way you add any homebrew app, and start PC5 from Home.
Put each game's installed folder (the one with its .exe) at the top of an exFAT USB drive, or in a Games folder there.
In PC5 press Options › Game maintenance › Install games from USB, then choose the game and press ✕ to play. Hold the touchpad and Options for two seconds to leave a game.
