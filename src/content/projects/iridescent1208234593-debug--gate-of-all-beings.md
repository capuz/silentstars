# Gate of All Beings — Ximu Hall · Gate of All Beings

*Gate of All Beings — Ximu Hall* is an unofficial fan co-creation project inspired by *The Legend of Luo Xiaohei*. You can bring your own original characters (OCs) to Ximu Hall and share their settings in the fan gallery, or live, explore, and furnish spirit-quality spaces with other hall residents in the online LAB.
