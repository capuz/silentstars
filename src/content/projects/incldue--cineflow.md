# CineFlow

A smooth, customizable desktop movie and TV recommendation app. (Published on the linux.do open-source community)

**CineFlow** is built with Electron + Vite. It uses TMDB as its data source with a Douban API fallback, and supports natural-language search, personalized recommendations, word-cloud-style search terms, Dynamic Island mode, unified management of video sources, in-app playback, and one-click casting to devices on the local network.

## Introduction

**CineFlow** is a desktop movie and TV show recommendation app focused on a clean UI, Dynamic Island mode, self-managed video sources, and in-app resume playback. It uses TMDB as its metadata source and supports discovery of movies, TV series, actors, keywords, and genres.

## Highlights

### Discovery and Browsing

- **Daily recommendations**: The home page shows today's movies and shows, with posters, ratings, genres, and synopses.
- **Dual movie and show search**: Covers both TMDB `movie` and `tv`, so TV series, variety shows, and programs can all be searched and recommended.
