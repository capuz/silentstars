## Promo video

A 3-minute look at Tobs: a group of bots with their own identities, personalities and memories, working together in a small town. The video was recorded when the old name DeepSeekBot was still in use. Click the cover to play.

Tobs（托布）is an MIT-licensed open-source GrokBot alternative developed by BotHarness. It runs as a plugin inside [DeepSeek Harness (DSH)](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness). You can create several bots responsible for research, writing or coding, chat with them privately, add them to groups and assign them tasks as you would with colleagues.
