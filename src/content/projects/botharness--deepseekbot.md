DeepSeekBot (DSB, short for DeepSeekBot, and not what it sounds like in Chinese, hmph) is an MIT-licensed open-source GrokBot alternative developed by BotHarness. It runs as a plugin inside [DeepSeek Harness (DSH)](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness). You can create several bots, each responsible for research, writing, or coding, and chat with them privately, add them to groups, and assign them work just like coworkers.
