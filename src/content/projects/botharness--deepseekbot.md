DeepSeekBot (DSB for short, an abbreviation of DeepSeekBot; it is definitely not a crude pun, hmph) is an MIT-licensed open-source GrokBot alternative developed by BotHarness. It runs as a plugin inside [DeepSeek Harness (DSH)](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness). You can create several bots that handle research, writing, or coding, chat with them privately like coworkers, add them to groups, and assign them tasks.
