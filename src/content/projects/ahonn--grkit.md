# GRKit

English | [简体中文](https://github.com/ahonn/GRKit/blob/HEAD/README.zh-Hans.md) | [繁體中文](https://github.com/ahonn/GRKit/blob/HEAD/README.zh-Hant.md) | [日本語](https://github.com/ahonn/GRKit/blob/HEAD/README.ja.md) | [한국어](https://github.com/ahonn/GRKit/blob/HEAD/README.ko.md)

GRKit adds film looks, XPan and other crops, date stamps, glow, and grain to RICOH GR III, GR IIIx, and GR IV. It runs from the SD card and never writes to the firmware. Take the card out and the camera is back to stock.

Photo: [MIKI Yoshihito](https://www.flickr.com/photos/mujitra/53311001084/), [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/).

## Features

Press the ADJ lever while shooting and pick the sixth item, GRK.

- **Film Look**: film looks such as Portra 400 and Tri-X 400 with a live preview, plus your own LUTs and Lightroom presets ([sources](https://github.com/ahonn/GRKit/blob/HEAD/effects/NOTICE.md)).
- **Framing**: XPan, 2.35:1, 16:9, 4:3, 5:4, 7:6, or 1:1 photos, or frame lines only.
- **Crop**: 35, 40, 50, or 70 mm crops (50 to 100 mm on GR IIIx).
- **Guides**: thirds, center, golden ratio, diagonals, golden spiral, or dynamic symmetry.
