# Novelty

**Modern, simple, and transparent.**

A cross-platform novel viewer built with Flutter, redesigned from scratch for reading works from 小説家になろう and カクヨム.

## Overview

Novelty is a client application that supports **小説家になろう and カクヨム**, developed to deliver the best reading experience.
Based on an **offline-first** design philosophy, you can keep reading smoothly from your saved library even in unstable network conditions or out of coverage.

A site abstraction layer (`NovelSource` / `NovelSite`) lets you use なろう and カクヨム with the same operating feel, and it is prepared for adding future providers ([docs/architecture/multi_provider.md](https://github.com/L4Ph/Novelty/blob/HEAD/docs/architecture/multi_provider.md)).

While pursuing simplicity that strips away complex requirements and features so you can focus on reading, it provides the essential functions expected of modern reading styles.
