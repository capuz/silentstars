> [!WARNING]
> **TiviBro is in alpha testing.**  It runs every day on real LG TVs, from a 2019 webOS 4 OLED to webOS 5, 22 and 25 sets, along with the Android phone app, and beta is next.  Expect some rough edges, and settings or features may still change between builds.  Keep a backup before updating (**Backups and restore points** in the phone app or the [web editor](#the-web-editor)).  Found a bug?
