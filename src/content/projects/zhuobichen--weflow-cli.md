> Bring chat records, official account reading, and personal knowledge workflows back to your local computer.

**WeFlow CLI turns WeChat into your local second brain.** Four things, all done on your machine. It doesn't log into your account and doesn't upload chat content:

| | What you get | Where to start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 💬 **Chat records** | Query and search by person, by group, or by time; export to HTML you can browse directly (including images and voice transcription) | `weflow-cli export` |
| 📰 **Official accounts** | Organize subscribed account articles into a daily digest with AI summaries; can be sent by email | `weflow-cli daily` |
| ⭐ **Favorites** | Read the local favorites library and search by type and keyword (articles, images, links) | `weflow-cli fav list` |
