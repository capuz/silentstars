## About This Project

Counter-Strike Website 2000s brings back the look and feel of early Counter-Strike community portals from the 2000s, but runs on a modern Flask backend. It targets server admins, retro gaming fans, and anyone wanting a lightweight community site with that classic low-bandwidth style.

The design blends early-web visual cues—pixel fonts, bold banners, simple layouts—with the reliability and easy deployment of Python's Flask framework. The result is a functional, customizable site that loads quickly and uses few server resources, ideal for small gaming communities or personal projects.
