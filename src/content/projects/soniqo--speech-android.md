# Speech Android

📖 Read in: [English](https://github.com/soniqo/speech-android/blob/HEAD/README.md) · [中文](https://github.com/soniqo/speech-android/blob/HEAD/README_zh.md) · [日本語](https://github.com/soniqo/speech-android/blob/HEAD/README_ja.md) · [한국어](https://github.com/soniqo/speech-android/blob/HEAD/README_ko.md) · [Español](https://github.com/soniqo/speech-android/blob/HEAD/README_es.md) · [Deutsch](https://github.com/soniqo/speech-android/blob/HEAD/README_de.md) · [Français](https://github.com/soniqo/speech-android/blob/HEAD/README_fr.md) · [हिन्दी](https://github.com/soniqo/speech-android/blob/HEAD/README_hi.md) · [Português](https://github.com/soniqo/speech-android/blob/HEAD/README_pt.md) · [Русский](https://github.com/soniqo/speech-android/blob/HEAD/README_ru.md)

On-device speech SDK for Android, powered by [ONNX Runtime](https://onnxruntime.ai) and [speech-core](https://github.com/soniqo/speech-core).

Low-memory streaming speech recognition and an optional larger TDT model, both covering 25 European languages, plus text-to-speech, voice activity detection, and noise cancellation — all running locally. No cloud APIs, no data leaves the device.
