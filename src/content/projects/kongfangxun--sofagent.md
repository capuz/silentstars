## What is this

> 💬 **One-liner version**: On entry, it helps you clarify and document your business logic; after departure, every time your digital workers modify code or change files, they pass through a security check, leave a record, and create a snapshot—when issues occur, you can trace them and roll back.

> 🏢 **Organizational perspective version**: The bottleneck for AI implementation has shifted from "is the model smart enough" to "does the organization dare to adopt it"—can it integrate into organizational structure, does it have an account, how is performance measured, how are mistakes rolled back. sofagent is the institutional framework for…
