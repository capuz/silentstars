## What is this

> 💬 **One-sentence version**: When you join, it helps you figure out the business and write it down in files. After you leave, every time your digital employee changes code or files, each change is checked against those files, logged, and snapshotted, so problems can be investigated and rolled back.

> The bottleneck for AI adoption has shifted from “is the model smart enough” to “will the organization dare to adopt it”: whether it can join the org chart, whether it has an account, how performance is measured, and how mistakes can be reversed. sofagent is the onboarding system for digital employees.
