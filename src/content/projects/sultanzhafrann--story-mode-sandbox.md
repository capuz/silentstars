# 🌟 GTA 5 Single-Player Trainer — A Sandbox of Infinite Possibilities

> **Repository:** `gta5-single-player-trainer`
> **Tagline:** Transform Los Santos into your personal playground — no online access required.

---

## 🚗 A Different Kind of Joyride

Imagine Los Santos as a giant clockwork music box. Every pedestrian, every police siren, every thunderstorm rolling over Mount Chiliad is a tiny brass gear inside it. Most players just listen to the tune the game decides to play. This project hands you the winding key.

This is a **single-player-only enhancement suite** for Grand Theft Auto V's story mode. It does not touch GTA Online. It does not reach across the network. It simply sits beside your offline save file and lets you rewrite the rules of the world you're already allowed to roam.

Think of it as a librarian for chaos: you request a vehicle, a weather pattern, a wanted level, a time of day — and the librarian quietly fetches it from the shelves without ever leaving the reading room.
