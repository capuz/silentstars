## 👥 Meet your free research crew

Good research assistants are expensive and hard to find. **Free-Research-Crew** gives you a whole team for free: multiple AI research assistants divide up the work to write a paper the way a real research group would, and every key decision goes back to you, the PI.

| Team member | What it does for you |
|---|---|
| **PI** (you + main session) | Defines the question, makes the plan, approves every checkpoint |
| **Scout (literature)** | Finds existing research and verifies every reference one by one |
| **Designer** | Locks the research design before seeing any results |
| **Econometrician** | Social-science track: data, estimation, robustness |
| **Experimenter** | CS track: experiments with random seeds and fair baselines |
| **Data RA** | Acquires and records data, with source and variable documentation |
| **Figures RA** | Every figure is generated by a script from a logged run |
| **Writer** | Drafts the LaTeX paper based only on recorded findings |
| **Replicator** | Rebuilds the environment and reruns all results from scratch |
