## What is the OISY wallet

OISY is a new browser-based, network-custodial and multi-chain wallet powered by Internet Computer's [chain fusion](https://internetcomputer.org/chainfusion) technology.

## Features

The OISY wallet provides a convenient user experience known from custodial wallets but without their strong trust assumptions. In contrast, OISY provides trust assumptions based on network custody, and comparable to self-custody.
