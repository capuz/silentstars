Free and open source · Windows / macOS · Local storage · MIT License</p>

**DouyinDataReview is a free and open-source desktop app for collecting, exporting, and analyzing Douyin data.** It reads your Douyin watch history, likes, favorites, and friend chat messages through the session of your own logged-in account, saves them locally as JSON, and generates ongoing reports and a Douyin year-in-review (Douyin Wrapped). The workbench also provides video downloads, live chat, content search, Creator Center, trending charts, and live stream viewing. Optional AI video parsing and AI editing can use the large model API you configure, or the Codex / Claude Code CLI already logged in on this machine.

It suits backing up your personal Douyin records, managing saved content, reviewing creator data, and making your own annual recap. The project targets Douyin (the Chinese version) and does not support the international TikTok version. Installers are available for Windows x64 and macOS Apple Silicon. It is an independently developed, unofficial tool with no affiliation, authorization, partnership, or endorsement relationship with Douyin, ByteDance, or their affiliated entities.
