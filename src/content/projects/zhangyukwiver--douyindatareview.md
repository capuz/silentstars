Free and open source · Windows / macOS · Stored locally · MIT License</p>

**DouyinDataReview is a free and open-source desktop app for collecting, exporting, and analyzing Douyin data. It reads your Douyin watch history, likes, favorites, and friend chat records through the session of your own logged-in account, saves them locally as JSON, and generates ongoing reports and the Douyin annual report (Douyin Wrapped). The workbench also offers video downloads, real-time chat, content search, the Creator Center, trending charts, live stream viewing, and Douyin recommendation feed filtering. The AI video workbench has been split into a standalone app, [Film-Breakdown Workshop (ReelLab)](https://github.com/ZhangYukwiver/ReelLab), and the content library and explore page let you send content there with one click.**

It suits backing up your personal Douyin records, managing saved content, reviewing creator data, and making your own annual recap. The project targets Douyin (the Chinese version) and does not support the international version, TikTok. Installers are provided for Windows x64 and macOS Apple Silicon.
