# TimeSplitters: Future Perfect Recomp

Keyboard & mouse · 4K & ultrawide · Custom controls · Online multiplayer

**[Install & play](#start-playing)** · [Launcher guides](#guides--support) · [Multiplayer](https://github.com/TheDoffman/timesplitters-future-perfect-pc/blob/HEAD/docs/players/multiplayer.md) · [Build from source](https://github.com/TheDoffman/timesplitters-future-perfect-pc/blob/HEAD/docs/development/building.md)

**Stable multiplayer release:** [Game 0.7.0](https://github.com/TheDoffman/timesplitters-future-perfect-pc/releases/tag/v0.7.0)
and [launcher 1.7.0](https://github.com/TheDoffman/timesplitters-future-perfect-pc/releases/tag/launcher-v1.7.0)
bring Online Arcade hosting and joining into the game's **Multiplayer** menu on
all three platforms. Update the launcher and game before playing together.

> **Important**
>
> You need your own supported **US Xbox ISO or extracted game data**. Import it through the launcher.
> Original game files are not included. No platform version needs an emulator.
