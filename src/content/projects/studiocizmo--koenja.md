<p>I'm building this as a Mac input method, iOS keyboard, Android keyboard, and Windows input method.</p>

Type everything on the English (QWERTY) layout.<br>
KoEnJa checks <b>each word</b> to decide whether it is English, Korean (2-set, dubeolsik), or Japanese (romaji), and inserts the matching characters.
