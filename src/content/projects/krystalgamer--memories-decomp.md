# Yu-Gi-Oh! Forbidden Memories Decompilation

A byte-matching decompilation of the seven PlayStation releases of
**Yu-Gi-Oh! Forbidden Memories**. Every change must rebuild each retail
executable and overlay exactly.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> No game data or proprietary Psy-Q tools are included. Place legally obtained
> copies beneath `game/` (ignored by Git); see [setup](https://github.com/krystalgamer/memories-decomp/blob/HEAD/notes/setup.md).
