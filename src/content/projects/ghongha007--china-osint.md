# China OSINT

Public records, open data, research databases and tools for investigating stories about China, including Hong Kong and Macau. Every link was checked on the date shown beside it.

**170 sources. Last full check: 2026-11-19.** 135 loaded for us; 35 could not be reached from our checker and are marked in the table. Many mainland government sites are slow or block visitors from outside China, and some Western research sites block automated checks, so a source marked that way may still work for you in a browser.

## How to read the tables

- **Access** says what stands between you and the data: *Open* (nothing), *Free login*, *Captcha* (free, but a captcha on each search), *Paid*, or *Partly paid*.
- **Run by** tells you whose data it is. A government portal, a company, a think tank and a volunteer group each have different reasons to be right or wrong.
- **Checked** is the date we last opened the link. Portals move and die often, and Chinese platforms remove content without notice. If the date is old, check the link before relying on it.
- Notes in *italics* are caveats we hit while testing.
