Orbis lets you keep using AI programming agents on your computer from your phone. Step away from your desk and still continue conversations, check task progress, review file changes, and download generated files to your phone.

Run **Orbis Host** on your computer, install **Orbis App** on your phone, pair them by scanning a QR code, and pick up your familiar work where you left off.

## On your phone, you can

| Feature | Experience |
| --- | --- |
| Live chat | Send tasks, view replies and tool execution progress, and add ideas or stop the current task at any time. |
| File review | View files modified in this round and compare changes item by item. |
| File download | Browse files on your computer and download the results you need to your phone. |
| Session management | Find sessions by computer and project directory, switch tasks, and continue past conversations. |
| New session | Choose an Agent and a project directory on your computer to start a new task. |
| Subscription quota | View quota for supported accounts, including usage and reset times. |
| QR pairing | Scan the QR code on your computer to connect to it; you can also add multiple computers. |
