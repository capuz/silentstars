Preview posts from the list without opening them, and automatically refresh the list. Hides users and posts you don't want to see, and shows IP information (carrier, country, VPN) and memos next to authors.

## Installation

| Browser | Installation |
|---|---|
| Chrome (including Chromium-based browsers such as Edge and Whale) | [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/pmfifcbendahnkeojgpfppklgioemgon) |
| Firefox | [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/ko/firefox/addon/dcrefresher-reborn) |

Works on Chrome 140 and Firefox 144 or later.

After installing, click the extension icon on the toolbar to open the popup. In the popup, you can turn modules on and off. The gear button opens the options page (settings, blocklist, memos, shortcuts, data, info).

## Usage
