Preview posts from the list without opening them, automatically refresh the list, and hide users and posts you don't want to see. Displays IP information (ISP, country, VPN) and notes next to the author.

</p>

## Installation

| Browser | Installation | Minimum Version |
|---|---|---|
| Chrome (including Chromium-based browsers like Edge, Whale, etc.) | [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/pmfifcbendahnkeojgpfppklgioemgon) | 140 |
| Firefox | [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/ko/firefox/addon/dcrefresher-reborn) | 140 |

After installation, click the extension icon in the toolbar to open a popup. In the popup, you can turn modules on and off, and click the gear button to open the options page (Settings, Blocking, Notes, Shortcuts, Data, Information).

## Usage

### Preview
