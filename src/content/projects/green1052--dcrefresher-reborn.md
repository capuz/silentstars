Refreshes the post list automatically and lets you preview posts from the list without opening them. Hides posts and users you don't want to see, and shows IP information (carrier, country, VPN) and notes next to each author's name.

## Installation

| Browser | Installation |
|---|---|
| Chrome (including Chromium-based browsers such as Edge and Whale) | [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/pmfifcbendahnkeojgpfppklgioemgon) |
| Firefox | [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/ko/firefox/addon/dcrefresher-reborn) |

Works on Chrome 140 and Firefox 144 or later.

After installing, click the extension icon in the toolbar to open the popup. In the popup, you can turn modules on and off. The gear button opens the options page (Settings, Blocking, Notes, Shortcuts, Data, Info).

## Usage

- **Right-click** a post title in the list to open the preview window. Left-click still goes to the post as usual, but if `미리보기 키 반전` is turned on in settings, the two are swapped.
