# The Tao of Open Source: Exploring the Ideas, Knowledge, and Value of Open Source!

* **Open source is the way of innovation!**
* **Open source is the way of collaboration!**
* **Open source is the way of democracy!**

The Tao of Open Source promotes open source through writing. Its content covers open source culture, methodology, business, cognition, psychology, a systematic IT project, and more. It aims to spread open source principles, bring the local community closer in step with the world, and respond to a changing world in innovative ways.

## Community

As its influence has grown, the Tao of Open Source has gradually gained recognition, and people with similar views have naturally begun to gather, forming a niche community. Reading, writing articles, recording podcasts, giving talks, holding meetups... The key is that the community drives the production of a certain amount of value.

For communication channels and information publishing, please see the [Community Guide](https://github.com/OCselected/ttoos/blob/HEAD/content/Community/communication.md).
