### 💡 Project Overview

**医不帮 (yibubang)** is an open-source, cross-platform professional question-practice tool for medical students. Traditional quiz apps often suffer from pain points such as mandatory online connectivity, bloated ads, lack of desktop support, and question banks tied to a single app. 医不帮 uses **Flutter** and an **Offline-First** approach to deliver a consistent, smooth, high-performance practice experience on phones, tablets, and computers (Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS).
