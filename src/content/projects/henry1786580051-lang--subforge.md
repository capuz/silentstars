SubForge puts media import, speech transcription, segmentation and translation, subtitle editing, and export in a single workspace, aimed at video creators and subtitle localization work. When local speech recognition is selected, audio processing happens on the device; when a cloud translation service is used, the required subtitle text is sent to the selected provider.
