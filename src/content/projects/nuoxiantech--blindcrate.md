# blindcrate

🚀 blindcrate —— Four-player sealed auction · five-round blind bidding · blind-box unboxing battles. All bids are kept secret from the table, and players compete for a cabinet of unknown collectibles through clues, skills, and acting.

## 📖 Project Introduction

**blindcrate** is a multiplayer sealed-bid auction platform centered on "unknown collectibles." Each game has four players and five rounds of sealed bidding. You cannot see anyone's bids. You can only guess what the cabinet is worth from clues unlocked round by round, character-specific intelligence skills, and the hesitation each opponent shows when raising their paddle. The real tension begins after the gavel falls: the collectibles are revealed one by one from the fog, and you might uncover a family heirloom or end up with a pile of junk. The one who laughs last is the person who can both calculate accurately and fool others.
