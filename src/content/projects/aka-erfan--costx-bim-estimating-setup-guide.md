# 🏗️ CostX 2026 — Construction Estimating & BIM Workspace for Windows 11 / 10

---

## 🧭 Overview

CostX 2026 is a meticulously engineered desktop workspace for **construction estimating**, **quantity takeoff**, and **BIM-driven cost intelligence** on Windows 11 and Windows 10. It is a digital drafting table where every beam, brick, and bolt of a project finds its price — a place where spreadsheets stop being the bottleneck and start becoming the backbone.

Rather than forcing estimators into rigid pipelines, this environment treats the building itself as a living dataset. Drawings, model elements, assemblies, and rate libraries all breathe inside the same interface, so that the moment a wall thickness changes, the cost narrative updates with it. Think of it as a translator fluent in both the language of architects and the ledger of accountants.

The repository you are reading houses the public distribution notes, setup walkthrough, feature matrix, and the roadmap for the 2026 release line.
