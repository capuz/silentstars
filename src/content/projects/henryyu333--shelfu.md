## What is ShelfU

When you move files between apps, you usually need somewhere to put them for a moment. ShelfU puts that place in the notch. It stays out of sight until you need it: hover the notch or click it, and a small black island grows out — the track that is playing on the left, one icon per page on the right. Drag something onto the notch instead and the tray opens on its own, already waiting for the drop. Click an icon and that page opens from the island, 420 × 250 whatever the page holds, so it fits under your menu bar instead of covering it. Only the black shape on screen takes clicks: the menu bar beside it…
