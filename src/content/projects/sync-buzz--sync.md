# Sync

### Quality Control and Project Management System for AI Agents

Sync is the project management and quality control layer your AI agents work against. You define the project — its specs, rules, decisions, and roadmap — and Sync holds every agent accountable to it, ensuring they follow your architecture and constraints without hallucinating or repeating past mistakes.
