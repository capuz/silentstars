# YareLampGo

简体中文 | [English](https://github.com/ninsmiracle/YareLampGo/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

> Turn the robotic arm lamp into a desktop companion that ordinary people can play with: it can listen to you, see the environment, move by itself, and respond to you with actions and expressions.

YareLampGo's goal:
Lower the barrier to using robotic arms and embodied intelligence, allowing ordinary people without a technical background to play with it.
In the past, such robotic arms were more like laboratory equipment, making it difficult for ordinary people without a technical background to get started;
YareLampGo connects motors, lights, cameras, microphones, and large models into a local software system, allowing developers, creators, and ordinary players to quickly create interesting desktop interactions using web pages, command lines, natural language, or AI Agents.

The `lampgo` in the repository still serves as the internal abbreviation for the **YareLampGo** project, used to simplify Python package names, CLI commands, and configuration directories.
