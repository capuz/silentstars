# Lumina SFX

An offline sound studio for coding agents. Find a sound among 2,882 editable effects, layer it with others, and export a WAV or wire it into your game.

Describe where the listener is and what they should hear first. A pilot can follow an engine through rain while someone on the beach hears the same aircraft pass overhead. Choose a speaker mix, binaural headphone WAV or independently moving game emitters. [Spatial audio and scene mixing](https://github.com/LuminaBench/lumina-sfx/blob/HEAD/references/listening-scenes.md)

**New in v1.5.0** — Hear footsteps circle the listener on headphones, move from open rain into shelter, or sync a recorded swipe to a moving panel.
