# MIXXTOPIA 💙

A pixel-art narrative adventure web game inspired by **NMIXX《Strange Muse》** (their 6th mini album, title track 《긴 소원 (Birthday Wish)》).

> The wishes made on a birthday night turn into stars scattered through dreams. Go find them together with six muses.

- 🕹️ **Gameplay**: top-down exploration + NPC dialogue + puzzle solving and collection. 6 stages, each corresponding to one of 6 tracks.
- ⚔️ **Light combat**: F for light attack / G for heavy attack (mobile B / C). Only locks onto enemies within the 180° frontal fan that are in line of sight. When the passive energy bar is full, press F or G to release an energy blast with 2.2× the range of a basic attack.
- 🧱 **Tech**: pure HTML/CSS/JavaScript (Canvas), zero dependencies
- 🚀 **Current beta**: <https://ideer0126.github.io/MIXXTOPIA/beta/> (stable version not yet released)
