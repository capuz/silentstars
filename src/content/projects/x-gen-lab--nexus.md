# Nexus embedded platform

Nexus is a statically assembled C11 platform for industrial MCU software. It
provides reusable CPU, controller, typed I/O, optional OS and component mechanisms.
Applications, workers, product roles, private PCB data, Flash reservations and
recovery policy live in [nexus-examples](https://github.com/X-Gen-Lab/nexus-examples)
or private product repositories. [Chinese entry point](https://github.com/X-Gen-Lab/nexus/blob/HEAD/README_CN.md).

**This checkout uses Nexus 0.2.0 for development and Source SDK previews.**
Published previews and their immutable source identities are recorded in
[GitHub Releases](https://github.com/X-Gen-Lab/nexus/releases). A version value
does not establish software or hardware qualification. Consumers recompile source
under the exact selected configuration and toolchain. Read the
[changelog](https://github.com/X-Gen-Lab/nexus/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md) before integration.
