## 简介

Explore the "far from ordinary" coworker bond between you and the AI girls in the gaps while Harness is doing its work～(∠・ω< )⌒★

`dsh-whale-galgame` is a standalone multi-character Galgame interface plugin built specifically for DeepSeek Harness Web and Desktop. By blending seamlessly into your daily workflow, it turns cold AI interaction into warm, lasting companionship:

- **6 AI girls with distinct personalities**: supports DeepSeek (大肥鱼, Big Fat Fish), Claude, GPT, Gemini, Kimi, and Grok. Sprites, affection, memory, and backgrounds are each independent.
- **Appearance and core decoupled**: love the DeepSeek mecha but want deep conversations with Claude or GPT (NTR?)? Character appearance and the underlying reply model can be mixed and matched freely.
- **"Mini theater" mode**: say goodbye to single Q&A. Unlock multi-AI-girl daily interactions, short work-retrospective skits, and exclusive milestone storylines.
- **Bond system that grows with you**: both daily interaction and accumulated work tokens raise affection, unlocking exclusive intimate tones and AI-generated commemorative CGs.
- **Light and cute desktop pet**: resides in a corner of your screen.
