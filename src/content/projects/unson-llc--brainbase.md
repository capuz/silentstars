# Brainbase

## Don't limit your judgment power to just one person.

Brainbase turns your purpose, priorities, judgment criteria, and past decisions into a local judgment OS that multiple AIs can use. Codex and Claude Code can think deeply from the same judgment axis and advance research, design, writing, and development.

> **Use your smart self as both a thinking partner and an execution team.**

Humans decide the purpose, judgment criteria, and scope of work to delegate. AI references these to explore, provide counter-evidence, and advances work within the permitted scope.

## Why It's Needed

At companies, even when documents and meeting notes remain, the following tend to remain in people's heads:

- Why this customer was prioritized
- Why profitable proposals were rejected
- What we protect by choosing that policy
- How much can be delegated to AI or other staff members
- What should be changed after seeing the results of execution

In this state, the same explanation is needed for AI every time, judgment quality drops when staff changes, and important decisions keep being returned to management.
