# Local Office

Private AI for the office, as a native Mac app. Drop in paper scans, PDFs, photos and Office files, then ask questions and get answers that cite the exact page. A built-in wiki (your "second brain") compiles what your documents say about companies, people and deadlines, and the Desk lets you write documents, spreadsheets and presentations with the assistant. Everything runs on the Mac, and no file leaves the computer.

## Features

- **Documents:** drag & drop PDFs, scans, photos (JPG, PNG, HEIC), TIFF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and text. Digital PDFs use their text layer; scans and photos go through OCR. Tables are kept.
- **Folders:** connect local or shared folders; they stay in sync, strictly read-only.
- **Skills:** folders with a `SKILL.md` ([Agent Skills format](https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/agents-and-tools/agent-skills/overview)) that teach the assistant an office task: deadline reports, invoice tables, Word reports, supplier checks. Ask normally and the right skill is picked, or type `/` to choose one.
