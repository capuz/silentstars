# Amir: Free Iranian Accounting and ERP Software

**[English version](https://github.com/Jooyeshgar/FreeAmir/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)**

**Project Status:** Amir is under active development and is not yet recommended for operational use without independent review and testing. Before using in a Production environment, please review and test the sections you need with test data.

## Introduction

**Amir** is an open-source accounting and ERP software for Iranian businesses. The project's focus is on double-entry accounting, sales and purchase invoices, inventory and cost management, fiscal year operations, attendance and absence, payroll, and compliance with Iranian tax requirements including the Modeian system.

### As an Alternative to Commercial Software

Amir is designed as a **free and open-source** alternative to commercial Iranian accounting software:

- **Iranian open-source ERP** with support for multiple companies and multiple fiscal years
- **Free accounting software** without licensing or subscription costs
- **Open-source Modeian system** with full integration to Iran's Modeian tax system
