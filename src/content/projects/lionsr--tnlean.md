**TNLean** is a [Lean 4](https://lean-lang.org/) library built on [Mathlib](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4) (Lean `v4.36.0-rc1`) and the companion quantum-information library [QICLean](https://github.com/LionSR/QICLean). It formalizes the mathematical theory of one- and two-dimensional tensor networks: matrix product states (MPS) and their canonical forms and gauge structure, parent Hamiltonians and spectral gaps, symmetries and topological phases, matrix-product density operators (MPDO) and unitaries (MPU/QCA), quantum circuit state preparation, projected entangled pair states (PEPS), and entanglement area laws with tensor-network approximations in 1D and 2D. Blueprint tensor diagrams are rendered by [tenkz](https://github.com/LionSR/tenkz), pinned in [`tenkz.toml`](https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/blob/HEAD/tenkz.toml).
