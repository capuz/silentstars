# Zhaohuo System: In-Game Trainer for *Legend of Mortal*

Simplified Chinese | [Traditional Chinese](https://github.com/Toufff/zhaohuo-system/blob/HEAD/README.zh-Hant.md) | [English](https://github.com/Toufff/zhaohuo-system/blob/HEAD/README.en.md) | [Korean](https://github.com/Toufff/zhaohuo-system/blob/HEAD/README.ko.md)

An in-game trainer for *Legend of Mortal* (Legend of Mortal), running as a BepInEx plugin. Press F8 in-game to open the panel, where you can:
- View which events this action may trigger, and choose which one to trigger;
- Directly edit attributes and affection;
- Preview the outcomes of dialogue options ahead of time;
- Choose dice roll results;
- Enable god mode and one-hit kills in combat.

The mod UI follows the game language and supports Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Korean.

## What it can do

- **Events**: Before each action, the panel lists events that may trigger on this action and their trigger conditions. Conditions include the current value (e.g., "Action count 3>=1"); satisfied conditions are shown in green. Under each event, the resulting changes to attributes, affection, etc. are listed, with increases in green and decreases in red.…
