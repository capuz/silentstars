## Perhaps the most powerful file manager you've ever used

Tabs and split panes keep multiple directories visible at the same time; Super Preview lets you see the content first; image, PDF, archive, and rename tools keep your next step within reach. Switch between fewer apps and finish the work in front of you.

## However many you open, they stay organized

Put reference materials, files you're editing, and delivery directories into separate tabs, grouped by project and color-coded. Drag frequently used files and folders into the favorites bar, then build multi-level groups by client or purpose.
