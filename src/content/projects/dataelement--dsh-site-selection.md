# Store Location Selection Workbench

**Simplified Chinese** | [English](https://github.com/dataelement/dsh-site-selection/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

DSH (DeepSeek Harness) Plugin. **Point to any location on a real 3D city map and instantly get site selection analysis for that point**——
Tell your requirements to DSH in plain language, it batch finds locations based on the same model and writes them into a list; once you like a location, collect rental properties available there; after site visits, fill back in feedback, and the chat on the right can understand which location you selected throughout the process.

## Features

- **Click Anywhere to Analyze** — Millisecond-level analysis for any location on the map: scores across three dimensions, percentile for each metric, risk alerts, customer demographic composition
- **Score by Business Type** — Opening a coffee shop vs. a pharmacy yields different scores at the same location: based on the distribution of existing stores of the same business type in the city as a baseline
- **Find Locations in Plain Language** — "Within 200m of metro, plenty of restaurants, no more than 3 similar shops, find 10 locations", DSH translates into…
