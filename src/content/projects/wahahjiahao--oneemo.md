## 💡About OneEmo

English | [Chinese](https://github.com/waHAHJIAHAO/OneEmo/blob/HEAD/README_zh.md)

OneEmo targets video emotion intelligence, unifying emotion perception, emotion understanding, and emotion interaction within a single multimodal reasoning model. It covers sentiment analysis (MSA), basic emotion recognition (B-MER), open-vocabulary emotion recognition(OV-MER), intention recognition (MIR), humor & sarcasm understanding (MHU & MSU), empathic response generation (ERG) and emotional support conversation (ESC).
