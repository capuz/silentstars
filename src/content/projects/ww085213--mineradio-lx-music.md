# Mineradio - LX Music / MR

> A secondary development and long-term maintenance project of an immersive desktop music player based on Electron.
> Continuous iteration around music playback, lyrics, visualization, local network control, resource stability, and AI Agent.

[**Windows Download**](https://github.com/ww085213/Mineradio-LX-Music/releases/download/v1.6.1/Mineradio.Setup.1.6.1.exe) · [**GitHub Release**](https://github.com/ww085213/Mineradio-LX-Music/releases/tag/v1.6.1) · [**Douyin Project Demo**](https://v.douyin.com/eRlkIQrTOTw/)

## Project Overview

### Main Interface

Immersive homepage display: daily recommendations, recent playback, platform charts, playlist plaza, music radio, local network remote control, and real-time spectrum features.

### AI Agent "XiaoM"

XiaoM is not just a chat window; it can convert natural language commands into actual player operations, such as searching and playing songs, adjusting volume, switching audio quality, managing playlists, and more.

### Lyrics and Visualization

Particle visualization, bilingual lyrics, and real-time spectrum combined display, showcasing the immersive visual capabilities of the desktop music player.
