# Claude 防封 skill

Simplified Chinese ｜ [English](https://github.com/futureYYY/claude-antiban-skill/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

A battle-tested, end-to-end handbook for preventing Claude account bans: from choosing a US residential IP, setting up Clash chained proxies, plugging DNS / IPv6 leaks, and configuring an AdsPower fingerprint environment, to registration and account warm-up, subscription payment, remote use of Claude Code, and the order of self-checks after a ban.

> It documents "a method", **not a promise of "never getting banned"**. Whether an account is banned depends on how well the steps are executed, the account source, whether IP / payment / KYC come from the same origin, and platform policy.
