# ConstructCraft

Free and open source **Building Information Modeling (BIM)** software, written in Rust and independent of any commercial vendor.

**[Download](#download)** · [Português (Brasil)](https://github.com/francisreboucas/constructcraft/blob/HEAD/README.pt-BR.md) · [Discord](https://discord.gg/y8ySJZjpK) · [Contributing](https://github.com/francisreboucas/constructcraft/blob/HEAD/CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/francisreboucas/constructcraft/blob/HEAD/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [Security](https://github.com/francisreboucas/constructcraft/blob/HEAD/SECURITY.md)

A single parametric model is the source of truth. Floor plans, ceiling plans, sections, elevations, 3D views, legends, schedules and sheets are *queries* over that model and update automatically after every change.
