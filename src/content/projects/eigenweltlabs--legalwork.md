# LegalWork

A local computer-use agent for law firms, from Eigenwelt Labs. Point it at a folder of documents and ask in plain English — it reads, drafts, and redlines them on your own machine, using the model you connect. Everything stays owned by the firm.

User documentation: [LegalWork guide](https://github.com/eigenweltlabs/LegalWork/blob/HEAD/docs/user-guide/index.md), including settings, integrations, email, calendar and recorder help. The bundled `legalwork-help` skill makes this guide available to the agent.
