# AI-CloudOps

> **[English](https://github.com/GoSimplicity/AI-CloudOps/blob/HEAD/README.en.md) | Chinese**

An AI-based cloud-native operations management platform that integrates Kubernetes management, monitoring and alerting, intelligent analysis, and more.

## Project Introduction

AI-CloudOps is a modern cloud-native operations management platform designed to improve operational efficiency and system stability through artificial intelligence.

### Core Features

- **Intelligent Operations**: Machine learning-based anomaly detection, failure prediction, and automatic remediation
- **Kubernetes Management**: Complete cluster management, application deployment, and monitoring
- **Monitoring & Alerting**: Comprehensive Prometheus-based monitoring and intelligent alerting
- **Permission Management**: RBAC-based interface permission control within a shared operations workspace
- **Ticketing System**: Complete operations ticket workflow management
- **Resource Management**: CMDB asset management and service topology visualization
