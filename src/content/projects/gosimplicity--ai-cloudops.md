# AI-CloudOps

> **[English](https://github.com/GoSimplicity/AI-CloudOps/blob/HEAD/README.en.md) | Chinese**

An AI-based cloud-native operations management platform that integrates Kubernetes management, monitoring and alerting, intelligent analysis, and other features.

## 1.0 Delivery Entry

[Acceptance Records](https://github.com/GoSimplicity/AI-CloudOps/blob/HEAD/docs/release/verification.md) · [Pinned Source and Image Manifest](https://github.com/GoSimplicity/AI-CloudOps/blob/HEAD/docs/release/release-candidate.json) · [Deployment and Upgrade](https://github.com/GoSimplicity/AI-CloudOps/blob/HEAD/docs/release/README.md) · [Stage Changes](https://github.com/GoSimplicity/AI-CloudOps/blob/HEAD/docs/release/CHANGELOG.md)

Place the three repositories in sibling directories. Cloud resource support in 1.0 covers Alibaba Cloud ECS, and automatic ticket notifications support immediate triggering. Other providers and advanced triggers are explicitly disabled. The workspace uses API-level RBAC. Models, clusters, SSH, and notifications must each be configured with their own dependencies. For specific test results and boundaries, see the acceptance records.
