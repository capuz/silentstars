# AI-CloudOps

> **[English](https://github.com/GoSimplicity/AI-CloudOps/blob/HEAD/README.en.md) | Chinese**

A cloud-native operations management platform based on artificial intelligence, integrating Kubernetes management, monitoring and alerting, intelligent analysis, and other features.

## 1.0 Release Entry Points

[Acceptance Records](https://github.com/GoSimplicity/AI-CloudOps/blob/HEAD/docs/release/verification.md) · [Pinned Source and Image Manifest](https://github.com/GoSimplicity/AI-CloudOps/blob/HEAD/docs/release/release-candidate.json) · [Deployment and Upgrade](https://github.com/GoSimplicity/AI-CloudOps/blob/HEAD/docs/release/README.md) · [Changelog](https://github.com/GoSimplicity/AI-CloudOps/blob/HEAD/docs/release/CHANGELOG.md)

Place the three repositories in sibling directories. In 1.0, cloud resource support covers Alibaba Cloud ECS, and ticket auto-notifications support immediate triggering; other providers and advanced triggers are explicitly disabled. The workspace uses API-level RBAC. Models, clusters, SSH, and notifications must each have their own dependencies configured. For specific test results and boundaries, see the acceptance records.
