# About the System | About the System

## Arabic

**SARP AI Pro (Sarp for Digital Intelligence) — Digital Intelligence System for Student Affairs and Registration** is a system developed by the SARP project. It employs artificial intelligence algorithms and digital transformation techniques to improve data management, automate procedures, and raise performance levels.

The system aims to support student affairs and registration operations in higher education institutions, organize academic data, facilitate administrative procedures, and support the preparation of reports and digital documents according to the functions available in the system.

The project is officially defined as **the first digital intelligence system for student affairs and registration in higher education**.

For system information and documentation, please visit [the official SARP AI Pro wiki](https://github.com/CiH99ITSecurity/SARP-AI/wiki).
