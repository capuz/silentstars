## About

1. The status `failing` indicates no updates in the short term.
2. You can get the most recent `new` CVE project information from the [new.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sari3l/CVE-Monitor/main/new.json) file.
3. You can get the most recent `update` CVE project information from the [update.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sari3l/CVE-Monitor/main/update.json) file.
4. You can get the complete information for the year from the `README.md` in the annual directory.
5. You can get the newly added and updated CVE content for the day from the `dateLog` directory.
