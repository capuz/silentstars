**bothouse: put Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and DeepSeek on your computer into one group chat — an AI team that shares one memory and divides up its own work.**

## Promo video

Watch bothouse in 3 minutes: one message goes in; the crew splits the work, takes it on and hands it back. Click the cover to play.

The setup wizard offers **Only when addressed** for quiet group participation without a routing model. Smart routing can use a local 0.6B model; it must provide logprobs and is not downloaded automatically. See [routing choices](https://github.com/zuiho-kai/bothouse/blob/HEAD/docs/connect-discord-feishu.md#5-jev-和主角色).
