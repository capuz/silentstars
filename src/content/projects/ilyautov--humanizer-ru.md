# humanizer-ru: Russian text editing for AI agents

> Removes bureaucratese and template phrasing from Russian text. The skill gives an agent a procedure for local editing: find excess, fix specific spots, check meaning and voice against the source. Includes a scanner, CLI, MCP, and a Chrome extension. 67 patterns in the catalog, 21 hard bans with genre exceptions.
