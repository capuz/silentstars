**A public-document tool that puts Korean documents in AI's hands.** Read, create, and check documents right where they are, with just a sentence.

- Give it a received official letter and say “draft a reply,” and you get a Hangul file (HWPX) in your agency's format.
- Right-click a document in Explorer and press “Entrust to KorDoc AI…” to hand it a task on the spot.
- Among files named “최종_진짜최종_수정 (2)” (roughly “final_really_really_final_revised (2)”), it finds the true final version (no AI needed).
- The document engine, Hangul editor, and AI all run inside this PC, so it works on work networks cut off from the internet. Originals are never modified; results are always new files.
