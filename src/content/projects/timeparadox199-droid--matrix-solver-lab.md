# 🧮 Linearis — The Equation Forge

> *Where numbers stop being a chore and start being a conversation.*

A tactile, browser-first training ground for anyone who wants to get genuinely good at solving linear equations — not by memorizing tricks, but by building an intuition that survives contact with real problems.

Rendered for humans, tuned for curiosity, and designed to be picked up in under a minute.

---

## 🌟 Why Linearis Exists

Most equation trainers feel like flashcard drills from a decade ago. You type an answer, you get a red X, you move on — and nothing actually sticks. Linearis takes a different stance.

Think of it like a **gym for algebra**. Every session adjusts to your current strength, adds a little resistance at the right moment, and never lets you plateau silently. The result: a habit of solving that feels almost meditative rather than punitive.

Whether you're a student brushing up before an exam, a teacher looking for a warm-up activity, a parent supporting a learner at home, or a lifelong tinkerer who simply enjoys the elegance of `ax + b = c` — Linearis meets you where you are.
