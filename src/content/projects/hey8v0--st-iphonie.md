# ST-iPhonie 📱

Give your tavern a little phone.

Characters can message you from their phones, call you, and post to Moments. Lines of dialogue in the narrative can be read aloud in the character's own voice. Scenes from the story can be automatically drawn as images. You can also view the narrative as a visual novel, and when you need a break, go play a game for a while.
