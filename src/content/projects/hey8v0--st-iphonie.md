# ST-iPhonie 📱

Put a little phone into your tavern.

Characters can use the phone to send you messages, call you, and post to Moments. Lines in the main text can be read aloud in the character's own voice. Scenes from the story can be automatically drawn as pictures. You can also read the main text as a visual novel, and when you're tired, go play a game for a while.
