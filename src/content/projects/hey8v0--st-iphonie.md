# ST-iPhonie 📱

Install a little phone into your tavern.

Characters can use the phone to send you messages, call you, and post to Moments. Lines of dialogue in the narrative can be read aloud in the character's own voice. Scenes from the plot can be automatically drawn as images. You can also view the narrative as a visual novel, and when you're tired, go play a game for a while.

> Currently in beta (0.8.84). Trying it out and giving feedback is welcome. For recent changes, see 'Recent Updates' below. The full record is in **[Changelog](https://github.com/hey8v0/ST-iPhonie/blob/HEAD/更新日志.md)**.

## What it can do

### Life on the phone

- 💬 **Chat**: QQ-style private and group chats, with red envelopes, transfers, 'pat' (拍一拍), voice messages, and song sharing. You can have several chat windows open with one person. Your wallet and shop let you buy bubbles, pendants, backgrounds, and gifts to give to characters, and characters will also send you things. When you finish chatting, you can 'bring it into the story,' and the next narrative passage will pick up from there.
