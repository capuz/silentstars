# Verovo

**Know what an AI task really costs, and what its result is worth.**

Verovo connects buyer logs, supplier charges, financial records and business outcomes. Review charges and delivery, allocate direct and shared costs to tasks, and calculate cost per verified success and ROI when the evidence supports it.

For teams buying or operating AI services, the output is a reproducible economic record: task costs, allocation rules, verified results and explicit evidence gaps. The local `economics` command runs on an explicit case, and `intake` assembles that case from a local billing-period bundle; automated optimization and insurance remain future work. [Feature status and build/integrate decisions](https://github.com/roy-tong/Verovo/blob/HEAD/docs/feature-status.md).
