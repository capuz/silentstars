## What Is SIGHTLINE?

SIGHTLINE is a lightweight, open-source aim trainer that operates entirely inside your web browser. Designed around the muscle memory demands of tactical shooters, it offers specialized drills for flicking, tracking, peeking, and target switching. There is no installation, account sign-up, or external dependency—just open the page and start training.

The 2026 version brings refined difficulty scaling and broader customization. Players can now fine-tune target size, spawn speed, and session duration to match their skill level. Local score and accuracy tracking let you monitor progress over time without any server-side storage.
