**Off The Cloud**
==============

Off The Cloud is free, open-source software that gives your family its own private social network and home cloud on a Raspberry Pi 5: share photos and videos with the friends you accept, from device to device, back up every phone and keep your files in sync, instead of Instagram, Google Photos, iCloud, Google Drive or Dropbox. With iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows and Linux apps.

[Website](https://off-the.cloud) · [Live demo](https://demo.off-the.cloud) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6814658734) (iPhone, iPad and Mac) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cloud.offthe.otc) · [otc-sync for Windows and Linux](https://github.com/alonsovidales/otc/releases/tag/desktop) · [Press kit](https://github.com/alonsovidales/otc/blob/HEAD/docs/press)

The social network is decentralised: each family's posts live on its own device, and friends' devices fetch them from each other, with comments, likes and notifications, newest first: no ads, no algorithm, no strangers, and no company server holding everyone's photos.
