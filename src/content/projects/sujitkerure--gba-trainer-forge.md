# 🌱 GBA Forge Companion — ROM Enhancement Toolkit

---

## 🧭 A Different Kind of Cartridge Whisperer

There is a particular kind of magic that lives inside a Game Boy Advance cartridge. It is not the kind of magic that shouts — it is the kind that hums softly behind a screen, waiting for the right hands to shape it. **GBA Forge Companion** was born from that hum.

Where the original *GBAATM-Rebirth* project carved a path by offering a way to weave training companions directly into the fabric of a GBA ROM, this repository takes that philosophy and pushes it into a broader, more gentle, and more accessible direction. Instead of thinking of this as a single-purpose utility, imagine it as a small atelier: a workshop where you can prepare, refine, and personalise the experience of retro-inspired journeys without ever needing to speak the language of low-level assembly.

This README is long on purpose. A large project deserves a large welcome mat. Pour a cup of tea, sit back, and let's walk through what lives inside.
