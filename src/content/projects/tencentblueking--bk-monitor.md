BlueKing Cloud Monitoring Platform (BLUEKING-MONITOR) is a monitoring platform product officially launched by BlueKing Cloud. In addition to rich data collection capabilities and large-scale data processing capabilities, it is simple to use and provides more platform extensibility. Built on BlueKing PaaS, and unlike traditional C/S architectures, it forms a closed-loop monitoring capability across the entire BlueKing ecosystem.

Committed to meeting the needs and capabilities of different monitoring scenarios and improving the timeliness, accuracy, and intelligence of monitoring, to safeguard online businesses.
