# DexLab

**Using classical physics laws and established empirical formulas as references, this project studies how contact and friction affect robotic grasping through reproducible experiments, and assesses the credibility of physics simulation.**

We use inclined-plane friction, one-dimensional collision, and gripping experiments to check the forces, motion, and contacts in simulations against analytical solutions, conservation laws, and empirical relations with cited sources. This repository provides **experimental conclusions, reproducible code, and raw data**, analyzes which measurable factors affect grasping performance, and states the conditions under which the conclusions apply.
