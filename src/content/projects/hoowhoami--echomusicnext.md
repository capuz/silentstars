# EchoMusicNext

> Current version is **0.1.0, in development**. Features, platform adaptation, and release deployment are continuously being improved.

---

## ✨ Core Features

- **Desktop experience**: Supports light and dark modes, theme colors, interface scaling, sidebar quick entries, and playback bar layout adjustment.
- **Music recommendations**: Browse the home page, daily recommendations, charts, free listening, and song swiping to discover more music.
- **Multi-dimensional search**: Supports searching songs, lyrics, artists, albums, playlists, and MVs.
- **Personal music library**: Manage self-created and favorited playlists, liked songs, followed artists, and saved albums and videos.
- **Playlist management**: Supports adding and removing songs, setting playlist covers, and adjusting order.
- **Advanced playback**: Provides queue management, playback modes, audio quality selection, volume, progress, playback speed, and sleep timer.
- **Personal FM**: Listen to personalized recommendations based on your preferences, and keep discovering music through playback and feedback.
- **Music cloud drive**: Browse and manage cloud drive songs, and upload local audio files.
