# BigGo Shopping Helper

**Automatic price comparison, price history, and cashback — right on the pages where you shop.**

Traditional Chinese | [English](https://github.com/Funmula-Corp/BigGo-Extension/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

> **BigGo Shopping Helper** is a browser extension for the shopping search engine [BigGo](https://biggo.com), covering Taiwan, Japan, Southeast Asia, and the Americas. It adds automatic price comparison, price history, and cashback directly on the e-commerce sites and search results you already use.
>
> This repository is the **open-source upstream** of this extension.

## Features

- **📉 Price History** — See how prices change over time to judge whether a "sale" is really a good deal, and subscribe to price-drop notifications.
- **💰 Cashback Tracking** — Enable and track cashback when shopping at supported stores.
- **🛍️ Find Similar** — Recommends similar products (with lower prices) while you browse.
- **🌏 Multi-region Support** — Supports multiple markets, with localized store coverage in each region.
- **🏆 Store Featured** — Listed as a "Featured" extension in both the Chrome Web Store and the Microsoft Edge Add-ons store.
