# ✨ Fish It! Rare & Secret Fish Tracker — Rarity and Event Profiles

**Fish It! Rare & Secret Fish Tracker** focuses on rarity filters, keep/sell rules, event profiles, catch tracking, fishing-zone presets, and saved configurations.

This variant is aimed at players who care more about rare catches, secrets, events, and collection progress than general farming.
