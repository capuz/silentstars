# Flame Dragon Knights 2 Reverse Engineering and Remake

## Introduction

This project uses the legal original version of "Flame Dragon Knights 2: The Legend of the Golden City" as a behavior reference, preserving its data formats, interface, audio, and game mechanics from the DOS era, and establishes an editable and extensible clean-room remake engine using Go/Ebiten.

What we are doing is recreating the classic, not trapping players in endless archaeological work. The original campaign remains the baseline for faithful mode; dialogue, events, battles, towns, shops, churches, preparations, teams, and save files gradually move away from hardcoded programs to become data and rules that can later extend new campaigns.

The original program and copyrighted assets such as art, text, and music are not included in this repository. Players must provide their own legal original version; this project does not represent the original developers or rights holders.
