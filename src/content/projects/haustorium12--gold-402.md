# gold-402

> The gold standard for x402 resources. **734 curated entries** — paid endpoints probed for a live 402 before listing, libraries and repos checked for real activity, and the whole shelf re-knocked every night with the result dated. No filler. Entries that stop answering move to [DEPARTURES.md](https://github.com/Haustorium12/gold-402/blob/HEAD/DEPARTURES.md) and keep getting knocked.

gold-402 is small on purpose. A person reads every entry before it goes on the list, and any paid endpoint submitted today has to answer a live 402 before we merge it. We say what we checked and what we didn't.
