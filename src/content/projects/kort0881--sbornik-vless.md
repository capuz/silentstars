# sbornik-vless

**Automatic collector and publisher of subscriptions** for the VLESS, VMESS, TROJAN, SS, Hysteria2, Hysteria, HY2, and TUIC protocols.

The system performs the following tasks: aggregation of source configurations, filtering of non-working nodes, generation of structured subscription files, and their publication to Telegram. The process is fully automated and requires no manual intervention.

> An automated solution for keeping VPN subscriptions up to date.

## Purpose of the repository

This repository is a **showcase of ready-made subscriptions**, not a storage of "raw" keys.
