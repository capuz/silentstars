# sbornik-vless

**Automated collector and publisher of subscriptions** for VLESS, VMESS, TROJAN, SS, Hysteria2, Hysteria, HY2, TUIC protocols.

The system performs the following tasks: aggregation of source configurations, filtering of non-working nodes, formation of structured subscription files, and their publication in Telegram. The process is fully automated and does not require manual intervention.

> Automated solution for maintaining up-to-date VPN subscriptions.
