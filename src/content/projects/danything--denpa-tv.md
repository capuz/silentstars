# denpa TV

An app for watching [denpa](https://github.com/danything/denpa) on Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV (Jetpack Compose for TV).
Recording (resume where you left off, CM skip, time-shifted playback), live TV (channel switching and recording), and switching subtitles and audio are supported.
