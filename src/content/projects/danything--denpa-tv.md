# denpa TV

This is an app for watching [denpa](https://github.com/danything/denpa) on Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV (Jetpack Compose for TV).
It supports recordings (resume playback, CM skip, chase playback), live TV (channel switching, recording), the program guide (reserve, cancel reservation), and switching subtitles and audio.
