## What Is bluesky-social-bot?

bluesky-social-bot is a desktop application for Bluesky Social users who want to automate routine engagement tasks. Rather than manually searching for relevant posts and profiles, the tool handles liking and following in the background, freeing you to focus on content creation. It suits community managers, content creators, and anyone aiming to grow their network efficiently.

The software runs discreetly on Windows, applying keyword filters to locate relevant content and accounts. By automating these repetitive interactions, you maintain a steady presence on the platform while saving hours of manual work.
