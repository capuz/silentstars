## What this project is

30nama does not have a native, up-to-date app for Mac, Windows, Android TV, or Apple TV. This project fills that gap with four apps that work with your own account at [30nama.com](https://30nama.com).

- **Mac and Windows** are built for mouse and keyboard, and also support downloading and playing MKV files. Their look and features are the same.
- **Android TV** and **Apple TV** are built to work with a TV remote. The Android TV version looks the same as the Mac version, sized for a TV.

The Mac app and the Apple TV app are built from the same Swift code. The Windows app has its own code, written in C#, and the Android TV app is written in Kotlin. However, the logic of both is ported from the same core, so any change in one reaches the others.

The starting point was the Tizen client [msharifi99/30nama-tizenos](https://github.com/msharifi99/30nama-tizenos) written by Rebin Sharifi. From it we learned how the 30nama TV API works. The apps themselves were written from scratch with SwiftUI.
