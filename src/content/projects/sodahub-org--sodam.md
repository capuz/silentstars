Linux.do open-source promotion thread · Issue feedback and discussion

SodaM is a **third-party desktop client for Soda Music**, for Linux / macOS / Windows, natively rendered with GPUI, emphasizing **high performance, low resource usage, and high visual appeal**. It focuses on four things: **operation feel close to the official client, stable local playback, lossless audio playback for member accounts, and a clean, adaptive theme system**.

> Unofficial project: developed and maintained by the community, unaffiliated with the official Soda Music; account login and music library still come from Soda Music's official services.
