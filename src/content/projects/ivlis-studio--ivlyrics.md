# ivLyrics

Enjoy music in your own language.

Read lyrics on Spotify PC, sing along with the pronunciation, and check the meaning too. Display the original text, pronunciation, and translation in any combination you like, and customize the look to your taste, from a lyrics-focused screen to video backgrounds and LP mode.
