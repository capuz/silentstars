# <img src="assets/icon.png" width="36" alt=""> HushPine

HushPine is a set of [Morphe](https://morphe.software) patches for PineDrama, TikTok's short drama app. You bring the PineDrama bundle, Morphe Manager patches it on your phone, and the patched app gets a HushPine page in its settings with a switch for most of what it does. Hide ads and clutter, save videos without the watermark, set your own speed and quality, and stop the app reading things it has no need for.

Most switches start off, so a freshly patched PineDrama looks and works the way it ships until you turn something on. Changes are listed in the [changelog](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/HushPine/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md). This repo split off from [Hushfeed](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/hushfeed), which now covers TikTok only.

HushPine v0.0.1 contains 97 patches for PineDrama 47.5.3. New in this one: PineDrama's patches move out of Hushfeed into their own bundle, and pressing and holding any tab at the top of the feed opens HushPine's settings. It needs Morphe Manager 2.3.0 or newer.
