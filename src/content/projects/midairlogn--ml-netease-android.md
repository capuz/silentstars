## ✨ Main Features

### 🎵 Netease Cloud Music Integration

- **Song Search**: Support for searching and playing songs from Netease Cloud Music library. Support searching by name, or directly parse using song ID.
- **Playlists and Details**: View song details, album information and playlists, support adding shortcuts, one-click parsing and playback.
- **Account Support**: Support setting `Music_U` Cookie for login, unlock higher quality audio options.

### 🎧 Music Player

- **Full Control**: Support play, pause, previous track, next track.
- **Playback Modes**: Support list loop, single song loop, random playback, sequential playback.
- **Background Playback**: Support background service running, continue listening when switching out of the app.
- **Batch Download**: Support custom download functionality, can batch download songs.
- **Local Audio Support**: Support auto-scanning local audio files, manually add local files and play directly.
