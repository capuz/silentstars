## Operation Method (60 minutes per session)

- Regular meetings: **Every Sunday at 9:30 PM (21:30)** · 60 minutes per session
- LinkedIn posting is **optional** (freely publish before or after the meeting if you want)

| Time | Content |
|------|------|
| 0–5 min | **Chapter Lead** key summary (concept compression, questions) |
| 5–55 min | **Each person presents "1 key concept"** (10 minutes per person) |
| 55–60 min | **Retrospective · Commit** on the spot |

**Rules**
- Each person selects **different concepts** (30 seconds of lead traffic organization at the start of the session → combined to cover the entire chapter)
- "Meaning" is not a book summary, but written as **application to my work/project** (personal insights are the content)
