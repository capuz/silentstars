# OceanBase Leaderboard Live Dashboard

**Database Competition Overall Leaderboard · seekdb AI Training Camp 11 Tracks — full roster, province filtering, score-release prediction, all on one page**

## ⚡ Powered by OceanBase

The official page only shows the Top 10. This site shows **all teams**. Open it directly, no login required:

- **Live version** <https://board.nerv-base.com/> — new results are pushed to the page automatically
- **Backup version** <https://zzstar101.github.io/oceanbase-live-board/> — if the live version can't connect, it automatically falls back to a snapshot refreshed every 10 minutes (the top bar shows "Static snapshot")
