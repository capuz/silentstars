AI sees the metadata of your 1С configuration and generates accurate BSL code. Works with any MCP-compatible client.

### Works with local models

MCP-1C is not tied to a specific neural network. It works with any MCP-compatible client:

- **Local models** (Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp) - your data does not leave your network
- **Cloud services** (Claude, GPT, YandexGPT, GigaChat) - via the corresponding MCP clients
- **IDEs with AI** (Cursor, VS Code + Continue/Cline, JetBrains)

Your code and 1С data stay with you. MCP-1C is a local process that communicates only with your database.
