# 悦谱 Dolce

**Chinese** | [English](https://github.com/lodebar2026/dolce/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

> An open-source editor, reader and typesetter for jianpu and staff notation: write, read, typeset, audition and export scores. It runs in the browser with no installation, and there are also Windows and macOS desktop versions. The interface is bilingual in Chinese and English.
