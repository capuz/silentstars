## About the Simulation

Counter-Manager is an HTML5 management game set in the competitive CS:GO and CS2 environment. Start with a custom club, build its lineup, place players into a five-role structure, tune their attributes, and lead the organization through matches and important tournament rounds.

A campaign covers more than match results. You will work with a round-by-round economy, monitor a live kill feed, negotiate through the transfer market, oversee club finances, and follow HLTV-inspired global rankings. Browser LocalStorage preserves campaign progress so a management career can be resumed later.
