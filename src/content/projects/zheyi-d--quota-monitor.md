Real-time tracking of Hong Kong Immigration Department **ID card replacement** appointment quotas, with automatic notifications via **Feishu groups + Feishu private messages** when new quotas are released.

---

## 📱 Method 1: Join Feishu Groups (Recommended)

Bot automatically pushes quota updates, **instant message delivery**, join and use immediately, no configuration needed.

> 📖 **[Group Information & How to Join](https://scn7uo58gnuo.feishu.cn/wiki/QSFlwcMBmil7sGkZRBTcAWqwnCf)** (Multi-group solution, automatic load balancing when group is full)

   - 📅 **Subscribe to specific dates** — only follow the day you booked
   - 🔔 **Subscribe to all dates** — get notified for all quota releases
