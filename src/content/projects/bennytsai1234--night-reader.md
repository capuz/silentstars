A novel reader for Android built for long-form fiction: your own book sources, your own typesetting, and readable even offline.

---

Night Reader searches, reads, and downloads web novels from the book sources you import, and can also read local TXT files. Its book source rules are compatible with [Legado (閱讀)](https://github.com/gedoor/legado), reimplemented in Flutter. The app does not bundle any book sources or novel content.

## Features

- **Comfortable typesetting**: Font size, line height, letter spacing, paragraph spacing, and indentation are all adjustable, and Chinese punctuation and mixed Chinese-English text are neatly aligned. There are ten styles (Paper Ink, Bamboo Green, Azurite Blue, Tea Brown, Plain, Rouge, Lotus, Yellow Scroll, Amber, and Frost Night), each paired with light, dark, or system-following modes. The accent color for switches and buttons can also be chosen separately.
- **Automatic offline bookshelf**: Books added to the shelf are automatically downloaded in full, new chapters are fetched when they appear, and books remain readable without a network connection.
