# TangleClaw

**Latest release:** `v6.6.2` — “adds an audited interactive-choice module and per-engine health indicators, improves V6 chat filtering and stability, and hardens scratch-server logging and cleanup” ([release status](https://github.com/Jason-Vaughan/TangleClaw/blob/HEAD/docs/releases/v6.6.2.md) · [📊 **Autonomous Sprint Reports Hub**](https://jason-vaughan.github.io/TangleClaw/reports/) · [`v6.6.2` Sprint Report](https://jason-vaughan.github.io/TangleClaw/reports/v6.6.2/) · [`v5.68.0–v5.71.0` 4-Sprint Kaizen Retrospective](https://jason-vaughan.github.io/TangleClaw/reports/v5.68.0-v5.71.0-retrospective/)).

One AI coding agent is easy. Five are not. They forget what the last session decided, edit the same files, fight over the same ports, and wait silently for an answer you did not know they needed. You end up watching terminals.

TangleClaw is the layer above the agents that deals with that. It runs on your own Mac, starts and supervises each agent session, and keeps the things a session cannot keep for itself.
