# 🎮 MinimalScrcpyGUI - Simple Wireless Android Screen Mirroring


---

MinimalScrcpyGUI is a straightforward app that helps you mirror your Android screen to a Windows PC. It works well with handheld gaming PCs and supports wireless connections. This guide shows you how to get the app running on your Windows system step by step.

## 🔍 What Is MinimalScrcpyGUI?

MinimalScrcpyGUI gives you control over your Android device’s screen on your PC. It is designed to be simple, fast, and easy to use. The app is DPI-aware, which means it looks good on screens with different resolutions. It uses scrcpy, a well-known tool for displaying and controlling Android devices, and adds a clean graphical interface.

You can use it to play games, manage apps, or control your phone without cables. It works over Wi-Fi or USB, so you can pick what fits you best.
