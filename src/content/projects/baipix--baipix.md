# Baipix

**A free online pixel art editor, with the interface of a modern design tool.** Open source, runs in the browser, no account needed, and exports clean SVG you can paste straight into Figma.

**[Open the editor](https://baipix.app/app/)** · [Website](https://baipix.app) · [Watch the video](https://youtu.be/Or_5nmTCQO0)

The sky above is [`docs/sunset.baipix`](https://github.com/baipix/baipix/blob/HEAD/docs/sunset.baipix): open it in Baipix (Open a .baipix file, or drop it on the canvas) to explore its layers.

## Baipix in 30 seconds

## Pixel art that leaves as clean vector

Every color becomes a single SVG path, with neighboring pixels merged: light files, no seams between pixels, and one click to recolor in your design tool. Layers and groups stay apart, named as in Baipix. **Copy as SVG** and paste straight into Figma or Illustrator, pixel gap included.

## Features

- **Drawing tools:** pencil with pixel-perfect mode and a stabilizer, eraser, lasso fill, spray, paint bucket (contiguous or global), a gradient tool (linear, radial, angular, diamond) in palette colors with dithering, eyedropper.
- **Selections:** rectangle, lasso and magic wand; Shift adds, Alt takes…
