# Gan Dialect General Input Method GonnyuGeneralIME | Gan Pinyin GonPin

**Simplified Chinese** | [English](https://github.com/Doohaey/GonnyuGeneralIME/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

> A digital writing scheme for the Gan-Poyang region.

Currently supports: **Nanchang dialect (Lancong), Fenyi (Fenni), Fengcheng (Fungcen), Lichuan (Tiqien), Xinyu (Sinyi), Shanggao (Songau), Susong (Seusong), Ji'an (Jingon), Yiyang-Hengfeng (Yikyan-Henfeng), Jishui Badu (Jisuibaedu), Suichuan (Xyucuênn), Xingzi (Singzï), Gao'an (Goungon)**. More regions are welcome to join, and existing regions are welcome to be expanded!

**A one-click install, easy-to-use, everyday-usable Gan input method. If you know Pinyin, you can use it, and Gan Pinyin is compatible with Mandarin. You don't need to speak Gan to play with it! Thinking in Gan also lets you easily output large passages of Mandarin text (such as this document)!**

**Now available on the iOS App Store ("Gan Input Method"), with quick installation on macOS, Android, Windows, and Linux, plus Rime resource packs for each platform.**
