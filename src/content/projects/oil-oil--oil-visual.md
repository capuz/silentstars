Use the same comic ink-line style to create two kinds of images that can be used directly: one kind is complete explanatory diagrams with short labels that can explain a concept or process on their own; the other is transparent illustration assets that can be placed into presentations, articles, or cards.

Transparent assets can show only the glasses-wearing stick figure, the warm-yellow Border Collie, a combination of both, or only objects such as folders and tools. Characters appear as the composition requires; objects do not need an added person or dog to maintain the style.
