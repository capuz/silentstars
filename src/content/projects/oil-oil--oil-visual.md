Using a unified set of comic ink-line style, create two types of ready-to-use images: one type is complete explanation diagrams with short labels that can independently explain concepts or processes; the other type is transparent illustration materials that can be placed in presentations, articles, or cards.

Transparent materials can feature only a bespectacled stick figure, a warm-yellow Border Collie, a combination of both, or only objects like folders and tools. Characters appear as needed for the composition, and objects don't require adding a person or dog to maintain the style.

## Image Style

- Use clear and bold black ink lines to draw outlines and details.
- Use circular halftone dots to represent gray surfaces and shadows, without using soft gradients.
- A minimalist bespectacled stick figure with round glasses and a chubby warm-yellow Border Collie are optional characters that can appear alone, together, or not at all.
- Objects and decorative elements follow the same set of outlines, ink lines, halftone dots, and restrained color palette, without automatically adding characters.
