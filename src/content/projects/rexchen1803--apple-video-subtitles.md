# Apple Video Subtitles

Project overview:

- Chinese subtitles for Apple TV F1 and film/TV content
- Supports Stash, Shadowrocket, Quantumult X, and Surge

Included content:

- Race replays (official commentary portion) for 13 Grands Prix of the F1 26 season, from Australia through Spain, excluding Italy
- F1: Drive to Survive / DTS Season 8
- Apple TV membership movies: Jurassic World, Black Hawk Down, and 45 other films (47 in total). Availability is determined by Apple TV's actual catalog.
- iTunes Store: Interstellar

---

## Usage Instructions

1. Generate, install, and trust the root certificate on your client. See the [certificate installation guide](https://www.behoss.com/insights/library/2026-09-12-apple-video-subtitles-installation-guide/).
2. Enable MITM (HTTPS decryption) and scripting in your client.
3. Download and enable the module in your client using the URLs below.

### Stash (Override)

```text
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rexchen1803/apple-video-subtitles/main/stash/apple-video-subtitles.stoverride
```
