**Why teams call Grok Imagine Video (`grok-imagine-video`) through APIMart**

- **One key, entire catalog.** The same `https://api.apimart.ai/v1` base URL and `Authorization` header reach Grok Imagine Video (`grok-imagine-video`) and 300+ other image, video and language models — switch the `model` field, not your client.
- **Per-second billing.** A job is charged for the seconds it actually generates at the resolution you picked.
- **The charge comes back in the response.** Every completed task reports the amount billed (`cost` / `credits_cost`); a task marked `failed` is refunded.
- **Async by design.** Submit, take the `task_id`, poll `GET /v1/tasks/{id}` — batching and retries are ordinary queue work, not a bespoke integration.
