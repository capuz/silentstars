## 🌌 A Different Way to Think About Backup

Most backup tools behave like a filing cabinet — they take your files, put them in a drawer, and hope you never need to open it. **Arq Vault Companion 2026** is different. It behaves like a *vigilant night watchman* for your digital estate: it walks the halls of your drive on a schedule you choose, notes what changed, quietly archives the differences, and never asks for attention unless something genuinely requires it.

This repository is the community-facing companion project to the **Arq-Backup-2026** family — a Windows 11 & 10 oriented cloud backup toolkit built around the idea that resilience should be effortless, transparent, and entirely under your own control. If your data is the estate, this project is the caretaker.

Whether you are safeguarding a decade of family photographs, a busy freelance workstation, or a small studio's project archive, this companion smooths the journey from installation to first successful cloud upload — and then stays out of your way.
