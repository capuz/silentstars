## About

Most podcast apps call an open API, do word‑for‑word search, show Apple charts, parse the RSS feed to play audio and call it a day. Misspell a show name and you get nothing. Ask for a *topic* and you get title matches that miss the point. And once you’re in, the home screen rarely feels like *yours* — same rails for everyone, little that learns from what you actually play.

**boxlore** is built around both gaps. Find a show when the spelling is messy, or find shows and episodes by *idea* when you only know the vibe. Then the app gets personal as you listen: Home, Explore, queue, and downloads re‑rank on‑device from your taste and fetches content that actually matters to you from our vector db using semantic matching — daypart rails, **Because You Like**, and **For You**…
