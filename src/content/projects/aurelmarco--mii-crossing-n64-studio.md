# 🌿 Animal Crossing: New Horizons PC Edition – *The Island Sanctuary Project* 🏝️

## 🎮 A New Horizon for Desktop Island Living

Welcome to the *Island Sanctuary Project*—a reimagined, self-contained PC experience that respects the spirit of **Animal Crossing: New Horizons** while unlocking its full potential on desktop environments. This is not an emulator wrapper, nor a direct port. It is a **purpose-built companion application** that bridges the warmth of Nintendo’s beloved life simulation with the flexibility of modern PC architecture.

Inspired by the desire to **preserve, personalize, and project** island life beyond the Switch ecosystem, this repository provides a stable, feature-rich desktop client for enthusiasts who want to manage their island, share Mii data, and interact with the game’s ecosystem from any Windows, macOS, or Linux machine—without compromising the original’s charming aesthetic.

**Designed for 2026 and beyond**, this project leverages responsive UI principles, multilingual support, and optional cloud integration to create a seamless bridge between your Switch (original, OLED, or Switch 2) and your daily computing environment.
