# muse·tv

**A local short-drama and short-video workbench: manage episodes, characters, and scenes within a project, with materials and finished videos staying on this machine.**

muse·tv is a macOS desktop app. A short-drama project can have more than ten episodes, dozens of characters, and several scenes. Each has its own library, and they are not mixed together. For simple introductory short videos, use a “Short Video” project.

[Download macOS Apple Silicon version](https://github.com/museav-ai/muse-tv/releases/download/app-v1.9.0/muse-tv-1.9.0-darwin-arm64.zip) · [Release notes](https://github.com/museav-ai/muse-tv/releases/tag/app-v1.9.0) · [Website](https://museav-ai.github.io/muse-tv/)
