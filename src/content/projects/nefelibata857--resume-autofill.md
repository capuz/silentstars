# Resume Autofill Helper · Resume Autofill

> Enter your resume information once; afterwards, click a browser bookmark on any corporate online application page and the entire form fills in automatically.

**Use online (no download or installation required)**: <https://nefelibata857.github.io/resume-autofill/>

---

## Three ways to use it, pick what fits

| What you want | How |
|---|---|
| **Use online** (recommended, up and running in one minute) | Open the URL above, enter your information → click 'Install form-fill bookmark' → press Ctrl+D to save it as a bookmark |
| **Download and use locally** (don't want to rely on the website / want offline use) | [Click here to download the whole project](https://github.com/Nefelibata857/resume-autofill/archive/refs/heads/main.zip), unzip, then double-click `index.html` |
| **Customize** (adapt to your industry and your fields) | Fork the repository or download and modify `index.html`; see the [modification guide](https://github.com/Nefelibata857/resume-autofill/blob/HEAD/修改指南.md) |

> The downloaded version has full functionality: resume recognition, bookmark generation, and data self-check all work.
