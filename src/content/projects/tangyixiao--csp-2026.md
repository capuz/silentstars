Archives the first-round papers and reference materials, and the second-round problem statements and contestant files for CSP-J/S 2026, for post-contest reading, comparison, and study. Contestant files are organized by region, division, contestant number, and problem, and can be viewed online or downloaded as needed.

## Second-Round Results and Ratings

CCF published the second-round rating rules and lists on November 13. The [results and ratings page](https://tangyixiao.github.io/CSP-2026/ratings.html) summarizes the first-tier score cutoffs, first-tier counts, starting points for second and third tiers, six official lists, individual lookup entries, and appeal deadlines for 32 regions, and provides CSV downloads.
