# video-recreation

From local videos or share links, extract keyframes, break down shots and actions, write complete prompts, and recreate a video, redo parts of it, or re-edit it as needed.

**Get reference → visual and audio review → action timeline → prompts and production → comparison acceptance**

The merged version can optionally connect to [rea-video](https://github.com/JuneYaooo/rea-video) to deepen analysis of production sources, subtitles/stickers, picture-in-picture, materials, and audio. It also keeps the download, dance extraction, camera movement selection, generation resume, and media acceptance workflows.
