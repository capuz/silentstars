# Modeling Workbench

Simplified Chinese | [English](https://github.com/Lucasuiii/modeling-workbench/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

A set of AI agent workflows based on the National University Student Mathematical Modeling Contest: starting from official materials, connecting modeling, computation, verification, papers, and final delivery into a traceable, auditable chain of evidence. Skill also provides question-type guidance, self-selected topics, and Chinese-English writing methods for other modeling competitions.

**Fixed Official Materials → Problem Decomposition → Candidate Model Trial Calculation → Formal Computation → Independent Verification → LaTeX Paper → PDF QA and Delivery**

It does not provide ready-made answers, nor does it judge whether your model is correct for you; it ensures that **every conclusion written into the paper can be traced back to the official problem and a real computation that actually occurred**, and returns decisions to you at three critical decision points: model selection, conclusions before writing the paper, and final delivery.

Supports **Codex** and **Claude Code**. Current version **v0.6**; not backward compatible with older workspace versions.…
