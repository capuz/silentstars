# 🌊 MorseWave Nexus — Adaptive CW Trainer for the Next Generation

**Turn silent dots and dashes into fluent on-air rhythm — Koch discipline, Farnsworth pacing, and a dark, focused workspace built for 2026 and beyond.**

MorseWave Nexus is a standalone, browser-based Morse code (CW) training environment that walks a newcomer from their first hesitant “dit” all the way to confident, on-air conversational speeds. It borrows the time-tested Koch method as its backbone, layers Farnsworth spacing on top, and wraps everything in a calm, dark interface that keeps your eyes and ears where they belong: on the signal.

Where the original MorseWave introduced a scrappy, no-frills trainer, **MorseWave Nexus** reimagines the whole journey. It is not merely a practice app — it is a *dojo*, a *gymnasium for the ear*, and a *lighthouse* for anyone navigating the noisy waters of radio communication.
