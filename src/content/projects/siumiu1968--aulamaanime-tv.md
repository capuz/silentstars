**Traditional Chinese** · [Cantonese](https://github.com/siumiu1968/AulamaAnime_TV/blob/HEAD/README.yue-Hant.md) · [Simplified Chinese](https://github.com/siumiu1968/AulamaAnime_TV/blob/HEAD/README.zh-CN.md)

**Aulama Anime TV** is a free-to-download **Android TV/Google TV Chinese anime app** designed specifically for remote controls and large living room screens. It supports Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese name search, anime schedules, new episode collection alerts, multiple playback sources, cross-device viewing history, and one-click opening/ending theme skipping. From searching for anime, tracking series, selecting episodes, to resuming playback—all can be completed via D-pad.

> **Skip opening and ending themes, enjoy a Netflix-style intuitive experience.** Aulama Anime TV exclusively integrates opening and ending theme timing data, displaying a one-click skip button at appropriate moments; long-pressing the remote **OK/Confirm button** allows temporary **2×** playback, immediately returning to normal speed upon release.
