## Why do you need it?

| Pain point | AI Grading Assistant |
|------|------------|
| Each subjective question takes 2~3 minutes to grade | **AI auto-scoring**, with score + comments |
| Eyes ache from grading until midnight | **Unattended mode**: grades automatically while you're away, so you can just go to sleep |
| Hard to keep grading standards consistent | **Custom grading criteria**: AI scores everything with the same yardstick |
| Manually entering scores and clicking submit for every question | **Fully automatic**: recognize → score → fill in → submit → next paper |
| Want to look back at a paper after grading | **Grading history**: review with one click, export to HTML/CSV/JSON/material package ZIP |
