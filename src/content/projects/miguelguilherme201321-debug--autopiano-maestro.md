# 🎹 AeroKey Virtuoso — Autonomous Performance Engine for Browser-Based Pianos

> **AeroKey Virtuoso** is a next-generation, browser-native performance companion that turns ordinary sheet music into a choreographed symphony of automated keystrokes. Born from the same lineage of curiosity that inspired earlier virtual-piano automation experiments, AeroKey Virtuoso reimagines the entire concept from the ground up — a conductor's baton made of code, a metronome that never tires, a stagehand that never misses a cue.
