## About The Project

I felt the lack of an open-source app for the Jewish library, for computers.

Torat Emet is old and no longer maintained, and the Sefaria app is great, but it does not work well on computers.

So I decided to build one myself. At first I did not know Dart and Flutter at all, but it was fun. I **love** learning new technologies!

The database itself is accessible to everyone thanks to the important work of the Sefaria organization, so a big thank you to them for that.

* The software is free and will remain free forever.
