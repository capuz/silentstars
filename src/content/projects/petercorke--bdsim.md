## Synopsis

**bdsim** bridges the gap between mathematical block diagrams and executable Python code. Unlike traditional graphical-only tools, it treats modelling as code, allowing you to define, simulate, and analyze continuous-time, discrete-time, or hybrid systems within a modern software engineering workflow.
