## What It Does

DouMiningMachine adds three automated machines to Minecraft Fabric servers and modpacks, letting players hand off repetitive digging tasks. The mod includes a mining machine, a stone digger, and a dirt excavator — each built to clear or harvest specific block types inside a player-defined area. All three run on coal fuel and operate across a configurable radius from 1 to 100 blocks, with speed settings adjustable from 1x to 10x.

This version refines machine behavior and expands filter options. The mining machine now includes a filtration slot that can target ores and various other materials.
