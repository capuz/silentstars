Codalus IDE is an open-source (MIT), cross-platform IDE for .NET that runs natively on Windows, macOS and Linux: a rich semantic editor, a real project system, and integrated build, debugging and Git.

**Website: [codalus.dev](https://codalus.dev/)**: [download](https://codalus.dev/download/), [features](https://codalus.dev/features/) and the [user guide](https://codalus.dev/docs/).

> **Status: pre-release (0.x).** The MVP (milestones M0–M7) is done, and the post-MVP milestones are under way ([roadmap](https://github.com/stavroskasidis/codalus-ide/blob/HEAD/docs/roadmap.md#status)). Installers for Windows, macOS and Linux are on the [download page](https://codalus.dev/download/) and the [releases page](https://github.com/stavroskasidis/codalus-ide/releases): stable releases come from `master`, and nightly builds follow `develop` ([installing](https://github.com/stavroskasidis/codalus-ide/blob/HEAD/docs/user-guide/getting-started.md)). The UI stack is Avalonia ([ADR 0001](https://github.com/stavroskasidis/codalus-ide/blob/HEAD/docs/adr/0001-ui-stack.md)).
