# Options Monitor (OM)

**On your own computer, review options candidates, account cash, and position risk.**

OM is for options investors who make their own trading decisions. It supports Futu accounts and Longbridge stock and cash connections. It combines market data, account cash, stock holdings, and the strategy conditions you set to screen cash-secured put (CSP) and covered call (CC) candidates, and it provides position, options cash flow, and risk reports.

**OM does not place orders for you, and does not guarantee returns.** Scans connect to external data sources and save results locally. When required data is missing, OM clearly indicates that data is unavailable or results are incomplete, and does not fill the gaps with guessed values.

On first use, it is recommended to complete one scan of a real account on a Mac. Notifications, Bot Q&A, and background scheduled runs can be configured later.
