## <a id="introduction"></a>💡 Introduction

**UniPhysGen** is a unified physical grounding model designed to process segmented 3D objects with heterogeneous part decompositions and generate structured physical semantics for simulation-ready assets. These outputs include part-level physical semantics and intrinsic properties, articulation kinematics and structure, and object-level scale and mass.
