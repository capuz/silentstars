## What It Does

This resource offers a straightforward method to present a custom logo or watermark on a player's screen while they are connected to a FiveM server operating the QB-Core framework. The watermark remains constantly visible, reinforcing server identity without disrupting normal gameplay. When a player gets into a vehicle, the overlay gradually moves to the top-right corner, keeping the logo accessible yet unobtrusive.
