# Lantern for Claude Code

A Mac app for the `claude` CLI you already have: see what Claude is doing as cards and diffs, and steer it one reviewable step at a time. **[See all features →](https://github.com/alyakan/lantern/blob/HEAD/docs/FEATURES.md)**

## What it does

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **The Desk: every chat at a glance** | One board for all your projects: what needs you now (with Allow and Deny right there), each project's chats, branch, release and to-dos, and Your work: where each of your pull requests and unpushed branches stands, as colour-coded nodes from Local to Release, with the next step a click away. The sidebar's Timeline lists what happened in every chat as it happened, unread answers marked. Ask across projects how something stands, or say what to do and it goes to the right chat. |
| **On your phone** | An iPhone and iPad app pairs with your Mac (on your Wi-Fi, or anywhere over Tailscale): your chats, Claude's questions to swipe to allow, messages, a lock-screen card of what is working, and notifications when a chat needs you. See [docs/MOBILE.md](https://github.com/alyakan/lantern/blob/HEAD/docs/MOBILE.md). |
