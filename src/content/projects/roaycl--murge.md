# Murge

**Another [Mihomo](https://github.com/MetaCubeX/mihomo) GUI**

Murge is a modern Mihomo graphical client built on Electron + Vue 3, focused on providing a clean and efficient proxy management experience.

## Recent Project Status and Feedback

Murge currently meets my personal daily needs, and I will keep improving it based on real-world issues and everyone's feedback. If you find a bug or want a new feature, feel free to submit an [Issues](https://github.com/RoaycL/Murge/issues). Codex and Claude will look into it together right away to investigate problems, evaluate requirements, and move development forward.

When reporting a bug, please include the app version, reproduction steps, and relevant logs or screenshots if possible. When proposing a new feature, please also describe the use case and expected behavior so we can understand and handle it more quickly.

You're welcome to join the [Telegram group](https://t.me/murgeapp) to share your experience, exchange configurations, and discuss new features. For bugs and feature requests, please submit them to Issues as well to make tracking progress easier.
