# Virtual World SOLIS

A state machine that gives a Bot a space, a daily routine, and memory.

## What it is

SOLIS is a plugin running on AstrBot that takes over replies in whitelisted sessions. She is not a responder with a new set of lines. The world clock keeps running. She has her own map and location, her own schedule and routine, a body state and mood that change over time, and memory archived by person. When nobody is talking to her, her life goes on as usual. When you speak, her reply is based on "where she is right now, what she is doing, and what is on her mind."

The plugin is simple to install, adds no extra Python dependencies, and automatically generates a ready-to-use world configuration on first launch.

Everything she does leaves a record, shown in the editor as a readable timeline. What she wanted to do, why she chose to speak at this moment, and why she stayed silent can all be reviewed afterward.
