共工空间 is a self-hosted AI collaboration platform for small teams. Each member connects their own machine, and Claude Code / Codex on that machine becomes a **Bot** that anyone in the group can @. People and Bots discuss, assign tasks, and hand off work in the same group. The process, changes, and results are visible to everyone.

## Why we built it

- **Context scattered across individual terminals**: You had Claude change an interface, but a teammate's Codex doesn't know about it. In the group, even messages that don't @ a Bot are passed to it as context, so everyone shares the same background.
- **Machines sitting idle**: Your work machine has the repository, dependencies, and a logged-in Agent, but teammates can't use them. Once bound, group members can @ your Bot directly and work on your machine.
