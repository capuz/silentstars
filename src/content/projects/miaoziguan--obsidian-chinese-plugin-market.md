# 🀄 Chinese Market —— An Obsidian Plugin Marketplace That Chinese Users Can Actually Use

> No more being discouraged by screens full of English plugin names. Enter a phrase, and quality plugins from the Chinese region appear instantly.

---

## 🔥 Why Do You Need It?

You open the Obsidian community plugin marketplace and are faced with **over five thousand** English-named plugins——`dataview`, `templater-obsidian`, `obsidian-linter`…… names you can't understand, descriptions you can't finish reading, and Chinese users looking for a "mind map" plugin need to spend half an hour translating.

**This is unreasonable.**

Chinese Market turns this around completely: **search in Chinese, view in Chinese, install smoothly**. It is an Obsidian community plugin search and translation tool built specifically for Chinese users——not just another shell of an English marketplace, but a real tool that breaks down the "language barrier."
