<h1>Hive</h1>

  <p>A 100% Swift/SwiftUI native iOS client for Buzz, built for human-agent collaboration.</p>

  <p>
  </p>
</div>

Hive is a native iOS client for [Buzz](https://github.com/block/buzz), the Nostr-based messaging platform for human-agent collaboration. It is a usable daily driver and pre-1.0: you can pair with Buzz Desktop, read and send in channels, DMs and threads, react, mention people and agents, and reach your conversations from Siri and Spotlight. Several upstream features are not built yet; [Features & support](#features--support) is the honest list, and [PARITY.md](https://github.com/jtvargas/buzz-ios-client/blob/HEAD/PARITY.md) tracks the v0.4.11 milestone.
