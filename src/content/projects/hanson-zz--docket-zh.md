# 码头 Dockling

### A free-to-download macOS Dock shelf with an upgradable Pro tier, featuring a red panda desktop pet and a Simplified Chinese interface.

**Website: [docket.shiduo.app](https://docket.shiduo.app)** · Simplified Chinese | [English](https://github.com/hanson-zz/docket-zh/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

---

## What Dockling Can Do

- **Shelf**: Attaches next to the system Dock, holding live widgets, app groups, and folders. It can also replace the Dock entirely.
- **60+ widgets**: Calendar, to-do, focus timer, weather, radio, clipboard, screenshots, “Suan” (unit conversion + exchange rates + calculator), and more. Each opens with a detail panel.
- **Red panda desktop pet**: Lives on the Dock, temporarily holds files for you, fetches newly downloaded files, and reminds you to get up and move around.
- **Window and app switching**: A ⌘Tab-style app switcher, three window switcher styles, and full-size previews.
- **Message Dynamic Island**: Messages from apps like WeChat pop out from the notch.
- **Multiple layouts**: Switch via keyboard shortcut, Focus mode, or ⌘ + scroll wheel. Layouts can also be written back to the system Dock.
