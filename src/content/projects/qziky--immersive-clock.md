## 💡 Project Overview

沉浸式时钟·Immersive Clock is a full-screen time dashboard designed for classroom projection, study spaces, and personal desktops. It keeps time and the information you need at the moment always clear at a glance, in a way that is clean, quiet, and suitable for long periods of display.

## ✨ Core Features at a Glance

Four time modes, study information, environment reminders, and appearance customization are organized within the same low-distraction interface, covering everyday time checking, activity timing, and long-duration focus display.
