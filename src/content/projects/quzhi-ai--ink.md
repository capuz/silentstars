**Ink scans every AI tool on your machine, bills your real token usage at API list prices, and turns the result into a card worth sharing.** $400/month subscription, $6,177 at list prices, a 66× return — numbers like that deserve a beautiful card. Each one is unique: different format, different quote, different visual concept. Export as high-res PNG, ready for Twitter/X, WeChat Moments, Instagram, or anywhere you want to show off your AI life.

No Figma. No templates. No made-up numbers. Just one command.

```
npx skills add quzhi-ai/ink
```
