# Jiuji · Myriad Trees and Night Shadows

Current version: **4.2.0 · Myriad Trees and Night Shadows**. Twelve tree species now have distinct bark, canopy, and seasonal appearances. The character gains elbow and knee joint animation, and five categories of night creatures are connected to procedural models and state feedback. Old save files automatically gain the new tree species. Gathering, planting, and the Level 1–9 growth rules are unchanged.

[Domestic trial](https://www.daguojueqi.site/jiuji/) · [Official site and downloads](https://www.daguojueqi.site/jiuji/official/) · [4.2 release notes](https://github.com/chencheng666/jiuji/blob/HEAD/docs/releases/4.2.0.md). The status of the web version, offline packages, and desktop releases depends on each version and the actual downloaded files.

[Twelve-Tree Observatory](https://www.daguojueqi.site/jiuji/forest-observatory.html) · [Night Shadow Observatory](https://www.daguojueqi.site/jiuji/night-observatory.html). The observatories use the game renderer and demo state. They do not read or write player saves.
