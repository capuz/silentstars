# MusicOne

*An Android music player focused on unified visuals, smooth experience, and intuitive motion effects*

[📥 Download installation package](https://github.com/HGODLK/MusicOne/releases) • [📡 Multi-source integration](#-多音源整合) • [📑 Playlist system](#-歌单体系) • [🎼 Audio format support](#-音频格式支持)

---

MusicOne is an Android music player built with Jetpack Compose, supporting Android 6.0 and above.

It forgoes cluttered social modules and ad promotion, focusing on smooth interaction gestures, phone/tablet adaptive layouts, local lossless decoding, and clean audio output.

> **Tip**: The app has a built-in Profile performance configuration. On first launch or after an update, the system may briefly heat up or drop frames while background optimization runs. Wait a moment and it will recover.

## 📡 Multi-source integration

MusicOne follows the design philosophy of "sources as capability." With playback capability at its core, sources are divided into **primary source** and **supplementary sources**:

- **Primary source**: Select one platform as the primary source, which provides the core home recommendations, featured playlists, search and discovery, and personal favorites…
