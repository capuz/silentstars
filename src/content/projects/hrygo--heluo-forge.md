# Heluo (河洛) · Heluo Forge

Heluo Forge (河洛) is designed for enterprise employees and combines an **enterprise-grade intelligent R&D platform** with an **intelligent personal assistant**. Within a personal work environment hosted by the DeepSeek Harness (DSH), it connects requirements, code, and delivery, and also links email, communication, office tools, and professional collaboration.

By extending existing capabilities, Heluo reuses the enterprise's current identity, model, and business systems, so the personal work experience aligns with enterprise permissions, data, and governance requirements. Each employee uses their own work environment, and every capability follows the permissions of the actual authorized user and connected systems.
