# Feathrise (羽立)

**Simplified Chinese** · [English](https://github.com/feathrise/feathrise/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

**A high-performance Bevy 3D editor for large scenes and rapid iteration.**

Feathrise (羽立) brings scene production, project preview, and Rust development into one open-source workbench, aimed at developers and content creators building 3D projects with Bevy.

[**Download Windows preview**](https://github.com/feathrise/feathrise/releases) · [Quick start](#快速开始) · [Bevy version](#bevy-版本对应表) · [User guide](https://github.com/feathrise/feathrise/blob/HEAD/docs/使用说明.md)

## Story Presentation

Arrange camera positions, character and prop actions, subtitles, and Yarn dialogue on a timeline. Project rules can trigger real-time cutscenes, and the same assets can be used to export H.264 promotional videos. [Story presentation and video export](https://github.com/feathrise/feathrise/blob/HEAD/docs/剧情演出与视频导出.md)
