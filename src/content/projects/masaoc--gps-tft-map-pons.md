# PONS — GPS/GNSS navigation device for human-powered aircraft for the Japan International Birdman Rally v7.1

**PONS (Pilot Oriented Navigation System)** is a navigation device designed to be easy for human-powered aircraft pilots to use. It is specialized for the Birdman Rally on Lake Biwa.  
As the electronics of a Birdman team (pilot instruments and GPS navigation), it conveys the map, ground speed, heading, attitude, variometer (vertical speed indicator), and wind direction and speed through a single screen and voice.
The PCB (KiCad), firmware (Arduino), and 3D-printed case data are published as open source.
