# MBrain

**Enable AI Agents to call your Android phone capabilities.**

Device Information · App Management · File Operations · Root / Shizuku · MCP Service Aggregation

[Quick Start](#quick-start) · [Connect Agent](#connect-agent) · [Integrate MCP Services](#integrate-mcp-services) · [FAQ](#faq) · [Report Issues](https://github.com/powercess/mbrain/issues)

---

MBrain is an MCP gateway running on Android. It aggregates your phone's native capabilities and integrated MCP services into a single endpoint, allowing MCP-compatible AI clients to read device status, manage applications, operate files, or execute commands. You can choose which capabilities to expose on your phone and stop the service at any time.
