## 🎯 What is Aniimo-Master-Hack?

**Aniimo-Master-Hack** is a complete external hack tool for **Aniimo** — the free-to-play creature-collector open-world RPG from Pawprint Studio and FunPlus . It provides instant taming, unlimited capture orbs, max bond levels, infinite resources, super damage, no aggro, fly mode, and more. Dominate every creature encounter.

Aniimo features a unique **Twining** mechanic — you fully synchronize with your active Aniimo, gaining its complete movement options and skill set . This hack helps you unlock every Aniimo's full potential from the start.

The hack runs as an **external process** — no DLL injection, no memory modification inside the game process. This makes it safer than internal cheats while still delivering all the features you need.
