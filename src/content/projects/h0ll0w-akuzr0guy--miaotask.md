**把重复浏览，交给助手。**

A local web page that manages repetitive tasks on phones, tablets, and emulators.

[下载使用](#开始使用) · [功能与界面](#功能与界面) · [参与开发](https://github.com/h0ll0w-AkuZr0guY/MiaoTask/blob/HEAD/docs/CONTRIBUTING.md) · [发布记录](https://github.com/h0ll0w-AkuZr0guY/MiaoTask/blob/HEAD/docs/RELEASES.md)

After connecting a device, you can enter an activity with one click, read tasks, browse or search, and then return to verify progress. **Taobao's Double 11 energy earning, Taojinbi daily tasks, Babafarm, red packet check-in, and the achievement center are each treated as separate activities**, selected and ordered as needed. Alipay 'Monthly Earn' browsing tasks are also supported. Initial setup for optional in-person check-ins is handled by the user. Device connection, screen reading, scheduling, and logging are separated into layers, and screenshots and OCR are processed locally on the computer.
