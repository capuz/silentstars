# 🛰️ Revenant Vault — The Digital Archaeologist for Lost Bytes

**Because deleted does not mean gone.** Every storage medium is a palimpsest — a surface written over, time and again, each layer whispering the faint echo of what once lived there. Revenant Vault is an open-source investigation toolkit for people who refuse to accept that a misplaced file is a farewell letter. Instead of a blunt instrument, think of it as an excavation site: patient, methodical, and built for the curious.

Where conventional utilities hand you a progress bar and wish you luck, Revenant Vault hands you a map. It scans, catalogs, and reconstructs the faint residues that operating systems leave behind after a deletion. It doesn't promise miracles. It promises diligence.
