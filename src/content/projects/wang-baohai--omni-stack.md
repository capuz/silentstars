# Omni-Stack

A microservice scaffold based on Spring Boot 4 + Vue 3 that helps teams build enterprise back-office systems with tenant isolation, permission control, and business extensibility. Source code, project facts, illustrated tutorials, and AI collaboration guidelines are maintained in the same repository.

[English](https://github.com/wang-baohai/Omni-Stack/blob/HEAD/README.en.md) · [Japanese](https://github.com/wang-baohai/Omni-Stack/blob/HEAD/README.jp.md) · [Korean](https://github.com/wang-baohai/Omni-Stack/blob/HEAD/README.kr.md)

## Key Highlights

- Read the Wiki directly on the official site; the personal workbench centralizes authorized applications, and business modules open in new tabs.
- Multi-tenancy, feature permissions, and data scope; captcha login, social login, and OAuth2 authorization.

- The independent Workflow service supports BPMN modeling, publishing, approval, and countersignature.

- XXL-JOB uniformly manages system tasks and personal tasks.

- CRM covers leads, customers, opportunities, and follow-ups.

- SRM → Procurement → Asset links supplier, procurement, and asset collaboration.
