# Waterfall · ICT.RUN Photography

Photography works site for ICT.RUN, online at **[p.zli.li](https://p.zli.li)**. The same works can be viewed in two ways:

- **GNOMON** (site homepage): a circular 3D gallery. Walking one loop takes you from early morning to late night, and the light in the corridor changes with the time.
- **Classic** ([/classic/](https://p.zli.li/classic/)): the original theme masonry waterfall layout, with an EXIF watermark tool. When a device can't handle 3D, it automatically falls back to this view.

## GNOMON

> A day of light, in one loop.

GNOMON is the needle on a sundial that casts the shadow. The works are divided into five time periods based on the light in the picture (First Light, Rush Hour, High Noon, Golden Hour, Night Shift), hung along the outer and inner walls of the circular corridor.
