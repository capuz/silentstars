# 🦾 Embodied-SuperMimic

### Let robotic arms learn in a world model, and verify in simulation.

## 🌐 [Open the online experiment showcase](https://fbddcz.github.io/Embodied-SuperMimic/)

**🎬 Watch the arm execute · 🔎 Switch embodiments and policies · 🧠 Learn about the online world model · 📖 Check results and protocol**

No installation needed, just open it and watch. Select Panda, ARX5, PiPER, UR5e, or xArm7, pause, drag the progress bar, and observe motions and contacts.

[🎥 Watch recordings](https://fbddcz.github.io/Embodied-SuperMimic/#gallery) · [🚀 Run locally](#quickstart) · [📊 Experiment results](#results) · [🧪 Reproduction notes](https://github.com/FBddcz/Embodied-SuperMimic/blob/HEAD/docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md)

**An independent robot manipulation research project.** Starting from each robot's own task experts, it explores how online world models and policies can improve each other.

- **Real saved simulation recordings**: hanging a mug and placing a lemon; five robots and eight policy demonstrations, filterable by embodiment and task with one click.
