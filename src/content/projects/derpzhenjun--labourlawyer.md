# LabourLawyer

**Choose a region, fill in information, generate labour arbitration documents.**

A document and procedure assistant for ordinary workers. Fill in the arbitration application, organize the evidence list, then check local official material requirements and submission methods. No need to create a case or learn a management system first.

> This platform is a labour arbitration assistance tool, **not an official government service website**. Documents are not guaranteed to be accepted; jurisdiction, materials, and acceptance conditions are subject to the latest judgment of the local arbitration institution.
