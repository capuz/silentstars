# LabourLawyer

**Choose a region, fill in information, and generate labor arbitration documents.**

A document and procedure assistant for ordinary workers. Fill in the arbitration application, organize the evidence list, then check the local official material requirements and submission methods. No need to create a case or learn a case management system first.

> This platform is a labor arbitration assistance tool, **not an official government service website**. Documents are not guaranteed to be accepted. Jurisdiction, materials, and acceptance conditions are subject to the latest determination of the local arbitration institution.

## From filling in to submission

1. Select the province, city, district/county, or functional zone. The handling region must be confirmed based on factors such as place of work and the employer's location. Do not select based only on household registration (hukou).
2. Choose an application or an evidence list. The form provides clearly labeled fictitious examples. Replace them with your own information before formal use.
