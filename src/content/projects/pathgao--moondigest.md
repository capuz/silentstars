# MoonDigest · Moon Team Video Digest

Videos you saved but never watched, and web pages you meant to read: let AI read them for you, organize them, and pick out the ones really worth your time.

Videos and web pages you've saved, MoonDigest helps you read through with AI and organize. Your data stays in your browser; nothing goes to the author's servers.

## Quick Start

1. Install from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/bnjihpbocpimaipifanfkjjpfdbigggd).
2. Settings → 'AI Platforms' → '+ Add platform', choose a preset, enter your API key, and click 'Test connection'. When the browser asks whether it may access this address, click Allow. Settings save automatically when changed; there is no Save button. Supports DeepSeek, Zhipu GLM, Kimi, MiniMax, OpenRouter, and any OpenAI-compatible API. You can add several and switch in the sidebar under 'Select AI platform'; the triage desk's AI also uses the platform selected here.
3. Log in to Bilibili. Bilibili only shows subtitles (including AI subtitles) to logged-in users, and the triage desk also requires login to read your favorites folders.
