# 🦌 99 Nights in the Forest Deer & Kids Radar — Rescue Tracker

**99 Nights in the Forest Deer & Kids Radar** focuses on threat and rescue information: The Deer, missing-kid markers, players, night status, distance filters, map range, and rescue-oriented profiles.

This variant is designed around the most recognizable parts of the actual game rather than generic Roblox survival terminology.
