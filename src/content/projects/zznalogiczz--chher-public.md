## Reading and Discussion

Read reviews, go through long threads, follow replies, and share your own experience. Chher organizes its interface around these everyday actions, keeping content and ongoing discussions in view.

### Long posts, read continuously

Read the body text continuously, tap images to view them, follow links to the site directly, and play Bilibili videos within the app. Portal reviews and forum posts can both be browsed in familiar ways.

### Replies, follow the conversation

Comments can be grouped by quote relationships, you can switch back to the original floor, or view only the original poster's posts. Replies within a thread expand in place, quotes support jumping, and long discussions are easier to follow.

### From reading to participating

Replies, new posts, article comments, and private messages are all completed within the app. Image attachments support compressed uploads, and edits are kept as drafts. When a captcha appears, you can switch to the original site's web page to continue.
