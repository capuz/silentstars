# TWIN AI

A Windows conversation studio that brings your background, voice, and avatar together. Ask a question through a microphone or by selecting text. Get a written answer, a spoken reply, or a lip-synced avatar on your virtual camera.

**[Download for Windows](https://twinaistudio.com/download/) · [Watch the one-minute film](https://twinaistudio.com/media/launch-film.mp4) · [Quick start](#quick-start)**

## See a conversation in action

How does a store keep two shoppers from buying the last item? In this demo, a fictional developer selects the interview question, reads the reply, and answers a follow-up on camera.

**Installing the Windows preview** — Choose the setup wizard or extract the portable ZIP. The installer uses a self-signed preview certificate, so Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning. Models download separately.
