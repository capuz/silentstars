# Gothic AI

**Gothic II: Night of the Raven, where the residents hear you, answer in their own voices, do what they agreed to, and remember. And you can also lie to them, and the lie works until it is exposed.**

This is the original game on the [OpenGothic](https://github.com/Try/OpenGothic) engine with our changes. Language models stand behind the characters. You hold a key and speak, and the one you addressed hears you, answers aloud, acts, and remembers the conversation in your save. Over the course of the game the director writes new quests. Combat and physics have been reworked, and the world is gradually being remade.

[Site and development diary](https://kaifaty.github.io/GothicAI/) — in Russian and English.

> **Status: early experiment.** The project runs from source on macOS. The Windows build is produced by GitHub, but it has not yet been verified on a real computer. You need your own copy of the Russian Gothic II Gold (with Night of the Raven).
