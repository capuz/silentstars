# Gothic AI

**Gothic II: Night of the Raven, where the locals hear you, answer in their own voices, do what they agreed to, and remember. And you can lie to them, and the lie works until it's exposed.**

This is the original game on the [OpenGothic](https://github.com/Try/OpenGothic) engine with our changes. Language models stand behind the characters. You hold a key and speak, and the one you addressed hears you, answers aloud, acts, and remembers the conversation in your save. Over the course of the game the director writes new quests. Combat and physics are reworked, and the world is gradually being rebuilt.

[Site and dev diary](https://kaifaty.github.io/GothicAI/) — in Russian and English.

> **Status: early experiment.** The project runs from source on macOS. GitHub builds the Windows version, but it hasn't been verified on a real computer yet. You need your own copy of the Russian Gothic II Gold (with "Night of the Raven").
