# MOCKA
MOCKA Modula-2 Compiler System, originally by GMD

Follows the 3rd edition of Wirth's *Programming in Modula-2*, aka PIM3&nbsp;[[1, 2](https://github.com/trijezdci/MOCKA/blob/HEAD/README.md#references)]

### Historic Background

The MOCKA Modula-2 compiler was developed between 1988 and 1992 by the former German
National Research Centre for Computer Science (GMD)&nbsp;[[3](https://github.com/trijezdci/MOCKA/blob/HEAD/README.md#references)] at
its former research lab at the University of Karlsruhe&nbsp;[[4](https://github.com/trijezdci/MOCKA/blob/HEAD/README.md#references)].
GMD has since been dissolved. All its research institutes were merged into and all its assets transferred to
[Fraunhofer Society](https://www.fraunhofer.de/en.html).

GMD followed a dual-licensing approach:
* MOCKA compilers for commercial Unix platforms and architectures were sold as closed source commercial software and included industrial strength optimisation through AST transformations prior to code-generation<sup>[*](#footnotes)</sup>.
* The Linux and BSD version of MOCKA with a backend for the 32-bit Intel&nbsp;x86 architecture but without optimisation was released open-source under GPL licensing…
