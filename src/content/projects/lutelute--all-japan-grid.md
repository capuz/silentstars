# All-Japan-Grid

**A map of electricity that no one owns.**
An open model of Japan's national transmission grid, built automatically from OpenStreetMap and checked
against what the utilities publish. It solves power flow, draws every substation and lets you test
disaster scenarios. Every modelling assumption is listed in the intervention registry.

**Scale** — extracted from OSM: 40,087 transmission lines, 6,962 substations, 19,138 power plants across 10 regions. The canonical model used for power flow (`docs/data/built/`) has 17,744 nodes and 19,909 branches. The intervention registry lists 50 assumptions added to the model. Release v1.9.0, distributed dataset v1.9.0.
