# All-Japan-Grid

**Look at, verify, and use Japan's nationwide transmission grid, with sources.**
A nationwide grid model assembled mechanically from OpenStreetMap and cross-checked against materials published by utilities. Beyond viewing it on a map, you can solve power flows, draw the inside of substations, and test what happens in a disaster. Every assumption made in the model can be traced in a registry.

**See, check and use Japan's transmission grid — with sources.** An open model of the national grid,
built automatically from OpenStreetMap and checked against what the utilities publish. It renders on a
map, solves power flow, draws every substation, and lets you stress-test it — and every modelling
assumption is listed in a registry you can read.
