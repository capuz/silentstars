# All-Japan-Grid

See, check, and use the national transmission grid of Japan, with sources.
A national transmission grid model assembled automatically from OpenStreetMap and cross-checked against utilities' published materials.
Beyond viewing it on a map, you can solve power flow, draw inside substations, and test what happens during disasters.
Every assumption made in the model and where it was applied can be traced in the registry.

**See, check and use Japan's transmission grid — with sources.** An open model of the national grid,
built automatically from OpenStreetMap and checked against what the utilities publish. It renders on a
map, solves power flow, draws every substation, and lets you stress-test it — and every modelling
assumption is listed in a registry you can read.
