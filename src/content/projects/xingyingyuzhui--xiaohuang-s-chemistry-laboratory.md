# Xiao Huang's Chemistry Laboratory

A local application for **middle school chemistry teaching and self-study**. On classroom displays, teacher computers, or during students' self-study, it uses a unified interface to present periodic table explanations, 3D molecule and reaction diagrams, calculation exercises, electron configuration demonstrations, and classroom interaction.

All data is stored locally in SQLite. Optionally, you can connect to the **DeepSeek API** for AI-generated questions, hints, molecule/reaction generation, and similar features. Only the prompts for each call are forwarded; business data such as rosters and exam papers stays on the local machine.
