# CardTree Bookmarks

**Redefine your bookmark management experience on the new tab page with a card tree**

## Features

- **Card tree view** — Bookmark folders are presented as expandable/collapsible cards with a clear hierarchy
- **Drag-and-drop sorting** — Custom pointer-event drag system supporting single and batch dragging, and moving across folders
- **Rubber-band selection** — Drag in an empty area to box-select multiple bookmarks
- **Context menu** — Full add, edit, delete, and move operations
- **Keyboard navigation** — Arrow keys to browse, Enter to open, Delete for batch deletion, `/` or Ctrl+F to focus search
- **Search** — Real-time debounced search that automatically expands matching folders
- **Recycle bin** — Deleted bookmarks can be restored to their original location within 30 days
- **Left sidebar** — Quickly jump to any folder, with drag-to-reorder support
- **Theme switching** — Light / dark / follow system, with a frosted-glass UI
- **Access tracking** — Records bookmark usage frequency and most recent access time
