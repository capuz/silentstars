# formal-ai

**Don't know everything, know just enough to get to know anything**

Formal AI is a Rust implementation of a symbolic, deterministic assistant that exposes OpenAI-shaped interfaces without neural-network inference.

It belongs to the tradition of [symbolic artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbolic_artificial_intelligence) (a.k.a. GOFAI): its knowledge is an inspectable [semantic network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semantic_network) of human-readable links rather than hidden neural weights. The [associative technology stack guide](https://github.com/link-assistant/formal-ai/blob/HEAD/docs/associative-tech-stack.md) links every component and distinguishes direct runtime dependencies from architecture and development tooling.
