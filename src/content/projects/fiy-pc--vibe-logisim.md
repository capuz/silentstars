# Vibe Logisim

**Build circuits, run simulations, and find problems together with AI.**

Vibe Logisim is a digital circuit workbench with AI collaboration. You can draw circuits yourself, or hand requirements and materials to the built-in AI, which can implement circuits, explain principles, troubleshoot errors, and refine the results with you.

Circuit editing, reading materials, AI conversation, and simulation all happen in the same window. Your work is saved in your own folder as `.circ` files that you can keep editing and running.

[Download](https://github.com/FIY-pc/vibe-logisim/releases/latest) · [Product introduction](https://lovej.site/vibe-logisim/) · [Report an issue](https://github.com/FIY-pc/vibe-logisim/issues/new/choose) · [Contribute to development](https://github.com/FIY-pc/vibe-logisim/blob/HEAD/docs/development.md)

## What you can do with it

**Build circuits.** From a full adder or counter to a datapath made of multiple subcircuits, you can manually place components, wire them, and modify properties, or use natural language to have the AI implement them. Existing projects can be opened and continued directly without starting from scratch.
