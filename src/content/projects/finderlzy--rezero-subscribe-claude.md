## What is this

《如何订阅 Claude》 was originally a very long article. This site splits it into a guide you read chapter by chapter, and you can read it in your browser. Each page covers one chapter. Choose your route based on your situation (node plan, phone OS), then keep clicking “Next” until you reach the afterword.

## Let an AI Agent guide you

The site has a **subscription Skill**. Install it into your own AI Agent, and it will guide you through each step along your chosen route. Commands, configuration, and cleanup on the computer are handled for you. For the phone, your account, and payment, it only guides you through the steps so you do them yourself.
