# ChatSum

You open a chat after a couple of days of silence, and there are already a thousand new messages.
One command, and instead of an endless feed you get a neat breakdown: everything sorted by topic, condensed to the essentials, with every conclusion backed by a quote.
You skim the headings and stop only at what matters.
Want more detail? Read short excerpts from participants' own words and follow a link straight to the spot in the chat where the debate happened.

Thousands of messages in a chat, one command, and you get a tidy breakdown: the main topics, who said what, important links and numbers. No need to scroll through a week of chat. The bot reads everything itself and sorts it into neat categories.

## What it can do

- **Summary for a period:** "what was in the chat over the last 12 hours / 2 days".
- **Summary by count:** "break down the last 50 messages".
- **Scheduled summary:** every morning you get a chat digest automatically, without commands.
- **Export without a neural network:** save the conversation as text to review manually.
