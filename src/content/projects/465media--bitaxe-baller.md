# Bitaxe Baller

**v1.19.0** — Live dashboard + tuner for Bitaxe miners on your local network. Native desktop app for Mac and Windows, an Umbrel community app for self-hosters, and companion apps for iOS and Android. Click the icon, a window opens with every Bitaxe on your network — temps, hashrate, tuning recommendations, pool config. Also monitors NerdQAxe and other AxeOS-fork miners (full support) and Braiins OS mini miners like the BMM 101 (monitor-only — hashrate, temps, shares, best diff; tuning stays in Braiins' own UI). Other devices on your LAN reach the same dashboard at `http://bitaxe-baller.local`. **Public leaderboard:** [bitaxeballer.com/leaderboard](https://bitaxeballer.com/leaderboard) is free to enter for all users. Top miner in each Bitaxe model wins one free month of Pro every month; places 2-5 get a 20%-off coupon. Free-tier users sign up with a display name + verified email; Pro users authenticate with their license key. **Pro:** remote dashboard access from outside the LAN via the bitaxeballer.com relay — outbound-only, license-key auth, one-click toggle. Free, MIT-licensed, no telemetry beyond the once-per-hour update check.
