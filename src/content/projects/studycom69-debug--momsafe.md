# MomSafe AI
### The Continuous Intelligence Layer for Modern Maternal Healthcare

---

> **The Problem:** Traditional prenatal care is episodic. A 15-minute checkup once every four weeks leaves expectant mothers unmonitored for over 95% of their pregnancy—the exact window where complications like preeclampsia, gestational hypertension, and cardiac distress develop silently.
>
> **Our Solution:** **MomSafe AI** builds the missing continuous bridge. Pairing an ergonomic, medical-grade physiological wristband with our cloud intelligence layer, MomSafe AI synthesizes continuous biometrics into real-time, explainable guidance for mothers and obstetric care teams.
