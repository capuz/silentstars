# Smart-WorkFlow-aPaaS-server

Smart-WorkFlow-aPaaS-server is the backend service for **CH-aPaaS** (Java 21 / Spring Boot 3.4). CH-aPaaS is a low-code platform for building your own business applications: model your business as forms and processes, then start, approve, and track them from the workbench. On top of this, it includes AI agents, device access, and message notifications.

Companion frontend: [Smart-WorkFlow-aPaaS-Web](https://github.com/Chikaaho/Smart-WorkFlow-aPaaS-Web) (currently 1.1.1; this service 1.1.0)

## Features

- **Forms**: drag-and-drop form design with attachments, images, sub-tables, default values, and display linkage; submitting a form automatically starts an approval.
- **Workflow approval**: countersigning, conditional branching, and parallel approval by multiple people; approval comments can determine the direction of the process and the next assignee.
- **AI agents**: graphically orchestrate agents connected to mainstream large models; they can call tools, answer questions using knowledge bases, and start approvals and tickets on their own.
