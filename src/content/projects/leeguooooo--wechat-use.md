wechat-use turns WeChat on macOS into a **local API** for AI agents and bots. Nothing goes to the cloud, there is no iPad protocol, and the implementation is purely local. Your data stays only on your Mac.

Get the overview in 40 seconds: searching chat history, sending messages, connecting AI agents, and the local standalone copy. The screen is a functional illustration with fictional messages.

> `wechat-use` is a member of the **`*-use` series**, which lets AI agents operate your real local tools and devices directly:
> [`profile-use`](https://github.com/leeguooooo/profile-use) (local personal profile form filling) ·
> [`iphone-use`](https://github.com/leeguooooo/iphone-use) (driving a real iPhone) ·
> [`chrome-use`](https://github.com/leeguooooo/chrome-use) (browser automation) · **`wechat-use`** (macOS WeChat).
> Unified positioning: **local-first, no cloud, users fully in control of their data**.
