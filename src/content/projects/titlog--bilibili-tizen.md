# bilibili on Samsung TVs

**Chinese · [English](https://github.com/titlog/bilibili-tizen/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)**

A bilibili client for Samsung Tizen TVs, fully operated with the remote. The TV connects directly to bilibili: **no backend, no proxy, no server; account credentials exist only on the TV itself.**

One TV can be logged in to multiple accounts at once, each with its own watch history that doesn't interfere with the others. Something you started on your phone can be picked up on the TV.

> LG webOS has a great [bili-webos](https://github.com/asdf17128/bili-webos), but Samsung has had nothing. This project fills that gap.
>
> **Not affiliated with bilibili or Samsung.** Personal project, MIT license.
>
> Sibling projects: [douyin-tizen](https://github.com/titlog/douyin-tizen) (watching Douyin on Samsung TVs), [telegram-tizen](https://github.com/titlog/telegram-tizen) (playing videos from Telegram groups and channels on Samsung TVs).
