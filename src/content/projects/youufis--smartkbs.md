# SmartKBS — Smart Teaching Platform

> **🌐 [Chinese](https://github.com/youufis/SmartKBS/blob/HEAD/README.md) | [English](https://github.com/youufis/SmartKBS/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)**
>
> **An AI smart teaching platform for three school stages: primary school, middle school, and high school.** Subjects, grades, and classes are all driven by configuration, and the platform **is not limited to any single subject**.
>
> It covers five main lines: **Teach · Learn · Practice · Evaluate · Manage**. The 48 functional areas are grouped into six chapters by main line, with 50+ features in total:
>
> - 🏠 **Entry · Home and To-Dos** — Smart dashboard, student to-do list
> - 🧑‍🏫 **Teach · Teaching and Resources** — Course outlines, AI podcasts, AI teaching assistant, smart question bank, online exams, intelligent test paper assembly with Word export, AI resource recommendations, AI-generated HTML resources, resource center and management, file center
> - 🎓 **Learn · Study and Practice** — AI dialogue (knowledge Q&A), AI study companion, course exercises, synchronized practice, mistake review, daily highlights and hot news, code practice
> - 🎪 **Practice · Classroom Activities** —…
