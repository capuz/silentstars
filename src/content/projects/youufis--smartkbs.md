# SmartKBS — Smart Teaching Platform

> **🌐 [Chinese](https://github.com/youufis/SmartKBS/blob/HEAD/README.md) | [English](https://github.com/youufis/SmartKBS/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)**
>
> **An AI smart teaching platform for primary, junior high, and high school levels** — subjects, grades, and classes are all driven by configuration, **not limited to any single subject**.
>
> It covers five main lines: **Teach · Learn · Practice · Evaluate · Manage**. The 48 functional areas are grouped into six chapters by these lines, with 50+ features in total:
>
> - 🏠 **Entrance · Home and To-Dos** — smart dashboard, student to-do list
> - 🧑‍🏫 **Teach · Teaching and Resources** — course outline, AI podcast, AI teaching assistant, smart question bank, online exams, smart exam paper generation with Word export, AI resource recommendations, AI-generated HTML resources, resource center and management, file center
> - 🎓 **Learn · Learning and Practice** — AI chat (knowledge Q&A), AI learning companion, course exercises, synchronized practice, mistake consolidation, daily picks and trending news, code practice
> - 🎪 **Practice · Classroom Activities** — in-class quizzes, classroom polls,…
