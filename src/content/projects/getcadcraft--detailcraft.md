# DetailCraft

Model it once. Every shop drawing follows.

**[Website](https://getcadcraft.com/apps/detailcraft) | [Use it in your browser](https://getcadcraft.com/use/detailcraft) | [Download CadCraft Studio](https://getcadcraft.com/download#studio) | [Help and tutorials](https://getcadcraft.com/tutorials/detailcraft) | [News](https://getcadcraft.com/news) | [Suggest a feature](https://getcadcraft.com/suggest)**

DetailCraft is the steel & rebar detailing app in the [CadCraft](https://getcadcraft.com) family: Structural steel members and connections, rebar placement and bending schedules, shop drawings and NC files from one model.

## Use it

Use it now at https://getcadcraft.com/use/detailcraft, or install CadCraft Studio, which carries all eight apps. Your files stay on your computer either way.
