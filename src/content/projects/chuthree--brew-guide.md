An open-source app for managing coffee bean inventory, recording tasting notes, and assisting with brewing.

[Website](https://chu3.top/brewguide) · [Download](#使用) · [Tutorial](https://chu3.top/brewguide-help) · [Changelog](https://chu3.top/brewguide/changelog) · [Web version](https://coffee.chu3.top/)

## Features

- **Bean inventory**: Photograph coffee beans to recognize their info, quickly deduct usage, and view inventory and best-flavor dates.
- **Tasting notes**: Review tastings by bean, time, and rating, and compare scoring dimensions with radar charts.
- **Brewing timer**: Preset the pouring method, time, and water amount for each stage, and check progress in real time while brewing.

For more feature details, see the [Website](https://chu3.top/brewguide).
