## Inspiration

Thor Command Panel stands on the shoulders of two projects:

- **AYN's stock Dual-Screen Assistant** (`com.odin.dualscreen.assistant`) — the built-in app this replaces. Thor Command Panel rebuilds its layout, behavior and hardware integration so it feels native on the Thor.
- **[Better Dual Screen Assistant](https://github.com/stormpanda/better-dual-screen-assistant) by stormpanda** — the source-available project that first showed how a normal app can stand in for the system assistant on this hardware. Everything shipped here is either my own work or re-derived from AYN's stock app. See [Upstream & licensing](#upstream--licensing) at the bottom.
