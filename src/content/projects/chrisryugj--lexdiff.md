# LexDiff

**Laws made easy. Smart with AI. The beginning of public Legal AI.**

> *30 minutes with Google, hallucinations with GPT, 30 seconds with LexDiff — with legal and case law sources.*

---

## 💡 What can you do with LexDiff?

When you ask legal questions in everyday language, AI answers **based on verbatim law texts and case law**. It's not just simple search.

* **🧠 AI Legal Search** — "I didn't receive severance pay, what should I do?" → The Themis engine (Claude) directly queries laws and case law from the Legislative Council's API, and provides real-time streaming answers with sources. Simple provision or case law queries run instant (fast-path) without LLM, and subsequent questions remember context.
* **⚡ Comparison of Old and New Provisions** — Changes before and after amendment are highlighted in color. AI summarizes "what changed" in one line. **You can choose the desired point in the amendment history** to view the comparison of new and old provisions at that point and AI explanation.
