## Autonomous OS: The "Android" for Robots

Robots have been around for years but have never been autonomous — someone has to drive them with a remote, and they've stopped at scripted demos. Autonomous OS brings autonomy to robots: install it on your robot and it comes alive.

- **Your robot thinks.** Everything it sees and hears goes to an agentic reasoning engine running on the robot itself — [Hermes](https://github.com/autonomous-ai/autonomous-os/blob/HEAD/runtimes/hermes/), [Claude Code](https://github.com/autonomous-ai/autonomous-os/blob/HEAD/runtimes/claudecode/), or [whichever you choose](https://github.com/autonomous-ai/autonomous-os/blob/HEAD/runtimes/) — that decides what to do next.
