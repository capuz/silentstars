# ResolveAI — E-commerce Dual-Side and Multi-Agent Intelligent Customer Service Platform

ResolveAI is a runnable AI application. Customers register, log in, and chat directly with e-commerce customer service. They can also browse products, place orders, pay, and check logistics. Merchants manage products and all orders, and take over chats and after-sales issues that need human handling. The project uses LangGraph to orchestrate seven collaborating nodes, combining a mall database, a RAG knowledge base, and controlled tools to generate traceable replies.

The project is not connected to real payment, real logistics, or enterprise business channels, and does not handle real funds. Being runnable does not mean it has passed commercial production acceptance. Product facts come from merchant input, and the system does not treat model-generated content as business operations that have already been executed.

## Current Capabilities

- **Customer and merchant dual sides**: only two roles, customer and admin. After login, users enter chat or the back office respectively. Users can continue conversations, switch history, link their own orders, rate replies, and confirm resolution.
