# ivLyrics iOS

Korean | [English](https://github.com/ivLis-Studio/ivLyrics-IOS/blob/HEAD/README_EN.md)

Enjoy the music you play on Spotify with its lyrics. ivLyrics iOS is an app that shows lyrics in sync with the song that is playing, and displays translations in the language you choose along with pronunciation to help you read along. It is made for people who want to view lyrics in large text on an iPhone or iPad, or enjoy them in landscape mode, Picture-in-Picture (PiP) mode, or LP mode. Spotify handles music playback.
