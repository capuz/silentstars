# ivLyrics iOS

Korean | [English](https://github.com/ivLis-Studio/ivLyrics-IOS/blob/HEAD/README_EN.md)

Enjoy the music you play on Spotify along with its lyrics. ivLyrics iOS is an app that shows lyrics in sync with the song that is playing, and it also displays translations in the language you choose, along with pronunciation guides so you can follow along. It is for anyone who wants to view lyrics in large text on an iPhone or iPad, or enjoy them in landscape mode, picture-in-picture (PiP), or LP mode. Music playback is handled by Spotify.
