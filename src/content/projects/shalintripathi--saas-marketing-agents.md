# SaaS Marketing Agents

**AI marketing agents for Claude Code: a B2B SaaS marketing team you install in two commands.**

```
/plugin marketplace add shalintripathi/saas-marketing-agents
/plugin install saas-marketing@saas-marketing-agents
```

Then ask for real work: *"Plan a product launch for [feature] aimed at [ICP]."* CATALYST splits the brief across 94 specialists in 17 disciplines (19 skills) and hands back the files. Anything it can't back up from your `brand-context.md` comes back as `[NEEDS INPUT]`, not a made-up stat.
