## About ShadowStore

[<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LOWERTOP/Shadowrocket-First/refs/heads/main/img/ShadowStore_ScreenShot.png" width="100%">](https://shadowstore.dpdns.org/ "Visit ShadowStore")

**Website: [ShadowStore.dpdns.org](https://shadowstore.dpdns.org)**

**ShadowStore** is a **resource index and navigation platform** for Shadowrocket and related open-source communities. ShadowStore **is not a resource hosting platform, nor a third-party resource mirror site**. We aim to centrally index public resources scattered across different open-source repositories, project homepages, and resource directories, and to preserve as much as possible the direct link between each resource and its original author.
