# Nibomo

AI-powered, open-source flashcards for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, and the web.

Prepare for exams, learn vocabulary, and memorize technical terms and facts. Create and improve cards with AI, study offline on mobile, and review with FSRS spaced repetition.

## Available on

- [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6760538964?pt=128797295&ct=github_readme&mt=8&platform=iphone) — including iPhone Duo; iOS 18+; Home Screen widgets.
- [iPad](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6760538964?pt=128797295&ct=github_readme&mt=8&platform=ipad) — iPadOS 18+; Home Screen widgets.
- [macOS](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6760538964?pt=128797295&ct=github_readme&mt=8&platform=mac) — macOS 15+ on Apple silicon via Mac Catalyst.
- [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flashcardsopensourceapp.app&utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=github_readme) — Android 11, 12, 12L, 13, 14, 15, 16, and 17; Home Screen widgets.
- [Web](https://app.nibomo.com/review) — study in your browser.
- [AI assistants via MCP](https://nibomo.com/docs/mcp-connector/) — connect at `https://mcp.nibomo.com/mcp`.
