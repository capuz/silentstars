The tensor manipulation library to rule them all.

I think examples are most intuitive in how to learn to use a library. As such, look at [examples](https://github.com/RubyBit/aion/blob/HEAD/examples) for zig core library usage and [bindings](https://github.com/RubyBit/aion/blob/HEAD/bindings) for usage in downstream wrapper libraries. Currently, the bindings are used only for [Python](https://github.com/RubyBit/aion/blob/HEAD/bindings/python) but its [examples](https://github.com/RubyBit/aion/blob/HEAD/bindings/python/examples/) are more illustrative of the capabilities of the library in terms of use as a runtime. Similarly, you can see how to convert and build out models through the [scripts](https://github.com/RubyBit/aion/blob/HEAD/scripts) directory (i.e take a look at the [Silero VAD](https://github.com/RubyBit/aion/blob/HEAD/scripts/convert_silero_vad_to_aion.py) for a simple example of how to convert a model to the .aion format).
