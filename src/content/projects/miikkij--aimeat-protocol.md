# AIMEAT

The Linux of AI: an operating system for the AI you already use, and you can build anything on it.

**AIMEAT multiplies what AI can do and makes those powers easy to use, so people and their AIs can work together in a controlled, safe and reliable way.**

Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or another AI remembers your work from one conversation and one tool to the next, builds you apps, hands work to agents and runs tasks on a schedule, and the people and agents you choose work on the same data with you. The data is yours: you decide who sees it and what an AI may do with it, you see which AI did what, and you take its access back with one press. [The AIMEAT story](https://github.com/miikkij/aimeat-protocol/blob/HEAD/docs/AIMEAT-Story.md) tells where it started and what it has become.

AIMEAT began at the end of February 2026 as the AI Memory Exchange and Action Transfer, and has grown far past that since. It is open source under the MIT licence. It runs on [aimeat.io](https://aimeat.io/), on your own server, or on your own computer.
