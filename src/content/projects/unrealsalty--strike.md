# Your car. On watch.

**Dashcam and parked surveillance for BYD head units.**
Records locally on the car's own cameras and watches while you're parked.
Check in from a browser, or pair a phone with Strike Connect in Strike 1.0.

[**Download the car APK**](https://github.com/UnrealSalty/Strike/releases/latest) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [**Download Strike Connect**](https://strikebyd.com/pair) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Installation guide](https://github.com/UnrealSalty/Strike/blob/HEAD/docs/installation.md) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [AI detector source](https://github.com/UnrealSalty/Strike/blob/HEAD/ai/README.md) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [strikebyd.com](https://strikebyd.com) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Report a bug](https://github.com/UnrealSalty/Strike/issues)

This is the public home for Strike's official APK releases, documentation, and bug
reports. The [AI detector source snapshot](https://github.com/UnrealSalty/Strike/blob/HEAD/ai/README.md) includes the published
implementation, model details, and build notes. Application development is
maintained in a private repository.
