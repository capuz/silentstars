# Big Red Report

**English** · [Español](https://github.com/BigRedReport/bigredreport/blob/HEAD/README.es.md) · [Français](https://github.com/BigRedReport/bigredreport/blob/HEAD/README.fr.md) · [Italiano](https://github.com/BigRedReport/bigredreport/blob/HEAD/README.it.md) · [Português](https://github.com/BigRedReport/bigredreport/blob/HEAD/README.pt.md) · [Deutsch](https://github.com/BigRedReport/bigredreport/blob/HEAD/README.de.md)

A fan-made port of **Big Red Racing** (Big Red Software, 1995) for modern computers: the wildest racing game of 1995, from jeeps and speedboats to helicopters and Martian buggies, now with modern graphics, current controllers, split-screen, LAN and online multiplayer, and the original AI rivals.
