# GeneWeb

## Open source genealogy software with a web interface written in OCaml

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **GeneWeb 7.1 writes a new database format, shared with Geneanet.**
> A base built by GeneWeb 7.0 or a 7.1 beta is still displayed, but it is
> read-only: the edit forms are refused until the base is rebuilt. Keep a
> copy of the base, export it with the `gwu` of the new version, then
> rebuild it with its `gwc`, which also writes the index of its
> dictionaries (`base.inx` and `base.dat`). A rebuilt base cannot be read
> by an older version. See [CHANGES.md](https://github.com/geneweb/geneweb/blob/HEAD/CHANGES.md) for details.

---

## Why GeneWeb?

Created by [Daniel de Rauglaudre](https://github.com/roglo) in 1998, GeneWeb is the engine behind some of the largest genealogical databases
in the world:

- **[Roglo](https://roglo.eu)** — Over **11 million individuals**, maintained
  collaboratively by nearly **300 contributors**
- **[Geneanet](https://www.geneanet.org)** — Used GeneWeb as its core engine
  for two decades
