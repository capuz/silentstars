# PrismAura · Aurora Music

**Built for TV, making music the star of the living room.**

Large screen for covers and lyrics, one press on the remote to start listening.<br>
Scan a QR code with your phone for input and management, keeping things effortless in your hands.

**Android TV 6.0+** · **MusicFree plugin compatible** · **MIT**

## Features

- 📺 **Pick songs comfortably with the remote**: navigate with arrow keys, play with OK, long-press or use the menu key to manage content. Select, skip, and favorite songs all from the couch.
- 📱 **Say goodbye to typing with the remote**: scan a QR code on the same LAN to search with your phone, paste audio source URLs, and manage the playback queue. Backup and restore are also supported, with no separate phone app needed.
- 🎤 **Lyrics follow the music**: displays original and translated lyrics in sync, and when a plugin provides word-level timestamps, words highlight automatically. Three themes to choose from. Standby…
