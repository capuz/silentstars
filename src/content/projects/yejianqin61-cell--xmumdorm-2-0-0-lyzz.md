## Campus Life, Happening Here

厦马小筑 (XMUMDorm) serves students of Xiamen University Malaysia (XMUM), connecting campus information, peer communication, and daily life services. Whether you're looking for a meal, posting a message, joining a club, or planning your own timetable, you can start here.

| Campus Community | Campus Life |
| :--- | :--- |
| Tree Hole (树洞) posts, comments, likes and bookmarks | Canteen info, dish reviews and rankings |
| School and college announcements, campus trending topics | Second-hand trading, errand help |
| Club updates, event posting and registration | Timetable, to-dos, diary |

## Key Features

### Plaza and Campus Information

- View notices published by the university, colleges, and professional organizations
- Browse campus trending topics, club updates, freshman guides, and one-stop campus information
- View campus events and partner promotional content in controlled carousel slots
