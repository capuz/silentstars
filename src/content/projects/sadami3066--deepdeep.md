## Overview

DeepDeep is designed to match the built-in assistants on all phone manufacturers. It supports waking up with a long press of the power button, and floating voice input and output.

It also comes with a complete Harness engineering setup, including tools, skills, long-term memory, MCP extensions, and a plugin system. It runs conveniently on your phone, with no server and no computer that must stay on. You can open it anytime, anywhere. Long-press the power button, say one sentence, and it starts working to get things done.
