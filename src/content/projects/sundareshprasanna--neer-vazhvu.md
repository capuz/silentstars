# Neer Vazhvu

**Urban Water Intelligence** - An open-source platform that turns public water data into actionable intelligence for Indian cities. Live for Chennai, Madurai, Bengaluru, Mumbai and Delhi today, with more cities on the way.

**Live:** [neervazhvu.org](https://neervazhvu.org)

Neer Vazhvu (நீர் வாழ்வு, Tamil for "Water Life") tracks reservoir levels, groundwater health, river water quality, flood risk, sewerage infrastructure, and water body loss across Indian cities.
