## 🤔 What it is

**Zapret Studio** is a convenient shell for the [zapret-discord-youtube](https://github.com/Flowseal/zapret-discord-youtube) strategy set, running on the [zapret](https://github.com/bol-van/zapret) engine.

The blocking bypass itself is done by the original zapret. Zapret Studio turns it into a proper program: a nice window, strategy cards, automatic selection, connection monitoring and a backup tunnel.

> 🧩 Simply put: **zapret is the engine, Zapret Studio is the dashboard.**
