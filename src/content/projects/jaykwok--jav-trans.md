# jav-trans

**One-click generation of Japanese, Chinese, or bilingual SRT subtitles for Japanese-language videos.** Select a video in the local desktop window, and it automatically completes Japanese speech recognition, timeline alignment, translation (local or API), and subtitle formatting and export.

[**⬇ Download Windows release package**](https://github.com/jaykwok/jav-trans/releases/latest/download/jav-trans-windows-x64.zip) · [Release notes](https://github.com/jaykwok/jav-trans/releases/latest) · [Quick start](#快速开始) · [Translation modes](#选择翻译模式) · [Upgrading](#升级到新版本) · [FAQ](#常见问题)

> **Requirements**: Windows 10/11 + NVIDIA GPU (≥ 8 GB VRAM).
>
> **Privacy note**: Videos and audio are always processed only on your machine. When you choose "Local translation" or "Japanese only," subtitle text also never leaves your machine. Subtitles are saved by default to the folder containing the video.
