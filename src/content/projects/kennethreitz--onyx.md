A native Mac app for your Obsidian vault, typeset like a book.</p>

---

- **Typeset reading** — small caps, collision-free sidenotes, hyphenation, `$` / `$$` math, the page as a book
- **Linter** — tidy Markdown like Obsidian Linter: one undoable edit for the current note, a preview before linting a folder, and optional lint on save ([docs](https://github.com/kennethreitz/onyx/blob/HEAD/docs/linter.md))
- **Read Aloud** — the current note or your selection, from the command palette, with macOS voices
- **Drag, drop, and paste attachments.** Drop files from Finder, drop images from a browser, or paste a screenshot. Each one is copied to your attachments folder and linked as `![[…]]`, following Obsidian's Files and links settings ([docs](https://github.com/kennethreitz/onyx/blob/HEAD/docs/attachments.md))
- **Attachments open in place** — click an image, PDF, audio file, or video and it opens in the pane: an image viewer with zoom and pan, PDFKit with find, a native audio player with a waveform, speed control, and your Record Audio transcript, and AVKit for video.
