# DAWLoop

**AI Music Creation & DAW Automation**

Bringing music creation, AI intelligence, and real DAW interaction together.

Analyze materials and create and edit MIDI in Desktop; generate and audition candidates around reference music in the Plugin; use Runtime to build Agent workflows that can plan, operate, observe, and verify.
