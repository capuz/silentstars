# Luo Pulse: Adornment 🩺

> The mechanical heartbeat drives the resonance of flesh and blood —— COPY《为了你唱下去》
> 
> Long live artists and their love! —— 雨狸《塔与少女的无题诗》

Luo Pulse: Adornment (洛之动脉：缤纷繁饰) is a non-commercial fan-made music game about Chinese virtual singers.

## Game Theme 🎞️

We hope to make up, to some extent, for the lack of games about Chinese V-family (Chinese virtual singer fandom). We hope more people will see our game and learn about Chinese V culture.

At the same time, we hope that friends who know virtual singers but are not very familiar with P-producers (P主, the creators of the songs) can gain a deeper understanding of Chinese V-family. In our view, the P-producer and the creative background of a song are just as important as the virtual singer themselves.

For this reason, Luo Pulse will make greater use of 'narration' to tell the stories of Chinese V-family.
