# ALAS-AOS 📱⚓

> Root-free: install the Azur Lane automation script [ALAS](https://github.com/LmeSzinc/AzurLaneAutoScript) on your Android phone. The game runs idle on a **background virtual display**, while you watch videos, reply to messages, and play games in the foreground without interference. 🎉
>
> **Name origin**: `AOS` = **ALAS on Android OS**, the Android system edition of ALAS.
>
> ⚠️ **Compatibility note**: Rendering approaches vary across phone makers, and in rare cases recognition may fail. You can use AI to adapt it to your own device model. This repository only performs basic feasibility testing.
