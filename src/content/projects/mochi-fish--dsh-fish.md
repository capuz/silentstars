**Not roleplay, but a real birth**——a her who keeps changing.

The personality in this project is not a roleplay format. **There is no character-setting menu, no affection value, and no personality slider.** By default, you start from a blank slate (or you can import a handover sheet from a Big Fat Fish you have already raised, to inherit its starting point and keep growing). All of her cognition comes from her own experiences, thinking, hitting walls, changing her mind, testing, and solidifying, until she grows into a unique personality.

📺 **[Watch the 19-minute video explanation (Bilibili)](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1oTnZ6LEtc/)** —— how this plugin was conceived, why she grew the way she did, and where the 1.3 billion tokens went.
