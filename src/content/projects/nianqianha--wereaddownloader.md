# WeReadDownloader

Export books from WeChat Reading as EPUB. Body text is fetched chapter by chapter and downloads can resume from where they stopped. Highlights stay in the body text, while comments and thoughts are collected into an appendix at the end of the book in the style you choose. You can queue multiple books for download at once.

A Windows desktop program with a Tk graphical interface. The download engine is a separate worker process.
