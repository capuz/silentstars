# V9968 + Geo3D Sample Demos

## SEASON GLOBE — Four Seasons Snow Globe

MSX turbo R + V9968 (built-in 98h / external 88h) + Geo3D V2 viewing demo. Press SPACE to shake the dome, and use the left and right keys to switch seasons. The BGM includes "Distant Music Box," converted for MSX-MUSIC (OPLL). Verified working on BlueMSX+ v3.2.0-alpha.

[Release ZIP](https://github.com/kanon-ai/V9968_Geo3D_SampleDemo/releases/tag/season-globe-v1.1.0) · [Controls and preview](https://github.com/kanon-ai/V9968_Geo3D_SampleDemo/blob/HEAD/demos/season-globe/README.md) · [Terms of use and disclaimer / License](https://github.com/kanon-ai/V9968_Geo3D_SampleDemo/blob/HEAD/demos/season-globe/LICENSE.txt)

## SKYBOUND — Flight Demo Ver2.0 / Japan Tour

**256-color automatic flight, Geo3D V2 required.** Take off from Tokyo, fly past Mount Fuji, Nagoya and Osaka, turn at Kyoto and return. Includes live cockpit instruments, internal-98h and external-88h ROMs (**4 MiB / ASCII16**). Silent; developer openMSX/TurboR verified. Hardware, BlueMSX+ V2 and Z80 performance remain unverified. Earlier editions are retained.
