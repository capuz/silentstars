# ✨ Aura

### An elegant voice input tool

Speak and it types. Aura recognizes your speech, organizes your expression, and writes the result into the current editor.

It can replace Typeless and Qianwen IME as a lightweight voice entry point for daily work.

## Features

- Press right Alt to start recording, press again to stop
- Results are automatically written at the current cursor or copied to the clipboard
- Automatically follows the screen where the current input box is located and shows an elegant floating window
- Voice organization and selection rewriting
- Remove AI-style wording and Prompt Engineering Skill
- Voice translation
- Real-time microphone waveform detection
- Dictation history and personal dictionary

The model services are fixed to Doubao streaming ASR 2.0 and `doubao-seed-2-0-mini-260428`. We compared speed, latency, and cost, and chose the combination that responds faster, has lower latency, and is more affordable.
