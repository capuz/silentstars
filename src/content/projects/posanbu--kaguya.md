## 🌙 Meet Kaguya

Kaguya (辉夜) is a chat agent based on large language models. It aims to be a friendly, natural AI group member: chatting casually together, teasing each other, replying at the right moments, and leaving room for listening and silence.

Kaguya continuously perceives its surroundings, places fragmentary information back into the unfolding context to understand it, then decides whether, when, and how to interact naturally. Through shared experiences, it gradually builds memories and personality.

Supporting this goal is an **event-driven framework**. A continuously running Agent can connect to multiple scenarios such as group chats, private chats, live streams, tools, or devices. New information arriving is an opportunity to re-observe the environment, not an automatic trigger to start a question-and-answer round. A unified information foundation stores traceable facts, and each module then selects the full context according to its own responsibilities, rather than treating a single incoming message as the entire context.
