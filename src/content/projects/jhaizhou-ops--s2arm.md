## A native port, not an emulator

The game's own ARM64 code is rewritten at build time and then runs directly on the processor in your iPhone, iPad or Mac. There is no CPU emulator in between. Graphics calls are translated straight to Metal, and system services come from a native host. That is where the speed, the low heat and the battery life come from.…
