## What Is Cyvora Budget Calculator?

Cyvora Budget Calculator provides a simple online workspace for working with budget figures and organizing practical financial decisions. Because it runs in the browser, users can access a focused finance utility without relying on a traditional desktop application.

The project follows a productivity-first approach to online tools. Rather than attempting to provide broad financial management capabilities, it concentrates on budget calculations and the day-to-day planning decisions those figures can support.
