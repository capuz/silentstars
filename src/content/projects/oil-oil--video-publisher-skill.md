小红书 · 抖音 · Bilibili · 微信视频号 · YouTube<br>

Prepares videos, titles, and covers as verified multi-platform drafts, with support for resuming uploads, stopping by default before the final publish.

> **Important**
>
> The Skill does not click the final publish button by default. Only when the user explicitly asks to publish this content to a specified platform will it re-verify the draft and complete the final publish. Preparing drafts, uploading, and long-term account configuration do not count as authorization.

## How far verification has gone

As of 2026-09-06, there is historical acceptance testing for complete four-platform workflows, including interruption recovery. YouTube single-platform has also been verified for metadata, a 16:9 thumbnail, private visibility, and a no-op review. Recent page adaptations for 小红书, 抖音, Bilibili, and 微信视频号 have separate partial live tests.
