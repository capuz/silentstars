**Personal Cognitive Resilience Architect — Offline-First Agentic Mindset Training & Neuro-Linguistic Drilling Platform**

## Overview 🌐

Training the body is half the victory. The other half—the decisive half—lives in the space between stimulus and response. **ReflexForge** is a next-generation personal AI platform that treats cognitive agility with the same disciplined, methodology-driven approach as physical strength training.

Inspired by the offline-first, multi-source import architecture of IronClaw, ReflexForge reimagines how humans build mental reflexes. Instead of importing recipes for meals, you import *mental models, decision trees, micro-scripts from YouTube lectures, Instagram thought-leaders, web articles, and even photographs of handwritten notes*.
