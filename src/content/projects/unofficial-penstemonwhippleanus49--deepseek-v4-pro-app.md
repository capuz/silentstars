# 🤖 DeepSeek-V4-Pro-App - Smart AI reasoning for your desktop

## 🎯 About this application

DeepSeek-V4-Pro-App provides access to advanced AI technology on your Windows computer. It uses a large-scale architecture that helps the software process complex tasks and large amounts of text. This application acts as your personal agent. It assists with writing code, analyzing documents, and solving logic puzzles. The software handles up to one million tokens of memory. This allows the system to remember long conversations and deep project details. It runs locally to keep your private data secure.

## ⚙️ System requirements

Ensure your computer meets these standards before you start. The software needs hardware capable of handling large AI models.

- Operating System: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit).
- Processor: Intel Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 equivalent or better.
- Memory: 16 GB of RAM or higher.
- Storage: 20 GB of free space on your hard drive.
- Graphics: A dedicated graphics card with at least 8 GB of video memory provides the best results.
