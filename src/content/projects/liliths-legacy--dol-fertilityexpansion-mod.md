# Fertility Expansion (FertilityExpansion)

This mod is a gameplay extension for DOL's fertility-related features.
On top of the vanilla game, it adds breeding and raising gameplay to DOL.

## Contents

- Pregnancy expansion:
  - (Updated in v1.2.0) Adds an optional setting that lifts the conception and impregnation restrictions for Bailey, Leighton, and Gwenevan, who are sterilized through code in vanilla;
- Fertility expansion:
  - Adds an editable and displayable profile to all babies, which can be used to record a baby's origin and story;
  - Adds five innate attributes with hereditary tendencies to all babies;
  - (After passing certain attribute checks) can be backcrossed with adult **animal-transformed offspring** to obtain more targeted offspring;
- Child-rearing expansion
  - Adds a safe house dedicated to raising children next to the farm (upgrade items are under development)
    - (Updated in v1.5.0) Adds full safe house features including a bedroom and bathroom, plus an event where the child climbs into bed with you when sleeping in Eden;
    - (Updated in v1.5.0) Adds Eden renovation items to the furniture store in the mall, which slightly boost upbringing stats;…
