## Project Overview

**灵桥（Linkora）** brings an enterprise-grade AI assistant into teams' daily IM tools (DingTalk / Feishu / WeCom). An AI avatar that 'can understand enterprise knowledge, call tools, and be manually reviewed' appears directly in the group chats and direct messages you already use.
