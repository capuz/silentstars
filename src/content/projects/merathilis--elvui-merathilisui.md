**A complete, polished look for ElvUI - plus the features ElvUI doesn't ship.**

MerathilisUI is a plugin for ElvUI (Retail). It gives your interface a clean, transparent design with class-colored gradients and adds a set of modules that go beyond what ElvUI offers out of the box. Everything is modular: turn off what you don't need, keep the rest.

## Why MerathilisUI instead of plain ElvUI?

-   **One-click complete UI** - The installer sets up ElvUI, your chat, action bars, data texts, nameplates, unit frames and profiles for popular addons in a few steps. Choose between a **General**, **Dark** or **Gradient** layout.
-   **A consistent design everywhere** - Blizzard windows, ElvUI and supported addons get the same shadows, gradients and flat widgets, including the ElvUI options window itself.
