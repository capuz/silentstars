# A-Share Short-Term Trading and Factor Validation

This repository uses A-share daily data for short-term research. It mainly examines limit-up (涨停), consecutive limit-ups (连板), streak breaks (断板), reversal-after-break (反包), failed limit-ups (炸板), call auctions (竞价), and turnover (成交额), then validates factors one at a time.
Every result includes its sample size, cost basis, and trade-by-trade details, so you can open them and check each trade individually.
