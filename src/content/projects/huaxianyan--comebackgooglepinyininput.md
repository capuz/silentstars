# Google Pinyin Input Creative AI Edition

  **Comeback Google Pinyin Input**

This is a non-commercial compatibility maintenance project based on Google Pinyin Input 4.5.2. The project aims to preserve the native input, candidate, learning, dictionary, handwriting, theme, paging, and touch experience as much as possible, while fixing compatibility issues of the legacy app on modern Android, and building in a reproducible, auditable way.

> The rights to Google Pinyin Input, the original program, resources, lexicon, names, and related trademarks belong to Google LLC, Google Inc., or their respective rights holders. This project is not affiliated with Google and has not received official endorsement from Google.
