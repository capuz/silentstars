# MealMuster

Reusable event meal ordering. Someone organising a meal sends each guest a private link, the guest says whether they are coming and chooses food for themselves and any guests they bring, and the organiser gets a clear order to hand the venue. _MealMuster by DRMSITE._ The name is provisional, and I have not checked its availability as a trademark.

Status: version 0.1.0, offered as a pre-release. It was built from a product brief ([SPEC.md](https://github.com/DRMSITE/MealMuster/blob/HEAD/SPEC.md)) for one real group meal, and the names, address and menu from that event are replaced here with made-up ones, because a venue's menu is the venue's to publish. The plan it was built to is in [IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md](https://github.com/DRMSITE/MealMuster/blob/HEAD/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).
