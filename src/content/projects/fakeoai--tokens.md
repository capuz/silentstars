# Tokens [](https://hub.docker.com/r/fakeoai/tokens)

A Tokens management platform that schedules a pool of accounts to convert models from major overseas AI platforms into the API interface standard formats of OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini and others, supporting terminal use with Claude Code, Codex, GeminiCli, and more.
