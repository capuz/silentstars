# Humanlike Core Companion

> **She has a body, a schedule, and emotions.**
> Not just a persona line, but a full day of life.
> The plugin handles her body, schedule, emotions, and relationship context; how she speaks is still decided by the LLM according to her persona.

---

## What it does

“Humanlike Core Companion” is a humanizing plugin for AstrBot. It gives her a body that changes over time, a full day’s schedule, and fluctuating emotions, so the character is more than a fixed persona line.

- She has a **body** that changes over time: energy, drowsiness, sleep debt, hunger, and menstrual cycle. It updates without requiring you to speak first;
- She has a **schedule** for the whole day: arranged dynamically, and it can be interrupted. Interrupted activities are put on hold, and she can return to them herself later;
- She has **emotions and relationships**: affection, mood, and days known. These change gradually with interaction and do not start from zero with every message;
- She knows **where she is, what time it is, what the weather is, and what day it is**;
