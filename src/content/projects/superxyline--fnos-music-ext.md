# fnmusic-ext Feiniu Music Extension Proxy (Secondary Development Edition)

> **This repository is a secondary development edition of [javycoder/fnos_music_ext](https://github.com/javycoder/fnos_music_ext)**, adding capabilities such as "heart-favorite auto-download" on top of upstream v2.3.0. **Heartfelt thanks to the original author [@javycoder](https://github.com/javycoder) for their excellent open-source work.** All core capabilities of this repository (Socket takeover, three audio sources, WebUI, the favorites/playlists/history system) come from the upstream project. For the secondary-development additions, see the [二次开发功能](#二次开发功能) section.
