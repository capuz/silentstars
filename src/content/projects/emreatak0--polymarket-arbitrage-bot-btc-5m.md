# Polymarket Arbitrage Bot

**High-performance Polymarket arbitrage bot** built in **Node.js + TypeScript**. Automates detection and execution of arbitrage opportunities on Polymarket, focusing on crypto Up/Down markets (BTC, ETH, SOL, etc.), binary events, and cross-venue inefficiencies.

 

**Fast • Reliable • Type-Safe**

## Features
- **Real-time Arbitrage Scanner**: Monitors Polymarket 5-min Up/Down contracts and other markets for mispricings.
- **Low-Latency Execution**: Built with Node.js `async/await`, WebSocket support, and optimized RPC calls.
- **Multiple Strategies**:
  - Intra-market arb (YES/NO parity).
  - Cross-venue opportunities.
  - Short-term momentum on crypto Up/Down.
  - Merge/Redeem optimizer.
- **Risk Management**: Position sizing, stop logic, slippage protection, and configurable exposure limits.
- **Notifications**: Telegram, Discord, or custom webhooks.
- **Dashboard**: Optional Express + WebSocket UI for live P&L monitoring.
- **Battle-Tested**: Designed for Polymarket's unique challenges (latency, fills, volatility).

Perfect for quant traders building a **Polymarket arbitrage bot** in TypeScript.
