# AI Ready School

An AI-powered educational platform for students, teachers, and administrators. Built with Next.js 16, TypeScript, and Supabase. Supports multi-tenant deployments across **aireadyschool.com** and **learnia.ai**.

## Overview

AI Ready School provides 70+ AI-driven tools organized into six categories: **Learning**, **Research**, **Creative**, **Tech**, **Marketing**, and **Career**. The platform serves students, teachers, school administrators, and competition participants (DAT program), each with a tailored experience and role-based access control.
