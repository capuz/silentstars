# 涛声 TaoSheng

**替你听，替你记，替你想。**

One Android phone is enough: all-day audio capture → automatic transcription → speaker identification → AI organizes it into to-dos, commitments, and memories.<br>
No hardware to buy, no account to register, and the data stays only on your own phone.

🌐 Website: [www.taosheng.app](https://www.taosheng.app). Thanks to [@feitian124](https://github.com/feitian124) for helping produce it. International expansion may be considered later, and things may change.

## 💡 Why TaoSheng

Every day you say thousands of sentences. How many do you remember?

- Promises made in meetings are forgotten right after;
- A phone number a friend mentions in passing, or a time you agreed on, is forgotten a couple of days later;
- An idea you've mentioned several times never gets written down.
