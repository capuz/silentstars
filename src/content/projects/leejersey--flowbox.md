# 🧊 FlowBox — AI Desktop Productivity Tool

> **Local-First · AI-Powered · Privacy-Centric**
>
> FlowBox is a macOS desktop AI productivity tool for individual developers and knowledge workers. It integrates seven core modules: to-do management, inspiration quick notes, Pomodoro focus, smart clipboard, voice memos, productivity analytics, and Dev Trending. All data is stored locally, and AI capabilities are connected on demand.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 📋 To-Do Management
- Create, edit, and archive to-do items, with priority sorting
- Tagging system and batch management

### 💡 Inspiration Quick Notes
- Capture inspiration anytime, with tag classification (inline editing / color marking)
- Waterfall card browsing; long text is automatically truncated in previews
- Click a card to open a popup with full details (source, time, tags)
- Supports three sources: manual input, voice entry, and clipboard capture

### 🍅 Pomodoro Focus
- 25/5/15-minute Pomodoro timer, can be linked to to-do tasks; supports a Mini always-on-top floating window and Zen mode (`Esc` to exit quickly)
- Automatically records the duration of each focus session
