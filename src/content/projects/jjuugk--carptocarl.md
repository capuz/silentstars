# CarPToCarL — Let an Android phone act as a CarPlay sender and project native CarLife to the car head unit

An ordinary Android phone (not rooted) plays the **CarPlay sender (iPhone role)** and connects to a head unit that supports CarPlay. The screen content comes from the **H.264 stream of the phone's native CarLife+ engine**, which this app intercepts and **passes through** to the head unit (no re-encoding).

> **Unofficial project**: a personal experimental work. It is not affiliated with Apple Inc., Baidu, or any automaker, is not authorized or certified, and is **not an MFi product**. CarPlay / AirPlay / CarLife are trademarks of their respective owners and appear only to indicate compatibility targets.
>
> **Driving safety**: configure and operate only while parked. Relying on this solution while driving may interrupt navigation, so use the factory navigation as a safety backup.
