# Neetan (ねーたん)

An emulator for the PC-9821, PC-9801, PC-88VA2, PC-8801, PC-8001, PC-6601, PC-6001,
MSX, Fujitsu FM Towns, Fujitsu FM-7, Sharp X68000, Sharp X1 and the IBM PC/AT
(DOS/V) written in Rust.
