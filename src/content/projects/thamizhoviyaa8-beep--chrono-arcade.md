# ⏱️ ChronoFinger — The Reflex Metronome for Analog Clock Mastery

**Read any clock face in under 0.8 seconds — train your temporal intuition with a gamified, neuroscience-backed practice arena.**

ChronoFinger is not another clock-reading worksheet. It is a **cognitive reflex gymnasium** where your brain learns to translate the angular dance of hour and minute hands into instant, subconscious time awareness. Whether you are preparing for timed exams, improving your situational awareness in high-stakes professions, or simply want to win every "what time is it?" bet among friends, ChronoFinger transforms a mundane skill into a lightning-fast neural pathway.

Built as the spiritual successor to `clockTrainer` (a minimalist practice tool), ChronoFinger introduces **adaptive difficulty curves, real-time performance analytics, and a competitive global leaderboard** — all wrapped in a tactile, responsive interface that feels like a precision instrument rather than a flashcard app.
