# 🌻 Bee Swarm Simulator Quest & Field Helper — Tokens, Routes & Profiles

**Bee Swarm Simulator Quest & Field Helper** focuses on bear quests, field presets, pollen routes, token collection, honey conversion thresholds, event profiles, and saved configurations.
