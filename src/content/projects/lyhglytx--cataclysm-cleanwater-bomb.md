# Cataclysm: Cleanwater Bomb
"Cataclysm: Cleanwater Bomb" is a roguelike single-player turn-based survival game set in a post-apocalyptic background with a science fantasy setting.

In this game, you will face a procedurally generated complex environment filled with constantly resurrecting zombies, harsh and ongoing survival pressures, and terrifying incursions from otherworldly realms. You need to struggle to survive in a broken world, searching for the remnants of a dying civilization, looking for food and equipment. In the future, we hope to add some cool gameplay elements, such as industrialization and rebuilding homes.

Based on [CleverRaven/Cataclysm-DDA](https://github.com/CleverRaven/Cataclysm-DDA) `cdda/master` branch commit: **221c786e7d**
