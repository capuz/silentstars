# 🧳 TraceDot · 迹点点

**Turn every ticket stub into a map of China that belongs to you.**

An integrated travel souvenir ticket studio: **ten ticket designs** can be made, collected, plotted on a map, and strung into a travel chronicle, all in one place.
It is a pure front-end single HTML file, about 12 MB. Double-click to open it, it works offline, and your data never leaves the browser.
