Bring Meizu's Flyme small-window interaction to ColorOS: swipe diagonally inward and upward from a bottom corner of the screen to bring up a fan-shaped menu, slide to the target and release to immediately open it as a freeform floating window.

## Preview

## Features

### Fan menu

- **Trigger**: swipe diagonally inward and upward from a bottom corner; options: left and right, left only, right only
- **Stay active**: the menu stays open after release, so you can keep sliding to pick an item
- **Range and size**: corner detection zone 24–160 dp, icon hit radius 40–140 dp, icons 32–56 dp; the screen previews live while dragging
- **Pause**: landscape and game mode only pause triggering; it resumes automatically after exiting

### Fan apps

Up to six items; apps, clones, sidebar tools and quick entries can be mixed. Long-press and drag to reorder; sliding over a slot enlarges it as feedback, and releasing launches it.

### “More” panel

The “More” item at the end of the fan is taken from the smart sidebar’s “All”, and supports favorites, recents and name search; it closes automatically when the fan is opened.
