### What is Neon?

**Neon** is an all-in-one, **client-only assistant toolkit** built specifically for Mindustry.

Whether you focus on frontline combat, love blueprint building, enjoy multiplayer teamplay, or dig into map making and logic programming, Neon brings scattered useful assist features together into **a single mod**. No more hunting through a dozen separate mods: one install package, one unified settings menu, and one clean floating panel handle everything.
