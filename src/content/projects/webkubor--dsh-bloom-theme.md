### Why choose Bloom?

| 15 palettes · 12 curated | ≥ 4.5:1 | 0 runtime dependencies |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Cinnabar, Qing Lian, Dai Blue and more: low-saturation Chinese-style palettes, each with a light and a dark version | Primary and background colors of the 15 palettes: 30 contrast pairs in total, all ≥ 4.5:1 | No extra runtime dependencies; one-click theme switch in the top bar |

- **Colors that are both pleasant and clear.** Colors are tuned in OKLCH, keeping readable colors for text and buttons separate from large ambient background colors. When the color changes, the panels, input areas, and background change together.
- **Glass and backgrounds, mix freely.** Three materials: liquid, frosted, and blur glass. Light watercolor backgrounds and dark backgrounds are each remembered separately. At night, open 'Starry Sky': starlight and shooting stars change with the theme color.
