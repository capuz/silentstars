Testing VR games on Linux. See [linux-gaming-compat](https://github.com/cubee-cb/linux-gaming-compat) for non-VR titles.

Subfolders include scripts or configs that I use. Configs are available under [dotfiles](https://github.com/cubee-cb/linux-vr-compat/blob/HEAD/dotfiles). See [build-scripts](https://github.com/cubee-cb/xr-build-scripts) for, well, build scripts I use for my XR Stack.
