description: "大肥鱼Dock (BigFishDockpro) is a desktop organization and beautification tool for Windows 10 / 11 x64. Frequently used apps can be dragged into the Dock, arranged in groups, launched quickly, and managed in batches. Files, folders, and shortcuts can be automatically sorted into desktop cards, which can be placed independently, merged into paged tabs, and searched. When collapsed, resource capsules show key readings and reminders; when expanded, they show hardware status, network traffic, weather, AI usage, and schedules. The desktop can also add sticky notes, clocks, a lunar calendar, a daily image, and a music spectrum on demand. The Dock and desktop widgets support multiple materials, including liquid glass, and colors, transparency, and layout can be freely adjusted. The Windows 11 Start menu, native search, and notification panel can also adopt the Dock's appearance. Idle hidden pages automatically release resources and load on demand when reopened."
