> **Unofficial project | Open-source public service | Information aggregation** A static front-end project for querying advisor and faculty information from the School of Biological Science and Medical Engineering and the School of Medical Science and Engineering at Beihang University. It focuses on verifiable public advisor selection support: it prioritizes public sources such as official school websites and faculty homepages, and provides search, research direction filtering, data completeness hints, and lightweight decision support without introducing subjective rankings.

[Live system](https://donaldjoker2025-arch.github.io/my-buaa-app/)

## Project Goals

This project does not replace official school websites, the annual admissions catalog, or confirmation by advisors themselves. Its goal is to organize scattered public pages into a workspace better suited for narrowing down options in the early stage:

* Quickly narrow candidates by school, research direction, and keywords
* Continue verification through homepages, email addresses, admissions clues, and data completeness
* Finally, confirm through official websites and advisor homepages
