## 🎮 What is This?

**FC-FIFA-Mod-Manager** is the ultimate all-in-one mod management solution for **EA Sports FC 26, FC 27, FC 25, FC 24** and all **FIFA** games from FIFA 19 to FIFA 23. It supports .fifamod and .fbmod files, providing drag & drop import, load order control, conflict detection, BepInEx integration, and a powerful Live Editor for Career Mode. Perfect for enhancing graphics, kits, faces, stadiums, gameplay, and career mode.
