## Overview

Baroid Core Practice and Study Portal is an HTML-based learning application for people preparing for Baroid Core Exam material. Its study content brings together theory quizzes, business-process practice, product information, exam formulas, and drilling-fluids exercises in a responsive browser interface.

Alongside its review material, the portal offers interactive calculation and operational practice. Available activities include API 13B-1 alkalinity, barite mass balance, solids analysis, hole volume, and circulation.
