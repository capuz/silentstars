## What it can do

- **One-click image sending**: Click 😊 next to the input box, search, filter by library or mood, and click an image to send it.
- **Proactive memes**: The AI picks images on its own to match the chat mood and joins in without waiting for you. In the settings page, you can turn "AI proactively sends stickers" on or off, set the send probability (0–100%), or rewrite the prompt to adjust the style.
- **IM / mobile memes**: QQ, WeChat, Feishu, and others connected through dsh-im send stickers as native image messages, with no text markers needed. On a phone or tablet, open DSH Web through the computer's LAN IP, and turn on "LAN access to library" in the settings page to send and receive (off by default, see below). Large QQ images are easily stretched by the client as image messages. You can turn on "QQ send size" in the settings page to shrink a copy to 200–450px before sending, while Web still uses the original.
