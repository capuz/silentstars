# usdtweak

usdtweak is a free and open source editor for [OpenUSD](https://graphics.pixar.com/usd/release/index.html#). usdtweak can already be used for small and simple tasks like cleaning or preparing assets, creating and editing layers, inspecting and fixing usd stages. It works on windows, macos and linux.

This project is written in C++ and is powered by [ImGUI](https://github.com/ocornut/imgui) for the UI and [GLFW](https://github.com/glfw/glfw) for the windowing system.

## Status

usdtweak has been actively developed since 2020. It started as an idea to bring editing to usdview, for artists, technical directors and anyone who doesn't know the OpenUSD ascii syntax or Python. Today it is a full editor for stages and layers, and the goal driving development is to match usdview's functionality while making everything editable.

Development is independent and community-driven. Feedback, bug reports and contributions directly shape the roadmap.
