# ShuangxiuGo · 双休购 (Two-Day Weekend Shopping)

Turn the KPIs your boss uses to evaluate you into voting currency that workers use to evaluate the boss.

> **Project scope**: This repository is the main site of ShuangxiuGo (双休购), deployed as a static GitHub Pages site. The account also hosts two other repositories: `shuangxiugou-guide` (a static backup of the info site) and `shuangxiu-go-archive` (an archive of a third-party site). Their data is not mixed with this main site.
>
> **Inclusion principles**: Objective and neutral, with no emotional wording. Cases with incomplete evidence chains are still rated, not avoided. Ratings are based only on monthly rest days, and U-level ratings must include an 'evidence-missing note'. The relationship between the two sites' data, the full rating criteria, and past corrections are documented in **[README-详情.md](https://github.com/dujeongil-galaxy/shuangxiugou/blob/HEAD/README-详情.md#scope)**. This page keeps only a summary to avoid making the homepage too long.
