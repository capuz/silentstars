# 双休购 · ShuangxiuGo

Turn the KPIs your boss uses to evaluate you into currency votes that workers use to evaluate the boss.

> **Project positioning**: This repository is the main site of 「双休购」 (deployed as a static GitHub Pages site). The account also has two other repositories: `shuangxiugou-guide` (a static backup of the information site) and `shuangxiu-go-archive` (an archive of a third-party site). Their data is not mixed with this main site.

> **Inclusion principles**: Objective and neutral, with no emotional wording. Even when the evidence chain is incomplete, rulings are not avoided. Ratings are based only on the number of rest days per month, and U-grade entries must include an “evidence missing note”. The data relationship between the two sites, the full rating criteria, and past corrections are documented in **[README-详情.md](https://github.com/dujeongil-galaxy/shuangxiugou/blob/HEAD/README-详情.md#scope)**. Only a summary is kept here to avoid making the homepage too long.
