# ShuangxiuGo

Turns the KPIs your boss uses to evaluate you into ballot votes that workers use to evaluate the boss.

> **Project scope**: This repository is the main site of Shuangxiugou (双休购) (GitHub Pages static deployment). The account also has two other repositories: `shuangxiugou-guide` (static backup of the info site) and `shuangxiu-go-archive` (archive of a third-party site). Their data is not mixed with this main site.

> **Inclusion principles**: objective and neutral, with no emotional wording. Rulings are not avoided even when the evidence chain is incomplete. Grading is based only on monthly rest days, and U-level ratings must include an 'evidence missing' note. The data relationship between the two sites, the full grading criteria, and past corrections are written in **[README-详情.md](https://github.com/dujeongil-galaxy/shuangxiugou/blob/HEAD/README-详情.md#scope)**. Only a summary is kept here, to avoid an overly long homepage.
