## Features

- **React instantly** with ultra-low latency streaming and decoding.
- **See every detail** with AV1, HEVC and H.264 video, plus HDR10.
- **Keep motion fluid** with high refresh rates and VRR like ARR frame pacing.
- **Sharpen your stream** with client-side FSR and Snapdragon GSR upscaling.
- **Play your way** with USB and Bluetooth controllers, including DualSense.
- **Stay in control** with touch, trackpad, mouse, keyboard and [on-screen keys and desktop shortcuts](https://github.com/RamazanKara/rubylight-android/blob/HEAD/docs/controls.md).
- **Choose your PC display** with per-PC exclusive, extended and separated display layouts on compatible hosts. Separated displays share the same Windows session. See the [host feature matrix](https://github.com/RamazanKara/rubylight-android/blob/HEAD/docs/PARITY-MATRIX.md).
- **Choose a Snapdragon GPU driver** for PyroWave with a compatible driver ZIP and system-driver fallback. See [driver requirements](https://github.com/RamazanKara/rubylight-android/blob/HEAD/docs/video-and-latency.md).
- **Know your stream** with Artemis-style Statistics and detailed Debug overlays for frame rates,…
