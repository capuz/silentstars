# Komiho

**Native Android comic reader**

Supports multiple comic sources, including **Komga, local files, WebDAV, SMB**, and more

Package name `cn.ruzhe.komiho` | Version 1.5.0 (14) | Android 8.0+

[Chinese](https://github.com/ruzhe85/Komiho/blob/HEAD/README.md) | [English](https://github.com/ruzhe85/Komiho/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

---

## ✨ Features

### 📚 Multi-Source Reading

Komiho brings comics from different sources together into one unified browsing and reading experience:

* **Komga** — Connects to a self-hosted Komga instance to browse libraries, series, and comics, with support for multiple servers, multiple libraries, and reading progress sync.
* **Local files** — Browse and read comic files directly on the device.
* **WebDAV** — Connects to WebDAV services such as NAS devices, allowing you to browse and read remote comics online without downloading entire volumes.
* **SMB / CIFS** — Accesses shared folders on the local network and reads network comics directly.

### 📦 Supported Formats

Format support varies slightly between sources:

* **Direct reading from Local / WebDAV / SMB**: comic archives `CBZ` `CBR` `CB7` `CBT` `ZIP` `RAR` `7Z` `TAR` `PDF` `EPUB`, as well…
