# AI_ChatterBox

### Boardroom and character chat (1.0.0)

- Press `Alt+Space` to open the Boardroom. The master nominates an assignee, and after the assignee's presentation and participant review, the results are compiled. While the answer is being written, the speaker's character moves.
- In the **ChatterBox** and **Boardroom** tabs of the remote web, you can open project chat and meeting rooms separately. Boardroom meeting records and progress are shared with the Windows meeting room.
- In **Connection settings → Character chat model**, you can choose the model used for individual conversations and meetings. Both chats display Markdown tables and web links, and you can open previews of public image links.
- In the Windows meeting room, when you select a research folder, it reviews based on the extracted text excerpts it finds. `Win+Space` opens an individual chat with the master.
