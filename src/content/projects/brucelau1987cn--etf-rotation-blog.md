# ETF Compass

A research and trading decision dashboard for A-shares, Hong Kong stocks, US stocks, and commodity futures: real-time quotes, rolling long/short signals, ETF rotation, macro constraints, historical validation, and research auditing. The frontend is built with Astro; production runs on Cloudflare Pages + Functions + D1.
