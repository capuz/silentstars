# Vital

**A personal operating system built AI First**: Capture (Inbox) → Execute (Todos) → Reflect (Reports).

> [English documentation](https://github.com/ximing/vital/blob/HEAD/README_EN.md)

Vital is a Chinese-first (`zh-CN`), single-user, self-hosted personal productivity system. Todos, read-later items, habits, threads, and reviews live together in one calm workspace. **Internally, a resident Agent organizes, breaks down, drafts, and remembers your preferences for you. Externally, it provides a skill so that Claude, Codex, and other Agents can operate on the same data directly.**

No chasing collaboration, no manufactured anxiety. One account is everything.

## Product Philosophy

Vital is **AI First** and also pursues **Calm Productivity**. When you open it, you see "what to do next" first, not the system itself. The Agent works in the background and proposes changes that only take effect once you approve them.

- **One closed loop, not a pile of tools.** Drop links into the Inbox first; once read, convert them into tasks with one click. Completed tasks flow automatically into the day's review, and ideas captured in reviews become…
