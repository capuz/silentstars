# CyberView

**Live site: https://cyberview.tools** · [Archive](https://cyberview.tools/archive.html) · [Trends](https://cyberview.tools/trends.html)

A live, verified picture of cyber threats worldwide for defenders: what happened, what is being exploited, and the outages around it, updated around the clock.

CyberView is always growing; its full, current description is generated from [`config/about.json`](https://github.com/xanderhutch/CyberView/blob/HEAD/config/about.json) and published on the [About page](https://cyberview.tools/about.html#what), and CI fails if a new page, view, map layer or routine is added without an entry there.
