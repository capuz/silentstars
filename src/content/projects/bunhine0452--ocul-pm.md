When an agent finishes a task, it says "Done." That statement does not show whether the tests actually passed, which files were changed and why, or whether anything was missed. To check, you have to reread the diff and logs from the beginning, and the next day's new conversation doesn't even have that context.

```text
/plugin marketplace add bunhine0452/Ocul-PM
/plugin install oculpm@oculpm
```

With these two lines in Claude Code, you're ready to start (no app needed). To view the records on screen, [download the app](https://github.com/bunhine0452/Ocul-PM/releases/latest)…
