# Genshin University Alliance

Welcome to the Genshin University Alliance project! This project aims to collect and organize lists of Genshin-related universities across the country (currently 312 universities, of which 83 are valid), providing a campus hub.

Non-university institutions may also be added~

If you like this project, feel free to give it a star on [GitHub](https://github.com/cokice/List-of-genshin-University).
**This list prioritizes displaying valid domains; within the same validity group, entries are sorted alphabetically by school name**
