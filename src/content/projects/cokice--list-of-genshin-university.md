# Genshin Impact University Alliance

Welcome to the Genshin Impact University Alliance project! This project aims to collect and organize a list of universities related to Genshin Impact across the country (currently there are 277 universities, of which 66 are valid), providing a campus gathering place.

Non-universities can also be added!

If you like this project, you can give it a star on [GitHub](https://github.com/cokice/List-of-genshin-University).
**The list is arranged in alphabetical order by school name**
