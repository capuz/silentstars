# OSRS TCG

A RuneLite plugin that adds a light collectible card experience. Play to earn credits, buy booster packs, collect cards, and trade with other players.

Join the community on [Discord](https://discord.gg/P4pPu6RnCj).

## Acknowledgments

Parts of this plugin were inspired by other RuneLite plugins:

- [Monster Monitor](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/monster-monitor) NPC kill credit tracking
- [Customizable XP Drops](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/customizable-xp-drops) XP drop value tracking

Card artwork is hosted on [osrs-tcg.net](https://osrs-tcg.net). Catalog entries may link to [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/) article pages.

## Disclaimer

OSRS TCG is a fan-made game for fun. Cards have no real-world or in-game value.
Do not buy or sell cards for money, bonds, gold, or items. Trade at your own risk.

## Local data

Per-account gameplay data (saves, attest spills) lives under `~/.runelite/OSRS-TCG/profiles/{sha256(accountHash)}/`. Legacy saves under `backups/` are used only for cloud migration upload.
