# Folio

  Research markets, understand your exposure, and maintain evidence-based judgments over time, with a clear view of what has changed.

> In one sentence: a market terminal tells you **What happened**; Folio's Agent helps you answer **Why does this matter to you**, records **What did you believe before**, and keeps tracking **What changed**.

## Our Product

Folio is a desktop research environment built for public market investors. It combines a calm, tidy financial workspace with an Agent copilot. The Agent can retrieve structured market data, explain evidence, and carry research forward into investment theses, alerts, and portfolio decisions.

Folio is local-first: sessions, credentials, and research state are stored on your device by default. Market data and model providers are explicit integrations, not hidden dependencies.

> Folio is a research and decision-support tool. By design it is read-only and provides no order placement or trading capability.
