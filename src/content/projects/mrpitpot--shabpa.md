# Shabpa

**A night watch for your Windows PC** - scheduled power control, gaming overlay, network tools, daily tasks, backups and system health in one light app.

[**Download**](https://github.com/MrPitPot/shabpa/releases/latest/download/ShabpaSetup.exe) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Website](https://shabpa.ir) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [فارسی](https://github.com/MrPitPot/shabpa/blob/HEAD/README.fa.md) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Changelog](https://github.com/MrPitPot/shabpa/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Telegram](https://t.me/ShabPa_App)

Code: [GitHub](https://github.com/MrPitPot/shabpa) ([releases](https://github.com/MrPitPot/shabpa/releases) and [issues](https://github.com/MrPitPot/shabpa/issues))

Shabpa started as a shutdown timer and grew into an everyday Windows assistant. It runs in the tray, keeps
your data on your PC, and speaks Persian and English with 21 colour themes.
