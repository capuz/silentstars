# xiaobaOS

  **An IM-native AI coworker Runtime that can deliver, be replayed, and evolve under control.**

  Send tasks through CLI, IM, or desktop. Base is responsible for communication and dispatching, while specialized Roles take over execution and return files, messages, and verifiable evidence; real traces can accumulate candidate capabilities, but only after being accepted by the Arena and explicitly promoted by humans can they enter production.

  <em>Complete work like a colleague, evolve like software.</em>

  [Quick Start Source Code](#快速开始) · [macOS v0.2.0 Preview](https://github.com/fightheyyy/xiaobaOS/releases/tag/v0.2.0) · [Working Method](#工作与进化) · [Controlled Evolution](#受控进化) · [English](https://github.com/fightheyyy/xiaobaOS/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)
