# CarPulse (formerly Subaru Dashboard, SSM, K-Line / CAN)

On-board computer for Subaru on an Android head unit (Teyes and similar) or a phone: gauges in any design, fuel consumption, engine and AT (4EAT) fault codes, a smart analyzer based on factory manuals, a 3D car and 3D engine, its own media player, a voice assistant, extra sensors on Arduino, and color USB displays.

Works with Subaru via the **SSM2** protocol (K-Line up to ~2008 and CAN on SH 2009+) through a **USB KKL cable**, **Tactrix OpenPort**, or **ELM327**, as well as with standard **OBD-II** and other cars. Experimental: sports ECU **Link G4+/G4X** over CAN.

Built and tested on a **Subaru Forester SG5 2003 (JDM, EJ20 turbo, 4EAT)** with a **Teyes CC3** head unit (Android 10, 1280×720). Owners of Forester SG/SH, Impreza, Legacy and other Subarus use it and send feedback.
