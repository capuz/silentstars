# Gier Revitalized
<br>

> This mod might be incompatible with other mods
<br>

### Gier Revitalized is intended to create what gier would’ve been, but within my vision.<br></div>

**Features:**</div>
- Endless gier sectors
- Randomly Generated gier sectors (Currently bugged)
- large amounts of modded blocks
- IOS support
- And split campaign on a different planet called kela

## Though expect: </div>
- Unfair Generation (Bound to happen)
- Constant Updating (This mod updates frequently but most are small)
