## Overview

CoreOne CAD is designed for FiveM dispatch setups that need a web-accessible way to manage incidents and coordinate response work. It pairs CAD-style dispatch controls with a contemporary browser interface, letting operators keep track of active situations without depending on a heavyweight local client.

This version is centered on the practical demands of dispatch work: receiving incidents as they arrive, seeing assigned units at a glance, and keeping decisions anchored to a map-first layout. Since it runs in the browser, it fits neatly into existing environments where fast access and minimal friction are important.
