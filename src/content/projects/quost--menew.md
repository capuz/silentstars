## Highlights

### 🧠 Memory — <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>K</kbd>

Never lose what crossed your screen. Press ⌃⌥⌘K and pick an area or window: Menew keeps the image, reads the text and notes where it came from (app, window and the browser tab’s link). With Apple Intelligence it adds a title, a one-line summary, tags and the named things in it (tools, people, projects). Search it all in the Memory section or the palette, or save a copied text or image from the clipboard with one click. **Ask your memory** in plain words (“which was that charts library in Swift?”, or “remember …” in the palette): the Mac’s AI turns the question into search terms with synonyms and translations, looks through memory, notes and clipboard, and answers with numbered sources you can open. Memory items also show up in Spotlight.
