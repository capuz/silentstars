# Invest Series: Investment Analysis Guide

Buying a fund is like hiring a steward; buying stocks is like going into business as a partner. People who don't understand, people who aren't reliable, and people who charge too much cannot be entrusted with your money.

This tool helps you with three things: **see clearly what you are investing in, judge whether it is worth investing in, and determine whether the price is reasonable**.

**Supports 17 data sources (APIs) and 11 analysis methodologies**:
- **Data sources**: no-key sources (Tencent Quotes / SEC EDGAR / FRED / yfinance) + configurable sources (Tonghuashun / East Money / Miaoxiang / FMP / Tiingo / Wind / Yingmi / Tiantian Fund official ttskill) + long-tail capabilities (Tencent Micro Securities, 40+ subcommands)
- **Methodologies**: covering individual stocks, funds, fund selection, single assets (bonds / convertible bonds / commodities / REITs), asset allocation, risk management, in-depth company analysis, macro environment, institutional-grade outputs, and multi-perspective consultation
