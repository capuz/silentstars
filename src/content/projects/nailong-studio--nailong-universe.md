# NAIWA ART UNIVERSE · Naiwa Art Sanctuary

**Masterpieces as its body · joy as its light**

> The museum officially declares: Naiwa is neither a frog nor a Nailong, but a yellow emotion that reproduces itself on the internet.
> The museum's duty is to provide this emotion with a resting place that complies with museum fire-safety regulations.
> Admission is free; laughter is at your own expense.

> **Offering box (aka the Star button):** The sanctuary charges no entry fee, only stars. Click the ⭐ at the top right and you have dropped a lamp into the donation box. Naiwa will bless your memes so they never run out.
