# Lolly

**Make graphics, documents and video from simple inputs.**

Lolly is an open-source creative platform. Pick a tool, add your words, images or data, and download a finished file. Your design system supplies the colours, fonts and assets.

Its purpose is **content sovereignty**: control over how your content is created, governed, stored and moved across the systems you choose. Keep making and editing your work, use your own infrastructure where needed, and connect an existing asset library. [What content sovereignty means](https://github.com/lolly-tools/lolly/blob/HEAD/docs/content-sovereignty.md) explains the controls and their limits.

Build a layout once, choose what other people can change, and share it as a tool. They fill in the fields; the tool handles the layout and design rules.
