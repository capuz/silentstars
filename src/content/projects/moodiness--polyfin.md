Polyfin is a self-hosted server that speaks the Jellyfin API. It turns Stremio addons, music addons, IPTV sources and your own folders into libraries that any Jellyfin app plays, with real user accounts and transcoding, and without Jellyfin installed.

## What you get

- **Your addons as libraries:** Stremio catalogs become libraries and collections, and their streams become versions of a title, torrents too once you turn them on. [Addons](https://github.com/moodiness/polyfin/blob/HEAD/docs/addons.md), [Libraries](https://github.com/moodiness/polyfin/blob/HEAD/docs/addons-and-libraries.md), [Torrents](https://github.com/moodiness/polyfin/blob/HEAD/docs/torrents.md)
- **Your own files:** folders on the server, SMB and WebDAV shares, S3 buckets, SFTP servers and rclone remotes. [Local folders](https://github.com/moodiness/polyfin/blob/HEAD/docs/local-folders.md), [Naming and matching files](https://github.com/moodiness/polyfin/blob/HEAD/docs/naming-and-matching.md)
- **Live TV and IPTV:** M3U playlists, Xtream Codes accounts and HDHomeRun tuners, with a programme guide, recordings and Replay.
