## Why Choose RegisterHub?

Traditional automation scripts often come as bare command-line black boxes, are hard-wired to a single website, or depend heavily on a full Docker stack or paid third-party captcha-solving / SMS-receiving platforms. **RegisterHub Open-Source Community Edition** packages a full production-grade workbench as **zero Docker, zero Node.js, pure Python standard library driven**, ready to use out of the box:

- **Zero-external-dependency desktop-class Studio workbench**: double-click `run_web.bat` (on macOS/Linux, run `./run_web.sh`) or run `python main.py --web` to instantly open the full-featured control panel (`http://127.0.0.1:18886`), with one-click **dark / light theme switching** and multiple classic color schemes.
