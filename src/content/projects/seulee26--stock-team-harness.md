# Stock Research Harness · AI Research Center and Hedge Fund Team

Ask an AI about stocks, and it will split the work across multiple teams, like a brokerage research center and a hedge fund. They investigate separately, cross-verify each other, and organize the results into a **Korean-language report that even people who don't know stocks can read**. It is used with Claude Code and Codex.

- Just ask in your everyday way, like "Should I buy Samsung Electronics now?" or "Analyze NVIDIA."
- 34 AI agents work across strategy, news, US stocks, Korean stocks, industries, ETFs, futures and macro, quant, risk, tax, editing and compliance, documents and wiki, and broadcasting.
- Calculates Korean investment taxes, such as capital gains tax on stocks, dividend tax, gift tax, and tax-saving accounts, with the legal basis.
- Scores 28 open-source GitHub projects on five public criteria. It calls 6 directly through commands and implements part of the calculation method for 4 directly.
- Reports you create can be stored in a research wiki on your PC, Google Drive, or Notion, and revisited later.
