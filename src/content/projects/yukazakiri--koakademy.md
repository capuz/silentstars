# 🎓 KoAkademy

**School management that institutions can own — and developers can help shape.**

An open-source, self-hosted platform for enrollment, academics, finance, and day-to-day school operations. Built with real school workflows in mind, starting in the Philippines.

[Explore the project](#what-is-koakademy) · [Run it locally](#try-koakademy-locally) · [View the architecture](#under-the-hood) · [Contribute](#join-the-community)

> **Caution**
>
> **Beta / not production-ready.** KoAkademy is evolving and has [open security, data-integrity, and performance audit findings](https://github.com/yukazakiri/koakademy/issues/440). **Do not expose it to the public internet with real student, staff, health, or financial records until those issues have been resolved and the deployment has been independently reviewed.** Explore with synthetic data in an isolated development or staging environment. See [current limitations](#status-and-limitations) and [security guidance](https://github.com/yukazakiri/koakademy/blob/HEAD/SECURITY.md).

## What is KoAkademy?

Every school deserves tools it can understand, adapt, and operate without being locked into someone else's pricing or roadmap.
