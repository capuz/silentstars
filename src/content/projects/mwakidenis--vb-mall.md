## 👋 Welcome  <a name="introduction"></a>
This is an e-commerce platform designed to empower villagers of Nchiru by providing them with a nationwide marketplace to sell their eco-friendly products across Kenya. This platform bridges the gap between rural artisans and urban consumers, ensuring that their sustainable creations reach a wider audience.
