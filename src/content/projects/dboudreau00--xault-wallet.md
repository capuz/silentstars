Encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM (Argon2id). Drives the official <code>monero-wallet-rpc</code> and never
  reimplements Monero's cryptography. Its own Tor, verified when installed and fail-closed while it runs,
  with circuits of its own for every wallet. Built in: a duress decoy against coercion, and anti-clipboard
  hijacker, anti-InfoStealer, anti-remote desktop and anti-hVNC protection against malware (the last two
  with Enhanced Security, on Windows).
