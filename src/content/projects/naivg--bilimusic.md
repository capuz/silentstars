# BiliMusic

BiliMusic is a Bilibili music player written in Flutter. One codebase covers Windows, Linux, Android, and HarmonyOS, with experimental support for macOS and iOS. It also includes a Xiaomi Vela watch client and a Chrome extension.
It only does what a music player should: search, organize, continuous playback, and cross-device sync.
