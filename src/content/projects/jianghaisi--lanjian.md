Lanjian (岚笺) is a local novel-writing tool. The manuscript, reference materials, character profiles, outlines, and story events all live in the same book project. You can write on your own, or connect a model, ask the AI assistant (助笔) to discuss a plot passage, and then decide which content to keep.

The current release is **0.1 Developer Preview**, which provides a Windows x64 installer. Many details still need polishing, and feedback is welcome from anyone who tries it with real writing problems. Keep backups of important works.

## Why Lanjian

When writing a long novel, there is a lot of tedious work beyond typing: checking what a character said earlier, finding an inspiration you noted a few days ago, confirming the order of two events, and syncing a revised setting into the outline. With a lot of material, flipping back and forth between documents, notes, and chat logs can easily break your train of thought.

Lanjian aims to bring this work together. Open a book and you can find its manuscript, characters, references, and plans. When you get stuck, you can also discuss the problem with the book's background at hand, without re-explaining it every time.
