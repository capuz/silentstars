# Pragma Chess

An open source chess database for studying, training and playing: light,
simple, with what really matters. For Windows, macOS and Linux.

**Web site: <https://yafb.net/pragma-chess/>** (English, Italian and Spanish, with
screenshots, downloads and [the guide](https://yafb.net/pragma-chess/en/guide/)) ·
**[Discussions](https://github.com/francescobianco/pragma-chess/discussions)**
for questions and ideas · **[Issues](https://github.com/francescobianco/pragma-chess/issues)**
for problems. **[Contributing](https://github.com/francescobianco/pragma-chess/blob/HEAD/CONTRIBUTING.md)**: report a wrong
explanation, translate, write code. See [DESIGN.md](https://github.com/francescobianco/pragma-chess/blob/HEAD/DESIGN.md) for the vision and
architecture, [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/francescobianco/pragma-chess/blob/HEAD/CHANGELOG.md) for what changed, and
[DISTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/francescobianco/pragma-chess/blob/HEAD/DISTRIBUTING.md) for how we make it known.
