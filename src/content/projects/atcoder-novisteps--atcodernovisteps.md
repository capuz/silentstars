# AtCoder NoviSteps

    [Unofficial] A website for recording your progress on problems from the competitive programming site <a href="https://atcoder.jp/">AtCoder</a>.<br>
    Each problem is finely graded by difficulty level, allowing you to gradually acquire the necessary knowledge.

## Features

- **You can record your answer status yourself**: You can choose from "AC (Correct Answer)", "Explanation AC", "In Progress", "Not Attempted".
- **Challenge problems one step ahead**: Problems are graded in 17 difficulty levels, making it easy to find problems suited to your skill level.
- **Grow strengths and overcome weaknesses with problem sets**: Through example problems and related problems, you can learn from basics to advanced methods for each topic.
- **Grow content with the community**: As voting aligns difficulty evaluations for problems, it becomes easier to practice according to your skill level.
