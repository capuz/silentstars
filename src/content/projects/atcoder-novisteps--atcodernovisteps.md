# AtCoder NoviSteps

    [Unofficial] A site for recording your progress on problems from the competitive programming site <a href="https://atcoder.jp/">AtCoder</a>.<br>
    Each problem is finely graded by difficulty, so you can gradually build the knowledge you need.

## Features

- **Record your own answer status**: Choose from "AC (Accepted)", "AC with editorial", "Attempting", or "Not attempted".
- **Take on problems one step ahead**: Problems are graded on 17 levels, so you can find problems that match your skill.
- **Strengthen your strengths and overcome weaknesses with problem sets**: Through example problems and similar problems, you can learn each topic from the basics to advanced methods.
- **Grow the content with the community**: The more the difficulty ratings of problems converge through voting, the easier it becomes to practice at your skill level.
