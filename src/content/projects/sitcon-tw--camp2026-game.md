## Staff Guidelines for Distributing Small Stones

When distributing small stones, staff should prioritize them as a record of "what the participants have done in the camp" rather than simply as a reward for combat power. The principle is to first give basic small stones, then evolution items; only for more difficult, complete, or particularly outstanding questions and tasks should high-level small stones be given directly.

### Distribution Levels

| Level | When to Give | Content Distributed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | Participation in courses, answering introductory questions correctly, completing basic tasks, first demonstration of corresponding behavior | Basic small stones of the corresponding type |
| First-level Items | Answering basic question sets correctly, completing a small exercise, providing clear but limited responses | First-level evolution items of the corresponding branch |
| First-level Small Stones | Answering medium questions correctly, completing a segment of practical work, able to explain "why" | First-level small stones of the corresponding branch |
