## Why Choose Freecat Blog

**Data in Your Own Hands**

- Article manuscripts are saved on your computer and GitHub repository
- Even if cloud deployment or platform services fail, you won't lose ownership of your content
- Not locked in by a single platform, you can back up, migrate, or republish anytime

**Powerful Features**

- Blog posts can be freely pinned and hidden
- Supports custom one or multiple tags
- Markdown supports rendering mathematical formulas, charts, flowcharts, sequence diagrams, Gantt charts, etc.
- Supports audio and video playback
- Supports external embed display for regular websites
- Long code blocks are automatically collapsed with built-in floating controllers for back-to-top/bottom and expand/collapse

**Beginner-Friendly**

- You can choose not to write metadata at all and directly write the main content
- Through simple filling or checkboxes in Markdown metadata and control files, you can intuitively customize website appearance,…
