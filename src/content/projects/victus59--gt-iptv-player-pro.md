# GT IPTV Player Pro 1.9.7-r0

Added **mHub v2 support for Live TV**. Enter your own manifest URL to browse categories and play channels directly from the source.

Press the **green button** on the **Add Source** or **Manage Sources** screen to open mHub v2.

**HD Satelit compatibility note**

On the tested HD Satelit image, category names may appear blank on the Movies screen. The issue was reported to the HD Satelit team.

Signed stable updates: Settings > Software update. Publishing: [UPDATING.md](https://github.com/VicTuS59/GT-IPTV-Player-Pro/blob/HEAD/UPDATING.md).
