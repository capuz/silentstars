# Lingolay

**Play any game. Watch any video. In your language — in real time, fully offline.**

Lingolay reads the subtitles on your screen, translates them with Meta's NLLB‑200 neural model running *on your own PC*,<br>
shows the translation in a floating overlay — and can even **dub it out loud** with a natural neural voice.

[**⬇ Download for Windows**](https://github.com/keremss7/lingolay/releases/latest) · [Quick start](#-quick-start) · [How it works](#-how-it-works) · [Contributing](#-contributing) · [🇹🇷 Türkçe](https://github.com/keremss7/lingolay/blob/HEAD/docs/README.tr.md) · [🇷🇺 Русский](https://github.com/keremss7/lingolay/blob/HEAD/docs/README.ru.md)

> 🆕 **New in v2.5: Translate screen.** Press `Ctrl+Alt+T` and every text on the screen (menus, notes, item descriptions, HUD) is translated **in place**, in the game's own style, like a camera translator.
