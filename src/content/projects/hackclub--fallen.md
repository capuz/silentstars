### A 4-day hardware adventure in Shenzhen, China!

- [`platform/`](https://github.com/hackclub/fallen/blob/HEAD/platform/) is the main app & setup instructions are in [`platform/README.md`](https://github.com/hackclub/fallen/blob/HEAD/platform/README.md).
- [`program/`](https://github.com/hackclub/fallen/blob/HEAD/program/) breaks down how we're organizing this event!

## Who can join?

Any teenager aged 13 to 18 (inclusive)!

## Is this legit?

Yes! [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and we've run programs like
[Fallout](https://fallout.hackclub.com) and [Outpost](https://outpost.hackclub.com) before.

## Is this really free? What's the catch?

No catch! Fallen funds your project and the in-person event.

## Why Shenzhen, China?

We'll be demoing our projects at Maker Faire Shenzhen, in the manufacturing capital of the world. While we're there,
we'll visit local hardware spots like the world's largest electronics market.

## What do I build?

One large hardware project (think a month and a half of dedicated work!). Keep in mind you'll be demoing this project
in Shenzhen!
