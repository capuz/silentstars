# Y-Link

**Cultural Product Inbound/Outbound and O2O Reservation System**

An all-in-one inventory management platform for cultural and creative products, intangible cultural heritage, stores, and event scenarios

---

Y-Link covers the complete workflow of online reservation, offline write-off, outbound order creation, supplier stock-in, inventory tracking, and customer feedback. It has two ends, admin and client:

- Admin side: used by administrators, operations staff, and suppliers. Responsible for products, inventory, inbound and outbound records, write-off, users, configuration, and auditing.
- Client side: used by regular users. Responsible for registration and login, the product hall, shopping cart, reservation orders, order viewing, personal profile, and feedback.
