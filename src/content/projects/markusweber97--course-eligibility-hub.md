## Overview

Course Eligibility Checker simplifies qualification screening for students reviewing academic entry criteria. By filling out the required user details, applicants receive a dynamic assessment customized to their profile.

Engineered for friction-free evaluation, the application eliminates standalone software installations. Users launch the web-based utility, execute the questionnaire, and immediately determine whether they meet qualification standards.
