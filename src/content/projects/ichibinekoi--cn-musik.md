# Musik

**A lightweight, local-first music player for Android.**

Listen to the music on your device with playlists, favorites, a built-in tag editor and a carefully crafted interface — without accounts, ads or tracking.

## 🎵 Features

### Homepage

- The home screen brings your music library together in one place, giving you quick access to your music and the features you use most. With a clean layout and an intuitive design, it makes browsing your collection feel effortless. Designed to be more than just a starting point, the home screen provides a foundation for future additions such as smart playlists, personalized recommendations and dynamic music collections — helping you rediscover your favorite tracks and find the perfect soundtrack for every moment.

### Music library

- Browse your local music collection by **tracks, albums, playlists and favorites**.
- Search for tracks, artists and albums.
- Sort your library by name, date added or date modified.
- Choose between list and grid layouts, with configurable tile sizes.
