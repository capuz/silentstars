I built this project so I could keep writing the MathType formulas from my Word documents on an M-series Mac. Double-click a formula in Word to open the editor, make your changes, and they are written back to the original spot. You can also open the App on its own and write formulas with templates or LaTeX.

**[Download the 0.7.4 installer (build 108)](https://github.com/Ne1ther/EquationLab/releases/download/v0.7.4/Equation-Lab-0.7.4.pkg)** · [Release notes](https://github.com/Ne1ther/EquationLab/blob/HEAD/docs/releases.md) · [Installation and troubleshooting](https://github.com/Ne1ther/EquationLab/blob/HEAD/docs/install.md) · [All Releases](https://github.com/Ne1ther/EquationLab/releases)

> Supports **Apple Silicon (M-series) Macs running macOS 14 or later**. Word integration requires **the desktop version of Word for Mac**. The installer includes the App, the Word add-in, fonts, and the runtime, so there is no need to install MathType separately.
