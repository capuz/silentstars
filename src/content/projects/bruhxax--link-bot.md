# Link-Bot

Telegram bot, Mini App and web cabinet for Remnawave VPN subscriptions.

## Features

| Cabinet | Sales | Administration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Telegram and browser | Plans, trial, devices and traffic | Integrations, design and content |
| Subscriptions, balance and history | Payment systems and Telegram Stars | Finance, GA4 and Yandex Metrica |
| Tickets, FAQ and AI assistant | Promo codes, referrals and review rewards | Mailings and languages: RU / EN / FA |

The admin panel on a separate subdomain uses an independent dark gray palette.
The main page shows users, the distribution of active subscriptions, revenue
and charts for 7, 30 or 90 days, grouped by day, week and month.
Users and registration dates are taken from the entire Remnawave panel.
Plans are grouped by duration and traffic type: limited and unlimited.
The menu smoothly collapses to the logo and icons; charts highlight
the selected segment and show its count, and graphs animate period changes.
90-day data is loaded all at once: periods switch instantly, while the line
and chart fill smoothly change shape, keeping the current animation when pressed again.
