**The unofficial open-source Mac app for Higgsfield.**
Make video and images with Genjutsu, Seedance, Kling and Higgsfield's other models in a native studio, and keep every file on your disk.

[Download for macOS](https://github.com/jutsuhq/jutsu-studio/releases/latest) · [Quick start](#quick-start) · [Features](https://github.com/jutsuhq/jutsu-studio/blob/HEAD/docs/features.md) · [Website](https://jutsustudio.com/) · [Higgsfield prices](https://jutsustudio.com/higgsfield-pricing/)

## Demo

*Motion transfer with a location reference: drop the clip, add the image, write the prompt, generate. The queue shows each step, the result opens next to the source with a draggable before/after divider, and Compare plays two takes of the same clip side by side. [Full 53-second demo (MP4)](https://github.com/jutsuhq/jutsu-studio/blob/HEAD/docs/assets/demo.mp4)*

## Why Jutsu Studio

Higgsfield's web app is made for one generation at a time in a browser tab. Jutsu Studio is a native Mac app made for working through a project: many clips, many takes, and a library you keep.

- **Your files, on your disk.** Every result is downloaded to `~/Movies/Jutsu Studio/Videos` as soon as it's ready,…
