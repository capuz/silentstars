## Origin

When I was young, the elders in my family often subscribed to newspapers. However, as times changed, newspapers gradually disappeared from our view, replaced by the ubiquitous internet news.

In this vast ocean of information, content is abundant yet chaotic, containing a great deal of information. In reality, though, I don't care about many of the topics.

Therefore, I created this tool to automatically send tech news to my email every day.

This way, I no longer drown in a flood of information, but like when reading newspapers in the past, I can focus only on the parts I'm most interested…
