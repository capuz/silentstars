# Quosparr

A small sparrow perched in your menu bar, keeping watch over your Codex, Grok, and Claude quota.

Quosparr is a native menu bar / system tray app for macOS, Windows, and Linux. It shows Codex, Grok, and Claude Code subscription quota, reset credits, API-equivalent cost, and local sessions, with separate account management for each provider.

## Highlights

**Codex**

- Remaining quota for the 5-hour, weekly, and additional windows, plus the current account, subscription tier, and expiration.
- Reset credit count, status, and expiration time.
- Weekly allowance estimate from the official weekly percent and daily Credits, compared with previous weeks to spot quota cuts.
- Codex Reset Updates: whether Codex rate limits have reset today, with its own refresh…
