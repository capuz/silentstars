# <img src="assets/icon.svg" width="40" height="48" alt="Financial Intelligence Analysis" style="vertical-align: middle; margin-right: 10px;"> Financial Asset Intelligent Analysis and Diagnostic System

**Human-machine hybrid intelligence** financial quantitative research and engineering framework: it integrates **large-model analysis** with **machine learning forecasting**, covers **Hong Kong / A-shares**, and provides three general-purpose capabilities: **validation, diagnostics, and single-stock analysis**.

> ### In One Sentence
> This is a research platform that **runs, explains its results, and can be independently reviewed by third parties**. Its five capabilities carry equal weight:
> **① Hong Kong stock forecasting ② A-share forecasting ③ Hypothesis validation ④ Market diagnostics ⑤ Single-stock fundamental analysis**.
> Each capability documents its **inputs / outputs / scope of applicability**. Forecasting capabilities do not promise returns, and diagnostic/analysis capabilities do not replace investment decisions.
