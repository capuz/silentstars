# NetZapret

**For those who need more than one Zapret.** Desync fixes what the provider slows down, VPN handles what is blocked by country, and NetZapret decides where each traffic goes, for each site and service.

### [⬇ Download the latest version](https://github.com/RixyPow/netzapret/releases/latest)

Archive for Windows 10 and 11: unpack and run `NetZapret.exe`, nothing else needs to be installed.

Traffic dispatcher for Windows. For each service, domain or subnet it decides where traffic goes: **through desync** (Zapret / winws2), **through VPN** (sing-box) or **directly**, and launches the engines under one supervision.

It replaces the Zapret + VPN client combination and does what none of them does separately: **applies VPN selectively, without breaking desync for everything else.**

A VPN subscription is not required. Without it, desync, free WARP and a proxy for Telegram Desktop work: Telegram with photos and videos without any VPN.
