# NetZapret

**For those who can't settle for just one Zapret.** Desync fixes what your provider throttles,
VPN takes what's blocked in your country — and NetZapret decides where each goes, for every
website and service.

### [⬇ Download the latest version](https://github.com/RixyPow/netzapret/releases/latest)

Archive for Windows 10 and 11: extract and run `NetZapret.exe`, no additional installation needed.

Traffic dispatcher for Windows. For each service, domain or subnet, decides
where to route traffic: **through desync** (Zapret / winws2), **through VPN** (sing-box)
or **directly** — and launches engines under one control.

Replaces the Zapret + VPN client bundle and does what neither can do separately:
**applies VPN selectively, without breaking desync for everything else.**

VPN subscription is optional. Without it, desync works, free WARP
and proxy for Telegram Desktop work — Telegram with photos and videos without any VPN.
