# IWE — Intellectual Work Environment

> Operating system for intellectual work. Your knowledge. Your experience. Your environment — runs on top of any AI platform.
>
> **Repository type:** `Base/Formats` (FMT) — template distribution. After forking, it becomes your personal environment with AI agents.

---

## Problem

AI assistants can generate text, code, and answers. But most users face the same problems:

- **Context gets lost.** Every new session with AI starts from scratch. Yesterday's decisions, plans, and agreements are forgotten.
- **Knowledge stays in your head.** You took a course, read a book, solved a problem — but a month later you can't reproduce the line of thinking.
- **AI replaces thinking instead of amplifying it.** You get an answer, but you don't become more competent. Without AI — back to zero.
- **No system.** Plans are in notes, tasks are in your head, knowledge is in chats. Everything is scattered.
- **Time leaks away.** It's unclear what you worked on, what you accomplished, where you're heading.
