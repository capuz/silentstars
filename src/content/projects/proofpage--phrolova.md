# 🎬 Phrolova

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎯 **Multi-Platform Live & VOD Capture**: Reliable live stream recording and high-speed VOD capture for CHZZK, SOOP, YouTube, Kick, Twitch, TikTok, Bilibili, PandaTV, PopkonTV, RPLAY, CIME, TwitCasting, Spoon, and Berriz.
- 🔁 **24/7 Automated Guardian Monitoring**: Continuously checks broadcast statuses, automatically triggers recording when streams start, and recovers from CDN disconnects or token expirations without data loss.
- 📅 **Per-Channel Scheduled Windows**: Avoid 24/7 continuous polling by restricting monitoring to specific weekdays and hours with early check-ins and late-end protections.
- 📊 **Verified Resolution & Frame Rate Display**: Analyzes live HLS renditions to display exact stream dimensions and frame rates (e.g., `1920×1080 · 60fps`) in recording details and quality selectors.
- ⏱️ **CHZZK Time Machine Recording**: Rewind and record live CHZZK streams from the broadcast start, even if monitoring began late.
- ⚡ **Standalone Windows Executable**: Portable single-file release (`.exe`). No complex Python setup or environment configuration required.
