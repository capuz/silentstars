## About the project

**Mini-Station** is a non-commercial Russian-language project for Space Station 14. We develop our own build of the game: we translate content, add new mechanics and items, adjust balance, and maintain a server for our community.

The build is based on [Goob Station](https://github.com/Goob-Station/Goob-Station) and runs on the [RobustToolbox](https://github.com/space-wizards/RobustToolbox) engine.

- **Russian language.** Localization of the game, the wiki, and community communication.
- **Our own content.** Daily and weekly quests, voiceover of text (TTS), new items, roles, and events.
- **Ready-made builds.** Versions for Windows, Linux, and macOS are updated every night.
- **AI in the project.** We deeply integrate artificial intelligence: it helps us develop, translate, and maintain the server.
