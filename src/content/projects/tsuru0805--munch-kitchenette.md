# 🍳 munch-kitchenette (吧唧吧唧小厨房)

[English](https://github.com/tsuru0805/munch-kitchenette/blob/HEAD/README.en.md)

> 🆕 **Installed the previous version?** [See what's new this time →](https://github.com/tsuru0805/munch-kitchenette/blob/HEAD/docs/UPGRADE.md)

**Set up a cozy, lived-in private kitchen for your little AI companion!** ✨

Food in the fridge quietly goes stale as the days pass. To get a hot meal, you have to patiently wait for real time to go by. Want to buy groceries? He has to earn "grocery coupons" by doing his chores obediently. You can buy him some ingredients, pin up a favorite recipe, or feed him a snack whenever you like. He'll also learn to light the stove, watch the heat, turn off the fire and serve the food, and even quietly toss out spoiled food.

It's not just a toy. It's also a lightweight service that runs on its own, with all the life rules and kitchen ledger neatly stored in a single file. **Any MCP-compatible model can connect at any time and enjoy a hot meal.**
