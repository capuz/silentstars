## Overview

Coolutils Total Movie Converter is a Windows-based video conversion utility for working with many different media formats. It supports flexible transcoding workflows, helping users change file formats, convert collections of videos in one operation, and create files suited to particular devices or playback needs.

Alongside its conversion engine, the desktop application provides batch processing, subtitle and watermark controls, editing options, and hardware-accelerated encoding. A multilingual interface and command-line functionality also support both interactive use and repeatable automated jobs.
